# Yogya 1817

van der Kemp  
M.N.I.  
(Buitenzor in 1817).  

p. 24 31 March 1817 no. 33  
Residents of near Yogya  
ordered to make a population census in 2 weeks!  

Pop: census  
Post-Residents think this can only take place  
& a combined expedition from both courts  
with Kraton representatives.  
But they feared unrest would ensue population.  

1 May 1817 order rescinded.  

Report 14 Apr. 1817 by Nahuys important,  
not only for character sketch of PA,  
+ on argument for retirement  
+ European in VK.  
+ Dutch policy.  

Report of Nahuys:  
"Great number of Europeans in VK and home-trade.  
Standard of Industry.  
Javanese round Semarang, Surabaya, Kedu, Yogya  
at present ill at ease."  

Dutch NOHO poor manager on industry  
at present only initiative in hands of Chinese.  
Min[ister] Residents governing Dutch.  
Greater numbers Dutch should settle in interior  
would be a safeguard against attack by a  
European enemy.
