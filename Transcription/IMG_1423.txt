**1784**

---

*   Surma - By prediction is that great influence over Yogya Princes.
*   Get long-way money & arm.
*   Big ornament & arm.
*   ... for him to be exile from Java.
*   Umar Mahdi = Sentenced to death for part in Banten disturbance 1817 Jan. But sentence commuted because appear to be mad. Also had to exile from Java.
*   But to be exile over life to Ambon. To be next sent with *solid proof* to Semarang.
*   Good - to keep Surma in custody of Res. to be returned to Res. in Java.
*   Insp. Gen. Inkomster (Van Pabst)
    *   Govt. enquiry if arms in Java without Govt. European colonization - wool cloth etc. from Major Gen. population census.
    *   *Nyoteng* land rent.
    *   *Easy Transport/Access*.
    *   Encroachment for sugar culture - no ad hoc clause.
    *   *13 Col. Comm. Gen. 5 Dec. 1817 no 22.*
    *   For delivery of sugar to Govt.
