# Extortions - Yogya 1819 (Nov.-Dec. 1819)

---

## Pahinita Revenue (Nov.-Dec. 1819)

- **Nakuyt:** Rep. of Finance (Itineraray) & Report on *Wang Perati* – purveyor extorting tax raised by Chinese Tax Farmer. Should be poreaway with the *Nalagays*. **Riva currency**
- **Q. harz:** *Pahinita* – money paid to Bandang Tolgate for protection goods. Although no Riau currency.
- **③ harz Mutian** – Raised at Pasay Gede / Imegin / Tinlayat Capitan was to report on cost + write to Jara & order cloths.

---

## Nakuyt & Fin. Itineraray, 26 Nov. 1819

- **Ij & Imp-9:** Itineraray (Itinerary) – *Each year* 120 *Brah* rest (6 battus)
- **18 Nov. 1819:** 3 X p.a. (at each collection rest) given to Sutan.

---

## Nakuyt & Rs. Pasuruam, 22 Nov. 1819

- **7 Dec. 1819:** Br. Rawa takes 11 Jtuj provision in Residency guilty in malang.
- **7 Dec. 1819:** Id to Opicirer (Engu)
- **Painan:** Asks for Report or plantation of coffee in *Paitch*.

---

## Nakuyt & Imp., Finanza (Itineraray), 9 Dec. 1819

- Monies in Yogya: private persons rent enter, from govaut officials or Yogyarese.
- Illegal ground tax.

---

## Nakuyt & Ag. Necr., 1-21 Dec. 1819

- **Harvey Johnson:** Came to KN. 4 Aug. 1811. Permission to settle in N.I. 12 Nov. 1817 no. 6.
- Used surat permission to letter on landowner.

---

## Nakuyt Report Nall, 19 Aug. 1819

- **Allusions Toho on Solo River by Brit. servant** makes purchasing N. cost natl much dearer. But makunsmuggling more easy.
- **Restoration tolls on Solo R.**
- Chinese prov. on free & Swedish / Narabaya
