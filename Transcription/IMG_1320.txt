# Yogyakarta Aug 1816
## Nahuys to Commissioners General
P. H. van der Kemp, *Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1817, tot het vertrek der Engelschen naar oorspronkelijke stukken*, 's-Gravenhage, 1913.

### Nahuys reaches 9 August
Josya [^1] begon arrival of puter troops [^2].
14 Aug. takes over authority from Crawford.

[^1]: *Note: Likely refers to Jogya (Yogyakarta).*
[^2]: *Note: Likely refers to Pieter's troops.*
