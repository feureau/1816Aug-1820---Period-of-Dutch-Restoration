#### 4 Dec. 1818
* **Jogya**
* **v Comm. Gen.**

> E.H.B. it. will would (in order continue to make war) than receive an indemnity from the Government which would keep them else. Beside very little work in reward. Very legal

No one in Yogya willing test
A Buy Northern Vault:
* Comm. Gen. asked to ratify
* Appointments. Some Burgers in Yogya by Peri Governor.

---

#### 5 Jan 1818
* **VVB to Comm. Gen.**

> Binderi Attie & Kyai Moorma
> wowara Public auction:
> some income vanis hin/done for two relations:
> relations according to Javanese law should
> be included in the punishment.
> But pleased to get a loan from the
> Governor.
> Kyai Moorma also pleased
> that not all his belongings confiscated by the
> Government.

---
