10 Sept. 1816. Lettering Van IJ.
From Java:

Duran's letter to C-G = not wholly
compliments to Comm-Gen. as is
cost letting Duran & Randall.

Van IJ
Asks Patik of Noesa = says Duran thinks it
not suitable for a liaser to send compliments
to a greater = when they had not met.

But instead of Duran's letters = none attachment for
new Dutch govt. an Ahmad had von
Bnzith.

N.T. J.g. phrasing, but not equivocal.

Van IJ = knows attachment of Duran's British
not v. great.

But Patik answers Noesa-van IJ that
dura has no other meaning.
Pledging loyalty / R.B. IV
genuinely pleased return
9 Dutch.

Despite talk + Patik
necessary to keep an eye on Duran.
loodward / Koortswijk / Duran.

Between English + Sultan of Madura = closer relationship
was C-G been necessary.
No cause for Disquiet. But should keep an eye
on court.
Wait for Troop reinforcements in Java.
