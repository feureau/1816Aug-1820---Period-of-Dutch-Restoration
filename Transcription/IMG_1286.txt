## Body Text

Jane Doyo aput capture of Kyai Roonmo H.B.W. asks Krijgsman to spend a day in his garden at Djerogoas as I was riding a look my way out of my pocket. As I was riding I took my ward out of my pocket, willingly have with awarth. [^1]

Continuing that the Durang of Delo had diverse and cachecta to the Duran. There therefore forced to offer my gold watch, chain and cachecta to the Duran. Remarks that the capture of Kyai Roonmo had parted from the Duran.

To let H.B.W. know that the Govrnmt Regard the Affair as only the weakness of his mother. + had no suspicion against H.B.W.

*Or return to Katon:*
H.B.W. show the watch to the Raten who were delighted by it. Krijgsman that for compensation for my presence I can draw 200 jp.a. from the Residency Treasury.
