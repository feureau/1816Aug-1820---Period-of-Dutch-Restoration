IJsseldijk 1816
Rapport van W. H. van IJsseldijk omtrent de Vorstenlanden.
Band private coll. no. 306

11 Dec. 1816
In Band, private coll.?
26 a/2 - Signed in Jogja 22 Oct. 1816.

**p1.**
2 Aug. 1816: Van IJ is appointed ad Buitenzorg gezet

Leaves Batavia 02 Zeepleg = arrives Samarang 17 Aug. 1816.
Give notice to Res. & Ngala/Yogya.
See Appendix no. 1.

N.D. Kruseman = Secretary of gezantschap
aim to go to Batavia
but journey cancelled

{
Introductory Patingala -
Narrating rat.
+ other ambanazon from Ngala
}
compliments given to G.G.

**p2.**
Idea of fixing ceremonial for van IJ's visit to Surakarta - but rejected - would he worked out + van Haak first.
want letter G.G. to PB IV to take it back. But -
van IJ pry in to give it to van Haak.
thereupon Patih / ambassador to PB IV return.

**p3.**
24 Aug. 1816: arrival of Kruseman =
Bringing letters y van der Capelen -> Ngaran/Nultan.
Kruseman takes oath of van IJ in presence of Res. Parvé y Samarang.
