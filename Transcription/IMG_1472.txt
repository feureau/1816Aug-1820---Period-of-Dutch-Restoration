Yogyakarta Letters
1819.
Pj. Pr. 62 A
Jan. 1819 - March 1819
Pacitan.

### BCG, 7 Jan. 1819 no. 5
PACITAN

*   No. 355 26 Dec. 1817 no. 38 (11:1)
*   Res. to be included in karisklijk stelsel [^1]
*   Res. to report on pop. in Pacitan within 3 months
*   (i) Kach. Family to plant 500 pepper bushes.
*   (ii) + between pepper bushes = coffee to be planted. Res. to now coffee needs (2 piculs).
*   If not enough coffee plants
*   Res. to now coffee needs (2 piculs). [^2]
*   coffee/pepper to be grown exclusively to govern.
*   coffee/pepper to be production of cash crops.

## Footnotes

[^1]: *karisklijk stelsel*: Likely a misspelling or variation of *landsdrukkerij* or similar administrative term in the original Dutch colonial records.
[^2]: Repeated note in original text regarding coffee needs.
[^3]: Alternative spelling variant of *Pananjung/Jogtakarya* or associated official name.
[^4]: "aport" is likely a misspelling of *export* or *port* in the manuscript.
[^5]: "Actet der Zirakrich" reflects original spelling/handwriting of Dutch colonial administrative terminology.
