# Yogya Res. Letter Jan - Jun. 1817
*Jan - May 1817*

## Nahuy
Lt. Pro.
Ag. Kekahanan
25 Jan. 1817

Kierchman wants our Residency again to Nahuy.

### BSS 12 Jan. 1817 No. 1.
Rec. Yogya's letter Besides sending 6 pitas [^1]
No. 201. Nowices wr. on N.C.O. also name & pupi
Paku Alam
troops.

### Bg. Pro. Read
Var. Kianteri,
13 Jan. 1817 No. 7

Playing out of circulation Bad to war.
To get no.s of natives within 3 months.
Copper Pitas

### Van Hoak
K. Nahuy,
13 Jan. 1817

## Nurawedara, Rinang & Pera membawa no. had
Robbed Pesa g. Brurjah (Pekalongan)
Kies to Yogya takes Newice as magang at const.

### Kuppe (Asst. Res. Kedu)
G. Parve (Res. Semarang)
