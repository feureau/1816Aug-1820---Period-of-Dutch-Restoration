Dear Prof. Drewes,

I was extremely grateful for all your kind help in correcting the passages of the millenarian texts, and I really appreciated having the chance to meet you again in Leiden. As I think I mentioned to you, I have asked the Hon. Secretary and Editor of the Malay Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society to send you a copy of the *Babad Dipanagara* text and I would really appreciate your comments when you have had the occasion to look at it in the meantime I thought it might be of assistance if I sent you the original Dutch version of the Banyumas text which I used, in case you have any criticisms about my translation and interpretation. It reads as follows:
