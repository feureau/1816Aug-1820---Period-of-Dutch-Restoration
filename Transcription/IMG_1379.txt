# Yogya Res. Letter.
## Jan. - June, 1817
## Pr. Br. 60

Kippe (Temp. Res. Nata)
to Nakuyi,
9 Jun. 1817.

NEPOYN[^1]

Kippe -
Nakuyi,
2 Jul. 1817.

P. to DS
19 Jun. 1817.

de Nati (Ro. Kedhu)
to Nakuyi,
29 Jul. 1817.

## PACITAN

new opponent Krama.
Bringing 3 sepoys to Yogya
autorgh Kanca peddon from Res. of Batavia to travel to
Yogya - suspected of being in league w. P. Mangku bumi (Wela)
New in light Infantry Battalion.
Penutran Battalion.
Pretends to be engaged in trade in Wota.
But perhaps Exiles run from Nata.

## Footnotes

[^1]: Nepoyr.
