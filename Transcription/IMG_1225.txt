# Aug. 1816 - Sept. 1816

## August 1816

* **Bukit CCGS, 16 Aug. 1816**
  J. Crawford to remain in central Java, to see to embarking Brit. troops.
* **Comptroller (Penang) to Nakusyt, 17 Aug. 1816**
  Departing Sepoy troops from Klaten garrison - Burn to be garstangong.
* **BCCGS, 19 Aug. 1816**
  Official handing over Inland by British in Batavia.
* **CCGS, 26 Aug. 1816**
  Letter Nakusyt to CCGS 11 Aug. 1816 on removal pince hospital in Yogya. To be continued on name posting to Nakusyt no new patients to Kraden on.
* **Res. Birjoes to Res. Semarang Parve, 28 Aug. 1816**
  Disputing a will - Murjand had owned 8 houses in Yogya, 4 in Semarang + large store house in Gang Tengah in Semarang, 2000 Rp. D., 300 in cash, asking for report on Ng II Gold/ jewels.
* **Van In to Nakusyt, 30 Aug. 1816**
  Reporting arrival in Nala 30 Aug. 1816.
* **BCCGS, 30 Aug. 1816**
  [Redacted/Illegible notes regarding punitive expedition/raid]
* **Penang Nura Kidra 9, 30 Aug. 1816**
  Ridjery Penandgan at Nambana - on Bordus + Yogya area tentara kulon. Lurah Nara Tramiro, Nitokangan, undu Yogya - Republik.
