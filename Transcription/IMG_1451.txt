Regerings Reglement (Beleid van het Regering)
Art. 97 Reglement on conduct of government
To set up schools in Java
For primary Education.

*   Asks for information on native schools in Residency.
*   Ways in which schools can be improved.
    *   Facilities increased.
    *   According to recommendations by Bijbel zending Grootichap.

**Great numbers of schools in Batavia area:**
*   mainly teaching recitation Koran
    *   writing + Arabic letters.

**Jav. priests =**
*   paid by parents in children for education
*   some Chiwang poor parents given free.
*   No supervision of priest in pesantrys.

**Pupils only able to recite Arabic words; they do not understand.**
*   Govrut. should not involve it self in these schools.
*   popular r. attached to them.

**No schools of higher learning to teach rudiments of**
*   writing + Latin (Nups) arithmetic
*   *by Bijbel zending Grootichap.*

**Govrut. should encourage new schools = Free religion**
*   Teacher should not be appointed by *Bijbel zending Grootichap*.
*   **No statistics given.**
