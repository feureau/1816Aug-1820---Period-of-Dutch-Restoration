# Appendix VIIb: List of Kyai, Haji and Religious Officials Associated with Dipanagara


- List of kyai, haji and religious officials associated

- with Dipanagara[^1]

- Name Titles Location, position and relationship

- Abdulgani Haji panakawan of Pangéran Jayakusuma (post-1825, Pangéran Ngabèhi). Abduljaki Haji accompanied Dipanagara to Magelang. Abdulkadir Sèh Haji dullah of the Bulkio regiment, also known as Haji Mustapa (Adimustapa) and Tumenggung Urawan (Yudapenawang). Abdullah Sèh ulama ageng of Papringan. Abdullatip Sèh Haji ulama of the pesantrèn of Kasongan, killed in fighting at Selarong in October 1825. Abdulrahman Kyai guru guru and pradikan ageng of the pesantrèn (Sèh Abutalah) of Kuweron (Kedhu). Abdulrahman Kyai Gambiran. Abdulraup Kyai guru guru and ulama ageng of the pesantrèn of (Abdulrangup) Kasongan near Selarong. Abdulrahim Kyai Pulo Kadang. Abdulwahab Haji follower of Kyai Maja from the pradikan désa of Maja. Abukasan Kyai abdi-dalem of Dipanagara at Tegalreja and son of Kyai Jayamustapa. Abuyamin Kyai Pengulu pengulu of Yogya (1823-25) and supporter (earlier Ketib) of Dipanagara. Adam, Hasani Kyai pradikan désa of Wotgalèh, Maja.

- Name Titles Location, position and relationship

- Ahmad Sèh full name Sèh Abdul Ahmad bin Abdullah al-Ansari (alias Ahmad Ansar Sharif, alias Sèh Habib Ahmat al-Ansari). Arab trader from Jeddah who had married into the family of Pangéran Blitar I and served as adviser of Dipanagara at Tegalreja and during the war. Submitted January 1828. Ahmad Sèh son-in-law of the above and adviser of Dipanagara at Tegalreja. Killed in fighting at Selarong in October 1825. Ali, Iman Kyai from pradikan désa of Melangi. Ali Haji guru and pradikan ageng of Melangi (Muhamad bin-Ngali) (also known as Haji Muhamad Salim). Killed at Kalijengking, September 1825. See further Pekih, Muhamad. Ali, Muhamad Haji follower of Kyai Maja from Kali Cebong/ Karang. Exiled to Manado (1830). One of the two haji in Maja’s entourage (the other was Haji Amad Tajib) whom Dipanagara wished to have with him for a lengthy stay in Manado, but who refused to join him, Knoerle, ‘Journal’, 51. Alwi Haji wedana (commander) of Suranatan corps Amad Pekih Kyai Haji see under Pekih. Amad Rawi Kyai dullah of the Arkio regiment. Amattakar Haji confidante of Hamengkubuwana II; (Jayamenggala) later appointed demang of the désa of Samèn: a follower of Dipanagara and expert in gunpowder manufacture. Also known as Kyai Muhammadahir and listed as holding lands in Jatingarang (? in the Nanggulon sub-district of Kulon Praga), Carey and Hoadley 2000:332. Andataruna Kyai from Rejasa (Kulon Praga). Anggamerta Kyai from Grobogan, later appointed as bupati of Grobogan by Pangéran Sérang. Ansari Sèh see under Ahmad. Ansari Sèh ulama; served Dipanagara in Bagelèn. Asnawi, Muhamad Sèh ulama from Gadhing (Kedhu); also referred to as Sèh Muhamad. Atmawijaya Radèn son of a ? haji, served (Bagus Bakir) Hamengkubuwana II in Ambon; appointed Radèn Atmawijaya.

- Badarudin Haji lurah of the Suranatan regiment in Yogya, served Dipanagara in the same capacity; made the haj twice; from the désa of Ngawèn, west of Yogya and just to the north of Tegalreja. Appointed pengulu of Bagelèn after the Java War. Badhèran Kyai see under Wirapati. Bahwi, Muhamad Kyai pengulu of Ratu Ageng at Tegalreja (1793-1803), became the basah of the Bulkio regiment with the name of Muhamad Ngusman Ali Basah. Baina, Ahmad Kyai Kajoran. Baji Kyai Gorangan. Balad Kyai jurukunci pasaréyan Imagiri. Banjarsari Kyai pradikan ageng and guru of the pesan trèn Banjarsari (Madiun); supporter of Dipanagara, see also Tapsir Anom. Barmawi Sèh Kyai senior ulama from Celereng (Kulon Praga). Béji Kyai Cucuhan. Besari, Hasan Kyai see under Hasan Besari. Brajayuda Demang Krapyak. Budu Kyai Elo Gedhé. Bunari, Muhamad Kyai abdi-dalem of Dipanagara at Tegalreja. Busu Kyai Kemasan. Citrajaya Kyai Krapyak. Dhadhapan Kyai see under Gajali (Ajali). Diman (Ngadiman) Kyai Demangan. Duku Kyai Gurangan. Édris (Idris) Sèh Kyai associate of Umar Mahdi, Bagelèn (1817), pengulu of landraad (1830), associate of Hamengkubuwana II. Élias Bagus a son of Hasan Besari and nephew of Kyai (Tumenggung Pajang) Maja; pradikan of Karang; appointed as Tumenggung Pajang in 1825. One of Dipanagara’s bravest and youngest military commanders. Élias Kyai from pradikan désa of Maja. Gajali (Ajali) Kyai pradikan of Bendhasari (Kedhu), a son of Kyai Musa and a nephew of Kyai Taptajani. Gajali (Ajali) Kyai see Kyai Taptajani. Gajali (Ajali) Kyai Iman ulama from Dhadhapan (Kyai Dhadhapan) (Tèmpèl, Yogya); ? relation of Kyai Maja. Gamawijaya Kyai from Panjer (Bagelèn), a follower of Dipanagara. Gedhung Gubah Nyai wife of Haji Imanraji.

- Name Titles Location, position and relationship

- Gendhong Kyai Gadhing. Hasan Haji lurah of the Suryagama corps in Yogyakarta, served Dipanagara in the same capacity. Made the haj twice. Hasan Besari Kyai pradikan ageng and guru pesantrèn of Tegalsari (Panaraga); sympathetic to Dipanagara but afforded him no active support. Hasan Besari Kyai elder brother of Kyai Maja from Maja, served Dipanagara as a basah. May have died in prison in Batavia. Cleerens men tions the death of a ‘Kyai Hasan Besari’ on 16-1-1830, dK 49, Cleerens (Panjer) to H.M. de Kock (Magelang), 16-1-1830. Hasan, Muhamad Nyai sister of Kyai Maja from Pulo Kadang. Hasan Munadi see under Samparwedi. Husein Kyai Kejené. Ibrahim Haji a confidante of Hamengkubuwana II from the désa of Wanasari (near Boyolali). Served Dipanagara. Ibrahim, Pekih see Kusèn, Muhamad. Ibrahim Muhamad Wonggo. Follower of Kyai Maja. Idris Kyai see Édris Imam, Hasan Kyai Mugup. Imampura Kyai son of Kyai Maja, studied at Tegalsari. Imamraji Haji ulama from Papringan (also mentioned as from Ngapaingan to the east of Yogya); served Dipanagara as pengulu (1825-1828); earlier a pupil of Kyai Maja. Later married Nyai Gedhung Gubah. Imamraji Kyai Melangi. Iskak Kyai follower of Kyai Maja from Bojong. Ismangil Sèh Kyai ulama ageng from Gerajèn (? Krajan, south of Yogyakarta). Jaèlani, Muhamad Kyai abdi-dalem of Dipanagara at Tegalreja, from the désa of Béji to the west of Yogyakarta. Jagakarya Kyai from Tegal (pasisir); served Dipanagara. Jagaprawira Demang Pluning. Jagasura Sèh pradikan désa unknown. Jalasutra, Muhamad Kyai jurukunci from Jalasutra. Jamjani Kyai amad-dalem (jurukunci) of Kutha Gedhé (present day Kota Gede). Janom Kyai abdi-dalem of Dipanagara at Tegalreja; appointed as tumenggung by Dipanagara in 1825.

- Jayanodin Kyai abdi-dalem of Dipanagara at Tegalreja. Jayaprawira Mas Abdullah son of Kyai Melangi submits 1828. Jayamenggala see under Amattakar. Jemetri Kyai from pradikan désa of Maja. Jenal Ngabidin Kyai Pengulu pengulu of Madiun, and close associate of Kyai Banjarsari. Also known as Zainal Ngabidin. Jenari, Ahmad Kyai Pecawor (? Pejawaran). Jinan, Imam Sèh member of the Suryagama regiment in Yogyakarta and a follower of Dipanagara. Jirengga Kyai member of the pasukan (troop) of Jaya mustapa. Kalifah, Bagus see under Maja. Kalifah Kyai jurutulis (scribe) of Kyai Maja. Kamalodiningrat Haji pengulu of Yogya, see Abuyamin, Ketib. Kamprèt Kyai from pradikan désa of Maja. Karang Kyai from pradikan désa of Karang. Karip Kyai from pradikan désa of Maja. Kasidin Kyai Jelegong. Kasiman Kyai from pradikan désa of Maja. Kastuba Bagus ulama of Jatinom. Kastuba, Berangkal Sèh senior ulama in Bagelèn, guru of pesantrèn of Alang-alang Ombo. Kastuba, Muhamad Tumenggung earlier pengulu of Prapak, Medhana, Kedhu. Kawis Sèh ulama; follower of Kyai Maja. Kemis Kyai Getas. Kenapi Kyai Tulang. Kenapi Kyai Lungé. Kertajaya Kyai Imagiri. Kertajaya Kyai Candhèran. Ketib, Iman Kyai kaum from Yogya. Kuncong Kyai Bakungan. Kurmèn Imam nephew of Kyai Maja. Kusasi Kyai from pradikan désa of Karang. Kusèn, Muhamad Kyai nephew of Kyai Pengulu Pekih Ibrahim, pengulu of Yogya (c. 1755-1798); appointed as the pengulu of Dipanagara with the name of Pekih Ibrahim (1828-1830). Later friend of Radèn Tumenggung Wiranagara (post-1829, Pangéran Adipati Prabuningrat). Kuwaron Kyai from pradikan désa of Kuwaron; an older ulama well versed in the Qur’ān whom Dipanagara summoned to Selarong along with Kyai Maja at the start of the Java War.

Melangi Kyai see under Ali. Mesir Kyai from pradikan désa of Badhèran. Mopid Kyai ulama from Guyangan (near Balong), also served as a jurukunci. Follower of Dipanagara at Tegalreja. Musa Kyai Haji ulama from Kedhu, brother of Haji Mudha and father of Kyai Gajali, pradikan of Bendhasari. Mustapa Sèh Haji dullah of the Bulkio regiment; a colleague of Haji Abdulkadir. Ngarfah, Muhamad Kyai ulama from Kedhu. Joined Dipanagara at Selarong; appointed as Tumenggung Secanegara; a brother of Mas Mulyasentika (later Tumenggung Kertanegara). Ngarip, Muhamad Kyai ulama from Melangi, also known as Ahmad Ngarip, adviser of Dipanagara at Tegalreja. Later (1830) joined Pangéran Prabuningrat and became his guru. Ngisa Haji follower of Kyai Maja and pradikan from Pulo Kadang; served Dipanagara as a dullah and advised him at the Magelang conference (28-3-1830). Described as acting the role of jester (hansworst) in Dipanagara’s entourage at Menorèh (21-2-1830 – 6-3-1830), Chapter XII note 75.

- Ngusman, Amad Kyai lurah of the Suranatan corps in Yogyakarta and abdi-dalem of the kadipatèn; later tutor of Hamengkubuwana IV. Ngusman, Muhamad Ali Basah see Bahwi, Amad. Nitipraja Kyai pathok negari (ulama); son of the jaksa (magistrate) of Yogyakarta Kyai Nitipraja; appointed by Dipanagara to follow his father. Nurngali Sèh Bengali dhukun (physician and herbalist) of Dipanagara; possibly a sepoy deserter who stayed on after 1816. Paci Kyai Raman. Pekih, Muhamad Kyai Melangi. Also known as Kyai Melangi. Son of Haji Ali, guru and pradikan ageng of Melangi (killed September 1825). Rahman Kyai from Mamdu, pradikan désa of Maja. Rahmanudin Kyai Pengulu pengulu of Yogyakarta (1812-1823); a confidante of Dipanagara. Made the haj in 1825 after his dismissal as pengulu by Danureja IV. Raniman Kyai Maja. Resadilaga Demang Sambing. Resakusuma Kyai chief jurukunci at Imagiri. Married to a (Reksakusuma) daughter of Hamengkubuwana II. Resamenggala Kyai bekel wong pinggir from Wanakriya. Resanegara Tumenggung pradikan, follower and nephew of Kyai Maja from Pulo Kadang. Resayuda Kyai modin of Cilacap (Banyumas); afforded help to Kyai Modin, Kyai Jayanodin and Kyai Jayamustapa on their pilgrimage to Nusa Kambangan. Ripangi Kyai amad-dalem of Kutha Gedhé. Joined Dipanagara and appointed as Amad Dullah Tumenggung Resasentana, bupati of Kutha Gedhé (26-3-1826). Sahidin Bagus Santri from pradikan désa of Badhèran, later at Tulung Miliran. Semangun Kyai from traitors’ graveyard of Banyusumurup. Sepawi Kyai Suro (? Banyumas). Seri Kyai Gambiran. Setrawijaya Kyai from pradikan désa of Mutihan. Singadangsa Kyai Wotgalèh. Was one of two members of Kyai Maja’s entourage – the other was Haji Muhamad Ali – whom Dipanagara wished to have with him for a lengthy period in Manado, but who refused to join him, Knoerle, ‘Journal’, 51. Tapsir Anom Kyai pradikan ageng of Banjarsari (Panaraga). Taptajani Kyai ? of Arab descent; guru and pradikan ageng of Melangi until 1805; moved to Surakarta; associated with Kyai Maja and a brother-in-law of Kyai Musa. Tutor of Pangéran Adisurya, mystic brother of Dipanagara.

## Notes


[^1]: The details have been taken from BD (Manado), II-IV: B.Ng.
