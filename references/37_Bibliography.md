# Bibliography

## Manuscripts

### Javanese

#### Babad and other texts

- Babad Dipanagara. LOr 6547a-d. Copy of the original autobiography written under Dipanagara’s orders in Manado in 1831-1832. Copy probably made in the 1880s for Professor G.A.J. Hazeu. Four vols. 408 pp., 401 pp., 372 pp., 43 cantos.

- Babad Dipanagaran. (Jayabaya version). Nederlands Bijbelgenootschap (Netherlands Bible Society) MS 37, pp. 5-9. Dated AJ 1742 (AD 1814). Written in Yogyakarta.

- Babad Ngayogyakarta. Vols. I-III. Sana Budaya Museum (Yogyakarta) MS A. 135, A. 136, A. 144. Copies dated AJ 1833 (AD 1903), AJ 1834 (AD 1904), AJ 1836 (AD 1906). 407 pp., 336 pp., 460 pp., 100 cantos, 73 cantos, 76 cantos. Original written in Yogyakarta by Pangéran Suryanagara and Radèn Adipati Danureja V and completed in AJ 1805 (AD 1876).

- Babad Pakualaman. KITLV Or 188. Fragments of a deluxe table book in quadratic Yogyakarta script with coloured illuminated pages containing short texts. Late 19th century. Unfinished. 7 leaves in cardboard frames.

- Babad Spèhi. LOr 6791 (3). Romanised. 176 pp., 15 cantos. Copy of an MS written by Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I of Yogyakarta during his exile in Pulau Pinang (1812-1815). Original MS dated 8 Rejeb AH 1228 (AD 7-7-1813).

- Buku Kedhung Kebo. LOr 2163. MS commenced on 12 Sawal, AJ 1770 (AD 14-11-1842) and completed sometime in AJ 1771 (AD 1843). 623 pp., 50 cantos. Written in Purwareja (Bagelèn) on the orders of Radèn Adipati Cakranagara I, bupati of Purwareja (1830-1862) with the help of Dipanagara’s erstwhile army commander, Ali Basah Pengalasan.

- Buku Kedhung Kebo. KITLV Or 13. Dated 29 Jumadilakir AJ 1795 (7-11-1866). 200 folios, 18 cantos. Copied in Semarang by Radèn Panji Jayasupraja. An incomplete version of the Buku Kedhung Kebo written in Purwareja (Bagelèn) on the orders of Cakranagara I (see above).

- Diary of events which occurred in the Purwareja Regency (Bagelèn) 1830-1862. (Original German title: ‘Historischer Überblick über die Ereignisse in der Provinz Baglan auf Java wahrend der Ambtsführung der Residenten Jhhr. I.G.O.S. von Schmidt auf Altenstadt, R. de Filiotaz [Fillietaz] Bousquet und A.W. Kinder de Camarecq wahrend der Jahre 1830-1862, bearbeitet von Raden Adi Pati Tjokro Negoro, Regent von Purworedjo in Baglen. Besuch des Herzogs Bernhard von Sachsen Weimar in Baglen’). Berlin Staatsbibliothek, MS or fol. 568, 181 pp. Diary written in Purwareja (Bagelèn) by Radèn Adipati Cakranagara I (in office 1830-1862).

- Gambar-gambar kanthi keterangan plabuhan-Dalem dhumateng redi-redi saha dhateng seganten kidul nuju tingalan-Dalem jumenengan (verjaardag van de troonsbestijging). Museum Pusat (Jakarta) MS 934 DJ. (Ir. Moens Platen Album no. 9), 23 coloured drawings with Javanese legends.

- Makassar MS. Books I and II. MS privately owned by Raden Mas Jusuf Diponegoro and Raden Saleh Diponegoro, Jalan Irian 83, Makassar. Book I dated 5 Dulkijah AJ 1765 (AD 24-1-1838). 175 pp. and 241 pp., octavo. Prose. Written in Makassar by Pangéran Dipanagara in pégon.

- Patih séda kadaton; Anggitanipoen J.B. Tjiptawinata H.I.S. Wonogiri. LOr 11089 (II) n.d. (? 1927). 12 pp. Typed prose text written in Wonogiri in the late 1920s by J.B. Tjiptawinata, a schoolmaster in the local Hollandsch-Inlandsche School.

- Punika pémut yasa-Dalem kelangenan. (Dutch title: ‘Lyst van tuinen en lusthoven van den Sultan van Djocjo’). List of pleasure gardens (kelangenan-Dalem) and country pavilions (pesanggrahan) built in Yogya by the first four sultans. Nederlands Bijbelgenootschap MS 133 (VIII) pt. 39. Dated 25 Rejeb AJ 1757 (AD 20-1-1830). 3 pp.

- Rerepèn Babad Dipanagaran (Jayabaya version). Nederlands Bijbelgenootschap MS 37, pp. 5-9, date AJ 1742 (AD 14-12-1814 – 2-12-1815). Written in Yogyakarta.

- Salasilah Kyai Modjo Tondano. LOr 8652k. Dated Manado, 15-12-1919. 3 pp. Prose. Signed by a descendant of Pangéran Dipanagara.

- Serat candranipun para panjenengan-Dalem nata. Widyabudaya MS (Kraton Yogyakarta) C. 48. Character profiles of Yogyakarta rulers, written on the orders of Sultan Hamengkubuwana V (reigned 1820-1826, 1828-1855). 36 pp. Dated 17 Jumadilakir AJ 1783 (AD 6-3-1855).

- Serat salasilah para loeloehoer ing Kadanoerejan. Panti Budaya (Sana Budaya Museum, Yogyakarta) MS B. 29. Romanised copy made in Yogyakarta in 1932 by Th.G.Th. Pigeaud from an MS bought by Ir.
