About This Archival Portal: This digital edition presents Professor Peter Carey's research notes, primary Dutch and British official dispatches, Javanese/Pegon religious broadsheets, and fiscal-agrarian registers documenting the restoration of Netherlands Indies colonial authority across South-Central Java (August 1816 – December 1820).

Each transcription preserves original orthographic variants verbatim while expanding archival shorthand inline in square brackets and cross-referencing the immutable historical glossary.

  • Primary Archival Repositories: ANRI Jakarta, Nationaal Archief The Hague, Leiden University Library (BPL 616 Nahuys van Burgst Collection), KITLV, and India Office / British Library
  • Scope: 336 digitized archival scans (`jpg/IMG_1218.jpg`–`IMG_1561.jpg`) & 336 schema-validated Markdown transcriptions (`Transcriptionv2/`)
  • Transcription & Editorial Model: 3-Archetype diplomatic standard (`01_Archival_Transcription.md`) with verified citations to `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`
