CCGG.
26 Aug. 1816.
Letter of Nahuys to CCGG. 11 Aug. 1816.
On Venereal Disease Hospital in Yogya.
to be continued on same footing as under English.
No new patients to be taken on.

Oei Binjoe to Res. of Semarang Parvé
28 Aug. 1816.
Disputing a will = Husband had owned 8 Houses in Yogya & 4 in Semarang + large store house in Gang Tengah in Semarang + 2000 Sp. D. in cash. [300 in cash]

van der C to Nahuys,
28 Aug. 1816.
Asking for Report on MB II gold / jewels.

van IJ – Nahuys.
