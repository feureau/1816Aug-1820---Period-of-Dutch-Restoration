# Sept. 1816
### van IJ journey to courts

**MvK 4491, p. 60**

#### Secret Articles for van IJ:
2 Aug. 1816.

(I) Inquiry into what British had said about Return of Java to Dutch to courts.

(II) Should quieten — any feelings of Disquiet on part of Rulers vis-à-vis new Dutch govnmt. & abiding by contracts. Good attitude of Dutch to courts.

(III) To press on Independent Rulers — permanence of Dutch restoration & Treaty v English.

---

**pp. 61-62 Letter of Friendship from Commissioners-General to PB IV**
20 Aug. 1816. (C-G to PB IV)

* Appointment of William as King of Holland. Great increase in power over previous period.
* C-Gs sent out to Java to Reclaim colonies for Holland from British.
* King sends message of faith / trust in PB IV.
* Guarantee of C-Gs of all previous Rights enjoyed by PB IV & would hold themselves bound to points of Treaty v British (1812). Dutch govnmt. completely in accord v Treaty.
* Hopes that PB IV would fulfill his duties to Dutch govnmt. as Rep. of King William.
* Hope would not be necessary to use weapons.
* Letter to be taken by van IJ.
