# Transcription of IMG_1454.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1454.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Kabinet Dossier No. 2065: Ingekomen Berigten over het Inlandsch Onderwijs, 1831–1832)
- **Date Range:** 1831-11-26 to 1832-01-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Comparative education reports from Banyumas, Rembang, and Besuki, culminating in an extraordinary quantitative census of 410 Islamic *pesantrens* and *madrasahs* across the Residency of Surabaya and the Regency of Japan (Mojokerto) in November 1831. On 27 January 1832, Resident of Banyumas H. de Stürler reported that Banyumas possessed few pesantrens, lacked Mecca pilgrims (*Hajis*) and Arab merchants, and exhibited less religious fanaticism than coastal provinces; he recommended establishing a government school in Banjarnegara with teachers brought from other residencies to groom future native administrators. On 30 November 1831, the Resident of Rembang proposed a salaried Javanese writing teacher (f. 8 monthly) in every regency. On 26 November 1831, Resident of Besuki and Banyuwangi De Bruin Prince cataloged 500 to 600 village Quranic schools, noting that secular literacy was only acquired when youths entered priyayi service under bupatis or demangs. The document concludes with a landmark demographic census of Islamic schools in Surabaya dated 30 November 1831: documenting 410 schools, 3,096 boys, and 355 girls—achieving a total enrollment of 4,397 pupils across Surabaya urban districts (Kota, Jala Kota), Jenggala (divisions 1–4), Rawa Pulu, Gunung Kendeng, Kalang, Japan (Mojokerto), and Wirasaba.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes van den Bosch, Resident of Banyumas (H. de Stürler), Resident of Rembang, Resident of Besuki/Banyuwangi (De Bruin Prince), Resident of Surabaya, Regency Regents and Demangs.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Banyumas, Banjarnegara [Banjar Negara], Rembang, Besuki [Besoeki], Banyuwangi, Surabaya [Soerabaya], Japan [Mojokerto], Jenggala [Jënggala], Wirasaba, Rawa Pulu, Gunung Kendeng.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Pesantren* demographic census (410 schools, 4,397 pupils), female education (355 girls), *Hajis*, Arab merchant absence, Banjarnegara state school, free educational materials, civil service recruitment.

* **Document Nature:** Administrative intelligence dispatches and a detailed statistical demographic register.
