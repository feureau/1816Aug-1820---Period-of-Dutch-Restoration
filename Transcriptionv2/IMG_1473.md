# Transcription of IMG_1473.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1473.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A, *Feb.–March 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-02-19 to 1819-03-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Security and administrative dispatches across Central Java: Surakarta Resident Van Prehn alerting Nahuys to Kyai Iman Sampurna's secret millenarian Pegon tract; Kedu Resident Hardy reporting the desertion of a village bekel who enlisted into the Sultan's *prajurit Jagakarya* guard; Opzichter C.F. Engel's arrival in Pacitan and immediate deportation of Demang Nitrawijaya to Yogyakarta as a political threat; Governor-General's decree (*BGG 11 Mar 1819 No. 3*) fixing state honoraria for Chinese officers (Capitan Cina f. 1,000; Luitenant Cina f. 400) and funding the Chinese Hospital; trade dispute over coffee payments in Boyolali involving Sayyid Muhammad Kudsi; jurisdictional clash between Yogyakarta and Surakarta opium farmers at Tembayat; salt monopoly inspection tour by Van Lawick van Pabst; and intensive political surveillance by Kedu Resident Hardy tracking returned Mecca pilgrims (*Hajis*) arriving from Penang (including Sultan HB II's former royal *Suranata* cleric Haji Burhan).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Rijck van Prehn (Provisional Resident of Surakarta), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Kyai Iman Sampurna, F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), Jan Murwawinwara, Demang Nitrawijaya, C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Capitan Cina, Luitenant Cina, Sayyid Muhammad Kudsi, F.H. Hoorn, Hendrik Jacob Domis, P.H. van Lawick van Pabst, Haji Abdul Rahman (formerly Ranajaya), Haji Burhan (formerly Amad Ngapiah), Haji Muslim (formerly Nurbayan), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Kedu (District Ballah, Desa Cêkêlan), Pacitan, Semarang, Boyolali, Tembayat, Magelang, Penang (Pulo Pinang), Desa Kitilan, Desa Karang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Pegon script, *prajurit Jagakarya*, deportation, Capitan Cina, Luitenant Cina, Chinese Hospital, Opium Farm (*pacht*), Salt Monopoly inspection, *Suranata* (kraton priestly corps), *Hajis* (Mecca pilgrims), intelligence surveillance.
