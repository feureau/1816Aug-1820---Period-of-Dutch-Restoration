# Transcription of IMG_1548.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1548.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698 b: *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djokjokarta 1820* [AVJ 1820])
- **Date Range:** 1820-01-28 to 1820-12-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** General Report of the Yogyakarta Residency for 1820; records Governor-General Decree No. 12 (4 Oct 1820) annexing a territorial strip between Kedu and Pekalongan to ease postal road maintenance, levying grass-cutters from southern Yogyakarta desas (Tuwarna, Bulus, Rawa, Semas); newly acceded Susuhunan Pakubuwana V of Surakarta sends formal thanks to Yogyakarta and greetings to Ratu Ibu and Ratu Kencana; Resident Nahuys van Burgst audits the post-1814 Garnham regency established after the death of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, detailing the financial settlements for Danureja IV, Pringgadiningrat, Ranadiningrat, Mertanegara, and the 3,000 Spanish Dollar sinecure gifts paid to Prince Paku Alam I; records the death of Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton, grandmother of HB IV) on 12 July 1820; compiles a master fiscal inventory of the **34 royal tollgates (*bandar*)**, **2 weighing houses (*gedhong timbangan*)**, and **14 regional market tax farms (*pasar*)** leased across Yogyakarta, Bagelèn, Pajang, Madiun, and Pacitan.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana V, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Lieutenant-Colonel Robert C. Garnham, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Kedu, Pekalongan, Bagelèn, Pajang, Madiun, Magetan, Pacitan, Gamping, Bantul, Brosot, Wates, Boyolali, Kertasana, Ngrawa, Kalangbrèt.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bandar (toll-gates), Gedhong Timbangan (weighing houses), Pasar tax farms, Post stations (*postwegen*), Regency Board of 1814–1815, Dutch Guilders (Dfl), Spanish Dollars (`Sp. D.`).
