# Transcription of IMG_1253.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1253.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Instructie voor den Resident van Djocjocarta*, 29 July 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-07-29 to 1816-10-01
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official gubernatorial instructions issued by the Dutch Commissioners-General to Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst upon his appointment as Resident of Yogyakarta; protocol for the formal transfer of civil and military authority from British Resident John Crawfurd; provisional continuation of the 1812 treaty framework; transfer of the Paku Alam Legion (*Prajurit Pakualaman*) from British to Dutch colonial payroll; instructions to maintain harmonious relations with the garrison military commander; strict anti-corruption regulations prohibiting gifts, bribery, and administrative extortion; mandatory monthly salary ceiling of 1,500 Java Rupees.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, John Crawfurd, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Pangéran Paku Alam I, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, M. L. van Deventer.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia, Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Instructie voor den Resident*, Commissioners-General (*Commissarissen-Generaal*), Pakualaman Corps, Treaty of 1812, Anti-corruption codes, Java Rupee (J.R.).
