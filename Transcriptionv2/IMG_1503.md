# Transcription of IMG_1503.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1503.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 139 / ANRI Solo 139, *Translaaten en Verbaalen 1817 tot 1818*) (Archetype 1: Archival Translation (Contemporary Dutch Translation by J.W. Winter))
- **Date Range:** 1818-12-01 to 1819-08-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Folio 2 of Surakarta translator J.W. Winter's Dutch rendering of the Ajibarang/Banyumas prophecy: detailing the sacred directional realignment (the older brother moving south, the son of the Adipati of Brebes moving north); proclaiming the brother of the Brebes scion—a direct descendant of rebel monarch Sunan Kuning—as the *Vorst der Rechtvaardigen* (*Ratu Adil*); announcing that all European friends will accompany the Governor-General to renew the world; declaring universal mystical brotherhood with all men and heavenly marriage with celestial women; invoking the mystical powers of the seer of Ajibarang who could calm the raging breakers of the Southern Ocean (*de Zuid Zee konde stillen*); and mandating the hierarchical delivery of the broadsheet to Raden Tumenggung Udanagara and Pangéran Prangwedana.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** The Loegoe of Ajibarang, The Loegoe of Banyumas, Adipati of Brebes, Sunan Kuning (Raden Mas Garendi), Lurung Tengah (*Vorst der Rechtvaardigen / Ratu Adil*), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (*de Generaal*), Raden Tumenggung Udanagara, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (translator).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Brebes, Ajibarang, Banyumas, Southern Ocean (*Zuid Zee / Segara Kidul*), Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Vorst der Rechtvaardigen* (Ratu Adil), Lineage of Sunan Kuning, European alliance, Mystical brotherhood, Stilling the Southern Ocean, Hierarchy of submission.
