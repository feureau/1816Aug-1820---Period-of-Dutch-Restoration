Local Education 1819

[Top right:]
AN Besluit Alg. Secr. 8 Mar. 1819 no. 9
BGG 3 March 1819
no. 9
Local Education

Res of Pasuruan to Sec. of State 19 Apr. 1819:
Govt. order needed to make children go to school otherwise parents would keep them at home for domestic duties.
Duari/Modins — similar experience + vaccination.
V. little desire to give instruction near. Reading/writing at village level.

Res of Besuki, 26 Mar. 1819:
Children given religious instruction by Penghulu of district & wealthy parents at 5-6 years.
But Penghulu forced / constrained to accept children of both rich & poor parents.
Once he judges they have learnt enough, sent back to parents.
No teachers in Javanese language.
Children jav. officials instructed by family members in this.
1 teacher (not a priest) to be appointed in each Regency given 1 bouw of land + f 5. per month.

Res of Banten (V.d.W.) to Sec. of State, 19 Mar. 1819:
Reading/writing taught in Banten; Serang.
Most literate priests in [gigantic Environ? / Tangerang]
Preanger Rgs: Out of 800 mandurs in Banten = only about 100 can read/write.

Res of Cianjur, 24 Mar. 1819:
No govmt. attempts at local education in Preanger.
private tutor in Sundanese.
Tutor. — Tutors have complete authority but cannot inflict physical punishment.
Teaches from 7-11, 2-4 every day except Fridays.
If fathers rich = pay Guru; if not pupil acts as guru's servant.
Govt. should pay Guru because most Sundanese unable to pay & parents need children to work on land looking after Buffaloes, domestic duties.
In every kampong = school should be set up for Reading/writing Javanese.
In each District capital more advanced school for teaching sons of officials to go into Govhmt. service: Malay + reading Ar. characters.

Res of Tegal (J.W. Meilan) to Sec. of State, 29 Apr. 1819:
No real public/primary school in whole Residency.
Inhabitants insouciant about Education.
During making of Cadastral Survey for Land Rent -
out of 800 village heads = in every 50 only 3-4 could write their names.
There were Démang of coffee culture hath become just ordinary village
a position of Responsibility amongst Javanese. Honour.
