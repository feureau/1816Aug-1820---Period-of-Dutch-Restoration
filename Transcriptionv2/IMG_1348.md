# Transcription of IMG_1348.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1348.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys*, 22 October 1816; and *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historical diagnosis of endemic agrarian oppression in Central Java: deep-seated structural exploitation dating back long before the reign of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (pre-1792), remediable only through systematic administrative overhaul; policy recommendation: direct colonial administration of the common peasantry to eliminate corruption and extortion among Javanese chiefs and appanage holders; monetary fiscal crisis: severe difficulty experienced by village cultivators in paying taxes demanded strictly in cash/coin (*money Taxes*); peasant flight: unable to produce coin, villagers abandon their homesteads and become homeless wanderers (*bujangs* / *landlopers* / vagrants); radicalization: these displaced wanderers readily join the entourages and retinues of dissident princes or insurgent rebel leaders (*kraman*), turning to armed robbery, highway dacoity, and murder; geographical proliferation of banditry: alarming expansion of predatory robber bands across Bagelen, Pajang, and even the core royal district of Mataram itself; administrative appraisal of indigenous leadership: critical profile of Raden Adipati Danureja IV (Patih of Yogyakarta); Van IJsseldijk assesses him as professionally competent and not unsuited for the office, but universally despised by the Javanese population as a man with an impure, corrupt heart (*kurang bersih* / without a clean heart); condemnation of Danureja IV's harsh, mercenary administration, which operated largely through Chinese revenue-farmers and money-lenders; Danureja IV's absolute subservience to colonial power: terrified of Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, fully conscious that his lucrative tenure as grand vizier depends entirely on Dutch backing.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II), Raden Adipati Danureja IV, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Bagelen (Bagělèn), Pajang, Mataram, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Systemic peasant oppression, direct colonial rule, cash taxation crisis, *bujang* (unattached laborer / vagrant), *landloper* (tramp / vagabond), *kraman* (rebel / insurgent), *kècu* banditry, Patih of Yogyakarta, *kurang bersih* (unclean heart / corrupt), Chinese tax-farmers / tollgate keepers, colonial subservience.
