# Transcription of IMG_1534.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1534.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-05-09 to 1820-12-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official decision of Governor-General Van der Capellen in Council (9 May 1820) partitioning the rich annexed Residency of Kedhu into two separate native administrative regencies (*Regentschappen*): Magelang under Regent Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (governing Magelang, Bandongan, Ngawinan, Ballok, Prapog, and Samawara) and Ménoréh under Regent Radèn Tumenggung Sumadilaga (governing Probalingga, Ménoréh, Jêtis, and Limpuyangan); official appointment (16 June 1820 No. 18) of Mas Prawira Laksana as Sub-Demang of Parakan, Temanggung, in the Jêtis district; financial resolution (16 June 1820 No. 19) authorizing retroactive full salary payment for Assistant Resident Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe during his interim tenure following the dismissal of Resident Rijck van Prehn in Surakarta; formal resolution (21 Nov. 1820 No. 24) acknowledging the death of PB IV and ratifying the succession agreement (*bevestiging* / *accoord*) of his son as Sunan Pakubuwana V; dramatic diplomatic incident (5 Dec. 1820 No. 17) reviewing an official report from the Resident of Surakarta: on 16 October 1820, a solemn kraton deputation composed of the Patih (Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II) and senior bupatis presented Resident Nahuys with a treasure chest containing 16,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) as a gift from PB V to defray coronation ceremonies and personal residency expenses.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I, Radèn Tumenggung Sumadilaga, Mas Prawira Laksana, Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe, Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana V, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedhu, Magelang, Ménoréh, Bandongan, Ngawinan, Ballok, Prapog, Samawara, Probalingga, Jêtis, Limpuyangan, Parakan, Temanggung, Surakarta (Sala).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, Regency partition (*Regentschappen*), *demang* / *sub-demang*, *bevestiging* (royal ratification), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), coronation subsidy / royal gift (*kado*), diplomatic graft protocols.
