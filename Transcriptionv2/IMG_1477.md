# Transcription of IMG_1477.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1477.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A & Resolutions of the Governor-General, June–August 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-06-04 to 1819-08-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse political, infrastructural, demographic, and agricultural developments: Governor-General's resolution (*BGG 4 Jun 1819 No. 23*) authorizing the repatriation of Raden Ayu Napan Datia from Ceylon to Yogyakarta with the assent of Sultan HB IV; Kedu Resident Hardy contracting for Yogyakarta teak timber to bridge the torrential Progo and Pabelan rivers; Opzichter Engel's landmark Pacitan coffee register recording 157,834 coffee trees and 45,859 Dadap shade trees planted; Batavia demographic decree (*BGG 3 Jul 1819 No. 3*) ordering an age-stratified census of European inhabitants; naturalization and permanent residency charter for Scottish indigo planter Dr. Harvey Thomson; delivery of 56,000 additional coffee seedlings to Pacitan; and the sudden transfer of veteran translator C.F. Krijgsman to the military expedition in Palembang and Bangka.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Ayu Napan Datia, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Dr. Harvey Thomson (*Tuan Tom*), J. de Bruijn (Resident of Semarang), C.F. Krijgsman (translator), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Ceylon (Sri Lanka), Kedu, Progo River, Pabelan River, Pacitan, Aberdeen (Scotland), Semarang, Bangka, Palembang, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Exile repatriation, Teak timber procurement, Bridge civil engineering, Coffee nursery metrics, *Dadap* shade forestry, European census, Permanent residency (*acte van toelating*), Military expedition translator.
