# Transcription of IMG_1375.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1375.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60: Jan–Feb 1817 / *Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* [BvGG] 31 Dec 1817 No. 15)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-07 to 1817-01-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official forensic investigation and judicial depositions concerning the millenarian insurrection led by Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi at desa Sambirata in Bagelèn. On 25 January 1817, Provisional Assistant Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman formally handed over the residency back to Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, who reported directly to the Raad van Indië. The core dossier contains the sworn deposition of Haji Amidjah (personal name: Ibnu Hiaman) following the arrest of the Ngabèhi of Tanjong, Juma Arta. Umar Mahdi, acting as Demang of Sambirata, proclaimed himself a *Prajurit Sultan Rum* [Soldier of the Ottoman Sultan] under secret imperial mandate from Sunan Pakubuwana IV to defend Sambirata. He prophesied that the *Sultan Rum*, Sheikh Sayyidna Muhammad, Sheikh Sichnan Waliullah, and thirty holy sheikhs were voyaging from the Arab lands to Sambirata to inaugurate a new righteous monarch (*Ratu Adil*) during the holy month of Sapar. As part of this cosmic purification of Java, legendary figures were to assume military governorships—the revered Wali Sanga saint Sunan Bonang as Field Marshal (*Marsekal*) in Semarang, and the epic Islamic hero Umar Maya (*Uman Maija*) as Field Marshal in Kedu—while letters of divine command were dispatched to summon the faithful under *Tuwan Hadi Coris* [Tuwan Hadji Idris].
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi [Oemar Mahdi], Haji Amidjah / Ibnu Hiaman [Hadji Amidjah], Ngabèhi of Tanjong [Juma Arta], Johan Diederik Kruseman, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Sunan Pakubuwana IV [PB IV], Sunan Bonang, Umar Maya [Uman Maija / Menak epic cycle], Tuwan Hadi Coris [Tuwan Hadji Idris], Sheikh Sayyidna Muhammad, Sheikh Sichnan Waliullah.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Sambirata [Masaran], Bagelèn, Tanjong, Semarang, Kedhu [Kedu], Mecca / Arabia [Arab Lands], Rome / Istanbul [Rum / Ottoman Empire], Yogyakarta [Djogja], Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Prajurit Sultan Rum*, *Ratu Adil* [Just King], *Wali Sanga*, Sapar [Islamic month], *Marsekal* [Field Marshal / Daendels military title], Chinese merchants, tax rebellion, judicial interrogation (*proces-verbaal*), *Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal* [BvGG].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI, Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60 (Januari–Februari 1817); cross-referenced with BvGG (Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal), 31 December 1817, No. 15.
* **Document Nature:** Administrative registry of incoming/outgoing residency correspondence, police minutes, and Dutch translations of Javanese judicial depositions.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Reveals the syncretic fusion of classical Javanese messianic prophecy (*Ratu Adil*, Sunan Bonang), Islamic universalist legitimacy (Ottoman Empire, Mecca sheikhs, *Menak* epic heroes like Umar Maya), and Napoleonic military concepts (*Marsekal*) within early nineteenth-century peasant revolts.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Dutch residency officials (Nahuys, Kruseman), local priyayi and demangs (Ngabèhi of Tanjong, Umar Mahdi), and Islamic religious leaders (Haji Amidjah).
* **Archival Header:** Top left registers the repository file code (`[Header:] / A.V. Djogja Letters No. 60. / 1817 (Jan - Feb)`).
* **Carey Annotations:** Red ink rubrics identifying key themes (`[Red ink: Umar Mahdi Bagelen]`, `[Red ink: HADJI AMIDJAH]`, `[Red ink: BvGG 1817 31 Dec no. 15]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, interrogation rubrics, and administrative citations on the left; verbatim depositions, theological declarations, and structural summaries on the right.
