# Transcription of IMG_1519.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1519.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nahuys van Burgst Portfolio 6, Part 13 & Resolutions of the Governor-General, Feb. 1820) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Judicial Inquiry & Executive Dismissals))
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-01 to 1820-02-01
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** The sensational corruption scandal and official dismissal of Surakarta Resident Rijck van Prehn and court translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter: seven translated Javanese complaint letters from court nobles (Pangéran Aria Ciptasana, Raden Aria Mistanagara, Raden Panji Sumaatmaja) exposing unlawful administrative dismissals and property seizures; Van Prehn's desperate defense of his reputation (5 Oct 1819 No. 55); Sunan PB IV's fury over unauthorized court firings executed in his name; and Governor-General Van der Capellen's secret punitive resolution (*BGG geheim 1 Feb 1820*): summarily dismissing Van Prehn, placing him under judicial subpoena with a total correspondence embargo with the Vorstenlanden, firing J.W. Winter and summoning him to Batavia for criminal investigation before the Procureur-Generaal, and installing Lippe as Provisional Resident.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Rijck van Prehn (dismissed Resident of Surakarta), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (dismissed translator), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Pangéran Aria Ciptasana, Raden Aria Mistanagara, Raden Panji Sumaatmaja, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Jayaningrat, Lippe (Provisional Resident), Procureur-Generaal of Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Batavia, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *BGG geheim*, Executive dismissal (*ambtsontzetting*), Subpoena and correspondence ban, Criminal inquest (*gerechtelijk onderzoek*), Procureur-Generaal (Attorney-General), Unlawful property alienation, Provisional Residency.
