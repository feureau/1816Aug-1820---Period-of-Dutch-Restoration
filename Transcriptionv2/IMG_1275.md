# Transcription of IMG_1275.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1275.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 7: *Solo Secret Letters — Dispatches from Governor-General to Resident Pinket van Haak*)
- **Date Range:** 1791 to 1816-12-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official inquest into Sunan Pakubuwana IV's clandestine meetings with Kyai Murma Wijaya and the retrieval of the sacred heirloom dagger *Kyai Birawa*; historical reflection on the theft of the royal pusaka *Kyai Kuntung* from the Surakarta *Siti Inggil* twenty-five years earlier (1791); marriage and family ties between Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II and Kyai Murma; colonial surveillance of gunpowder disbursements; Governor-General Van der Capellen's decree of 3 December 1816 authorizing the delivery of 100 barrels/tiles of military gunpowder to Sunan PB IV, drawn from ordnance stocks originally allocated to Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) following the 1812 storming of Yogyakarta, earmarked for the Sunan's 35-day birthday jubilee (*weton* / *tumbuk*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Siti Inggil, Kepundhung, Jipang, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Heirloom blades (*Kyai Birawa*, *Kyai Kuntung*), Royal Siti Inggil, Gunpowder allocation (*kruid*), Royal 35-day birthday cycle (*weton* / *tumbuk*), Mangkunagaran ordnance stocks.
