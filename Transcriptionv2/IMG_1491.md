# Transcription of IMG_1491.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1491.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI H 698b, *Archief van Jogja 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-22 to 1819-11-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Vice-regal diplomatic dates and forensic fiscal dispatches: Governor-General Van der Capellen's official state visit to Yogyakarta (22–29 August 1819); administrative fees on tripartite private lease contracts (*Prijëms*) concluded between Europeans, Javanese, and Chinese (notary Jan Langheye receiving 2 duits per document); and Nahuys van Burgst's landmark tripartite taxonomy of internal customs and market dues: condemning *Wang Pêniti* (arbitrary tollgate surcharges) and *Pasarangs* (market dues) as ruthless extortion of the "small man" (*wong cilik*) that must be abolished; upholding *Wang Pakimitang* (ferry and toll protection fees) as legitimate customary law (*adat lama*); and categorizing *Wang Mutian* as an autonomous religious pilgrimage fee siphoned by royal tomb guardians (*jurukunci*) at Pasar Gedhé, Imogiri, and Tembayat outside colonial treasury revenues.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Jan Langheye (clerk/notary), Inspector of Finances in Semarang, Chinese tollgate farmers (*bandars*), Royal shrine guardians (*jurukunci*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Sala), Semarang, Pasar Gedhé (Kota Gede), Imogiri, Tembayat.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Vice-regal grand tour, *Prijëm* (notarial contract / *piagem*), *Wang Pêniti* / *Wang Pêkêti*, *Wang Pakimitang* (transit protection fee / *adat lama*), *Wang Mutian* (royal grave offering), *Pasarangs* (market fees), *wong cilik*.
