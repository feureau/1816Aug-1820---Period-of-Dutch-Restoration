# Transcription of IMG_1266.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1266.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djogja Corr. [October–November 1816] — Dispatches from Acting/Adj. Resident to Governor-General*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-11 to 1816-11-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Fiscal stringency and garrison salary arrears under interim residency administration; celebration of the anniversary of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV's coronation (12 October 1816); orders issued to Patih Danureja IV to compile a comprehensive land survey of the Sultan's domain lands (*nagara agung*); discovery and suppression of the messianic insurrection coordinated by Kyai Murma Wijaya at Desa Kepundhung (8 November 1816); mobilization of a Dutch punitive cavalry detachment (25 troopers) and Pakualaman cavalry; Kyai Murma's flight across the border into Surakarta territory; recovery of firearms, royal pikes (*waos*), and state regalia; identification of Pangéran Buminata of Surakarta and Kyai Taptajani of Melangi as Murma's high-level protectors; Assistant Resident sharply reprimands Patih Danureja IV for negligence and delayed reporting.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johan Diederik Kruseman, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Kyai Taptajani, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Pangéran Paku Alam I, Amat Djinal, P. J. F. Louw.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Kepundhung (Kapundhung), Surakarta (Solo), Melangi, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kyai Murma Conspiracy, Desa Kepundhung, Pakualaman Cavalry, Royal regalia theft, Sultan's land survey (*Louw Vol. I*), Java Rupees (J.R.).
