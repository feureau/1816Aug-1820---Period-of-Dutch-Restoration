## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1243.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Citations from P. H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816* ['s-Gravenhage: Martinus Nijhoff, 1911], pp. 266, 269–270; and M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, pp. 97–104)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-09 to 1816-12-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diplomatic mission of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk to Yogyakarta (September 1816); emotional reunion between Pangéran Paku Alam I and former Radèn Adipati Purwadipura (Windurèja); Van IJsseldijk's cordial reception by Ratu Kencana Wulan; John Crawfurd's return visit to Yogyakarta (11–13 December 1816) following British troop embarkation supervision at Semarang; Carey's historiographical hypothesis regarding Crawfurd acquiring the manuscript of *Babad Bêdhah Ngayogyakarta*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Pangéran Paku Alam I, Radèn Adipati Purwadipura (Windurèja), Ratu Kencana Wulan, John Crawfurd, P. H. van der Kemp, M. L. van Deventer.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogya), Semarang, The Hague ('s-Gravenhage).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Babad Bêdhah Ngayogyakarta*, British troop embarkation, Commissioner-General Extraordinary, Residency of Yogyakarta.
