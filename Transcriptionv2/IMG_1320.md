# Transcription of IMG_1320.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1320.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Notes on P. H. van der Kemp, *Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1817, tot het vertrek der Engelschen*, 's-Gravenhage: Nijhoff, 1913, pp. 24, 320–321)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-09 to 1816-08-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on the retrocession of Yogyakarta in August 1816; Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst’s arrival on 9 August (ahead of Dutch troops) and official transfer of authority from British Resident John Crawfurd on 14 August 1816; Nahuys's dispatch of 11 August 1816 (Bijlage III) praising Crawfurd's calming influence on the court; Ratu Ageng entrusting the care of the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV to Nahuys three separate times; the deep personal debt owed by Pangéran Paku Alam I to Nahuys (who had saved him from execution under Marshal Daendels in 1811); Paku Alam's relief that Tiedeman (Daendels's former secretary) was replaced by Nahuys; integration of the Pakualaman cavalry corps into Dutch service.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), John Crawfurd, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Herman Willem Daendels, Tiedeman, P. H. van der Kemp, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Residency House and Kraton).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** British-Dutch Administrative Transfer, Pakualaman Legion / Cavalry Corps, Daendels Execution Order (1811), Guardianship of Minor Sultan, Residency House Farewell Banquet.
