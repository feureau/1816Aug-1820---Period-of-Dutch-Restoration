* Paku Alam corps = 100 men.
* paid by PA, given horses by him.
* uniforms / arms provided by British.
* P.A. receives monthly sp. D. 750 from govment. for upkeep.

* also small corps of European Dragoons: or rather "christian troops"
* Dutch, German, and Inlandsche kinderen.
* many had earlier seen service v Dutch.
* **p. 322**: 25 + set up 8 months ago — ? Jan. 1816 in aftermath of Sepoy plot.
* mounted as hussars.
* Have to return weapons lent them by British from 22nd Dragoon Regiment.
* Since Sepoy Plot used for service in Kraton.
* Very well suited cf know Malay, "some even Javanese"
* **Corps to be taken into active service v Dutch.**

### Ceremony for returning Yogya to Dutch.

**Nahuys to Commissioners-General, 15 Aug. 1816, no. 7.**
* English Commandant annoyed. Wishes to march out of Yogya before arrival of Dutch troops.
* **14 August**: Lunch v P.A. + sons / Tan Jin Sing / Dutch / English in fort.
* **15 Aug.**: Raising of Dutch flag = Lowering of English flag.
* Nahuys reports official take over of Residency to HB IV.
