4 Feb. 1818   J.W. Winter nota:
Trading Journey to Grisik of Raden Dipawijaya from Solo = 11 Oct goes + 3 Boats + 13 koyangs Rice / 10 koyangs Indigo to Grisik but pays no tolls at 7 Bandars along River.

Returns 4000 hoekjes Trasi / 60 koyangs gember / 3 koyangs Djago / 1000 porcelain plates / 1 " salt / afstoffen

should pay 7 dubbeltjes for each boat at each Bandar.
