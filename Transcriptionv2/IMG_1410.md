- **Date Range:** 1817-05-16 to 1817-07-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Colonial viceregal ceremonial reception, the arrest and deportation of discharged Indian Sepoy veterans, and the multi-residency military mobilization suppressing the Malang insurrection. On 16 May 1817, ceremonial protocols for the reception of the Commissarissen-Generaal in Yogyakarta were drafted, modeled directly on the Napoleonic court etiquette introduced under Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels. On 14 June, three Indian natives of Bengal (including Byelesy and Sawrie) were arrested in Yogyakarta on suspicion of burglary and political intrigue; although holding honorable discharge papers from the British East India Company's Bengal Volunteer Infantry, they had participated in the 1815 Surakarta conspiracy. Nahuys dispatched them under guard to Batavia via Semarang, warning the central government that ex-Sepoys in the interior represented a grave danger due to their superior military training, ambition, and potential to lead indigenous rebellions, and formally requested that no further Sepoys be permitted to settle in the Vorstenlanden. In July, alarms from the Surakarta-Surabaya frontier led to the discovery of an aristocratic conspiracy in Malang; Nahuys rushed to Surakarta, coordinated with Acting Resident Von Lippe and Resident of the Oosthoek Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst, and secured elite reinforcements from Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) and Sunan Pakubuwana IV to crush the rebel band.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 11 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection: Djocja Correspondence, Mei–Juli 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Diplomatic protocol memoranda, police arrest warrants, high-level political intelligence reports, and inter-residency military coordination dispatches.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates colonial paranoia regarding Indian mercenary veterans lingering in Central Java after the British interregnum, alongside the swift civil-military containment of the July 1817 East Java uprising.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Residents Nahuys, Kruseman, Von Lippe, and Van Lawick van Pabst; princely military commanders (Prangwedana); and discharged Bengal Sepoy soldiers.
* **Archival Header:** Top registers shelfmark and chronological scope (`BPL 616 / N van B / PORT 5 / NO. 11. / DJOCJA CORR (GENERAL) / 9 May 1817 - / 1817 (May - July) / N van B ARCHIVE LEIDEN`).

* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, addressees, and police rubrics on the left; verbatim ceremonial notes, Sepoy threat assessments, deportation orders, and Malang troop mobilization reports on the right.
