## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1239.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2)
- **Date Range:** 1815 to 1816-03-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst's dispatch to the Minister of Colonies (Anton Reinhard Falck) analyzing the aftermath of the 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy (*Sepoy Plot*) at the Court of Surakarta; Raffles's diplomatic rationale and court anxiety over retrocession to the Dutch; native and Chinese merchant sentiment welcoming the return of Dutch governance.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Anton Reinhard Falck (Minister of Colonies), Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Leiden (Archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sepoy Plot (1815), Bengal Volunteer Battalions, Minister van Koloniën, Retrocession (1816), European officers security.
