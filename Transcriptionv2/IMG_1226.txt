A.M.T. de Salis
to CCGG
21 Aug. 1816

Desa Brunjak
Burning down 20 houses in Pekalongan on 5 Aug. 1816 Roofpartij.
14 Buffaloes taken + 258 Rups. worth of goods.
2 demangs have evil reputation.

BCCGG.
30 Aug. 1816.
Letter of Nahuys 19 Aug.
HB II asks British to send him Ratu Kencana / 2 R. Ayus from Yogya.
2 R. Ayus refuse to make journey.
Should be sent by sea from Semarang.

BCCGG.
Sept. 1816
Letter of Nahuys 23 Aug.
Ratu Kencana Wulan / R. Ay. Murtiningrat / Dayaningrat prepared to leave for Batavia.
Necessary passes to be given.

BCCGG.
4 Sept. 1816
Letter of Nahuys, 27 Aug.
Garebeg Puasa passes off without trouble.
Paku Alam gives 400 silver Rupiahs for Waterloo Fund. / BCCGG, 27 Sept. 1816.
