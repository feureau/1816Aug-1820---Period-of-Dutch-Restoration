# Transcription of IMG_1265.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1265.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, Vol. 4203 [J 181]: *Ingekomen Stukken*, October–November 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-04 to 1816-11-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Inspection of the British-founded Lock Hospital for women suffering from venereal disease in Yogyakarta by garrison surgeon Dr. H. B. Menke and the military commandant (31 October 1816), housing 67 quarantined female patients costing 9 Java Rupees per patient monthly; public health campaigns and cultural resistance to Jennerian smallpox vaccination (*koepokinenting*); profound opposition from conservative Islamic religious leaders (*priests* / *kauman*) whose funeral fee revenues (*burial ceremonies*) were diminished by falling infant mortality; parental resistance and physical sabotage of lymph vaccine pustules upon returning to villages; Nahuys's proposal to establish permanent rural vaccination clinics; violent assault upon royal uncle Pangéran Ngabèhi (4 October 1816) investigated by Patih Danureja IV under the 1805 inter-court penal convention and the 1812 British treaty.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Ngabèhi, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Dr. H. B. Menke, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, M. J. Coops.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Code River.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Vol. 4203 (J 181), Lock Hospital, Smallpox vaccination (*koepokinenting*), Islamic religious opposition to vaccination, Inter-court Treaty of 1805, Treaty of 1812, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Java Rupees (J.R.).
