# Transcription of IMG_1286.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1286.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Citations from P. H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816 naar Oorspronkelijke Stukken*, 's-Gravenhage: Martinus Nijhoff, 1911, p. 127; extracting dispatch from Acting Resident J. D. Kruseman to Commissioners-General, 19 November 1816, No. 20/190)
- **Date Range:** 1816-11-19 to 1816-11-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diplomatic garden outing between Acting Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman and the young Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV following the capture of rebel Kyai Murma; Sultan's coveting of Kruseman's European gold pocket watch, chain, and seal (*cachet*), citing the Sunan of Surakarta's collection; Kruseman compelled by protocol to surrender his watch as a personal gift to the monarch; Kruseman's diplomatic reassurance to HB IV that the colonial government viewed the Murma conspiracy purely as the moral weakness of his mother (Ratu Ibu) without casting suspicion upon the Sultan; delight of the palace queens (*Ratus*); Kruseman's formal petition to draw 200 Spanish Dollars from the Residency Treasury as reimbursement; gubernatorial rebuke by Governor-General Van der Capellen (*Besluit 29 Nov. 1816 No. 7*) refusing public treasury compensation for unauthorized gifts, though authorizing him to accept a reciprocal royal gift of equivalent value.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Kyai Murma Wijaya, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Sala / Solo), The Hague ('s-Gravenhage).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Diplomatic gift-giving protocol, Royal mango orchards (*mangas*), Gold pocket watch (*horloge*), Imperial audit, Besluit 29 Nov. 1816 No. 7, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
