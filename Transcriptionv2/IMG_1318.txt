Van IJ
Journey to Court
Sept. 1816

Speak for notie minutes & PB IV

PB IV returns to kraton.

4 Sept. Simple visit of Van IJ & Kruseman to Solo court. Crown Prince / Patih. Sent to Solo because old V.O.C. servants well known to court.
