# Transcription of IMG_1228.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1228.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Solo Brieven Bundel 37: *Report on Kyai Murma*, pp. 1229–1231)
- **Date Range:** 1816-11-06 to 1816-11-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official investigation and treasure seizure in the Kyai Murma scandal; on 6 November 1816 at 4:00 AM, Assistant Resident Kruseman arrives at Kyai Murma's residence in Surakarta supported by 80 dragoons (including 40 cavalrymen from Paku Alam's Legion) and inventories an immense seized treasure of gold jewelry, jewel-mounted krisses, sirih sets, muskets, and clerical robes; on 18 November (17 Besar), Surakarta court messengers locate Kyai Murma at Friday mosque prayer in the Yogyakarta enclave of Desa Kapundhung (five hours travel from Solo); Demang Radèn Wangsadikara of Kapundhung refuses to surrender Murma and his family to the Surakarta emissaries, insisting on inter-court jurisdictional protocol and personally escorting Murma to Yogyakarta to face legal proceedings under Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Murma Wijaya, Demang Radèn Wangsadikara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Assistant Resident Kruseman, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Desa Kapundhung (Kepundhung), Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Yogya).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kyai Murma affair, Pradata court jurisdiction, Pakualam Legion, Heirloom weaponry (*pusaka*), Kris fittings (*pendhok*, *mendhak*), Islamic calendar (*17 Besar*).
