### Physical Description and Layout
The document is written on a single sheet of aged, cream-colored paper with visible vertical fold lines across the center. Along the top edge, there are rust stains and small punctures from a metal fastener or staple.

The main body of the text consists of 18 lines of elegant, flowing Javanese cursive handwriting in dark ink (*Aksara Jawa* / *Cacarakan*).
