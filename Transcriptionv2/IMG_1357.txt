van IJsseldijk 1816
AN Geheim Besluit
Commissaris-Generaal
12 Dec. 1816 La A.

Letters about Tan Jin Sing

van IJ - van Haak,
21 Aug. 1816 Samarang
Patih of Solo + Bupatis come to Semarang to compliment van IJ
Does not wish special ceremonies on arrival at Solo.
Would attend Garebeg Puasa at court.

van IJ - van Haak,
28 Aug. 1816.
van IJ to journey to Solo following Wednesday 28 Aug.
Arrival on 29 Aug.
Would spend one night at Salatiga.

van IJ to van Haak, & Nahuys
29 Aug. 1816
Announcing arrival at courts as Buitengewoon Gezant
to announce to PB IV / HB IV
that N. Ind. is once more in hands of Dutch.

Major Nahuys to van IJ,
Yogya 11 Sept. 1816
Had informed HB IV of impending arrival of van IJ
only replies Terima kasih
Because of HB IV's little knowledge of Malay tongue was not able to say more.

van IJ - Kruseman
6 Oct. 1816
that Kruseman had received orders from Batavia to take over duty as Prov. Resident of Yogya during absence of Nahuys.

Parvé - van IJ,
9 Oct. 1816
J.P. Kruseman appointed as Prov. Resident of Yogya dating from 6 Oct.

27 Nov. 1816
J. Dirksen
