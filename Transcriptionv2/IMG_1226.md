# Transcription of IMG_1226.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1226.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' Bundel 41: *Aug. 1816 – Sept. 1816*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-21 to 1816-09-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Reports of border banditry and royal court diplomacy during the early weeks of the Dutch Restoration; A.M.T. de Salis reports an armed raid burning down 20 houses in Pekalongan on 5 Aug 1816 with the theft of 14 water buffaloes and 258 Rupiahs worth of goods; exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II petitions the British to send his consort Ratu Kencana Wulan and two Radèn Ayus to Batavia, but two court ladies refuse the journey; peaceful celebration of Garebeg Puasa in Yogyakarta; Prince Paku Alam I contributes 400 silver Java Rupees to the national Waterloo Fund; British Resident John Crawfurd transfers 50,000 Spanish Dollars to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV; Resident Nahuys van Burgst accounts for 1,219.24 Rupiahs spent on wine and celebrations for King William I's birthday and the post-Puasa court reception; Chinese petition from Nie Jok Lim reporting the unauthorized establishment of a market (*pasaran*) at Klaten; Lt-General Anthing commands Netherlands Indies troops deploying to the Vorstenlanden; reappointment of J.G. Diétrée as official government translator.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, John Crawfurd, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Ratu Kencana Wulan, Radèn Ayu Murtiningrat, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), A.M.T. Baron de Salis, Nie Jok Lim, Lt-General Carl Heinrich Wilhelm Anthing, J.G. Diétrée, King William I of the Netherlands.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Pekalongan, Desa Brunjak, Klaten, Batavia, Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Djokjo Brieven (Bundel 41), Garebeg Puasa, Waterloo Fund, Spanish Dollars (`Sp. D.`), Java Rupees (`J.R.`), Pasaran (local market), Residency Translator (*Translateur*).
