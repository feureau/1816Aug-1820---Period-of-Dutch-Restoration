# Transcription of IMG_1274.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1274.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, Nos. 5 & 7: *Secret Letters from Solo, November–December 1816–1817*; and P. H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816*, p. 127)
- **Date Range:** 1804 to 1816-11-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official secret decree of the Dutch Commissioners-General (19 November 1816) commanding Surakarta Resident Pinket van Haak to conduct an immediate security inquiry into Sunan Pakubuwana IV's complicity with Kyai Murma Wijaya; Pinket van Haak's diplomatic dispatch of 27 November 1816 reiterating PB IV's treacherous character (*"a weak and faithless man"*); surveillance of Arab merchant Sèh Abubakar, confirmed as a subversive intermediary and recommended for immediate banishment; historical review of the 1804–1810 alliance between Murma and former Surakarta Patih Danuningrat coordinating the 1810 Radèn Rongga uprising in Jipang/Madiun; Daendels's counter-insurgency purges; Murma's six-year period lying low (1810–1816) until his clandestine return to Surakarta days before the Kepundhung raid.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Sèh Abubakar, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Radèn Tumenggung Cakrakusuma, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Major Jeremiah Johnson, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Kepundhung, Jipang (Djipang), Madiun, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Commissioners-General Secret Decree (19 Nov. 1816), Inquest into Sunan's Treason, Sèh Abubakar's espionage, Radèn Rongga Revolt (1810), Patih Danuningrat dismissal, Daendels's banishment orders.
