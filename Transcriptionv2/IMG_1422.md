# Transcription of IMG_1422.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1422.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 61: January–June 1818)
- **Date Range:** 1818-01-05 to 1818-12-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Commercial inventories of regional textile exports to Semarang, timber auctions from the Sultan's forests in Serang, illegal timber extraction by Chinese tollkeepers, security diversions caused by the Cirebon insurrection, government stationery requisitions, public health vaccination audits, and high-level judicial proceedings against religious rebels. The document opens with a detailed commercial invoice (R.C.N. 21A80, 4 Dec. 1818) detailing textile shipments destined for Semarang: 44 packs of striped long cloths from Tana (f. 1,064.24 at 11 Spanish Dollars per *corge*), 6 packs from Wedi (f. 132), 10 packs of batik cloths from Jodok (f. 264), alongside 4 packs of fine batik jackets (*kabayas* / *borstkleedjes*) and headdresses (*dhestar*) manufactured in Tangkilan (each pack 5 *corges*, valued at f. 154 at 7 Sp. D. per *corge*). On 5 January 1818, the timber depot (*stapelplaats*) at Semarang reported the collection and sale of 2,110 pieces of timber and 1,000 felled planks harvested from Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV's forests in Serang. On 24 January, Resident De Salis reported that a Chinese Head Bandar had been illegally authorized by Demang Dermayuda to extract timber from Desa Mano Kertasana. On 28 January, Inspector-General of State Revenues Van Lawick van Pabst notified the Resident that widespread unrest in Cirebon had severed normal northern postal routes, forcing colonial communications between Batavia and the North Coast (*Pasisir*) to detour through the southern route via Banyumas, Cilacap, and Cipatut. On 18 February, Professor C.G.C. Reinwardt requested comprehensive 1817 vaccination statistics and child mortality returns for smallpox from Nahuys. Finally, on 14 March 1818, State Secretarial Decree No. 6 reviewed the ongoing imprisonment in Yogyakarta of the prominent court spiritualist Kyai Murma Wijaya and millenarian rebel Raden Mas Umar Mahdi, held since November 1816.

  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djogja], Semarang, Tana, Wedi, Jodok, Tangkilan, Serang, Mano Kertasana, Cirebon, Banyumas, Cilacap, Cipatut, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Textile manufacture (*batik*, *lurik*, *borstkleedje*, *kabaya*, *dhestar*), *corge* [kodi / unit of 20 pieces], Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.], Teak timber auctions (*stapelplaats*), illegal forestry cutting, Cirebon rebellion (1818), postal detour, smallpox vaccination (*koepok-enting*), infant mortality, detention of Kyai Murma & Umar Mahdi.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 61 (Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven van de Residentie Jogjakarta, January–June 1818).
* **Document Nature:** Commercial consignment invoices, timber depot inventories, postal security warnings, public health inquiries, and executive judicial decrees.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the rich economic integration of Central Java’s weaving centers with inter-island markets, while revealing administrative stress caused by the Cirebon revolt and the lingering judicial crisis surrounding Kyai Murma.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, Inspector-General Van Lawick van Pabst, Resident De Salis, Professor Reinwardt, and the colonial Council of Finances.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries register the residency dossier (`Yogya Res. Letters / Jan. - Jun. 1818 / [red ink: Dj. Br. 61 / Jan. - June 1818]`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Red ink margin notes highlighting weaving origins, currency sums, and file dates (`[red ink: (f. 24.16)]`, `[red ink: Tangkilan]`, `[red ink: 1768.]`, `[red ink: 1900]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, invoice registers, and official decree numbers on the left; verbatim itemization of textile consignments, timber statistics, security detours, medical audits, and state prisoner reviews on the right.
