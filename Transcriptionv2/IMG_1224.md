# Transcription of IMG_1224.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1224.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Solo Brieven Bundel 27 / Bv. 23)
- **Date Range:** 1816-07-22 to 1816-07-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson informs Lieutenant-Governor John Fendall that Sunan Pakubuwana IV has dispatched Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II and three senior tumenggungs (Wiraguna, Padmadipura, Sumadiningrat) on an embassy to Batavia to represent the Sunanate during the Dutch retrocession; the Dutch Commissioners-General appoint Willem Herman van IJsseldijk as Special Commissioner to the Vorstenlanden, directing the Surakarta envoys to remain at Semarang; the incoming Dutch Resident at Surakarta is instructed to maintain the auxiliary corps of Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagaran Legion); Chief Secretary Charles Assey instructs Resident Johnson to avoid alarming the Sunan and court, guaranteeing that the Anglo-Javanese treaties of 1812, royal stipends, and royal prerogatives will be fully upheld under the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson, John Fendall, Charles Assey, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Tumenggung Wiraguna, Tumenggung Padmadipura, Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Treaty of 1812, Corps of Prangwedana (Legiun Mangkunagaran), Patih office (*Kepatihan*), Special Commission, British Retrocession.
