# Transcription of IMG_1307.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1307.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], *Bundel Djokjo Brieven* / Javanese Letters of October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official contemporary Dutch translation (*Translaat*) of the confidential Javanese letter from Pangéran Adipati Paku Alam I to Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk regarding Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat / Djimsing); verbatim translation of Paku Alam’s earnest plea invoking the counsel of his late father Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (*Wijlen Zijn Hoogheid den Zulthan*), that permitting a Chinese favorite into familiarity with the monarch portends internal dynastic discord; manuscript contains original Javanese scribal endorsements and contemporary administrative dockets.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Paku Alam I (Pangeran Adispatte Pakoe Alam / Natakusuma), Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Djimsing / Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I / Sultan Swarga).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djocgocarta Kraton).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Translaat Javaansche Geschrifft* (official contemporary translation), Kapitan Cina, VOC First Resident (1786), Colonial Inter-court Diplomacy.
