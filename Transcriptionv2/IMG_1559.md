# Transcription of IMG_1559.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1559.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Portefeuille 6 No. 6, Nahuys van Burgst Archive; Surakarta Mortuary Accounts & Itinerary, Nov. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-02 to 1820-11-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion of Peter Carey's analytical research notes tracing the overland route, inter-court diplomacy, and total financial cost of the state funeral of Sunan Pakubuwana IV; withdrawal of Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) after escorting the cortege precisely two leagues (*paals*) outside the Surakarta Kraton; progress along the trunk post road: first major halt at the ruined former capital of Kartasura, placing the royal bier beneath a *pendopo* where local rural *santris* offered collective prayers; ascent of Mount Pitijes joined by 50 to 60 white-robed clerics; progress through Prambanan and Jagalan to the ancestral sanctuary of Pasar Gedé (Kota Gede), where the royal corpse rested overnight in the historic Great Mosque; arrival of a high diplomatic embassy sent by Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV of Yogyakarta consisting of five Tumenggungs and an Ngabèhi, who formally joined the cortege to express bilateral dynastic solidarity; reception by 150 priests of Imagiri upon crossing the Opak River; final mountain ascent and royal entombment on Mount Imagiri alongside the monarch’s sacred mummified turtle dove (*Kyai Kuwung*); geographical calculation: 16 hours of continuous marching covering approximately 50 leagues (*paals*); official Javanese treasury audit recording total state mortuary expenditures of 2,244 Spanish reals (Sp. D.); formal colonial and dynastic elevation of the Crown Prince as Sunan Pakubuwana V on 10 October 1820.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana V, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Kartasura, Pitijes Mountain, Prambanan, Jagalan, Pasar Gedé (Kota Gede), Opak River, Imagiri, Leiden (BPL archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Post road funeral itinerary, *pendopo*, *santri* vigil, inter-court diplomatic deputation, Opak River crossing, Imagiri royal necropolis, sacred animal burial (turtle dove *Kyai Kuwung*), Javanese treasury funeral accounts (*reals* / Spanish Dollars), imperial accession (*troonsverheffing*).
