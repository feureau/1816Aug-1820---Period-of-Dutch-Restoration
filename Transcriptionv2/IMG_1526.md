# Transcription of IMG_1526.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1526.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Yogyakarta & Surakarta Archives; Bundel PJ. Br. 63 & Solo Br. 131)
- **Date Range:** 1820-12-31 to 1831-06-21
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion of Resident Assistant J.C. Enger's late 1820 reporting on Pacitan, remarking that clearing and laying out new state coffee estates is excessively difficult and time-consuming, while recording the harvest of 351 edible swiftlet birds' nests (*sarang burung*) from the coastal sea cliffs at Watu Karang; archival excerpt from Surakarta Residency Bundel 131 (*Staat der Landen van Mangkoe Negoro 1830–31*), documenting high-level land and apanage settlement post-Java War involving Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) and Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry on 3 June 1831 (22 Dinar AJ 1758); dispute over the 63½ jungs retained by Pangéran Radèn Prabuwijaya versus apanage deductions (*looses jungs*) at Tuk Warga in Kediri; formal colonial resolution of 21 June 1831 confirming 63½ / 50½ jungs of hereditary pusaka lands granted by Sunan Pakubuwana VII to Prabuwijaya and Hadiwijaya, cross-referenced against Commissioner Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven's twin dispatches; vernacular Javanese archival endorsement on the bundle wrapper recording the royal grant of village apanages by the Supreme Government (*Kangjêng Supêrmèn*) amounting to 500 karya (cacah) east of the Brawi / Brantas river (*tanah sawetan Bêrawi*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** J.C. Enger, G.A. d'Abo, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Pangéran Radèn Prabuwijaya, Sunan Pakubuwana VII, Hadiwijaya, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan, Watu Karang, Surakarta (Solo), Tuk Warga, Kediri, Brawi (Brantas River).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Edible birds' nests (*sarang burung*), *Staat der Landen van Mangkoe Negoro*, *jung* (land measure, approx. 4 bau), *pusaka* (sacred heirloom / hereditary estate), *Kangjêng Supêrmèn* (Supreme Government / Hoge Regering), *karya* / *cacah* (household fiscal unit), Mangkunagaran apanages.
