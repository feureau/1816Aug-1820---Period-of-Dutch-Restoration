## Metadata

- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Soerakarta* [AVS] Gewoon 1816 / Fort Vastenburg & Treaty Financial Ledgers)
- **Date Range:** 1812-08-01 to 1816-12-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Promulgation of revised court ceremonial protocol for royal visits and Garebeg festivals at Surakarta (22 December 1816); mobilization of 170 corvée laborers and specialized artisans for reconstruction work on the Dutch fortress at Surakarta (Fort Vastenburg), requisitioned from the Mangkunagaran (*MN*) and the royal transport guild (*Gladhag*), including Kalang timber-hewers and Goweng sawyers (4 December 1816); official register of semi-annual British indemnity subsidies and appanage lease payments submitted by Resident Pinket van Haak (18 November 1816), covering tollgates, edible bird's nests, bazaar taxes, Desa Sumbereng rent to Pangéran Pakuwingrat, and military stipends to Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Pangéran Pakuwingrat, Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Fort Vastenburg, Desa Sumbereng.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AVS (*Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Soerakarta*), Fort Vastenburg construction, Gladhag (transport and corvée guild), Kalang (woodcutter caste), Goweng (sawyers), Mangkunagaran Legion, Treaty of 1 August 1812, Spanish Dollars (Sp.
