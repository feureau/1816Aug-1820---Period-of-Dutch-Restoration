H 698b.

AVJ (Gewoon)
1816
- YOGYA - (1816)

11 Aug 1816
NvB -> G.G.
Nahuys relates pleasure of P.A. at arrival of NvB at Yogya.
also gratitude to be relayed -> G.G. / Comm.-Gen.

Bodyguard of Sultan 25 men & 1 lieutenant.

13 Aug 1816
NvB -> G.G.
Instructions of British Government:
Other Public Instructions written with
an object of BLINDING and darkening
understanding of events
Treaty 1812 -> after attack on Kraton -> 50.000 Sp.D. to HB III

14 Aug
NvB -> Res Semarang
Dutch flag Raised & salute of 21 guns.

29 Aug
NvB -> Crawfurd
Crawfurd promises to pay ½ yearly allowance
of 50.000 to HB III if Paku Alam send 2 trustworthy
people to Semarang to collect money.

18 Aug
NvB -> G.G.
Brit. Res. 100,000 Rps / Dutch Res. only f 2215 p. month.

2 Sept
NvB -> G.G.
Relates salutary PROHIBITION of gambling / cockfights
by the BRITISH:
saves many lives!

9 Sept.
NvB -> G.G.
arrival of Van IJsseldijk

[Red Ink Heading]:
Ratu Kencono Wulan to Batavia to join HB II

23 Aug.
NvB -> G.G.
asks G.G. to look after Ratu Kencana [Wulan], R.Ay. Murtiningrat /
Jayaningrat / Dewaningrat who would
be travelling to Batavia to be with Sultan Sepuh.

23 Oct
NvB -> Res Kedu (Valck)
No regulations about tollgates:
earlier personal income of the Resident: cf Van den Berg.

30 Oct
Cr. -> Nahuys
annual stipend of HB III which Kedu has been paying
