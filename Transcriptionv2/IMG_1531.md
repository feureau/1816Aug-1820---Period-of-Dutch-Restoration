# Transcription of IMG_1531.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1531.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief Den Haag / Ministerie van Koloniën, MvK; *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, Geheim, 17 & 30 March 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-25 to 1820-03-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official complaint and corruption petition lodged by Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara (formerly Nêcawitrana, influential head of the Surakarta Kalang community) alleging that Residency Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter solicited and accepted bribes totaling 20,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) to secure official administrative appointments in Surakarta and Yogyakarta; series of urgent dispatches from Assistant Resident Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe (7, 9, 10, and 19 March 1820) reporting the explosive fallout inside the Surakarta Kraton; Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s intense outrage at Winter’s counter-allegation that Sécanagara was orchestrating intrigues between dismissed Resident Van Prehn and Lippe, which the Sunan interpreted as an intolerable assault on royal authority; punitive court degradation of Sécanagara, who was stripped of office, subjected to ritual public head-shaving, banished, and placed under a royal damnatio memoriae prohibiting subjects from ever uttering his name; PB IV’s resulting psychological fury that triggered his unilateral repudiation of European treaties and reassertion of pre-1808 Batavian sovereign rights; public indifference to the treaty declaration contrasted with intense popular gossip regarding the Sécanagara corruption scandal; diplomatic breakthrough achieved by Acting Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, who after three tense private audiences persuaded PB IV to retract his defiance and reaffirm colonial treaties.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara (Nicanêgara / Nêcawitrana), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Rijck van Prehn, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Ministerie van Koloniën (MvK), *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* (BGG Geheim), Kalang community, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), official bribery (*steekpenningen*), royal shaming / tonsure, *damnatio memoriae*, treaty repudiation, personal diplomacy.
