# Transcription of IMG_1558.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1558.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Portefeuille 6 No. 7, Nahuys van Burgst Archive; Surakarta Mortuary Rites & Order of March, Nov. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-01 to 1820-11-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Working analytical notes by Peter Carey summarizing the anointing, encoffining, and procession structure for the state funeral of Sunan Pakubuwana IV in Surakarta (1–2 October 1820); application of sacred sandalwood oil (*cendana*) into the sensory openings (mouth, nostrils, ears); wrapping of the royal body in five expansive sheets of fine white linen; deposition on the right side within a nail-less, peg-fastened teakwood coffin (*peti kayu jati*) lined with earth, sand, and Zamzam water brought by Javanese hajjis from Mecca; overnight prayers in the royal chapel (*surambi*) guarded by Islamic clerics; departure of the bier under four golden imperial parasols (*payung*) accompanied by the ritual passing of all blood descendants beneath the bier (*trobogan* / *nglulubi*); 40-day continuous incense offering maintained beside the royal deathbed (*peturon dalem*); coordinated artillery gun salutes from the ramparts of Fort Vastenburg and royal kraton batteries; complete structural analysis of the 22-part imperial cortege, including royal bodyguard regiments (*Miji Pamilih*, *Patang Puluh Abang*, *Sarageni*, *Nirbaya*), imperial regalia bearers, European trumpeters and coachmen, white-robed chanting *santris*, four *Kyai Jurus* strewing 5,000 rupees in coins and yellow rice (*udhik-udhik*), and the Dutch cavalry escort.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Sunan Pakubuwana V, Tumenggung Bratanegara, Tumenggung Prawiradipura, Tumenggung Mangkuyuda, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Fort Vastenburg, Mecca, Imagiri.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Mortuary anointing, sandalwood oil (*cendana*), Zamzam water / Mecca soil, peg-fastened teak coffin, golden *payung* (parasols), *trobogan* (under-bier crawl), 40-day incense offering (*slametan 40 dina*), artillery salutes, *Miji Pamilih*, *Patang Puluh Abang*, *Sarageni*, *Nirbaya*, *Kyai Jurus*, *udhik-udhik* (turmeric rice and coins).
