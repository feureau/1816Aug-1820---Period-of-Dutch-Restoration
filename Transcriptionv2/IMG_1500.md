### Header & Archival Classification

* **Collection:** Peter Carey Archival Research Notes
* **Archive Reference:** Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 139 (ANRI Solo 139) / Peter Carey Working Binder
* **Subject / Context:** Manuscript mounting sheet containing an original 1819 Arabic-script (*Pegon*) prophetic tract fragment by Kyai Iman Sampurna, serving as an archival cover/specimen.
* **Transcriber:** Peter Carey.
* **[Archival Note]:** `1. **Carrier Medium:** A standard cream lined binder leaf (horizontal blue feint ruling, red left vertical margin line) measuring approximately 210 x 297 mm, punched with two binder perforations on the left margin.`
* **[Archival Note]:** `2. **Affixed Manuscript Fragment:** Affixed to the lower left quadrant of the backing sheet is an authentic nineteenth-century paper leaf fragment from the Surakarta Residency archives (ANRI Solo 139).`
* The fragment measures roughly 120 x 140 mm.
* Distinct uneven, hand-torn edges along the left, bottom, and right boundaries.
