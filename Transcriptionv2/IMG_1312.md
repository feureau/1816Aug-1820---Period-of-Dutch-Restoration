# Transcription of IMG_1312.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1312.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI, *Solo Brieven* / Diplomatic Dossier: Van IJsseldijk to PB IV)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival mounting sheet and opening draft of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's formal diplomatic address to Sunan Pakubuwana IV of Surakarta dated 2 September 1816; announces the Dutch Restoration under King William I, conveys the official dispatches of the Commissioners-General, and guarantees adherence to the Anglo-Dutch territorial settlements (specifically recognizing the 1 August 1812 Treaty).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), King William I of the Netherlands, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, G. A. G. P. Baron van der Capellen, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Buitengewoon Gezant (Extraordinary Envoy), Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.), Treaty of 1 August 1812, Dutch Restoration of 1816.
