H. L. Domis (Res. Surabaya)
to V. d. Bosch,
16 Dec. 1831

Most schools in Surabaya not more than langgar. (prayer houses)
Instruction mostly:
Repetition of Arabic prayers — not even understood by teacher himself.
In some places instruction in Reading / writing Arabic
and very few study of other Books / Qur'ān on Religion
Setting up of langgar, entirely arbitrary = can be done by anyone for immediate family first and then also friends / relations / children of strangers.
People + money sometimes invite a priest to give Instruction in their House.
Teacher paid by voluntary gifts + Fitra. Rice + Duits.
breeding hotbeds of superstition / fanaticism.
& Destruction / rindus / opposition to govt.

*Acte van Verstand* to be given to schools by Resident + name of school + teacher registered.
Only schools which teach children to Read / write Javanese to be allowed to exist.
+ pupils to be given certificates once studies finished.
only " + such certificates to be allowed to enter govt. service.

Children below 12 years old — only to study for 3 hours + have 1 meal at school.
No Hajis to be allowed to become Teachers. per day.
