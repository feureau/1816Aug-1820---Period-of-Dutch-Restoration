# Transcription of IMG_1223.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1223.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Solo Brieven Bundel 27 / Bv. 23)
- **Date Range:** 1816-03-06 to 1816-07-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson reports to Raffles on the offense of Bansekoa (cutting waringin trees on the paséban and beating imperial gamelans without orders); Johnson assures John Fendall of peaceful conditions in Surakarta and absence of outside links to the 1815 Sepoy conspiracy; Sunan Pakubuwana IV gives formal approbation to the exile of his brother Pangéran Mangkubumi to the Moluccas under minimal restraint; Captain Methven advises dispatch of a royal envoy to Batavia upon arrival of the Dutch Commissioners-General; British Government rejects PB IV's request for a 16,000 Spanish Dollar loan; recruitment in Rembang for colonial regiments under the Dutch Crown.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson, Thomas Stamford Raffles, John Fendall, Charles Assey, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Captain Methven, Bansekoa (Banũkoa), Ahmad Jedik (Amad Sidik).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Paséban (Surakarta Court Square), Kraton Surakarta, Batavia, Moluccas (Ambon), Rembang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Solo Brieven, Sepoy conspiracy, Paséban, Waringin Kurung, Imperial Gamelan, Spanish Dollars (`Sp. D.`), British Retrocession.
