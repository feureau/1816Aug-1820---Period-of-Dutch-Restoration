## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1522.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63A / *PJ. Br. 63A*, Feb.–May 1820) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Commercial Invoices & Security Intelligence))
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-10 to 1820-05-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse commercial, criminal, agrarian, and counter-insurgency dispatches: itemized commercial invoice of Surakarta and Wedhi textiles shipped from Yogyakarta to Semarang valued at f. 4,208.10; Opzichter Engel reporting heavy monsoon rain damage to Pacitan plantations and a fatal kris murder at Desa Nalla; Governor-General's resolution (*BGG 21 Apr 1820 No. 12*) allocating a biennial clothing grant of f. 4,000 (f. 20 per dragoon) for the Pakualam cavalry corps; frantic report from Chinese tax farmer Kawa Kilroëy describing an armed night attack by 50 bandits who plundered the Kritik toll barrier while villagers ignored alarm drums; and Pasuruan Resident Ellinghuizen reporting the arrest at Desa Temporan (Rawa) of 13 Yogyakarta rebels and Cirebon santri students involved in the Malang messianic conspiracy.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Singadana, Brunadipa, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Kawa Kilroëy (Kritik tollgate farmer), J.C. Ellinghuizen (Resident of Pasuruan), Tirta Diraya, Markum, Amad Diyo, Amad Narjo, Dokirti, Krama-ari, Nur Sarip, Nur Rahman, Naliman, Jaka, Mêrtajaya, Santika, Wirajaya.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Wèdhi, Semarang, Pacitan (Desa Nalla), Kritik tollgate, Pasuruan, Desa Temporan (Rawa / Tulungagung), Malang, Cirebon, Magelang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Textile invoices (*corge* / *sarong* / *citis* / *ginggang*), Kris murder, Pakualaman Cavalry Corps clothing subsidy (*BGG in Rade*), Armed tollgate raid, Alarm signaling (*kenthongan* / riceblocks), Santri religious networks (*mengaji* / Quranic recitation), Penal extradition.
