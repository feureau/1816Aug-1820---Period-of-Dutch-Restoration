# Transcription of IMG_1309.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1309.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], *Bundel Djokjo Brieven* / Javanese Court Transliterations)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's Romanized Javanese transliteration: (1) Conclusion of Kyahi Adipati Purwa's memorandum (Section 8) detailing Tan Jin Sing’s usurpation of high aristocratic privileges (demanding *sembah* deference equal to the Patih’s consort, appropriating lands, maintaining female *bedhaya* court dancers, sitting as an equal beside Pangéran Mangkubumi at Kadanurjan assemblies, and demanding rice fields for promoting *bupatis*); dated 11 Dulkangidah AJ 1743 (4 October 1816); (2) Sections 9–10: Personal petition from Pangéran Paku Alam I to Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (*Tuwan Idelir*) recalling Sultan Mangkubumi’s (*Sultan Swarga*) admonition that Chinese proximity to the throne produces discord (*witing resan*); warning that while Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV is a minor, Tan Jin Sing's arrogance (*piangkuh*) undermines colonial governance and court harmony, formally pleading for his permanent expulsion (*kakesahaken*) from Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyahi Adipati Purwa, Radèn Tumenggung Mangundipura, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (Tuwan Idelir / vIJ), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I / Sultan Swarga), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat / Jim Sing).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Ngayogya, Kadanurjan).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Bedhaya* court dancers, *Nyembah* royal obeisance, *Witing Resan* (seed of civil strife), *Angresahi Pranata* (destroying constitutional order), Expulsion petition (*kakesahaken*).
