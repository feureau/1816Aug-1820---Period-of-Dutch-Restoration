Mostly Education in Native schools given to Jav. of Rank not common Jav.

Too few schools! Too far from most Desas!
Common Jav. cannot miss sons for so long and time for agriculture *

If common Jav. attend schools only learn Religious Instruction.
Teachers all priests some Hajis. — But not essential to have been to Mekka.
Pëngulus + mosque officials give instruction.
No set salary for Teachers — only given according to parents of children
+ some sědêkah = Religious meals
v. few common Jav. even v. few Lurahs in Pèkalongan who can Read + write. = not taught well in Pèsantrèns.
Setting up of Govrmt. schools Teaching Reading / Writing / Religion
+ placed near enough to Desas that children can still work on Parents land.
(1) Regents to supervise schools + choose Teachers. + Inspect schools at regular intervals.
2 schools needed in every District of Residency.
@ Teachers to get f. 60 per year and 1 1/2 Jung sawah rent free
(2) 24 schools needed for 12 Districts in Pèk.
24 Teachers @ f. 1440 p.a.
(3) Yearly Report of Res. on schools.

P. le Clercq (Res. of Semarang)
to van den Bosch,
