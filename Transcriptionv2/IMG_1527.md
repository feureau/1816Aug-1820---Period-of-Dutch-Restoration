# Transcription of IMG_1527.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1527.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Yogyakarta Residency Letters / Bundel PJ. Br. 51 B, *Afgaande Stukken 1820*, Jan.–Aug. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-01-20 to 1820-08-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official report from Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst to Governor-General Van der Capellen announcing the birth of a royal son to Ratu Kencana on 25 January 1820; request by Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I) for his son Pangéran Suryaningprang to receive a regular officer's commission in the King's Army comparable to Surakarta royalty, with Nahuys recommending the rank of Captain at 1st Lieutenant's pay; financial commutation of the Pakualam Legion's uniform equipment (*monteringstukken*) into an annual cash grant of 4,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.); death of Queen Grandmother Ratu Ageng (formerly Ratu Kedhaton, widow of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II) on 12 July 1820; statistical audit of Yogyakarta customs tollgate (*bandar*) returns demonstrating a dramatic doubling from f. 147,818 in 1817 to f. 417,540 in 1821 across 34 tollgates and 2 weighing stations (*gedhong timbangan*); comparison of gross opium tax farm revenues showing a decline from f. 560,988 in 1819 to f. 481,244 in 1820; Nahuys' urgent advisory to the Inspector of Vaccine in Batavia reporting that a single failed arm-to-arm smallpox vaccination severely compromised Javanese public trust, proposing that Islamic religious leaders (*santris* / priests) be enlisted as vaccinators under European medical supervision.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Ratu Kencana, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Pangéran Suryaningprang (Paku Alam II), Pangéran Purbaya, Radèn Tumenggung Cakradipura, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja / Yogya), Surakarta (Solo), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Afgaande Stukken*, Pakualaman Legion, *monteringstukken* (military accoutrements), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Dutch Florins (f. / Dfl), tollgates (*bandar*), *gedhong timbangan* (public weigh-house), opium tax farm (*bandar madat* / candhu), cowpox vaccination (*koepok-inenting*), *santri* / court religious officials.
