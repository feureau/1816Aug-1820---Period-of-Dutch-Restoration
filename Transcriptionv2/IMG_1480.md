# Transcription of IMG_1480.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1480.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A, *Aug.–Nov. 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-14 to 1819-09-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse military, commercial, forestry, and agricultural correspondence: appointment (*BGG 13 Aug 1819 No. 3*) of pensioned Lieutenant Thomas Paulus as Provisional Translator and instructor at the Semarang Military School; gubernatorial directive (*BGG 4 Sept 1819 No. 9*) ordering an investigation into indigenous Javanese textile fashions and market pricing to assist Dutch textile manufacturers; inquiry from Army Commander Major-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock regarding the origin and status of the Sultan's European Bodyguard; inventory of dilapidated household goods abandoned in Pacitan by the banished Demang Netrawijaya; Surakarta Resident Van Prehn reporting illegal timber felling in the Naganaga teak forests following the 1816 retrocession; residence permits issued to Louis Mille and Dr. Harvey Thomson; and Opzichter Engel's report that Tumenggung Jagakarya deployed 1,130 corvée laborers to Kêdhuwang to harvest pepper cuttings for planting beneath newly established Dadap shade trees.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Lieutenant Thomas Paulus, J. de Bruijn (Resident of Semarang), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Major-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock (Commander of Netherlands Indies Army), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Demang Netrawijaya, Rijck van Prehn (Provisional Resident of Surakarta), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Louis Mille, Dr. Harvey Thomson (*Tuan Tom*), Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya, Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Yogyakarta, Pacitan, Naganaga teak forests, Surakarta (Solo), Kêdhuwang, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Militaire School*, Dutch textile export promotion, Sultan's European Bodyguard (*Lijfwacht*), confiscated estate property, illicit teak timber felling (*sluikkap*), residence permits (*toelatingen*), corvée labor mobilization, pepper nurseries, *Dadap* shade forestry.
