# Transcription of IMG_1546.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1546.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV Leiden, H 698b; *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djokjokarta 1820* [AvJ 1820], Feb.–Sept. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-01-26 to 1820-09-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** General administrative and economic report for the Yogyakarta Residency (*AvJ 1820*); official report from Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst to the Director-General of Finances reviewing the lucrative monopoly concession on edible swiftlet birds' nests (*sarang burung*), noting that the cliffs of Karangbolong, Pautan, and Rawa were yielded to the colonial government, while Rawa (Ngrowo) in the eastern marches was leased to Patih Danureja IV for 400 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) annually; industrial survey of the royal limestone kilns at Gamping, producing 600 *pikuls* of lime monthly for the building trades in Yogyakarta; dynastic milestones: birth of a royal prince (the future Sultan Hamengkubuwana V) to Ratu Kencana on 26 January 1820 honored by a 9-gun artillery salute; ceremonial transfer of the Great Seal of State to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV on 27 January 1820 marked by a 15-gun royal salute; death of Queen Grandmother Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton, primary consort of HB II) on the festive day of Garebeg Puwasa (12 July 1820); detailed cost-benefit analysis by Nahuys on South Sea salt manufacturing submitted to the Department of Finances: 1 *koyang* of South Sea salt cost 35 Spanish Dollars, requiring 60 porters (*baturs*) earning 1 Java Rupee each to haul it 60–70 leagues overland to the North Coast (f. 60 transport cost per koyang), rendering southern salt production far more wasteful and uncompetitive than northern solar evaporation despite continuous processing during the wet monsoon.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Ratu Kencana, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Karangbolong, Pautan, Rawa (Ngrowo), Gamping, Surakarta, South Coast, Pasisir (North Coast).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Algemeen Verslag van Djokjokarta* (AvJ 1820), edible birds' nests (*sarang burung*), limestone quarries, *pikul* (shoulder load, approx. 125 lbs), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Great Seal of State (*cap kraton*), artillery salutes, Garebeg Puwasa, South Sea salt (*uyah kidul*), *koyang* (bulk volume measure), *batur* (coolie / porter), overland haulage costs.
