# Transcription of IMG_1543.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1543.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Surakarta Residency Secret Records / Bundel H 698b, AvS Geheim, Dec. 1820; *Procès-Verbaal der Terechtstelling van den Res. van Prehn en der Translateur Winter*, cont.)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-17 to 1820-12-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of the judicial cross-examination of dismissed Resident Rijck van Prehn and Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter; testimony of Dalem Bupati Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat confirming that Van Prehn routinely presided over the Sunan’s Supreme Court (*Raad des Soenans*); arbitrary judicial interference and extortion directed against Islamic religious figures: Mas Pengulu of Surakarta was unlawfully fined alongside three prominent religious teachers (*santris* / priests)—Mêndi, Regal, and Paninga—from the sacred pilgrimage center of Tembayat; the judicial fines were collected not in the Sunan’s court, but inside the private office (*kantoor*) of the Residency House and handed to Pangéran Cakrakusuma on public Report Day before the assembled bupatis; deposition from the Patih of the Kepatihan proving that Jakadana paid Winter a direct bribe of 158 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) to secure appointment as Head Priest (*Ketib* / *Imam*) at Tembayat; testimony of former Regent of Srengat Radèn Tumenggung Metadiningrat exposing that Winter extorted a 500 Sp. D. kickback from a 4,500 Sp. D. debt liquidation owed by Ngabèhi Suradirja to Chinese merchant Pue Hien; damning forensic proof that on 17 January 1819 Van Prehn personally sentenced the Ngabèhi of Sêlakatunggal and two accomplices to the barbaric sentence of 300 rattan lashes (*rottingslagen*) and lifetime banishment in chains from Java; conclusive finding by the commission of inquiry that European residents possess strictly review powers (*toezicht*) over Javanese court decisions under Article 8 of the 1812 treaty and Article 3 of the Resident’s Instructions, confirming Van Prehn’s actions as illegal tyranny.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Rijck van Prehn, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, Mas Pengulu of Surakarta, Pangéran Cakrakusuma, Jakadana, Radèn Tumenggung Metadiningrat of Srengat, Ngabèhi Suradirja, Pue Hien, Ngabèhi of Sêlakatunggal.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Tembayat (Bayat), Srengat, Sêlakatunggal, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Procès-Verbaal van Verhoor*, *Raad des Soenans*, *Pengulu* (chief Islamic judge), Tembayat pilgrimage shrine, judicial fines (*boeten*), Residency Kantoor, venality of ecclesiastical offices (*simony*), Chinese commercial debts, rattan lashes (*rottingslagen*), penal servitude in chains (*dwangarbeid in de ketting*), Article 8 of 1812 Treaty, Article 3 of Resident's Instructions.
