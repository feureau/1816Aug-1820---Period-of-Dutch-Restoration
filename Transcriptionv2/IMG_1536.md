# Transcription of IMG_1536.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1536.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, 5 Dec. 1820 Nos. 18 & 19)
- **Date Range:** 1820-11-24 to 1820-12-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resolution of Governor-General Van der Capellen in Council (5 Dec. 1820 No. 18) reviewing a report from Temporary Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (24 Nov. No. 72) regarding Cornelis van Vlissingen, government student of the Javanese language (*élève voor de Javaansche taal*), who married a Javanese woman; Van Vlissingen is granted the honorary title of Secretary at the Residencies of Yogyakarta and Surakarta under the strict statutory condition that he undertake no administrative burdens that might interfere with or disrupt his intensive philological study of the Javanese language; resolution (5 Dec. 1820 No. 19) recording the official letter from newly crowned Sunan Pakubuwana V (24 Nov. No. 73) expressing imperial gratitude to the Netherlands Indies Government for his coronation confirmation; delivery by the Sunan to the Resident of a royal letter addressed to his banished paternal uncle Pangéran Mangkubumi (exiled in Ambon since 1818), encased in yellow silk cloth (*kain kuning*) inside a small wooden chest, with orders for its secure maritime transmission via the port of Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Cornelis van Vlissingen, Sunan Pakubuwana V, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Semarang, Ambon, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, *élève voor de Javaansche taal* (government student of Javanese), interracial marriage, royal accession gratitude, political exile (*verbanning*), yellow royal silk (*kain kuning*), dynastic correspondence.
