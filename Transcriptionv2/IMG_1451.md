# Transcription of IMG_1451.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1451.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Kabinet Missive van den Gouverneur-Generaal, 19 Sept. 1831, No. 1431/1731: Local Education in Java, 1831–1832)
- **Date Range:** 1831-09-19 to 1832-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Launch of Governor-General Johannes van den Bosch's post-Java War general inquiry into indigenous primary schooling (*Kabinet Missive*, 19 Sept. 1831, No. 1431, dispatched from Tasikmalaya) and municipal responses from Batavia and the Priangan. Acting under Article 97 of the *Reglement op het Beleid der Regeering*, Van den Bosch directed the *Hoofd Baljuw* of Batavia and all Residents across Java to report on: (1) existing native school networks; and (2) practical measures to expand facilities, specifically evaluating petitions from the Netherlands Bible Society (*Nederlandsch Bijbelgenootschap*) and Missionary Society (*Zendelinggenootschap*). On 15 November 1831, *Hoofd Baljuw* Van der Vinne of Batavia reported hundreds of unregulated Quranic schools where students recited Arabic without comprehension, strongly advising Batavia against intervening in traditional pesantrens to avoid religious unrest, while urging secular state schools teaching arithmetic and Roman script without Christian missionary involvement. On 3 October 1831, the Resident of the Priangan Regencies reviewed the model primary school in Cianjur established under Governor-General Van der Capellen, which had successfully educated 20 to 30 sons of Javanese regents and radèns in Malay, Javanese, Arabic script, and arithmetic under European and native masters.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes van den Bosch, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hoofd Baljuw of Batavia (Van der Vinne), Resident of Priangan Regencies, Regents and Radèns of Cianjur.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tasikmalaya [Tasik Malaya], Batavia, Priangan [Preanger Regencies], Cianjur [Ciancur].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Regeeringsreglement* (Art. 97), Native primary schooling (*Inlandsch Onderwijs*), Netherlands Bible Society (*Bijbelgenootschap*), Missionary Society (*Zendelinggenootschap*), religious neutrality, Arabic vs. Roman script, Priyayi education.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Kabinet Missive van den Gouverneur-Generaal, 19 Sept. 1831, No. 1431 / Kabinet Dossier No. 20265 (31 Dec. 1832).
* **Document Nature:** Executive circular, judicial and administrative returns, and educational policy memoranda.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Links Van der Capellen's initial 1819 educational initiatives to the post-Java War reconstruction under Van den Bosch, highlighting colonial wariness toward Christian missionaries in Javanese schooling.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Governor-General Van den Bosch, Hoofd Baljuw Van der Vinne, and the Resident of Priangan.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries establish cabinet register numbers and repository series (`Kab. 19 Sept. 1831 no. 1731 / Local Education Java 1831 / [Top right:] Tasik Malaya / Kabinet no. 1431 / 19 Sept. 1831. / Regeerings Reglement.`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Cabinet decree citations, regional headings, and dates on the left; verbatim summaries of Van den Bosch's circular, Batavia baljuw returns, and the Cianjur priyayi school on the right.
