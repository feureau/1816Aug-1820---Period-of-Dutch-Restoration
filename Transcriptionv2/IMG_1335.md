# Transcription of IMG_1335.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1335.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, concluding synthesis)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s final conferences at Yogyakarta on 14 September 1816, accompanied by Resident Nahuys and Johan Diederik Kruseman; linguistic difficulties of the young Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (who spoke no Malay), with Patih Danureja IV translating ineptly and truncating the conversation; the boy Sultan’s private curiosity about Holland on the return journey; masterly historiographical synthesis contrasting the two principalities: Yogyakarta outwardly tranquil with no fear of an uprising despite court dissimulation; Surakarta characterized as inherently hostile to European rule, with Sunan Pakubuwana IV profiled as an incurable master of guile (*master of guile*) who has plotted the destruction of European power throughout his entire reign (from the 1790 Pakepung crisis to the 1815 Sepoy Rebellion), guided by subversive Islamic clerics (*santris* and priests) in a quest to restore the undivided Mataram empire.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Johan Diederik Kruseman, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton), Surakarta (Solo), Mataram, The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Dissimulation (*Veinzen*), Master of Guile, 1790 Pakepung Crisis, 1815 Sepoy Rebellion, Islamic Court Clerics (*Santri* Advisers), Restoration of Mataram Hegemony.
