1817 (Jan/Feb)

27 JAN 1817
VISIT from TJAKRA NEGARA
from exile in SURABAYA:
exiled for part in Raden Rongga affair:
followed by DANUNINGRAT: & exiled for pt. in RANGGA affair
T.N. organises connection SOLO - DJOCJA 1812 against BRITISH.
Exiled for part in affair:
asks G.G. to allow wives of T.N. to go to Surubaya:

1 FEB 1817
Haak -> G.G.
UNCLEAR HOW RR = CONNECTION UMAR MANDI.
KIJAI MOERMO.

5 FEB 1817
Besluiten van G.G.
REFUSAL to allow Tjakranegara to return to SOLO.

2 May 1817
Haak -> G.G.
van Iysseldyck ->
P. Adg. PURBA. SUNAN to marry away daughters: of P.W.
