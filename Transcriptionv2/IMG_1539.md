### Header & Archival Classification

* **Medium & Support:** Modern lightweight lined research paper (blue horizontal ruling) photographed on its verso (reverse) side.
* **Physical Condition:** Good archival preservation; clean white paper stock with minor edge curling and binder margin along the left edge.
* **Ink & Handwriting:** Blue ballpoint ink in the neat, legible academic hand of Peter Carey.
* **Spatial Arrangement:** The page is almost entirely blank with the exception of a single truncated docket inscription written horizontally across the upper margin: `"5 Dec 1820 No. 17: Res of Sala ="`. The remainder of the folio exhibits faint reversed ink show-through (*bleed-through*) from the densely written recto page (`IMG_1538`), which outlines postal relay routes and Javanese royal condolence correspondence.
