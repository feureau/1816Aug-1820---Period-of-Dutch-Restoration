# Transcription of IMG_1345.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1345.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys*, 22 October 1816; and *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical structural critique of public administration in the *Vorstenlanden* (Vl.); Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk warns that armed gang robberies (*kècu*) will continuously escalate so long as governance in the princely courts remains corrupt, inefficient, and arbitrary; warning that persistent rural instability will inevitably erupt into widespread anti-colonial rebellions; cynical observation that aggrieved royal princes at both courts welcome public disorders, as it enables them to "fish in troubled waters" and weaken colonial authority; Van IJsseldijk's four-point administrative program:
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Patih Danureja IV, Patih Sasradiningrat II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Vorstenlanden (Princely States), Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Kedu (Kědhu).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *kècu* (rural highwaymen / gang robbers), *tapa* / *japas* (ascetics / mystical recluses), *kyai* (Islamic scholars), *haji* (pilgrims to Mecca), *Patih* (prime minister), peasant displacement, land-rent reform, direct colonial annexation, separation of royalty from peasantry, Kedu model.
