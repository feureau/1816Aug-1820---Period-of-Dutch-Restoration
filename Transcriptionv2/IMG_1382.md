## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1382.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Exhibitum* [Exh.] 1 May 1817 La a Nos. 20, 21, 22, 46; Exh. 14/21 July 1817 Secret La A / Raad van Financiën & Surabaya Residency Dispatches)
- **Date Range:** 1817-03-31 to 1817-07-21
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Administrative and security challenges facing the colonial regime in mid-1817: the impossibility of executing Batavia's rapid population census order in the Vorstenlanden, and the outbreak of armed unrest in East Java (Malang/Pasuruan). On 31 March 1817, the President of the *Raad van Financiën* (Bousquet) ordered all Residents to submit comprehensive population returns within fourteen days. Assistant Resident R.C.N. d'Abo and Commissioner Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk responded that compliance was completely impossible; accurate demographic figures for Yogyakarta were entirely lacking because indigenous priyayi officials deliberately minimized population counts to reduce tax quotas and corvée labor obligations. D'Abo recommended establishing a mixed European-Javanese Commission to survey populations and measure road mileages (noting roads possessed no distance markers, citing John Crawfurd's 1812 *Statistieke Beschrijving van Pacitan* as a rare British benchmark). Meanwhile, secret dispatches from July 1817 document Major Nahuys van Burgst's return from an arduous tour of the Gagatan district to coordinate with Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) in suppressing an insurrection in East Java; a band of 40 armed men with muskets had appeared in Malang and Pasuruan, falsely claiming authorization from Prangwedana, before retreating to Srengat where their numbers swelled to 100.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo [R.C.N. d'Abo], J. Bousquet [Pres. Raad van Financiën], Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Pangéran Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II, John Crawfurd, Resident of Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Yk.], Pacitan, District of Gagatan, Malang, Pasuruan, Surabaya, District of Srengat, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Raad van Financiën*, *Statistieke Beschrijving van Pacitan*, population census (*volkstelling*), demographic underreporting, corvée concealment, road mileage markers, armed band / conspiracy (*oproer*), Mangkunagaran Legion.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** Nationaal Archief (The Hague) / ANRI, Exhibitum 1 Mei 1817 La a Nos. 20, 21, 22, 46; Exhibitum 14/21 Juli 1817 Secret La A.
* **Document Nature:** Official executive minutes, demographic reports to the Department of Finance, and secret military dispatches between regional Residents and the Commissarissen-Generaal.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the stark institutional gap between Batavia’s bureaucratic ambitions (demanding total demographic censuses within two weeks) and the practical realities of indirect rule in Central and East Java, paired with the persistent danger of armed bands exploiting princely names to destabilize eastern districts.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Colonial civil administrators (D'Abo, Van IJsseldijk, Bousquet, Nahuys), princely military leaders (Pangéran Prangwedana), and the Resident of Surabaya.
* **Archival Registry Citations:** Top margin and left-hand column feature formal archival ledger citations (`Exh. 1 May 1817 La a no. 20, 21. & 22 & / Exh. 1 May 1817 La a no. 46 / Exh. 21 [or 14] Jul. 1817 Secret La A`).
* **Marginal Notes:** Topical indicators (`Pop.`, `[margin: See Crawfurd]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Archival exhibitum markers, dispatch dates, and administrative rubrics on the left; dense summaries of demographic administration, colonial policy debates, and East Java military alarms on the right.
