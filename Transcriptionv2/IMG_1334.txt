# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

8 Sept. in Evening – van IJ goes to Yogya[^2].

Kruseman leaves for Klaten earlier to prepare keep food / get local Javanese etc for van IJ's party.

p. 23. van IJ arrives early in morning of 9. & goes on to Yogya in Nahuys' carriage.

Some Princes / Regents who had earlier known van IJ come to greet him.

12 Sept. 1816 Hands over to Sultan = letter from van der Capellen.

Then conversations HB IV / grandmother / mother. Ratu Kidhaton takes van IJ's hand in a very friendly fashion & places him by her side. Constantly tells him not to forget her grandson.

p. 24. Stately visit of HB IV & court to Residency House. =

also conversation about background of Returning Java to Dutch by British. Easier to explain than at Sala – Because knows many of the older persons / princes / Regents.

p. 25. Assurances of Paku Alam [van IJsseldijk personally known at courts] about Crawfurd's good faith in describing Returning Java -> Dutch.

cf letter of van IJ = 13 Sept about state of Yogya court.
