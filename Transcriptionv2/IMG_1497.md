# Transcription of IMG_1497.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1497.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 131, *Translaten en Verbalen 1816–1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1818-12-01 to 1819-08-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Scholarly English translation by Dr. Peter Carey of the celebrated prophetic and millenarian letter composed by two native seers from Ajibarang and Banyumas in the month of *Repit* (December 1818): foretelling a total cosmic and political inversion of the world following Governor-General Van der Capellen's vice-regal progress through Banyumas; prophesying the enthronement of Lurung Tengah (son of the Adipati of Brebes and direct descendant of the rebel monarch Sunan Kuning) as the Sovereign of the Righteous (*Ratu Adil*); claiming mystical brotherhood with all men and celestial marriage with all women in heaven; instructing that the text be submitted hierarchically to Raden Tumenggung Udanagara, Pangéran Prangwedana, the Patih, the two royal Sovereigns, and Governor-General Van der Capellen; and proclaiming that the sacred court cannon *Nyai Setomi* belongs by divine right to the Governor-General.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** The Seers of Ajibarang and Banyumas, Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (*Raden Generaal*), Baroness van der Capellen (*the Princess*), Adipati of Brebes, Sunan Kuning (Raden Mas Garendi), Lurung Tengah (*Ratu Adil*), Raden Tumenggung Udanagara, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih of Surakarta), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Ajibarang, Banyumas, Brebes, Southern Ocean (*Segara Kidul*), Surakarta, Yogyakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Jawi / Pegon prophetic letter, Month Repit (Dulkangidah / December), Cosmic inversion (*dunia terbalik*), *Ratu Adil* (Prince of the Righteous), *Sunan Kuning*, Pusaka cannon *Nyai Setomi*, Esoteric brotherhood, Vice-regal grand tour.

### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ajibarang & Banyumas Millenarian Letter**<br>*(Dec. 1818)*<br><br>*(Cosmic Revolutions & Van der Capellen)*<br><br>*(Inversion of the World)*<br><br>*(Lurung Tengah as Ratu Adil)* | **We, a true-born Ajibarang man and a true-born Banyumas man begin to write this letter on Sunday in the month Repit [December 1818].**<br>The latter, the Banyumas man, is the one who knows the revolution of heaven and earth, of the moon and the sun. He is also aware of the course of life of my lord the **Raden General [Governor-General Van der Capellen]**, who is very powerful, and who brought his brother and his consort and the Princess [the Baroness], and who is accompanied by eleven Mantris.<br>The aforesaid Raden General had departed one evening in the month of Repit on a journey, and already for two apits or years was staying in a Palace, but at present, just before the setting in of the good season, he is here.<br><br>The Ajibarang man also knows that my two rulers (?) were just now busy taking counsel in the Kraton in order to appoint four prominent persons, and **it shall happen this very day that the world will be reversed:**<br>• my lord who is in the east will go to the west,<br>• the elder brother of my lord will go to the south,<br>• and the son of the Adipati of Brebes will go to the north - this is a descendant of **Sunan Kuning [Raden Mas Garendi]** -, and his younger brother named **Lurung Tengah will be made king of the Righteous (or, perhaps, Ratu Adil?).**<br><br>All my friends the Europeans will accompany my lord the Raden General, and he who wishes to make the world anew will take father the General as a witness to his action. All those living in this world are my brothers, if they are men; all those who are in Heaven are my consorts, if they are women. |
| *[Stillness of the Southern Ocean]* | This is the instruction of my brother from Ajibarang who knew how to still the southern ocean. I felt very much worn out, having served the Raden Tumenggung for two apits or years and until now, just before the start of the good season. |
| *[Hierarchy of Presentation]* | **This letter must first be handed:**<br>1. to Raden Tumenggung Udanagara;<br>2. secondly to my lord Pangeran Prangwadana [Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II];<br>3. thirdly to my lord the Patih [Sasradiningrat II];<br>4. fourthly to my sovereigns the two Princes [Sunan PB IV & Sultan HB IV];<br>5. fifthly to father the General who has powerfully ruled and was possessed of all lands, and likewise to his daughter the Princess, my sovereign. |
| *[Pusaka Cannon Nyai Setomi]* | **It is also established that the cannon Nyai Setomi is the property of my lord and father the General, who through his great genius knows to fathom the intentions of every mortal.**<br>Finally, I recommend the content of this letter to the favourable notice of my superior, the mighty Raden General. |
