# Transcription of IMG_1474.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1474.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A, *March–April 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-03-28 to 1819-04-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical intelligence, agrarian, and political reports: colonial postal surveillance of Javanese Islamic clerics (*santri* and *priests*) residing in the Straits Settlements (Malacca and Penang) corresponding with family networks in Yogyakarta (Kyai Nuryadin at Pakuncen); ruthless agrarian extortion in the Ledhok district where Patih Danureja IV leased the sub-district of Kali Bebek to a Pekalongan Chinese merchant, who used Demang Netrawijaya and bandit gangs to force peasants to sell tobacco at half price; Batavia Residency inquiry into the historical 25-year exile to Ceylon of Raden Ayu Napan Datia (close relative of Sultan HB I) following the death of her husband; and European Opzichter C.F. Engel's first agricultural return from Pacitan, reporting that zero pepper or coffee could be planted due to a complete lack of seeds, prompting Tumenggung Jagakarya to request saplings from Pangéran Prangwedana's Surakarta plantations.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Haji Abdul Wahid (Malacca), Mas Nayawilaya, Haji Mundir, Kyai Nuryadin (Pakuncen), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Demang Netrawijaya, P.H. van Lawick van Pabst (Resident of Batavia), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), Raden Ayu Napan Datia, Raden Nurtamajaya, Raden Mertawinesa, Sultan Hamengkubuwana I, C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Malacca, Penang (Pulo Pinang), Kedah, Pakuncen (Yogyakarta), District Ledhok (Kali Bebek), Pekalongan, Batavia, Ceylon (Sri Lanka), Pacitan, Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Straits Islamic postal intelligence, *Patih's appanage leasing*, Chinese commercial lease, tobacco price coercion, rural bandit protection, Ceylon penal exile, forced cash crop startup (*landhuur*).
