Johnson – Fendall
22 Jul. 1816

PB IV dispatches R. Adip. Sasradiningrat + Tumg. Wiraguna / Padmadipura / Sumadiningrat.
Expect to reach Bat. in 20 days time.

PB IV expresses pleasure at receipt of letter. Convinced that interests would be Commissioners nominate Native
van IJ to go to court as special comm.
Not necessary for Ambassadors of PB IV to go to Bat.

Instructions sent to Semarang instructing Envoys to remain there until van IJ arrives.

new Dutch Res. at Solo to continue corps of Prangwedana as it was under a British.

Assey – Johnson Res.
27 July
To be shortly replaced by Dutch Res.

Any alarm in minds of PB IV + Court to be avoided. "Comm. give assurance that it is not intended to make any alteration in existing treaties of 1812."
Stipends / authority of PB IV under treaty of 1812 to be continued.

Friendly relations GB / Netherlands to be restored.
