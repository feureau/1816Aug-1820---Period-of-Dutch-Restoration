# Transcription of IMG_1260.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1260.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 5: *Djokja Corr. [Secret] 1816 [Sept.]*; duplicate copy in National Archives The Hague, Private Collection of J. C. Baud No. 306)
- **Date Range:** 1810-11 to 1816-09-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Nahuys van Burgst's retrospective historical analysis of the political instability of the Yogyakarta Sultanate under Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh); the 1810 insurrection of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III encouraged by HB II to attack Surakarta territory; the assassination of Patih Danureja II in the kraton (1811); structural weaknesses of the early British Interregnum; Thomas Stamford Raffles's tumultuous December 1811 visit to Yogyakarta and physical altercation over court throne etiquette (*dampar*); HB II's violent purge of Crown Prince supporters (HB III faction) in March 1812; installation schemes favoring Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I; discovery of treasonous secret correspondence between Yogyakarta and Surakarta prompting the British military assault on the Kraton in June 1812.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Raja Putra Naréndra), Thomas Stamford Raffles, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Patih Danureja II (Danureja Séda Kedhaton), Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Jean Chrétien Baud.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Klathèn, Leiden, The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** British Interregnum, Throne etiquette (*dampar*), Kraton Yogyakarta, Storming of the Kraton (June 1812), Secret court correspondence, Baud Private Collection (No. 306).
