# Transcription of IMG_1547.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1547.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV Leiden, H 698b; *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djokjokarta 1820* [AvJ 1820], cont.)
- **Date Range:** 1820-09-02 to 1820-12-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Climatological and technical comparison concluding Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst's survey of salt production: solar evaporation along the Indian Ocean south coast required months of continuous sunlight to equal what northern Java Sea pans (*Pasisir*) produced in a matter of weeks, exacerbated by high coastal humidity and rainfall; review of colonial forestry policies dating from Marshal Daendels' regime (1808–1811), advocating that compulsory timber-felling labor (*blandhong*) levied from the Sultan's and Sunan's royal domains be converted into wage labor compensated with adequate daily wages to curb peasant desertion and unrest; state ceremonies celebrating the Islamic holy festival of Garebeg Mulud (Maulid al-Nabi / Birthday of the Prophet Muhammad) on 16 December 1820, marked by a 17-gun artillery salute from the colonial fort at 10:00 AM and repeated in the evening when Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV paid his official ceremonial state visit to the Dutch Residency; Peter Carey's working editorial marginalia flagging the nascent expansion of the European sugar industry and forestry solutions in the Vorstenlanden.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (Sultan Séda Besiyar), Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sunan Pakubuwana IV / V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), South Coast (Indian Ocean), Pasisir (North Coast), Teak Forest Reserves (Blandhong districts).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Algemeen Verslag van Djokjokarta* (AvJ 1820), South Sea salt pans, solar evaporation, Daendels' Forestry Administration (*Houtbosschen*), *blandhong* (corvée forestry labor), daily wage rates (*daglonen*), Garebeg Mulud, 17-gun royal artillery salute, state residency visits, commercial sugar cane estates (*suikerindustrie*).
