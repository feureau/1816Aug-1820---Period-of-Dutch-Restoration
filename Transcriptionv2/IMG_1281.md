- **Date Range:** 1816-01-22 to 1816-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Comprehensive annual financial balance sheet and exchequer audit for the Residency of Yogyakarta for 1816; gross colonial revenues totaling 416,456 Java Rupees derived from six crown revenue farms (Opium monopoly, markets and tollgates, Pacitan district rent, Rawa bird's nests, post roads, and Karangbolong bird's nest cliffs); total exchequer expenditures amounting to 296,646 J.R. (including civil salaries, widows' pensions, 220,000 J.R. Sultan's ceded land indemnity under the 1812 treaty, Paku Alam and tollhouse police training, Sultan's Lifeguard dragoons appointed by Major Caulfeild, Pacitan municipal administration, Karangbolong bird's nest transport, five court festival allowances, residency surgeon, and hospital maintenance); yielding an annual net colonial operating surplus (*clear profit*) of 119,810 Spanish Dollars.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Major James Caulfeild, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Johan Diederik Kruseman.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Pacitan, Karangbolong (Kalleng Bolong), Rawa (Rowo), Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Residency Exchequer Balance Sheet, Opium Monopoly Farm, Pasar and Tollgate Farms, Deceded Lands Indemnity (Treaty of 1812), Royal Lifeguard Dragoons, Java Rupees (J.R.), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
