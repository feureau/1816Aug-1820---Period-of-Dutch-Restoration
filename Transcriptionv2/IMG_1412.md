# Transcription of IMG_1412.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1412.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60: July–December 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1817-06-23 to 1817-08-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official correspondence and logistical directives concerning garrison rations, rural security, fugitive priyayi, currency fraud, and the grand state tour of the Commissioners-General (*Commissarissen-Generaal*). On 4 July 1817, Dietrée submitted adjusted military food and provisioning price schedules to Resident Nahuys van Burgst, rectifying overcharges on rice (f. 3.19½ instead of f. 4.05 per pikul), coffee (f. 12.00 instead of f. 20.00), pepper (f. 16.20 instead of f. 20.25), and lamp oil (f. 0.06 instead of f. 0.08 per can). On 23 June, the Provisional Resident of Rembang reported the apprehension of an itinerant Muslim priest, Neda Muhammad, traveling without a colonial passport (*pass*) alongside a young man and two women, invoking Governor-General Daendels' decree of 16 September 1810 regarding religious agitators from Probolinggo. On 3 August, Resident of Kedu A.M.T. Baron de Salis notified Nahuys that Raden Prawiradimeja, former Demang of Probolinggo under sentence of death, had escaped military custody in Magelang and was fleeing toward Yogyakarta, where he possessed dangerous aristocratic connections inside Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV's guard (Raden Baurasra and Raden Rana-atmadja). On 13 August, the Council of Finances issued Resolution No. 32 warning of counterfeit banknotes circulating in the *Vorstenlanden*. Finally, on 14 August, Resident of Semarang Doorn communicated to Baron von Lippe (Provisional Resident of Surakarta) the itinerary and ceremonial entourage of Governor-General Van der Capellen, Commissioner-General Elout, Major-General De Kock, Professor Reinwardt, Secretary Baud, and military staff, arriving at Kalasan on 24 August 1817, requiring 200–400 porters (*batur*) and 50 horses.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Dietrée, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Major-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Professor Caspar Georg Carl Reinwardt, Jean Chrétien Baud, Adriaan Mauritz Theodorus Baron de Salis, Anthony Hendrik Doorn, Hendrik Ferdinand Baron von Lippe, Lieutenant-Colonel Le Sueur, Raden Prawiradimeja, Neda Muhammad, Raden Baurasra, Raden Rana-atmadja, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djogja], Surakarta [Solo], Rembang, Probolinggo [Prabalingga], Magelang, Kedu [Kědhu], Kalasan, Semarang, Tornala, Besuki [Besoeki], Panarukan [Panaroekan].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Commissarissen-Generaal, *valse bankbiljetten* [counterfeit banknotes], *passen* [internal travel passports], *prajurit* [royal soldiers], *batur* [coolies / baggage porters], *demang* [district head], garrison rations, *Vorstenlanden* [VK].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60 (Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven van de Residentie Jogjakarta, July–December 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Executive administrative register, intelligence dispatches, police warrants, and state tour ceremonial programs.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Bridges local fiscal administration in the Yogyakarta garrison with high-level imperial surveillance across Java, highlighting fears of rural religious mobility and the state visit of the Commissarissen-Generaal.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, Kedu Resident De Salis, Semarang Resident Doorn, Provisional Solo Resident Von Lippe, Garrison Commandant Lt.-Col. Le Sueur, and the supreme colonial executive (*Commissarissen-Generaal*).
* **Archival Header:** Top entries register archive reference and date range (`Yogyakarta 1817 / Jul. - Dec. 1817 / [red ink: Dj. Br. 60. / July - Dec. 1817]`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Red ink margin notes identifying archival documents and cross-references (`[red ink: Pangeran Arya]`, `cf Daendels Besl. 16 Herfstmaand 1810`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, correspondent headers, and official decree numbers in the left column; substantive details, food price comparisons, police reports, and ceremonial passenger lists in the right column.
