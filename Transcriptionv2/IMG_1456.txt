Sultan of Madura does not want to see Govant. school in area.
Too attached to old adat.

Sumënëp / Pamekasan
12 Oct. 1831

34 pesantrèns. Malay / Jav. language taught v. badly.

Bawean,
26 Oct. 1831

109 local schools (langgar) 2x per day tenors.
Taught in Religious texts (Mëngaji) Reading/writing of Arabic characters.
No instruction in Jav./Malay - Teachers themselves know v. little.
Only Qur'ān used as Book of Instruction.
After 3-4 years pupils finish course (Tamat).
Some pupils given 30-50 Reals by parents sent to continue education in Pasuruan.
Some come back having studied Islamic law/ + Reading/writing.
Sometimes money not enough for a long stay.

∴ Difficulty of procuring enough clerks in Residency who can Read / write.
Teachers given gift when pupils (Tamat) usually piece of cloth or chintz worth f. 2-4.
Parents + enough money send children to renowned pesantrèns in Java.

7-8 good govrmtl. schools should be established in Bawean area:
instead of numerous small langgar.
- 2 at capital Sangkapura.
- 1 Desa Nuwari
- 1 Desa Sukameng.
- 1 Desa Promahan.
- 1 Desa Kepoh
- 1 Desa Panwu
- 1 Desa Lebak.
Bamboo buildings to be built in these places to house 1-200 pupils.

Payment for schools by Raising Tax of 8 cents per Loyang on goods taken exported from Madura by boat.

Govrmt. should not Interfere in Religious Instruction in Native schools.
But Teaching of Reading/writing Malay - Jav. should be encouraged.

Education commission to be set up in Surabaya:
Res/Sec. of Res. / Regents

(Left margin: School from 8-11, 2-4 in order to avoid times of prayer)
(I) Surabaya / Grësik - each should have 2 schools + 1 in each Regency.
(II) Each school - 1 Teacher f. 15 per month in capital, f. 10 per month in village.
(III) Every Teacher Rank of Démang + allowed to have Payong of Prapati.
(IV) Teachers after 5 years could become village Heads again. See Reg. 24 Feb. 1824.
(V) After a time no Jav. to be appointed to official position unless able to Read/write Malay - Jav.
(Right margin: & children of Regents + Jav. Heads to be educated free of charge)
(VI) govrmt. to supply Books free of charge.
(VII) Schools to be set up at a cost of f. 150.
