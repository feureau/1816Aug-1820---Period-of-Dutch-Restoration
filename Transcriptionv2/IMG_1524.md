# Transcription of IMG_1524.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1524.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63A / *PJ. Br. 63A*, July–Sept. 1820) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Residency Outgoing Letterbook))
- **Date Range:** 1820-07-14 to 1821-07-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse dynastic, infrastructural, administrative, and agrarian developments: official notification (*BGG 21 Jul 1820 No. 13*) of the death of Queen Grandmother Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton) on 14 July 1820; colonial civil engineering budgets in Pacitan totaling over f. 27,900 (featuring a monumental f. 20,000 stone warehouse with ironwood shingle roof and f. 7,000 coffee drying sheds); inter-residency police pursuit of fugitive officials and literate scribes from Kedu (Nakidin, literate panakawan of Bupati Danuningrat who fled to become a royal *magang* in Yogyakarta; and Banuêntana, fugitive Patih of Ballah sheltering under Tumenggung Jayaningrat in Remame); Opzichter Engel's Pacitan return recording 164,826 estate coffee trees and landless pepper laborers; and wholesale delivery to the state of 315 corges of Yogyakarta textiles via the Capitan Cina.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton, grandmother of HB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya (Tumenggung of Pacitan), F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Bupati of Magelang), Nakidin, Banuêntana, Radèn Tumenggung Jayaningrat, Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Capitan Cina of Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Pacitan, Magelang (Coleppan, Ballah), Remame (Kali Nèngking / Probolinggo boundary), Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Court mourning, *Sirap* shingle-roof stone warehouse, Coffee drying sheds (*droogschuren*), Literate royal scribes (*magang*), Extradition of fugitive officials (*scheidedelicten*), Sawah landlessness, Living fence coffee (*pagêr coffee*), *Corge* (score of 20 pieces), *Narongs*, *Cêtis*, *Ginggang*.
