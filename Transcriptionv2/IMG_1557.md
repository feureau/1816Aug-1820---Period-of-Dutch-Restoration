# Transcription of IMG_1557.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1557.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Portefeuille 6 No. 7, Nahuys van Burgst Archive; Surakarta Dispatches & Literary Gazette Working Notes, Nov. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1818-04-15 to 1820-11-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historical evaluation of Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) extracted from Resident Rijck van Prehn’s dispatches to Governor-General Van der Capellen (April 1818), emphasizing the Mangkunagaran ruler’s exceptional military loyalty to European regimes: (1) suppressing the Cirebon peasant rebellion for Marshal Daendels (1808), (2) deploying the Mangkunagaran Legion during the British storming of the Yogyakarta Kraton (June 1812), and (3) escorting Resident Nahuys van Burgst to Surabaya (1817); working analytical notes on the deathbed of Sunan Pakubuwana IV (died 1 Oct. 1820 in his 54th year and 33rd year of reign, leaving 56 children and 146 grandchildren); analysis of Resident Nahuys' political presence at the royal bedside to prevent kraton succession riots; spatial layout of the palace hall (*Tierambie* / *dalem*) and state bed (*Kobong-ageng* / *krobongan*); ritual closing of the eyes and mouth of the dying monarch by his brother Pangéran Buminata to conform with ancestral Mataram mortuary taboos; filial transmission of the sacred unwashed royal headcloth (*udheng*) to the Crown Prince (Sunan Pakubuwana V); collection and interment of lifelong somatic relics (hair clippings, nail parings, shed teeth); two-hour Islamic corpse-washing ceremony (*memandikan jenazah*) using tamarind and burnt rice straw water; mandatory exclusion of women following purification; cleansing of the royal genitals by the Crown Prince, followed by the division and distribution of the sanctified washing linen to courtiers as sacred talismans (*jimat* / *berkah*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Rijck van Prehn, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Sunan Pakubuwana V (Crown Prince), Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Cirebon (Cheribon), Yogyakarta, Surabaya, Leiden (BPL archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Mangkunagaran Legion, British storming of Yogya (1812), royal deathbed vigil, *Kobong-ageng* (imperial state-bed), *Tierambie* (surambi / dalem porch), Mataram death taboos, sacred headcloth (*udheng*), somatic relics (hair, nails, teeth), *memandikan jenazah* (Islamic corpse-washing), tamarind and rice-straw water, *ngalap berkah* (distribution of relic linen).
