# Transcription of IMG_1338.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1338.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-09 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Physical assassination attempt and violent raid launched against Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing) by retainers belonging to the household of Pangeran Mangkudiningrat I; Tan Jin Sing is forced to maintain two armed European colonial soldiers sleeping permanently in his private residence to deter further nocturnal attacks; pervasive aristocratic dread and ongoing rumors of lethal food poisoning; Secadiningrat's perceived arrogance, haughtiness, and conspicuous consumption alienating the Javanese court; Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk questions why Tan Jin Sing does not abandon Yogyakarta and relocate his family to Semarang or another coastal commercial center; revelation that Tan Jin Sing is deeply ostracized, envied, and despised by the orthodox Chinese merchant communities in both Semarang and Surakarta due to his unprecedented political ascendancy and adoption of Javanese aristocratic habits; high estimation of Secadiningrat's intelligence utility by both former British Resident John Crawfurd and incoming Dutch Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst; Secadiningrat praised as an exceptionally astute, observant, and skilled informant who commands comprehensive intelligence networks spanning Yogyakarta, Surakarta, and the rural hinterland; Van IJsseldijk concludes that while previously attached to the British regime, Secadiningrat's survival requires equal fidelity to the newly restored Dutch colonial government; recommendation against banishment: Van IJsseldijk proposes that Secadiningrat be preserved at Yogyakarta as a colonial surveillance operative, but that court tensions be defused by requiring him to surrender his Javanese title of *Radèn Tumenggung* and military rank of Major, reverting strictly to his former municipal status as Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing, as held under Marshal Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing), Pangeran Mangkudiningrat I, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, John Crawfurd, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kapitan Cina, *tumenggung*, Major, European guard / bodyguard, assassination attempt, poison fear, Chinese community, intelligence asset, political surveillance.
