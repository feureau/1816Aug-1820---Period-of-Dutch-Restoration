# Transcription of IMG_1255.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1255.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Jogja Archive for 1816 — Letters from Nahuys to Van der Capellen*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-29 to 1816-09-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official report from Resident Nahuys van Burgst analyzing pervasive rural banditry and security breakdown in the Yogyakarta environs; details of the poison plot against a key Chinese witness by Wireng Demang (former royal concubine of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III); sharp critique of British administrative negligence under John Crawfurd regarding prison security and unchecked crime; devastating armed raid by over 50 bandits from the Bantul district (2 September 1816) torching four homesteads, plundering 4,000 Spanish Dollars, murdering Chinese merchants, and sacking the local tollgate; identification of a rootless, floating Javanese criminal subculture targeting Chinese bazaars and toll stations; Nahuys's tripartite security recommendations: permanent military detachments (3–6 troops) stationed at every tollgate post, mass banishment of masterless vagrants to Bangka, Borneo, or Maluku, and centralized judicial crime auditing.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, John Crawfurd, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Wireng Demang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Bantul, Bangka, Borneo, Maluku (Moluccas), Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Banditry (*kècu* / *wong durjana*), Tollgates (*bandar*), Pasar Tax Farms, Chinese merchants security, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Banishment decrees.
