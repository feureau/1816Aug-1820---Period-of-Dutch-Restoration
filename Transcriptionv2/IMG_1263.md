# Transcription of IMG_1263.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1263.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, Vol. 4203 [J 181]: *Ingekomen Stukken*, October–November 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-10 to 1816-11-01
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Cross-residency administrative and fiscal correspondence across Central Java; land rent dispute between Surakarta Resident Pinket van Haak and Yogyakarta Resident Nahuys over defaulting Chinese lessee Ghut at Desa Kamarakan; requisition for Bengal opium chests from Batavia for Surakarta; urgent demand from Kedu Resident F. G. Valck for historic Yogyakarta archival records regarding pre-1812 royal tax registers, bazaar dues, and tollgate returns; John Crawfurd's memorandum in Javanese on the mode of revenue payments from Kedu; natural science circular from Professor C. G. C. Reinwardt regarding agricultural surveys; formal resolution of the Governor-General in Council (1 November 1816) authorizing the establishment and public funding of a dedicated Lock Hospital (*Venereal Disease Hospital*) on the Code River for 30 female patients.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Major Jeremiah Johnson, Franciscus Gerhardus Valck, John Crawfurd, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Professor C. G. C. Reinwardt, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Batavia, Magelang (Kedu), Code River (Kali Code).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Vol. 4203 (J 181), Lock Hospital / VD Clinic, Code River bamboo compound, Opium farm shipments, Kedu tax archives, Raad van Indië resolutions.
