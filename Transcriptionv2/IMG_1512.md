# Transcription of IMG_1512.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1512.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Primary Printed Colonial Statute (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63 / *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, 1820, Page 4) (Archetype 1: Primary Printed Statute / Organic Public Health Regulation (Articles 15 cont.–18))
- **Date Range:** Circa 1820
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official promulgated text of the smallpox vaccination organic statute (*Reglement op de koepok-inenting*, Articles 15 to 18): disciplinary dismissal and temporary doubling of salaries for neighboring replacement vaccinators; special protection for vaccinators holding the rank of *Mantri* (requiring Governor-General ratification for dismissal); compulsory immunization of all institutionalized children in state and private orphanages (*weeshuizen*) and almshouses (*armhuizen*); strict sanitary cordons prohibiting the overland or waterborne transit of infected smallpox patients between Residencies; and maritime quarantine inspections barring infected ship passengers and crew from landing in colonial ports.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Regional Residents, Port Commanders, Orphanage Directors, *Mantri* Vaccinators.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Surabaya, Semarang, Regional Harbors and Ports.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Mantri* Vaccinators (*akten van aanstelling*), Orphanage immunization mandates (*weeshuizen*), Almshouse health inspections, Territorial *cordon sanitaire*, Maritime port quarantine (*quarantaine*).
