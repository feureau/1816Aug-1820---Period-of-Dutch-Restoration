## Main Text

### SECRET LETTERS
from 1705 to 1817
### Yogya Nov 1816

Question of K. Moermo:
Agrements GG & handling of Asst. Res. Kruseman of Kijaji Moermo affair.

Extreme speed to progress in investigation:
- relations of K. Moermo to Court ignore
- escape investigation giving presenting clè...

waits immediate report on implication of M.B. in Sepoy affair.

M.B. link & Sepoy plot reason:
for exile from Solo:
Recognised as proud and unruly man especially hated British Government.
espec influence on Emperor P.B. IV.

Removal of P.B. IV on second son Pang. Purboyo: m. to daughter of M.B.
cf annoyance at criticism of conduct by Crown Prince.
Crown Prince prepared to call on desa inhabitants to prosecute his rights against Purboyo.

Extreme unrest over losing Kedu in Solo:
Idea of Brit Govt to restore Solo areas of Kedu:
to buy back peace at court by creating Purboyo as C.P.

But disappointment leads to:
Sepoy plot originates & implicates M.B.
British support of Prangwedana.
Sepoys.
But jealousy of Prangwedana for Sunan.

essentially family quarrel Sunan - M.B.
enables Sunan to expel M.B. from court.
