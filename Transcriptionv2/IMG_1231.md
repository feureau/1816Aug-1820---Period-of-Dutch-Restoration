# Transcription of IMG_1231.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1231.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection / Leiden Univ BPL 616: *Military Dossiers on the Mangkunagaran Legion*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-16 to 1816-09-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Strategic memorandum outlining Dutch colonial military policy toward the independent house of the Mangkunagaran during the retrocession; emphasizes the extreme political and military necessity of attaching Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) firmly to the interests of the restored Kingdom of the Netherlands; urges the appointment of a qualified European officer to be seconded directly to the Mangkunagaran Legion; notes with concern that the Legion had fallen into a severely degraded material condition (*"in very bad state"*) due to financial impoverishment and unpaid equipment allowances at the end of the British administration.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Vorstenlanden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Mangkunagaran Legion (P.W. Legion), Dutch auxiliary military policy, European liaison officer (*gedetacheerd officier*).
