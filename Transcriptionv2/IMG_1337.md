# Transcription of IMG_1337.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1337.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-09 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Ratu Kencana (consort of HB III and mother of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV) presents a diplomatic gift of fruit to Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk, requesting him to convey her warm personal greetings to the exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II upon his return to Batavia; comprehensive analysis of the explosive political controversy surrounding Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing); Secadiningrat's elevation to the Javanese nobility (*tumenggung*) and endowment with 800 *cacahs* of appanage land by Sultan Hamengkubuwana III in gratitude for saving HB III's life from an assassination plot during the British crisis of 1811–1812; aristocratic outrage across Yogyakarta at having a Chinese merchant elevated into the high ranks of the *priyayi*; Pangeran Paku Alam I formally petitions Van IJsseldijk demanding the total banishment of Secadiningrat from the court; formal written grievances submitted against Tan Jin Sing by senior court grandees Kyai Adipati Purwa and Raden Riksanagara; Paku Alam's bitter personal enmity rooted in Tan Jin Sing's intervention in 1812, which secured the throne for HB III rather than allowing Paku Alam to seize power; court-wide condemnation of Secadiningrat as a collaborator with the British occupying regime; solitary affection shown to Secadiningrat by the young HB IV in memory of his late father; Secadiningrat's encyclopedic knowledge of court secrets and palace intrigues.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Ratu Kencana (later Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II / Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (HB III / Raja Putra Narendra), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing), Pangeran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I (P.A.), Kyai Adipati Purwa, Raden Riksanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kapitan Cina, *tumenggung*, appanages / land grants (*lungguh*), British invasion of Yogyakarta (June 1812), court factionalism, diplomatic gifts.
