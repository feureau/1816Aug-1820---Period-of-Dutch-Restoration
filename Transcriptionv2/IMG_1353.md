# Transcription of IMG_1353.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1353.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306, section 9 / page 13 continued: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys*, 22 October 1816; and *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Succession planning and ministerial grooming for the Yogyakarta vizierate: continuing assessment of Raden Tumenggung Mandura, with Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk noting that in his current undeveloped state nothing substantial can be expected from him; however, he observes that following prolonged administrative tutelage and close connection with European residency officials, one of the three Danurejan brothers could eventually be selected and installed as the next Grand Vizier (*Radèn Adipati* / Patih); review of the three surviving sons of Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma I (the venerable bupati of Grobogan who was strangled to death on the orders of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II at the royal cemetery of Banyusumurup in January 1812), who were also younger brothers to the executed Patih Danureja II:
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Raden Tumenggung Mandura, Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma I, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II / Sultan Sepuh), Raden Adipati Danureja II (DR II), Raden Mertadiwiria, Raden Kusumareja, Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma II (Mertawijaya), Raden Ayu Danukusumo (R.Ay. DK I), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Banyusumurup, Grobogan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Grand Vizier grooming (*Radèn Adipati*), Danurejan dynastic clan, Banyusumurup execution (January 1812), noble impoverishment, royal widow's petition, residency relief.
