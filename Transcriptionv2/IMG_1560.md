# Transcription of IMG_1560.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1560.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; Microfilm No. 4, *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal Buiten Raad*, Oct. 1820 – Jan. 1821)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-04 to 1821-01-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Vice-regal administrative resolutions enacted outside council (*Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal Buiten Raad*); decision (4 Oct. 1820 No. 12) addressing the bilateral agreement between Yogyakarta and Surakarta regarding the maintenance of the inter-capital post road (*postweg*): reviewing corvée labor requisitions from the border villages of Teranna, Bulu, Rawa, and Dlimas intended to provide forced road maintenance, bridge repairs, and grass-cutting for colonial post horses, Batavia rules that it is impossible to forcibly compel sufficient peasant laborers from these districts, acknowledging severe labor exhaustion and rural resistance; police security decree (12 Dec. 1820 No. 23) in the Buitenzorg Residency ordering the immediate roundup and deportation to Batavia of unemployed Bengali residents (former British Indian Sepoys and camp followers left behind after the 1816 retrocession) who lacked visible livelihoods and alarmed local police; fiscal gaming concession (26 Jan. 1821) granted by Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (acting for both Yogyakarta and Surakarta) in response to a petition from the Kapitan Cina, authorizing traditional gaming (*Tapho* / dice gambling) during the festive month of Chinese New Year (*Tahun Baru Imlek*) under a reduced concession rate of 2 guilders per day alongside a 150-guilder domestic license per household.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Resident of Buitenzorg, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Kapitan Cina (Surakarta / Yogyakarta), former Bengal Sepoys / camp followers.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogja), Surakarta (Solo), Teranna, Bulu, Rawa, Dlimas, Buitenzorg (Bogor), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal Buiten Raad*, post road corvée (*heerendiensten*), postal horse grass-cutting (*gras-snijders*), Bengali diaspora / demobilized Sepoys, vagrancy policing, Kapitan Cina, Chinese New Year gaming concessions (*Tapho* gambling licenses).
