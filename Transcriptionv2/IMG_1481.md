# Transcription of IMG_1481.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1481.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A & Solo Archival Reports, July–Nov. 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-07-20 to 1819-11-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Macro-financial, criminal, and counter-insurgency dispatches: comprehensive audited balance sheet of the Yogyakarta Residency for fiscal year 1819 (total income of f. 565,526.18 dominated by tollgates and opium; total expenditures of f. 314,882 dominated by subsidies to Sultan HB IV and civil salaries); judicial report of Sunan Pakubuwana IV and his Nayakas identifying the ringleaders of the armed gang (Banteksaran, Soarana, Sowantia) who robbed the country villa of widow Baumgarten; and alarming intelligence from the Resident of Pasuruan detailing the mass arrest of 81 religious conspirators in Malang centered at Desa Gondanglegi under Mas Arja Wijaya, an itinerant religious teacher originating from Karanganom and Blawon in Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** N.A. Holmberg de Beckfeldt, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Banteksaran, Soarana, Sowantia, Dr. Friedrich Baumgarten (widow of), Resident of Pasuruan, Mas Arja Wijaya, Embo Pijno.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Pasuruan, Malang (Desa Gondanglegi, Batu), Karanganom, Desa Blawon.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Residency Annual Balance Sheet, Tollgate leases (*bandar*), Opium farm (*candhu*), Royal court annuities (*Hofhouding*), Smallpox vaccination budget, Armed highway robbery, Millenarian insurrection, *Santri* network surveillance.
