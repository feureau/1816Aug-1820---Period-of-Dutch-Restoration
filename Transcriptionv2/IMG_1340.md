# Transcription of IMG_1340.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1340.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-24 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Relief of the Surakarta Crown Prince (Pangeran Sugandi / later Sunan Pakubuwana V) following acute panic over persistent rumors of a British military return; the Crown Prince receives firm Dutch colonial commitments guaranteeing his legitimate accession to the Surakarta throne; high-level political conference convened at the residency mansion of Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak in Surakarta, attended by Patih Sasradiningrat II, Pangeran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), and senior regents; Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk returns to Yogyakarta on 26 September 1816; sudden severe illness of Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, who is evacuated to Batavia for emergency medical treatment; the Commission-General in Batavia temporarily contemplates appointing Van IJsseldijk to serve as acting Resident of Yogyakarta, alongside Johan Diederik Kruseman; official dispatch of 11 October 1816 recalling Van IJsseldijk to Batavia; Van IJsseldijk's final audience in Yogyakarta: delivers a solemn lecture to Patih Danureja IV in the presence of Pangeran Paku Alam I, sternly reminding both leaders of their sacred treaty obligations and subservience to the Dutch Crown; Van IJsseldijk departs Yogyakarta on 23 October (concluding his mission on 26 October 1816); summation of the diplomatic embassy: Van IJsseldijk expresses confidence in having disabused both courts of British restoration myths and established a realistic perception of Dutch sovereign strength; deliberate decision to suppress Secret Article 7 (*art. 7*) of his instructions, judging that neither ruler had engaged in open defection justifying coercive threats.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Crown Prince of Surakarta (Pangeran Sugandi / Sunan Pakubuwana V / CP), Patih Sasradiningrat II, Pangeran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Patih Danureja IV, Pangeran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I (P.A.).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** British return rumors, dynastic succession guarantee, residency conference, Patih, Prangwedana, Mangkunagaran Legion, Secret Article 7 (*art. 7*), recall order, diplomatic mission conclusion.
