Yogya Res. Letters. Dj. Br. 60
Jan. - Jun. 1817 / Jan. - June 1817

Lippe (Temp. Res. Solo) to Nahuys, 9 Jun. 1817:
[Margin: SEPOYS. *]
Solo oppasser Krama.
Bringing 3 Sepoys to Yogya
although have a pass from Res. of Batavia to travel to Yogya - suspected of being in league w. P. Mangkubumi (Solo)
Serve in Light Infantry Battalion.
Desert from Battalion. [Margin: Sepoys.]
Pretend to be engaged in trade in cloth:
But decide to exile them from Solo.
