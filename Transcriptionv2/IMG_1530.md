# Transcription of IMG_1530.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1530.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief Den Haag / Ministerie van Koloniën, MvK 4497; *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, Geheim, 3 March 1820 no. 1)
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-25 to 1820-03-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Acute diplomatic crisis in Surakarta precipitated by Sunan Pakubuwana IV, who officially declared to colonial authorities that the Dutch government had granted him permission to reassert the full sovereign prerogatives he had enjoyed under the Batavian Republic prior to Daendels' 1808 administrative revolution, thereby unilaterally repudiating all post-1808 treaties concluded with the British and Dutch regimes; dispatch from Assistant Resident Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe (25 Feb. 1820 no. 2) reporting total lack of local authority in Surakarta to challenge the Sunan's repudiation; secret decision by Governor-General Van der Capellen in Council (*BGG Geheim*, 3 March 1820 no. 1) dispatching Yogyakarta Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst—the colonial official most intimately acquainted with Surakarta court dynamics—to Surakarta with plenipotentiary authority to confront PB IV and compel him to retract his declaration; appointment of Nahuys as temporary Acting Resident of Surakarta while maintaining oversight of Yogyakarta; strict vice-regal operational orders directing Nahuys not to engage the Sunan directly upon arrival, but to conduct clandestine inquiries through Javanese court subordinates to uncover the instigators behind PB IV's defiance.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Ministerie van Koloniën (MvK), *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* (BGG Geheim), Batavian Republic (1795–1806), colonial treaties (*verdragen*), sovereign regalia, diplomatic confrontation, dual residency command.
