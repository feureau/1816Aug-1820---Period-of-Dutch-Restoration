# Transcription of IMG_1469.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1469.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 51A, *Afgaande Stukken Aug.–Oct. 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-31 to 1819-10-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historical and administrative dispatches of Resident H.G. Nahuys van Burgst: post-mortem analysis of former British Resident John Crawfurd's disastrous private venture to monopolize the North Coast salt trade; glowing character testimonial for Dr. Harvey Thomson; termination of Lieutenant Paulus as provisional Javanese translator for failing to provide language instruction to young Indo-European cadets, with Dietrée proposed as replacement; mobilization of 500 Yogyakarta corvée laborers for the highway between Pekalongan and Batang; and an exhaustive military-historical report on the establishment, ceremonial duties, daily service, and elaborate uniforms of the Sultan's European Bodyguard (*Europese Lijfwacht der Sultans*), established in January 1816 by Dr. Daniel Ainslie in the aftermath of the Sepoy Conspiracy.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), John Crawfurd (former British Resident), Dr. Harvey Thomson (*Tuan Tom*), Lieutenant Paulus, Dietrée, Resident of Pekalongan, Dr. Daniel Ainslie, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Major-General of Netherlands Indies Army.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Pekalongan, Batang, Surakarta (Solo), Fort Vredeburg, Kraton Yogyakarta, Paseban Lor.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Salt monopoly speculation, *Inlandsche Talen* (vernacular education), European Bodyguard (*Lijfwacht van den Sultan*), Sepoy Plot (1815), Dragoons, Dolman, Shako, *Paseban*, Garèbèg pistol salutes, *Watangan* (Saturday lance tournaments), *Wachtmeester* / Brigadier.
