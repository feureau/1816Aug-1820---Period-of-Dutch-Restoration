# Transcription of IMG_1396.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1396.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: AvJ Court Registers & Patih Correspondence, 1819–1820)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-10 to 1820-12-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official court obituaries of senior Yogyakarta princes and administrative correspondence of Patih Raden Adipati Danureja IV concerning regional bupatis in Kedu and Banyumas. On 10 January 1819, the registry records the demise of Pangéran Arya Kusumayuda in Yogyakarta, followed on 13 December 1820 by the death of Pangéran Dèmang. On 27 September 1819, Danureja IV addressed an official dispatch to Raden Tumenggung Danuningrat I (British-appointed Regent of Magelang / Kedu), referencing Raden Tumenggung Mandura of Yogyakarta and recalling that Danuningrat had previously served as a subordinate patih / deputy (*patih jero*) under Danureja IV's murdered predecessor, Raden Adipati Danureja II (strangled in the Kraton in 1811). On 7 June 1820, Danureja IV communicated with Surakarta court officials regarding the Regent of Banyumas, Raden Tumenggung Cakrawidana.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Adipati Danureja IV [DR IV], Raden Tumenggung Danuningrat I [R.T. Danuningrat, Magelang], Raden Adipati Danureja II [DR II], Pangéran Arya Kusumayuda [P.A. Kusumayuda], Pangéran Dèmang [P. Dèmang], Raden Tumenggung Mandura [Res. Tum. Mardura], Raden Tumenggung Cakrawidana [Regent of Banyumas].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Yogya], Magelang [Kedhu], Banyumas, Surakarta [Solo].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Patih Dalem*, royal obituaries, *Patih jero* [deputy court administrator], Regency of Banyumas, Regency of Magelang, Kraton dynastic lineages.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698b; transcripts of Yogyakarta Residency Court Registers and Danurejan Letterbooks (1819–1820).
* **Document Nature:** Official court death notices (*daftar seda*), ministerial correspondence, and biographical retrospectives on regional priyayi administrators.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the institutional continuity and inter-generational memory linking the Danurejan family to the administration of British-annexed Kedu and the western *Manca Nagara* of Banyumas.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Grand Vizier Danureja IV, regional Regents (Danuningrat I of Magelang, Cakrawidana of Banyumas), and deceased royal princes.
* **Chronological Register:** Entries arranged in concise chronological sequence spanning January 1819 to December 1820 (`10 Jan. 1819`, `13 Dec 1820`, `27 Sept. 1819`, `7 June 1820`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates and ministerial signatories on the left; verbatim death notices, genealogical recollections, and bupati appointments on the right.
