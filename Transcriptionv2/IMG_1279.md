# Transcription of IMG_1279.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1279.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djocjakarta Corr. [Nov.–Dec. 1816] — Acting Resident to Governor-General*, 24 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1812-08-01 to 1816-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official retrospective report on the reign and sudden demise of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (died 3 November 1814) and the accession of the ten-year-old Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV; British refusal to return annexed territories under the Treaty of 1812 (Pacitan land rent, opium monopoly, tollgates, and Karangbolong bird's nests); British Resident Captain Robert Clement Garnham's rejected regency proposal (a joint council of Patih Danureja IV and Ratu Ibu); Raffles's appointment of Pangéran Paku Alam I as Prince Regent with the custody of the royal seal (*rijkszegel*) on 17 December 1814; financial scandal and embezzlement charges against Paku Alam I for diverting the young Sultan's private privy purse to acquire private landed estates; Paku Alam's dismissal from financial guardianship in late 1815 in favor of Ratu Ibu (Ratu Ageng).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Captain Robert Clement Garnham, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Pacitan, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Treaty of 1 August 1812, Regency of Yogyakarta, Royal Seal of State (*Rijkszegel*), Sultan's private exchequer, Pakualaman financial embezzlement, Opium Farm, Tollgates (*bandar*).
