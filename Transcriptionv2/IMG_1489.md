# Transcription of IMG_1489.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1489.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Letters of Adriaan David Cornets de Groot Jr., Koninklijke Bibliotheek, The Hague / *BKI* 1863)
- **Date Range:** 1820-01-10 to 1820-06-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Private correspondence of language student A.D. Cornets de Groot Jr. documenting major political and intellectual shifts in Surakarta: glowing scholarly evaluation of Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (extolling him as a 45-year-old erudite, Malay-speaking statesman who presented historical *Babads* to De Groot); the administrative scandal resulting in the official suspension of Surakarta Resident Rijck van Prehn on 21 March 1820; the appointment of Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst as Temporary Resident of Surakarta (unifying both princely residencies under his authority); and Nahuys' subsequent promotion to Lieutenant-Colonel and grand court presentation before Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Adriaan David Cornets de Groot Jr., Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih of Surakarta), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Rijck van Prehn (suspended Resident), Lieutenant-Colonel Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Surakarta Patih (*Rijksbestuurder*), *Babad* literature, Suspension of Resident (*schorsing*), Unified residency governance, Promotion to Lieutenant-Colonel (*Luitenant-Kolonel*).
