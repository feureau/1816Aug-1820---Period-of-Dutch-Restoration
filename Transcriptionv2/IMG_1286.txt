Some Days after capture of Kyai Moormo
HB IV asks Kruseman to mangas.
spend a day in his gardens to pick mangoes
as was common during English period.
As I was riding I took my
watch out of my pocket.
at which the Sultan seeing it told me he would
willingly have such a watch.
continuing that the Sunan of Sala had several
I was therefore forced to offer my gold watch, chain,
and cachet to the Sultan:
Remarks that since capture of Kyai Moormo some uneasiness.
had parted from the Sultan.
To let HB IV know that the
Govnmt Regarded the Affair as only the
weakness of his mother.
& had no suspicion against HB IV

On Return to Kraton:
HB IV shows the watch to the Ratus who were Delighted
By it.
I therefore ask that for compensation for my
present I can draw 200 Sp.D. from the Residency
Treasury
