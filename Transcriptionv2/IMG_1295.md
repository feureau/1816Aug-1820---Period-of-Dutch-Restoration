# Transcription of IMG_1295.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1295.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, Vol. I, Doc. XXVIII conclusion & Doc. XXIX, pp. 104–105)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-13 to 1816-09-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion of Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's 13 September 1816 diplomatic dispatch warning the Commissioners-General that suppressing rumors of the return of ex-Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) and Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I is paramount to preventing an explosion of Kraton rivalries; formal reception of the young Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV and royal court; followed by Document XXIX: official statistical report on the true state (*waren staat*) of the Residency of Pasuruan dated 16 September 1816; assessment of Raffles's land-rent system (*landrent*) showing increased peasant prosperity, agrarian security, and rapid population expansion (62,421 souls across 12 demang districts).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton), Surakarta (Solo), Pasuruan (Passourouang Residency), Bangil, Probolinggo, Tengger Mountains, Malang, Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Land-rent system (*huurders en bruikers van gouvernements landerijen*), Demangschappen (12 districts), Sawah and *Tagal* (tegal) cultivation, Coffee and timber cultures, Salt pans, Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
