# Transcription of IMG_1314.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1314.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI, *Solo Brieven* / Diplomatic Address: Van IJsseldijk to PB IV, Leaf 2)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02 to 1812-08-01
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Second leaf of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's diplomatic address to Sunan Pakubuwana IV of Surakarta; conveys the Commissioners-General’s high friendship toward the Sunan and the royal house; formally guarantees that the Dutch Restoration Government recognizes and will faithfully execute the Treaty of 1 August 1812 concluded with the British administration; accompanied by parallel Javanese translation in *Aksara Jawa*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Thomas Stamford Raffles.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Kraton of Surakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Treaty of 1 August 1812 (*Contract van 1 Augustus 1812*), Anglo-Dutch Diplomatic Continuity, *Aksara Jawa*.
