# Transcription of IMG_1328.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1328.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report of 11 December 1816 / 22 October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-02 to 1816-08-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes examining Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s comprehensive general report (*Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*); appointment on 2 August 1816; sea voyage from Batavia aboard the naval vessel *De Zeeploeg*, arriving in Semarang on 17 August; appointment of Johan Diederik Kruseman as legation secretary; preliminary conference in Semarang with Surakarta Prime Minister Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II and court envoys (intercepting their aborted embassy to Batavia); procedural dispute over court protocol: Van IJsseldijk’s refusal to surrender the Governor-General’s credential dispatches to the Patih before consulting Resident Jacob Andries van Braam; Kruseman’s arrival on 24 August with state letters and formal swearing-in before Resident Parvé of Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Johan Diederik Kruseman (Secretary of Embassy), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih of Surakarta), Jacob Andries van Braam, Parvé (Resident of Semarang), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Semarang, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Buitengewoon Gezant (Extraordinary Envoy), Naval Corvette *De Zeeploeg*, Intercepted Surakarta Embassy, Diplomatic Protocol (*Ceremonieel*), Secretaris van het Gezantschap, Swearing-in Oath.
