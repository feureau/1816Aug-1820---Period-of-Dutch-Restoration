5 Oct. 1819 pp. 25-26:
Visit to Jogya - shorter than Solo
not as pretty - but surroundings far more pleasant.
more cultivated land seen.
Sultan is like a farmer's boy [Boerenjonge] / Solo farmer's boy
lang, dik, bobbekop
zeer lomp (17 years old)
unmannered / toute bête
exactly opposite of the Sunan (PB IV)
who is so civilised that the G.G. himself says he would grace a European Court.

Visit to South Sea
Big carriage - crosses at Mancingan
Opak (Chinese Tax Farmer organise)

visit to Parang Tritis - 20 paces from the Sea 2 grottoes in cliff.
