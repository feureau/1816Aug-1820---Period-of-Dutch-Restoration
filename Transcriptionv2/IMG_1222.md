# Transcription of IMG_1222.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1222.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Solo Brieven Bundel 14c: *Surakarta / Yogyakarta 1816*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-01-02 to 1816-06-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** British administrative and military redeployments prior to the restoration; John Crawfurd takes over the Yogyakarta Residency from Dr. Philip Ainslie; British authorities approve Government funding for 235 transport coolies (235 Java Rupees) to march Captain Henry Dwyer's 4th Light Infantry Volunteer Battalion from Yogyakarta to Surakarta while maintaining family support allowances in Bengal; dispute over uniform accounts for Pangéran Prangwedana's Mangkunagaran Legion (100 men: 3 sergeants, 6 corporals, 2 trumpeters, 85 privates) with Government refusing payment for jackets bought from the 2nd Infantry Battalion; Major Johnson reports to John Fendall that Surakarta is peaceful following the removal of mutinous Sepoys; trial and conviction before the Pradata court of Ahmad Jedik (Amad Sidik), an 85-year-old Islamic guru and physician accused of spreading heretical millenarian doctrines linked to the Mahdi Sayyid affair; Sunan Pakubuwana IV writes farewell letters to Raffles; royal court entertained at the Surakarta Residency on 4 June 1816 for the birthday celebration of King George III.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (later PB V), John Crawfurd, Dr. Philip Ainslie, Charles Assey, Captain Henry Dwyer, Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson, J. Dupuy, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), John Fendall, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Ahmad Jedik (Amad Sidik), King George III.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Bengal, Klaten.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Solo Brieven (Bv. 14c), 4th Light Infantry Volunteer Battalion (Sepoys), Mangkunagaran Legion (P.M.D'n Legion), Pradata Court, Java Rupees (`J.R.`), Mahdi Sayyid affair.
