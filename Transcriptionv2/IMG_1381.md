## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1381.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* [BGG] 25 Feb 1817 La 3 / Exh. 25 Feb 1817 La a BCCG / Nahuys Dispatches Jan–Jul 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-24 to 1817-07-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official colonial adjudication and resolution of the Kyai Murma Wijaya religious affair. Following preliminary investigations initiated by Provisional Assistant Resident Kruseman and a formal directive from the Commissarissen-Generaal on 14 February 1817, Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst concluded that although Kyai Murma was highly culpable of religious intrigue, the Yogyakarta Kraton was far less implicated than initially feared. Murma had boasted to Ngabèhi Udajaya that Ratu Ibu (Ratu Kencana) frequently quitted the palace to visit his estate; however, inquiry revealed that Ratu Ibu was merely a "dupe, but not an accomplice," having urged Murma's arrest. Colonial authorities deduced that Pangéran Paku Alam I, harboring deep political hostility toward Ratu Ibu, had submitted distorted intelligence to blacken the royal court. Nahuys noted the adverse public reaction among Javanese in Yogyakarta caused by Dutch troops abruptly impounding Murma's estate at Desa Kepundhung, and recommended that his seized possessions be auctioned in Yogyakarta with proceeds remitted to Murma's family to appease local village resentment. The Commissarissen-Generaal officially ratified the sentence of perpetual exile to the Moluccas (Ambon).

  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Yogya], Desa Kepundhung [Kapundhung], Moluccas [Moluccan Islands / Ambon], Batavia.
