# Transcription of IMG_1332.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1332.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, pp. 12–15)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-04 to 1816-09-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes reconstructing Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's private diplomatic conference with Sunan Pakubuwana IV; physical seating arrangement: Van IJsseldijk and legation secretary Johan Diederik Kruseman seated with the Sunan, while Crown Prince Radèn Mas Sugandi (later PB V) and Pangéran Buminata sit at a respectful distance upon the floor; Van IJsseldijk's formal geopolitical lecture on European history delivered to the monarch: tracing Dutch independence under William the Silent against Spain, two centuries of global VOC naval hegemony, the French Revolutionary disruption under Napoleon, the Grand Alliance victory (Britain, Austria, Prussia, Russia), the dynastic marriage between Crown Prince William and Grand Duchess Anna Pavlovna of Russia, and the sovereign majesty of the House of Orange-Nassau ("Ruler by the Grace of God"); the Sunan's attentive, polite response.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Mas Sugandi (Crown Prince / Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Johan Diederik Kruseman, King William I of the Netherlands, William the Silent, Napoleon Bonaparte, Grand Duchess Anna Pavlovna, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo Kraton), Netherlands (Holland), Spain, Russia, The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Diplomatic Lecture on European History, Grand Alliance against Napoleon, Congress of Vienna Legitimacy, House of Orange-Nassau, Court Etiquette (Nobles Seated on Floor).
