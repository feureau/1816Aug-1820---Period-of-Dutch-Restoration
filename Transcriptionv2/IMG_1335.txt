# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

14 Sept – visit to kraton.
& Kruseman, Nahuys:

Discussions Restoration Netherlands.

young, inexperienced Sultan cannot speak Malay.
∴ Patih translates for him – But v. badly, cuts tale short.

Later HB IV asks questions about Holland on journey back from kraton.

Juwah [^2]

Surat Yogya – outwardly in v. good state
v. great dissimulating power at the courts.
no fear of uprising.

PB IV despite outward show of duty = master of guile:
in his heart. aversion to all Europeans.
[attitude PB IV] should not be trusted = whole reign from 1788 -> plans for lessening of European power.

often advised by priests & others with evil intent. [clover priests ...]
Sepoy Rebellion = PB IV wishes to regain old Mataram kingdom.

sometimes forced to give up his friends cf 1790.
