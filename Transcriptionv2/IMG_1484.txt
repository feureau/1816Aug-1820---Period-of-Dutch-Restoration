P.J. Br. 62 A
Yogya Letrs - 1819 - Nov. - Dec. 1819

BGG, 30 Nov. 1819, no. 16.
C.A. van den Berg: Students heading to positions.
J.A.V. de Sturler placed as élèves in de Javaans Taal in Yogya
fl. 50 per month. de Sturler given leave to be in Batavia 5 weeks.

C. Vos (Res. Pekalongan) to Nahuys, 2 Dec. 1819: Beginning of Rainy season impossible to continue work on Batang - Pekalongan Road. Yogya workforce to be sent home to work on sawahs.

J. de Bruin (Res. Semarang) to Nahuys, 3 Dec. 1819: Writing to ask for cloth from Yogya for Export to Moluccas.

BGG, 30 Dec. 1819, no. 11
Read letters Nahuys 24 Dec. 1819 no. 30
Declaring Regency of P.A. [ended]
1) Seal of State to be handed over to H.B. IV.
2) fl. 250 to be given to P.A. as token of thanks for guardianship.
Rec. 17 Jan. 1820.
Replied to 28 Jan. 1820.

Sec. of Kedhu (? Breton van Groll) to Nahuys, 30 Dec. 1819: Demang Ningdinanai Wiradrana from Gamping extract & delivery of 800 pikuls of limestone per month to Kedhu.
Asks for help of Resident in renting packhorses to transport limestone.
// GAMPING LIMESTONE //

Pacitan
Engel to d'Abo, 30 Dec. 1819:
- Dadap trees grow well although some eaten by worms.
- MUST [Coffee] / pepper plants doing as well as can expected.
- But Dadap trees planted too far apart (12') : not enough shade.

Pepper / Coffee Estates in Pacitan
[see letter Engel - d'Abo 30 Nov. 1819]:
Danaraja / Mudal / Naitang / Nundang / Rèdi / Boebong / Blom / Naitol / Nambang / Ngawen / Padangan / Dudowey / Dambarang / Pasang / Nukatan / Naruban / Kempir / Suci / Panyurgit / Asem / Getas / Karang Anyar / Gading / Mlatang / Trenggaro / Mantren / Kebon Dalem / Kedangang / Narayan.
