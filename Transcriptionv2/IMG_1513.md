# Transcription of IMG_1513.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1513.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Primary Printed Colonial Statute (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63 / *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, 1820, Page 5) (Archetype 1: Primary Printed Statute / Organic Public Health Regulation (Article 19 & Official Colophon))
- **Date Range:** 1820-04-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding article and official vice-regal enactment of the 1820 smallpox vaccination regulation: mandating that the Inspector of Smallpox Vaccination execute an annual inspection tour across the entire island of Java to personally audit vaccination clinics and review monthly returns; compiling an annual master report for the Commissioner of the Civil Medical Service; and formal legislative certification by Resolution of the Governor-General in Council (*Resolutie van den Gouverneur Generaal in rade*) on 11 April 1820, signed and certified by Algemeene Secretaris Jean Chrétien Baud.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Algemeene Secretaris Jean Chrétien Baud, Commissioner for the Civil Medical Service, Inspector of Smallpox Vaccination.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Java-wide inspection circuit.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Reglement op de koepok-inenting*, Island-wide medical circuit, Annual master report, *Resolutie van den Gouverneur Generaal in rade*, *Algemeene Secretaris*.
