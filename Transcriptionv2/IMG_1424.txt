Secret Besl. Sec. Staat. G.G. 24 Jun. 1818 no. 2:
pleas:
2 Letters from P. Aria Mangkudiningrat / Mērtasana in letter of Gov. of Moluccas 18 Mar. 1818 no. 6.
To Be allowed to Return home.
[red ink: Request of MD / MS to Return home.]
Say that Had Done nothing v. Eur. Govrmnt. + followed HB II of own Free will =
want to Return to live in Kērtasana / Madiun.
But letter of Nahuys, 16 Jun. 1818 Litt. A. secret
Reports that MD / MS taken prisoner + armed during attack on Yogya. 20 Jun. 1812.
Both Pangerans too Dangerous to Be allowed to Return to Kērtasana / Madiun = many Family Relations there.
\therefore Refusal to accept plea.
MD Exiled By Govrmnt.
MS goes of own free will?
