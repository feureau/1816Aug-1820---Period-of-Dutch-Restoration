# Transcription of IMG_1388.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1388.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Exhibitum [Exh.] 1 May 1817: Nahuys Dispatch of 14 April 1817 from Séla, continuation / Raad van Financiën)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09 to 1817-05-01
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst's pioneering philosophy of colonial governance, social diplomacy, tollgate militarization, and European agricultural colonization in the Vorstenlanden. Nahuys outlines his successful policy of aristocratic pacification: by hosting lavish dinners at the Residency and according equal honor to both the *Kasepuhan* (partisans of the deposed Sultan HB II) and the *Kadipaten/Karaton* (partisans of the ruling Sultan HB IV), he neutralized factional bitterness without taking sides. To permanently eradicate rural dacoity (*kècu*), Nahuys proposed stationing a three-man multi-ethnic military picket (one European, one Eurasian Christian, one Javanese) at every road tollgate (*bandar*), with maintenance costs funded entirely by the Chinese tax-farmers. Furthermore, European officers and non-commissioned officers were to be stationed at major customs barriers to learn the Javanese language, agrarian economy, and local customs. Nahuys articulated his progressive, Physiocratic vision: introducing European planters and capital into the interior of Java would uplift the Javanese peasantry through education and agricultural industry, enabling them to rival Chinese merchants in trade, while military posts at inland tollgates would project colonial power and suppress rural crime.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV [HB IV / Karaton], Sultan Hamengkubuwana II [HB II / Kasepuhan].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Séla, Semarang, Surabaya, Surakarta [Solo], Batavia, Vorstenlanden [VK].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Kasepuhan* [elder court party], *Kadipaten* [crown prince / junior court party], *Bandar* [customs tollgate], Chinese tax-farmers, tollgate military pickets, European colonization (*landhuur*), agricultural education, rural brigandage (*kècu*), Eurasian Christian soldiers.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** Nationaal Archief (The Hague), Exhibitum 1 Mei 1817; continuation of Nahuys van Burgst's memorandum of 14 April 1817 from Séla.
* **Document Nature:** Policy manifesto, administrative proposal, and socio-economic critique submitted to the Commissarissen-Generaal.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Represents the intellectual foundation of Nahuys's controversial policy of opening the Principalities to private European estate leasing (*landhuur*), arguing that commercial contact with Europeans would transform feudal agrarian Java into a modern capitalist society.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, colonial garrison detachments, Chinese customs monopolists (*bandars*), and royal palace factions.
* **Continuation Sheet:** Direct textual continuation of the Séla dispatch transcribed on `IMG_1387.jpg`.
* **Marginal Annotations:** Left column tracks sociological headings (`Kasepuhan / Karaton`, `Proposition & European Officers / N.C.O.s.`) with Carey's commentary on Nahuys’s visionary schemes (`[margin: WHAT A VNK / Vision]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Social diplomacy and security proposals on the left; verbatim agrarian theory, educational philosophy, and tollgate policing mechanisms on the right.
