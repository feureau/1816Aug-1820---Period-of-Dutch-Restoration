## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1561.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Portefeuille 6 No. 1 pt. 7, Nahuys van Burgst Archive; Surakarta Land Rental Register 1816–1825)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-01 to 1821-01-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Master statistical register recording private agricultural land rentals (*landverhuur*) contracted across the Principality of Surakarta during the transition from Sunan Pakubuwana IV to Sunan Pakubuwana V; documentation of landmark European and Chinese plantation leases that transferred entire Javanese villages, peasant populations, and compulsory corvée labor to private capitalists: (1) Bouwens van der Boyen and Grand lease Desa Kembang Anom for 15 years at 2,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) with 36 peasant families placed under direct planter control, authorized by the Sunan; (2) Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst personally leases Desa Jimpo and its dependencies (2 jungs) for 400 Sp. D., authorized by Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II; (3) Chinese merchant Djimkwi acquires village land (*landebukal*) at Radjanck for 4 years at 260 Sp. D. from Pangéran Aria Sasrakusuma; (4) Babah Jim leases 24 peasant households (*cacah*) in Sukawati for 6 years paying 200 Sp. D. upfront tax to Patih Sasradiningrat; (5) Babah Jarojing secures a 9-year advance tax lease as *bekel* over Desa Kebak Karang in Sukawati for 22.5 Sp. D.; (6) Babah Tianra acquires *bekel* rights over Kuntan / Petook worked by 60 peasants under Ranawijaya for 2,300 Sp. D. (including a 500 Sp. D. fee for demang status alongside annual Mulud gifts of luxury tea and refined sugar to Pangéran Aria Pamot); (7) European planter Mr. Verwoerd leases Desa Sepatram for 12 years at 1,200 Sp. D., commanding 25 peasant laborers.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** P.M.M. Bouwens van der Boyen, Grand, Sunan of Solo (Sunan Pakubuwana V), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Djimkwi, Pangéran Aria Sasrakusuma, Babah Jim, Babah Jarojing, Babah Tianra, Pangéran Aria Pamot, Ranawijaya, Mr. Verwoerd.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Desa Kembang Anom, Desa Jimpo, Radjanck, Sukawati, Desa Kebak Karang, Kuntan, Petook, Sepatram, Leiden (BPL archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Landverhuur* (private plantation land rentals), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D. / S.D.), *jung* (land measure, approx. 4 bau), *cacah* (peasant households), *bekel* (village leaseholder / tax collector), *demang* (district superintendent), annual feudal presentations (*persen* / tribute), Mulud festival gifts, agrarian commercialization.

#### Master Land Rent Register of Surakarta (October 1820 – January 1821)

*Source: Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Port. 6 No. 1 pt. 7 (Solo Land / Rent 1816–1825)*

| Date of Contract | Name of Renter | Cost of Rent (Specie) | Estate / Village Area Leased | Authorization & Lease Tenure | Socio-Economic Context & Feudal Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **27 Nov 1820** | **Bouwens [P.M.M. Bouwens van der Boyen] + Grand** | 2,000 Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars]<br>*(4,000 Sp. D. total valuation)* | **Desa Kembang Anom**<br>• 36 Javanese households (*cacah*) | **Sunan of Solo [Pakubuwana V]**<br>• 15 years duration<br>*(Option to surrender lease after 5 years)* | Entire village population transferred to direct planter authority; peasants bound to forced estate labor without land rights (*no land*). |
| **14 Dec 1820** | **Nahuys van Burgst [Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Resident]** | 400 Sp. D. | **Desa Jimpo + Dependencies**<br>• 2 Jongs [jungs, approx. 8 bau] | **Patih of Solo [Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II]**<br>• Multi-year lease contract | Direct personal acquisition by the Acting Resident of Surakarta, demonstrating institutional conflict of interest. |
| **27 Dec 1820** | **Djimkwi [Chinese Merchant]** | 260 Sp. D. | **Landebukal of Village Radjanck**<br>• Cleared arable land (*siti bukal*) | **Pangéran Aria Sasrakusuma**<br>• 4 years duration | Commercial lease of royal apanage tract by a prominent Chinese agricultural capitalist. |
| **11 Jan 1821** | **Babah Jim [Chinese Planter]** | 200 Sp. D. annual rent<br>*(1,200 Sp. D. total tax)* | **Sukawati [Sukuwati / Sragen]**<br>• 24 Tjatjas [cacah / households] | **Patih of Solo Sasradiningrat**<br>• 6 years advance tax paid down | Upfront liquidation of six years' imperial land taxes directly into the hands of the Grand Vizier. |
| **11 Jan 1821** | **Babah Jarojing [Chinese Entrepreneur]** | 22.5 Sp. D. annual advance | **Desa Kebak Karang in Sukuwati**<br>• *Bekel* estate jurisdiction | **Patih Sasradiningrat**<br>• 9 years tax paid in advance | Purchase of village tax-collection headship (*bekel*) through advance lump-sum monetization. |
| **6 Jan 1821** | **Babah Tianra [Chinese Lessee]** | 2,300 Sp. D. total<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• 500 Sp. D. for Dèmang title<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Annual *persen* tribute | **Kuntan / Petook**<br>• Worked by 60 peasant laborers<br>• Overseen by seorang Ranawidjaya | **Pangéran Aria Pamot**<br>• Multi-year contract<br>• Annual Mulud gift: 2 cases fine tea, 2 cases ordinary tea, 20 catties sugar, 30 catties powdered sugar | Acquisition of *bekel* and *demang* administrative status over 60 peasants, sealed with luxury European and Chinese tribute items presented at Garebeg Mulud. |
| **Oct 1820** | **Mr. Verwoerd [European Planter]** | 1,200 Sp. D. | **Desa of Sepatram**<br>• Worked by 25 peasant laborers | **Surakarta Court Authorities**<br>• 12 years duration | Long-term plantation concession commanding 25 indigenous agrarian workers for commercial export crops. |
