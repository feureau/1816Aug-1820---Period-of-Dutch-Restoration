# Transcription of IMG_1342.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1342.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk dispatches, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816; and *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys te Djocjocarta*, 22 October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-18 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Dispatch from Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk to Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak dated 18 August 1816, formally announcing his appointment as Extraordinary Envoy (*Buitengewoon Gezant*); records his arrival at Semarang on 17 August 1816 and plans to proceed inland to Surakarta upon receipt of final instructions from the Commission-General (C.-G.) brought from Batavia by Johan Diederik Kruseman; pathetic domestic plight of government interpreter C. F. Krijgsman: living in severely reduced financial circumstances with an exceptionally large family (drawing comparison to the extensive Eurasian coffee planting dynasty of Johannes Augustinus Dezentjé at Ampel); dating of Van IJsseldijk's master report at Batavia, 11 December 1816; transition to Van IJsseldijk's confidential handover instructions for the Yogyakarta residency: *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys te Djocjocarta* (dated Yogyakarta, 22 October 1816); detailed commentary on the acute impoverishment of royal relatives (*sentana*) across both generational court factions in Yogyakarta—the *Sepuhan* (adherents of the exiled HB II) and the *Kanoman* (courtiers of HB III and HB IV)—caused by the 1812 British territorial annexations; devastating assessment of senior kraton statesman Kyai Adipati Purwa: described as completely senile, his mental and intellectual faculties entirely dissipated (with the caustic observation that even in his prime he had little intelligence to boast of); high political standing and intimacy of Mas Tumenggung Wiraguna (later Raden Tumenggung / Major Wiranagara): exercises decisive influence over the Queen Mother, Ratu Ageng Ibu (Ratu Kencana), serving as her confidential translator and personal political advisor.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Johan Diederik Kruseman, C. F. Krijgsman, Johannes Augustinus Dezentjé (J.A. Dezentjé), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sepuhan), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III / IV (Kanoman), Kyai Adipati Purwa, Mas Tumenggung Wiraguna (Major Tumenggung Wiranagara), Ratu Ageng Ibu (Ratu Kencana).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Surakarta (Sala), Batavia, Yogyakarta (Djocjocarta / Yogya), Ampel.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Extraordinary Envoy (*Buitengewoon Gezant*), Commission-General (*Commissie-Generaal* / C.-G.), Provisional Resident (*Prov. Res.*), *Nota* (administrative directive), *sentana* (royal relatives), *Kasepuhan* / *Kanoman* court factions, territorial annexation, court translator, palace favorites.
