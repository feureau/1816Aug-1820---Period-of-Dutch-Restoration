# Transcription of IMG_1331.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1331.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, pp. 9–11)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02 to 1816-09-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s state mission at Surakarta; presentation of Governor-General Van der Capellen’s credential letter; linguistic critique of translator J. G. Krijgsman’s flawed Javanese court translations requiring supplemental Malay explanations; Sunan Pakubuwana IV opening and reading the state letter himself; reciprocal state visit of PB IV to the Residency; two private diplomatic conferences (*aparte conferentiën*) held on 4 and 8 September 1816; execution of Article 2 of the Secret Instructions: explaining the renewed sovereign power of the Kingdom of the Netherlands and dispelling subversive British rumors alleging that the retrocession was temporary and that British forces would reconquer Java within two to three years.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, J. G. Krijgsman (Jv. Translateur), Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo Kraton and Residency House), Semarang, Batavia, The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Secret Instructions (Art. 2), *Aparte Conferentiën* (private diplomatic conferences), British Reconquest Rumors (*Rumours of British Return*), Court Javanese Translation (*Krama Inggil* vs. Service Malay).
