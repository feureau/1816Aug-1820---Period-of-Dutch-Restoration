# Transcription of IMG_1273.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1273.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 7: *Secret Letters from Djogja to and from Commissioners-General — Continuation on the Murma Inquest*)
- **Date Range:** 1791 to 1820
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical evaluation of the custody of sacred heirloom weapons (*pusaka*) linked to Kyai Murma at Kapundhung, specifically the legendary blade *Kyai Birawa* (associated with Majapahit) and the historic disappearance of *Kyai Kuntung* (1791); analysis of personal and factional tensions between Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, former Patih Danuningrat, and Kyai Murma; diplomatic intermediary role of Sèh Abubakar and Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing); historical retrospective on inter-court hostility, highlighting how Sunan Pakubuwana IV secretly encouraged Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III's 1810 rebellion in Jipang/Madiun to stoke mutual hatred and damage Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Sèh Abubakar, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Kepundhung (Kapundhung), Jipang (Japan), Lojajar, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Pusaka weapons (*Kyai Birawa*, *Kyai Kuntung*), Inter-court animosity (*Solo-Yogya rivalry*), Radèn Rongga conspiracy (1810), Court genealogical alliances.
