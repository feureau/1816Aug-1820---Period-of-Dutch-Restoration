# Transcription of IMG_1336.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1336.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** W. H. van IJsseldijk's analytical assessment of the political mood, economic distress, and social destabilization in the *Vorstenlanden* following the Dutch restoration; assessment of princely disaffection in Surakarta fueled by pervasive aristocratic poverty; observation that no prince in either court possesses the daring, energy, or resources to mount a military campaign in the field as during the 18th-century succession wars; diagnosis of Yogyakarta as structurally weaker and more fragile than Surakarta; characterization of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV as too young, inexperienced, and dissolute (*wulps* / riggish) to command authority or stand without European support; mutual discord among regents and princes paralyzing collective resistance; urgent warning against the return of the exiled Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II), whose presence would crystallize smoldering disaffection; analysis of the catastrophic socio-economic consequences of Raffles's 1812 annexation of Kedu, which severely deprived the *sentana* (royal family) of revenue, intensified fiscal extortion on the remaining kraton lands, and triggered a surge in armed rural banditry (*kècu*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II / Sultan Sepuh), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogya / Yogya), Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Kedu (Kedhu), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *sentana* (royal relatives / princely kindred), *kècu* (rural highwaymen / bandits), *wulps* (dissolute / riggish / lewd), annexation of Kedu, appanage crisis, land pressure.
