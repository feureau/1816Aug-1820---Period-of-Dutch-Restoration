## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1379.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60: Jan–Jun 1817 / *Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* [BvGG] 26 Dec 1817 No. 38)
- **Date Range:** 1817-06-07 to 1817-12-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Inter-residency administrative, diplomatic, and economic correspondence between Surakarta, Yogyakarta, and Kedu during June–October 1817. On 9 June 1817, Acting Resident Von Lippe of Surakarta notified Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst of three Indian Sepoys arriving in Yogyakarta escorted by the Solo residency messenger (*oppasser*) Krama; although holding travel permits from Batavia and claiming to be cloth merchants, they were identified as deserters from the British East India Company's Bengal Light Infantry Volunteer Battalion and suspected of clandestine collusion with Sunan Pakubuwana IV's brother, Pangéran Mangkubumi of Surakarta, prompting orders for their expulsion. Additional entries detail the royal private debts of the Sunan's sister Ratu Purbanagara; the proposed dynastic marriage between Kyai Aria Pringgalaya and Nyai Putri Ayu Kavijah; regional grain price disparities between residency military depots (rice selling at 4.50 Dutch Florins per pikul at Klaten/Yogyakarta versus 3.00 Florins at Surakarta/Boyolali); the flight of Kertanentika, head oppasser of Kedu, to the Yogyakarta residency compound to escape debts; and colonial deliberations regarding the Pacitan tax-farm (*pacht*), wherein Nahuys reviewed a bid from entrepreneur C.H. Coster.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI, Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60 (Januari–Juni 1817); supplemented by BvGG (Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal), 26 December 1817, No. 38.
* **Document Nature:** Archival abstracts of incoming and outgoing residency correspondence between the Vorstenlanden Residencies (Yogyakarta and Surakarta), the regional government of Kedu, and the Algemene Secretarie in Batavia.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illuminates colonial vigilance regarding Indian Sepoy ex-soldiers in Central Java following the 1815 conspiracy, princely dynastic indebtedness, military commissariat economics, and the exploitation of fiscal monopolies in peripheral regencies like Pacitan.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Dutch colonial Residents (Nahuys, Von Lippe, De Salis), royal aristocrats (Pangéran Mangkubumi, Ratu Purbanagara), European commercial tax-farmers (C.H. Coster), and lower residency staff (*oppassers*).
* **Archival Header:** Top registers repository and file code (`[Header:] / Yogya Res. Letters. Dj. Br. 60 / Jan. - Jun. 1817 / Jan. - June 1817`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Distinct marginal notes highlighting political surveillance (`[Margin: SEPOYS. *]`) and archival decrees (`PACITAN [BvGG. 26 Dec. 1817 no. 38 Pacitan.]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, correspondents, and thematic markers on the left; verbatim abstracts, intelligence summaries, and grain price statistics on the right.
