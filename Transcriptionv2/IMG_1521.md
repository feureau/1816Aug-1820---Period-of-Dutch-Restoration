# Transcription of IMG_1521.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1521.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63A / *PJ. Br. 63A*, Jan.–May 1820) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Residency Outgoing Letterbook))
- **Date Range:** 1819-12-20 to 1820-03-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse inter-residency, military, agrarian, and commercial dispatches: maritime export of Central Javanese cotton textiles to the Moluccas (*Groote Oost*) aboard the *Lady Sophia Mariam*; tracking two European military deserters fleeing westward through Desa Prampelan in Panaraga (Ponorogo); textile export invoices (Solo chintz and sarongs valued at f. 2,288); Opzichter Engel reporting the exhaustion of pepper cuttings in Kêdhuwang and requesting 3 Spanish Dollars and opium rations for Rongkob birds' nest cliff climbers; commercial boundary clash between Pasar Godean (licensed by Radèn Tumenggung Sasranegara) and Pasar Nganjuk (Mas Ngabèhi Sasrakara); Capitan Cina paying tax farm installments in depreciated old copper *duits*, prompting Nahuys to request 1,500 new copper coins from Surabaya; and royal military commission (*BGG 8 Feb 1820 No. 9*) appointing Pakualam prince Pangéran Suryaprang as Captain in the Royal Netherlands Army with the salary of a 1st Lieutenant of Infantry.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** J. de Bruijn (Resident of Semarang), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Radèn Tumenggung Sasranegara (Bupati of Godean), Mas Ngabèhi Sasrakara (Nganjuk), Hendrik Jacob Domis (Resident of Semarang), Capitan Cina of Yogyakarta, Lippe (Provisional Resident of Surakarta), Pangéran Suryaprang, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Semarang, Batavia, Groote Oost (Moluccas), Panaraga (Ponorogo), Desa Prampelan, Pacitan, Kêdhuwang, Godang, Rongkob caves, Godean, Nganjuk, Surabaya, Kepundhung (Dèlanggu).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Maritime textile trade (*Lady Sophia Mariam*), European military desertion, Invoices (*facturen*), Pepper nurseries, Opium rations for cliff-climbers, Market toll competition (*pasar*), Copper currency depreciation (*duiten*), *BGG in Rade*, Royal Army commission (*Kapitein van het Koninklijke Leger*).
