# Transcription of IMG_1292.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1292.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, Vol. I, Doc. XXVIII continued, pp. 98–99)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-13 to 1815-11-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's 13 September 1816 diplomatic report on Yogyakarta; observation of the dramatic material impoverishment of the Kraton following the 1812 British sack (*Bedhah ing Ngayogyakarta*), visible in the substitution of coarse Chinese pottery and base silver for ancestral gold vessels; dynastic appeals by Ratu Ageng on behalf of the young Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV; verification of British Resident John Crawfurd's correct conduct regarding retrocession; three-point structural assessment of the Yogyakarta court factions; emotional outburst by Pangéran Demang decrying British contemptuous treatment of Javanese royalty as coolies (*batoors*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), John Crawfurd, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Demang, Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton and Residency House), Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Semarang, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bedhah ing Ngayogyakarta (1812 looting of court gold), *Batoor* (coolie / servant), *Obat* (medicine / healing code name), Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.), Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1814.
