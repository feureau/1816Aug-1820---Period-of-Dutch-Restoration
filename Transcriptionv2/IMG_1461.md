# Transcription of IMG_1461.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1461.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Scholarly Research Notes (Historiographical Excerpt: J.A. van der Chijs, *Tijdschrift voor Indische Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde* [TBG], 1864, pp. 212–214)
- **Date Range:** 1808-09-27 to 1864-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historiographical analysis by Peter Carey tracing the statutory evolution of colonial educational policy in Java from Marshal Herman Willem Daendels through the Commissioners-General to Governor-General Van der Capellen, citing J.A. van der Chijs' foundational study (*TBG*, Vol. 14, 1864, pp. 212–214). The notes record that Daendels was the first European ruler to formally mandate native education: Article 13 of his *Instructie voor de Regenten aan de Noorderkust van Java* (27 September 1808) placed the moral, legal, and religious instruction of Javanese youth—and the establishment of district schools—under the direct responsibility of the *bupatis*. However, Van der Chijs observes that following two centuries of total VOC indifference, Daendels' proclamation remained an empty statement of benevolent intent (*pia vota*) that was only realized half a century later. The notes then trace the legislative renewal of 22 December 1818 under the Commissioners-General (Elout, Buyskes, and Van der Capellen): Articles 99–100 of the *Reglement op het Beleid der Regeering* mandated state-funded primary schools across the archipelago for both indigenous and European youths, while actively promoting the study of Javanese and Malay among Dutch officials, culminating in Van der Capellen's seminal decree of 8 March 1819 (No. 9) launching the first comprehensive island-wide education inquest.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jacobus Anne van der Chijs, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Arnold Adriaan Buyskes, Dr. Peter B.R. Carey, Pasisir Regents.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Pasisir [North Coast of Java], The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Instructie voor de Regenten* (1808, Art. 13), VOC neglect of native education, *Reglement op het Beleid der Regeering* (1818, Arts. 99–100), Commissarissen-Generaal, State-funded primary schools, Vernacular linguistic training for Europeans, BGG 8 March 1819 No. 9.
