# Transcription of IMG_1426.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1426.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: Algemeen Verslag Solo [AvS], 1818: Residentie Dagregister van Surakarta)
- **Date Range:** 1818-01-01 to 1818-08-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official court calendar, residency social protocols, administrative transition, and the spectacle of the royal tiger-sticking tournament (*Rampok Macan*) in Surakarta. On 1 January 1818, the Resident of Surakarta, court regents, garrison officers, and leading burghers attended the Kraton to convey New Year compliments to Sunan Pakubuwana IV; while the Sunan exchanged cordial wishes, he politely declined an invitation to dine at the Residency House, sending instead Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Patih Sasradiningrat II, and the senior *nayaka* regents. On 19 January, newly appointed Resident Rijck van Prehn arrived in Surakarta to assume executive office (noted alongside Kyai Murma's simultaneous deportation to Ambon). On 20 January, the court celebrated Garèbèg Mulud, followed by an evening residency banquet attended by the Crown Prince (future PB V) and nobility. In February, the Kraton held twin celebrations for Sunan PB IV: his traditional Javanese astrological cycle day (*Wiyosan*) on 12 February, and his official birthday on 25 February. On 30 May 1818, Sunan PB IV made a grand public appearance before thousands of his subjects on the *Pagelaran* and Northern *Alun-Alun*, riding on horseback surrounded by the high nobility before presiding over the royal tournament (*Watangan*) and the ritual *Rampok Macan*: two royal tigers were released into the center of a dense circular ring of court pikemen, charging the bristling wall of lances until speared to death. On 22–24 August 1818, the court and residency celebrated the birthday of King William I of the Netherlands with exceptional luster.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (future Sunan Pakubuwana V / Sugandi), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Resident Rijck van Prehn, King William I of the Netherlands (WMI), Kyai Murma Wijaya.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Sala], Kraton Surakarta, Pagelaran, Northern Alun-Alun [Alun-Alun Lor], Residency House.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Garebèg Mulud*, *Wiyosan* [Javanese astrological calendrical day], *Nayaka* [executive regents], *Rampok Macan* [ritual tiger-sticking tournament], royal cavalry tournament [*Watangan* / *Senenan*], King's Birthday [*Koningsdag*], Residentie Dagregister.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698a (Algemeen Verslag Solo: Residentie Dagregister en Gewone Rapporten, 1818).
* **Document Nature:** Official administrative daily journal, court ceremonial register, and cultural ethnography of royal Mataram spectacle.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the delicate social choreography between the Dutch colonial residency and the Surakarta Kraton under Sunan PB IV, demonstrating how shared dining diplomacy coexisted with ancient sovereign displays of martial prowess (*Rampok Macan*).
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Prangwedana, Patih Sasradiningrat II, and Surakarta royal guard regiments.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries establish repository series (`H 698a. / AvS / 1818 / (Sala) / (1818)`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Red ink marginal reference to Kyai Murma's banishment (`[Red ink note:] Kyai Murma exiled to Ambon`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Chronological dates on the left; verbatim summaries of New Year greetings, Residency arrivals, Islamic court festivals, planetary birthdays, the vivid *Rampok Macan* tournament, and Dutch royal birthdays on the right.
