# Transcription of IMG_1270.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1270.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 5: *Djogja Corr. [Secret] 1816 — Letters from Governor-General to Resident and Vice-Versa*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-11-19 to 1816-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official handling and custody arrangements for arrested rebel Kyai Murma Wijaya under Acting Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman (19 November 1816); palace incest scandal and moral crisis involving the teenage Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, accused of cohabiting with a widowed consort of his late father (Hamengkubuwana III) or grandfather (Hamengkubuwana II), violating both Islamic Sharia and Christian natural law; comparison with the historic disqualification of Pangéran Ngabèhi from the royal succession; allegations of Ratu Ibu's complicity in facilitating the scandal; Resident's audience in the Kraton ordering the immediate expulsion of the younger royal widows from the palace; diplomatic mission to Surakarta reporting acute political paralysis, factional discord between Resident Pinket van Haak, Military Commandant Le Sueur, Patih Sasradiningrat II, and jealous princes; Commandant Le Sueur's clandestine lobbying to secure the Surakarta Residency.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Pangéran Ngabèhi, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Major Donkel, Commandant J. J. Le Sueur, Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, M. C. Ricklefs.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Palace incest scandal (*taboo violations*), Expulsion of royal widows, Succession disqualification (*Ricklefs p. 102*), Surakarta civil-military discord, Residency appointment lobbying.
