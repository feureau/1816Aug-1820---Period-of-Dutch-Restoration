# Transcription of IMG_1442.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1442.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5: Secret Letters from Solo / Van Prehn to Algemeen Secretaris, 30 March 1818, Folio 6)
- **Date Range:** 1818-03-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding folio and official signature of Resident Rijck van Prehn’s Secret Dispatch No. 2 addressed to the Secretary of State / Governor-General Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen. Van Prehn summarizes his diplomatic achievement in Surakarta: by reassuring Sunan Pakubuwana IV of the supreme power, stability, and protection of the restored Netherlands Indies Government, he guaranteed the Sunan’s sovereign existence as monarch of Mataram (*Vorst in de Mataram*), receiving the Sunan's formal compliance with colonial advice and directives. Van Prehn concludes with a glowing, optimistic assessment of regional pacification: if full credit can be given to the solemn vows pledged by the Sunan, peace across Java (*de rust op Java*) has never been more firmly established than under the auspicious auspices of Governor-General Van der Capellen’s administration. The dispatch concludes with Van Prehn’s official signature as Provisional Resident of Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Crown Prince Sugandi (future PB V), Secretaris van Staat.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Soerakarta], Mataram, Batavia, The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Concluding dispatch formula, sovereign existence (*Vorst in de Mataram*), colonial pacification (*rust en orde*), royal submission to colonial counsel, signature block of *De Provisionele Resident*.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5 (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Papers, Secret Letters from Solo, 1818, Folio 6).
* **Document Nature:** Secret executive dispatch (*Geheime Missive*), formal diplomatic conclusion, and official signature folio.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Closes the foundational March 1818 dispatch series on the Surakarta throne succession, illustrating the euphoric colonial illusion of permanent peace in Central Java seven years before the eruption of the Java War.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Rijck van Prehn and the General Secretariate in Batavia.
* **Archival Header:** Continuation and formal close of primary secret dispatch text from `IMG_1441`.
* **Marginal Annotations:** Bottom-right official address block (`Aan Den Heer Secretaris`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Diplomatic synthesis and geopolitical commentary on the left; verbatim nineteenth-century Dutch concluding text, signature, and address on the right.
