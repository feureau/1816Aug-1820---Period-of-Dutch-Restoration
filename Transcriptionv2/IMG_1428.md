# Transcription of IMG_1428.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1428.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: Algemeen Verslag Jogja [AvJ], 1818: Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven)
- **Date Range:** 1818-06-16 to 1818-12-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Techno-economic blueprint for modernizing the Javanese sugar industry, retrospective details of the violent 1812 *amuk* assault on Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing (Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Nahuys van Burgst's diplomatic mission to West Borneo, court religious festivals, and compensation assessments for coastal salt panning. The document outlines a comprehensive critique of native sugar manufacturing: existing processing stripped local forests of timber to fashion wooden cylinder crushers (which wore down rapidly) and provide firewood for boiling; draft buffaloes died in large numbers under the strain of turning heavy crushers; and inefficient boiling kettles wasted fuel. Nahuys proposed modernizing sugar technology by introducing durable cast-metal cylinders, modern boiling kettles, and watermills powered by Yogyakarta’s copious river streams, arguing that cheap native day-labor and rich water supplies guaranteed handsome profits for enterprising Dutch planters. On 16 June 1818, Nahuys transmitted a detailed report to Batavia on the return petitions of Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I and Pangéran Mertasana, recounting the dramatic 1812 episode where an *amuk* party of royal retainers assaulted the residence of Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing, wounding nine soldiers and spearing Secadiningrat with a pike. The document also notes Nahuys' departure for Batavia on 16 October 1818 prior to his appointment as Special Government Commissioner for West Borneo (21 Nov. 1818 – 5 May 1819; citing *BKI* 1902), the celebration of Garèbèg Besar on 11 October, and an official assessment by Assistant Resident R.C.N. d'Abo on 4 December regarding government indemnities for South Coast salt producers (covering 61½ *jungs* of land across coastal villages at 2 Rupees per capita monthly).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Arya Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Arya Mertasana, Tan Jin Sing (Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo, Inspector-General of State Revenues.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Yogya], Madiun, Kertosono [Kertasana], South Coast [Segara Kidul], Batavia, West Borneo [Pontianak / Sambas].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Modernization of sugar manufacture (*suikercultuur*), metal cylinder crushers, watermills, buffalo attrition, *amuk* attack (1812), Kapitan Cina residence assault, Special Commissionership to Borneo, *Garèbèg Besar*, Southern sea salt monopoly, compensation allowances (*schadeloosstelling*), *jung* [land measure].
