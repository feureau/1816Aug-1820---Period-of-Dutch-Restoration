# Transcription of IMG_1439.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1439.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5: Secret Letters from Solo / Van Prehn to Algemeen Secretaris, 30 March 1818, Folio 3)
- **Date Range:** 1818-03-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Third folio of Resident Rijck van Prehn’s Secret Dispatch No. 2 exposing the maternal palace cabal behind Pangéran Purbaya and recounting the dramatic royal confrontation over sumptuary regalia (*upacara*) inside the Surakarta Kraton. Van Prehn established that if any marriage overtures had been made to Pangéran Prangwedana for his daughter's hand, they were initiated not by Sunan Pakubuwana IV, but by the "Empress" (the Ratu, mother of Purbaya), who lavished unlimited funds on her sole son’s outward luxury and status. To prove that the Sunan firmly upheld the Crown Prince's supremacy, Van Prehn recounts a pivotal palace clash: Crown Prince Sugandi encountered Purbaya traversing the palace accompanied by a personal retainer carrying a ceremonial sirih/betel box (*serie doos*) in a royal form strictly reserved for the heir apparent. Sugandi immediately lodged a formal complaint with the Sunan, invoking sacred Javanese sumptuary laws governing regalia (*upacara*), dress, and royal state (*statie*). Sunan PB IV summoned Purbaya, physically confiscated the sirih box, and publicly humiliated him, sternly declaring that no outward pomp could ever equate him with his elder brother, reaffirming Crown Prince Sugandi as his sole acknowledged son and rightful successor.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (Sugandi / future Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII), The Ratu / "Keizerin" (mother of Purbaya), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Soerakarta], Kraton Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Javanese sumptuary laws, royal regalia (*upacara* / *statie*), ceremonial sirih box (*tempat sirih* / *serie doos*), palace protocol breach, maternal faction, royal reprimand.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5 (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Papers, Secret Letters from Solo, 1818, Folio 3).
* **Document Nature:** Secret executive dispatch (*Geheime Missive*), verbatim diplomatic reportage, and ethnographic analysis of Kraton sumptuary disputes.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates how material symbols of status (betel boxes and court umbrellas) functioned as high-stakes political battlegrounds in Javanese dynastic rivalries.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, and the rival princely households of Sugandi and Purbaya.
* **Archival Header:** Continuation of primary secret dispatch text from `IMG_1438`.
* **Marginal Annotations:** Analytical section markers identifying maternal intrigue and the royal regalia clash.
* **Two-Column Organization:** Diplomatic context and sumptuary analysis on the left; verbatim nineteenth-century Dutch text of the secret dispatch on the right.
