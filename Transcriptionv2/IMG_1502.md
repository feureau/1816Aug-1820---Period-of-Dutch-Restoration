# Transcription of IMG_1502.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1502.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 139 / ANRI Solo 139, *Translaaten en Verbaalen 1817 tot 1818*) (Archetype 1: Archival Translation (Contemporary Dutch Translation by J.W. Winter))
- **Date Range:** 1818-12-01 to 1819-08-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official contemporary Dutch translation prepared by Surakarta Residency Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter of the prophetic Jawi broadsheet circulated by the village elders (*Loegoe*) of Ajibarang and Banyumas: opening in the Javanese month of *Repit* (December 1818), recounting celestial revolutions of the heavens, earth, sun, and moon; detailing the vice-regal progress of Governor-General Van der Capellen (*de Radeen Generaal*) accompanied by his brother, consort (*de Princes*), and eleven high officials (*Mantris*); reporting secret royal deliberations within the Kraton to appoint four paramount officials; and prophesying the imminent cosmic and political inversion of the earth (*dat er de aarde zal worden omgekeerd*), beginning with the directional realignment of the eastern lord moving to the west.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** The Loegoe of Ajibarang, The Loegoe of Banyumas, Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (*de Radeen Generaal*), Baroness van der Capellen (*de Princes*), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (translator), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Ajibarang, Banyumas, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Javaansche Harab* (Jawi / Pegon script letter), *Loegoe* (village headman / seer), *Maand Repit*, *Apits* (Javanese calendar years), Kraton royal council, Cosmic inversion (*de aarde omgekeerd*), *Mantris*.
