# Transcription of IMG_1458.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1458.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Kabinet Dossier No. 2065: Ingekomen Berigten over het Inlandsch Onderwijs, 1831–1832 / Pekalongan and Semarang Residencies)
- **Date Range:** 1831-10-07 to 1831-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Educational reform proposals in Pekalongan linking agricultural labor cycles to village schooling, alongside an official demographic census of 110 Islamic teachers and 2,952 pupils across the four regencies of the Semarang Residency. In Pekalongan, Resident De Bousquet reported that peasant children were effectively barred from distant schools because households could not spare their labor during harvest and planting cycles. To overcome this, the Resident proposed establishing 24 district primary schools across Pekalongan's 12 districts—located within walking distance of villages so children could work fields before classes—endowing each native master with an annual cash stipend of f. 60 and 1½ *jungs* of prime irrigated ricefields (*sawah*) rent-free. On 24 December 1831, Resident of Semarang P. le Clercq submitted comprehensive statistical returns on traditional Islamic schooling across his four constituent regencies: (1) Regency of Semarang (25 teachers, 1,140 pupils); (2) Regency of Kendal (60 teachers, 928 pupils); (3) Regency of Demak (7 teachers, 519 pupils); and (4) Regency of Grobogan (18 teachers, 365 pupils), documenting an active scholastic body of 110 *kyais* and 2,952 students supported by voluntary communal food tithes and ritual feast offerings (*sedhekah*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes van den Bosch, Resident of Pekalongan (A. de Bousquet), Resident of Semarang (P. le Clercq), District Regents of Semarang, Kendal, Demak, and Grobogan, Islamic village teachers and Mecca pilgrims (*Hajis*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pekalongan [Pèkalongan], Semarang, Kendal, Demak, Grobogan, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Agricultural labor cycle compatibility, Rent-free sawah endowment (*1½ jung sawah*), *Sedhekah* [ritual communal feast offerings], Semarang school census (110 teachers, 2,952 pupils), *Grobogan*, *Kendal*, *Demak*, Sub-commission on Education.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Kabinet Dossier No. 2065 (1831–1832 General Secretariate Inquest on Native Education).
* **Document Nature:** Administrative policy memoranda, agrarian educational models, and statistical regency census returns.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates colonial attempts to synthesize Western primary education with Javanese village agricultural life through communal land endowments, while recording the vast scale of Islamic schooling in central North Java.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Governor-General Van den Bosch, Residents Le Clercq and De Bousquet, and regional bupatis.
