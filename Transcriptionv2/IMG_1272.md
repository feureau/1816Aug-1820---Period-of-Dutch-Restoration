# Transcription of IMG_1272.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1272.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 7: *Secret Letters from Djogja to and from Commissioners-General*, Solo November 1816; and M. C. Ricklefs, *Jogjakarta Under Sultan Mangkubumi*, p. 106)
- **Date Range:** 1810-11 to 1816-11-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Surakarta Provisional Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak's secret dispatch to Governor-General Van der Capellen assessing Sunan Pakubuwana IV's character (*"a weak and faithless man"*); deep historical ties between Kyai Murma Wijaya and the Surakarta Kraton dating back to the tenure of Patih Danuningrat (c. 1804–1810); Murma's historic role as secret diplomatic liaison coordinating joint Yogyakarta-Surakarta resistance against Marshal Daendels during Radèn Rongga's 1810 revolt; Daendels's retaliation resulting in the forced dismissal of Patih Danuningrat and the exile of Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I); role of Sèh Abubakar as an active intelligence go-between; Murma's clandestine entry into Surakarta 24 hours prior to the Kapundhung raid, holding secret audiences with Radèn Tumenggung Cakrakusuma and Sunan Pakubuwana IV himself.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Sèh Abubakar, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Radèn Tumenggung Cakrakusuma, M. C. Ricklefs.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Kepundhung (Kapundhung), Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** BPL 616 Port. 5 No. 7, Radèn Rongga's Revolt (1810), Clandestine inter-court diplomacy, Sèh Abubakar's camp, Secret royal audiences.
