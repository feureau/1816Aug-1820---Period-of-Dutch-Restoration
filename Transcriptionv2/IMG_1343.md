# Transcription of IMG_1343.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1343.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk to Major H. G. Nahuys van Burgst, *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys te Djocjocarta*, 22 October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk transmits confidential policy directives to Provisional Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (22 October 1816); character appraisal of Mas Tumenggung Wiraguna (later Raden Tumenggung / Major Wiranagara), evaluated as possessing a far firmer and better character than his father; strategic and psychological evaluation of Pangeran Paku Alam I (Natakusuma): praised by Van IJsseldijk as the single most intelligent, capable, and politically adroit prince in Yogyakarta, with Van IJsseldijk admonishing Nahuys to appreciate Paku Alam's indispensability; critique of Paku Alam's ambitious posturing: he arrogates to himself the exalted title of *Radèn Adipati* and behaves as a de facto Crown Prince (*masquerading as Crown Prince*); Van IJsseldijk advises that despite these royal pretensions, Paku Alam should be confirmed as guardian of the Great Seal of State until Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV attains his majority at twenty years of age; financial provisions: Paku Alam must be guaranteed an annual stipend of 6,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) charged directly to the Yogyakarta kraton treasury—an allowance originally promised by Raffles in 1812 but consistently withheld; general political diagnosis: neither Yogyakarta nor Surakarta contains truly enterprising princes capable of leading a field army in armed rebellion; analysis of escalating rural insecurity (*Robberies 1814–16*): dramatic surge in gang robberies and armed banditry (*kècu*) following the British annexations; security directives: princes and court nobles must be strictly prohibited from leaving court precincts or fraternizing with rural village populations (*negorij people*); armed robbery has become so rampant that raids now occur under the very walls of Semarang and within the immediate suburban perimeter (*smoke*) of the princely kratons.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Mas Tumenggung Wiraguna (Major Tumenggung Wiranagara), Pangeran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I (P.A.), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Thomas Stamford Raffles.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Great Seal of State (*zegel van staat*), *Radèn Adipati*, Crown Prince (*Pangéran Adipati Anom*), annual stipend (6,000 Sp. D.), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), British treaty obligations (1812), princely confinement, rural banditry (*kècu*), *negorij* (peasant villages).
