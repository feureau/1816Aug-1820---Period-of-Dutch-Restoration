Extortions / Tollgate Taxes.
Yogya letters 1819 Nov. - Dec. 1819

Nahuys to Insp. of Finances (Semarang)
30 Nov. 1819
1 Report on Wang Pêkêti — purely extortionate Tax Raised by Chinese Tax Farmer.
should be done away with like Walarangi.
River crossings:
2 Wang Pakhêmitan — money paid to Bandar of Tollgate for protection of goods. although no fixed Tarriff.
3 Wang Mutian — Raised at Pasar Gëdhé / Imogiri / Tëmbayat for Jurukunci of Royal graves.

Nahuys to Res. Semarang,
26 Nov. 1819
Yogya clothes to be sent to Moluccas.
Capitan Cina to Report on costs + write to Java to order cloth.

Id to Insp. of Finances (Semarang)
18 Nov. 1819
Each year 120 fresh nests (6 cattis) 3 x p.a. (at each collection of nests) given to Sultan.

Nahuys to Res. Pasuruan,
22 Nov. 1819
Outbreak of Disturbances in E. govrmt. Residencies — P. P. Wono S., MN to be asked to help.

Id to GG,
7 Dec. 1819
Bp. Rawa takes 11 Jav. prisoners in Residency guilty of Disturbances in Malang.

Id to Opzichter (Engel) Pacitan,
3 Dec. 1819
Asks for Report on planting of coffee in Pacitan.

Nahuys to Insp. of Finances (Semarang),
9 Dec. 1819
Houses in Yogya: private persons Rent either from govrmt. officials or Yogyanese.
Levy ground Tax.

Nahuys to Alg. Secr.,
1 Dec. 1819
Harvey Thomson comes to Jav. 4 Aug. 1811. permission to settle in N.I. 12 Nov. 1817 no. 6.
wants further permission to settle as a Landowner.

Nahuys Report on Salt in Yogya,
19 Aug. 1819
Abolition of Tolls on Solo River by Brit. Govrmt. makes purchasing N. Coast salt much easier.
But makes smuggling more easy.
Govrmt. survey of tolls to prevent smuggling of N. Coast salt.
*Restoration of tolls on Solo R.*
Chinese goods pass toll free to Grësik / Surabaya.
