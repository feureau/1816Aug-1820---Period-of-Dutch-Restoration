# Transcription of IMG_1259.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1259.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djokja Archive 1816 — Letters from Nahuys to Van der Capellen*; and P. H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816*, p. 267)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-13 to 1816-09-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Confidential palace discussions between Resident Nahuys van Burgst and Ratu Ibu (Ratu Ageng) regarding court rivalries and the exclusion of Ratu Kencana Wulan from Yogyakarta; Nahuys's candid private grievance regarding the dispatch of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (*"the sending of a special envoy did not please me very much... I do not wish prying into private affairs"*); Governor-General Van der Capellen's mandatory census of all European landowners, planters, and officials resident in Yogyakarta; accidental death of a Javanese villager during a colonial mounted police sortie and Nahuys's immediate payment of a 12-guilder death compensation (*indemnification*); administrative cooperation from the Marcolatan district.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Pangéran Paku Alam I, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Ratu Kencana Wulan, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia, The Hague, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Special Envoy (*Buitengewone Gezant*), Census of European Landholders, Police mounted patrol, Death indemnification, Dutch guilder (f / Dfl), Court factions (*kasepuhan* vs *karajan*).
