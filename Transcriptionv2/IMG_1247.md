## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1247.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Citations from P. H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816* ['s-Gravenhage: Martinus Nijhoff, 1911], pp. 222–223)
- **Date Range:** 1816-07-18 to 1816-08-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official bureaucratic appointment procedures for the Dutch civil residency in Yogyakarta during the restoration of 1816; initial appointment of Jan Tiedeman as Resident of Yogyakarta by decree of the Commissioners-General (*Besluit C.C.G.G.* 18 July 1816 No. 2); Tiedeman's formal petition to decline the post on private estate, financial, and family guardianship grounds; cancellation of Tiedeman's assignment and subsequent commissioning of Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst as Resident of Yogyakarta by decree of 22 July 1816 No. 1; appointment of Tiedeman to the Council of Finance (*Raad van Financiën*, Staatsblad 1816 No. 3).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Jan Tiedeman, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Second Lieutenant J.H. Knoerle, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia, The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Commissioners-General (*Commissarissen-Generaal*), Council of Finance (*Raad van Financiën*), Residency of Yogyakarta, *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, Restoration of 1816.
