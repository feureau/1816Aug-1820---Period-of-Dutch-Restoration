# Transcription of IMG_1306.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1306.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], *Bundel Djokjo Brieven* / Javanese Letters of October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Original manuscript letter in cursive Javanese script (*Aksara Jawa*); confidential royal petition addressed directly to Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (*Tuwan Idelir Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk*); invokes the dying wisdom of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (*Sultan Swarga*) warning that elevating Chinese individuals close to the throne produces discord and ruins the realm (*dados witing resan ing Negari*); urges Van IJsseldijk to intervene while Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV is still a minor; formally petitions for the expulsion of Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat) from Yogyakarta to preserve state order; bears official ANRI authentication stamp and blind-embossed seal.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (Tuwan Idelir / vIJ), Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat / Jim Sing), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I / Sultan Swarga), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA), Kyahi Adipati Purwa.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Ngayogya Kraton).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Aksara Jawa*, Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI), Blind-embossed seal, *Witing Resan* (root of strife / destruction of state), Expulsion petition (*kakesahaken saking Negari Ngayogya*).
