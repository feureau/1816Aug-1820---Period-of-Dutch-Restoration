# Transcription of IMG_1549.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1549.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV Leiden, H 698b; *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djokjokarta 1820* [AvJ 1820], Fiscal & Agricultural Chapters)
- **Date Range:** 1817 to 1820
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Longitudinal audit of gross tollgate (*bandar*) revenues in Yogyakarta showing explosive growth from f. 147,818 in 1817 to f. 417,540 in 1821 across 34 tollgates and 2 weighing houses (*gedhong timbangan*), auctioned in 25 leasing parcels; vital religious tax exemptions granted to the sacred pilgrimage centers of Imagiri (royal cemetery) and Kota Gedé (ancestral tomb of Senapati), where all tollgate revenues were retained by the *santri* royal retainers (*abdi-dalem juru kunci*) guarding the holy tombs; comprehensive economic dispatch from Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst to the Director of Finances diagnosing structural distortions in the rural economy: while commercialization turned marginal scrubland into irrigated sawah, excessive toll barriers severely crippled inland trade; catastrophic collapse in domestic food supply caused by reckless conversion of sawah into European commercial sugar estates, triggering a doubling in grain prices, acute famine, and a surge in highway robbery and homicides; agrarian corrective policy forcing planters to return estate lands back to peasant rice cultivation; administrative plea to auction village leases (*desa verhuring*) and tollgates on 3-year tenures rather than chaotic 1-year leases; customs transport regulations exempting riverboat market vendors from transit tolls while assessing overland pack traffic; maritime trade incentives for Javanese prahus sailing to Gresik for sea-salt, allowing 1 *koyang* of rice or coconut oil free of customs dues.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Director of Finances (Batavia), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, royal tomb keepers (*santri abdi-dalem*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Imagiri (Imogiri), Kota Gedé, Gresik, Pasisir (North Coast).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Algemeen Verslag van Djokjokarta* (AvJ 1820), tollgates (*bandar*), *Gêdong Tembakan* (tobacco weigh-houses), leasing parcels (*percelen*), tax exemptions, royal ancestral tombs, *abdi-dalem*, sugar crisis (*suikerindustrie*), grain inflation, food security, 3-year lease contracts, river vs overland tariffs, *koyang* (bulk shipping measure).
