- **Date Range:** 1817-07-07 to 1826-08-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Two distinct archival dossiers documenting military pacification in East Java (July 1817) and the catastrophic financial aftermath of the land-rent rescission in Yogyakarta (February 1824). In the upper section, Captain Frederick reports that the Malang rebel band remains active in the Surakarta borderland; he dispatches Major F.V.H.A. ridder de Stuers (renowned for his command of local languages and geography) and an officer from Bangil to assist the Regent of Malang, while 250 European and 150 Javanese soldiers stand ready in Semarang aboard the warship *Prins Frederik*. Meanwhile, Nahuys writes from Semarang on 8 July, accompanied by Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) and his three sons, formally vouching for the unshakeable loyalty of the Mangkunagaran house. The lower section records a landmark document of the 1824 land-rent crisis: an official letter from Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kencana) to Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert dated 27 Jumadilakir A.J. 1751 [28 February 1824]. Because Sultan Hamengkubuwana V was still a minor, Ratu Ageng addresses the cancellation of European estate leases (*landhuur*) in the villages of Somagitan, Rajawinangun, Kenayan, and Karanganyar, directly concerning the apanages of Pangéran Mangkubumi and Pangéran Dipanagara. Ratu Ageng authorizes Smissaert to compensate European planters (Tinne, Harvey Thomson, Nogent, Capt. de Cenvelaine) as he sees fit, requesting a colonial government credit advance if royal vaults lack cash, and suggesting the estates be placed under the administration of Raden Tumenggung Wiranagara or Raden Adipati Danureja IV to generate the required indemnity funds.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** F.V.H.A. ridder de Stuers, Captain Frederick, Major Le Sueur [comm. at Solo], Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Pangéran Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II [PWD], Ratu Kencana / Ratu Ageng [Ratu Agēng], Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V [HB V], Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara [PR III / Wiranegara], Leonard Pierre Joseph burggraaf du Bus de Gisignies, Harvey Thomson [*Tuan Tom*], Tinne, Nogent, Capt. de Cenvelaine.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Malang, Bangil, Pasuruan, Semarang, Surakarta [Solo], Yogyakarta [Yogya], Desa Somagitan, Desa Rajawinangun [Rojowinangun], Desa Kenayan, Desa Karanganyar.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Land rent rescission (*landverhuur* decree of 6 May 1823), estate indemnities, apanage lands, regency guardianship, *Prins Frederik* [warship], Mangkunagaran Legion, *Bijlage* [archival annex], Jumadilakir A.J. 1751.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** Nationaal Archief (The Hague), Exhibitum 14 Juli 1817 No. 1a Secret; Exhibitum 13 Juli 1817 No. La A; Archive of the Governor-General (Du Bus de Gisignies), Bijlage to Dispatch of 3 August 1826.
* **Document Nature:** Upper register: military garrison dispatches and troop deployment orders from 1817; Lower register: official Javanese court correspondence (translated to Dutch) concerning estate liquidations and princely indemnities on the eve of the Java War (1824).
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the direct institutional and financial chain connecting the land-rent policy of Governor-General Van der Capellen (1823) to the alienation of Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi, culminating in the outbreak of the Java War in July 1825.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Colonial civil-military officials (De Stuers, Nahuys, Smissaert, Du Bus), royal Kraton guardians (Ratu Ageng, Mangkubumi, Dipanagara), and European estate leaseholders (Thomson, Tinne, Nogent).
* **Bipartite Page Layout:** The sheet contains two completely distinct archival transcriptions separated by a prominent horizontal divider (`-------------------------------------------------------------------------`).
* **Upper Register (July 1817):** Secret military dispatches concerning East Java pacification and troop shipments aboard *Prins Frederik*.
* **Lower Register (Feb 1824):** Core administrative minutes concerning Ratu Ageng's letter to Resident Smissaert over land-lease indemnities involving Dipanagara.
