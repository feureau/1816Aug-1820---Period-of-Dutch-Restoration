# Transcription of IMG_1339.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1339.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-09 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Precedent analysis regarding Chinese dignitaries in Javanese territorial administration: comparison drawn with Han Tik Ko (the prominent Kapitan Cina of Pasuruan who acquired extensive landlord estates under Marshal Daendels before the tragic 1813 Probolinggo uprising); deliberations on whether Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing) must relinquish his appanage lands and royal loans (*lungguh*); Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst fully concurs with Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk that Secadiningrat cannot legitimately remain invested with the Javanese noble title of *Radèn Tumenggung*; detailed humanitarian and political directives issued regarding Yogyakarta court society: Resident Nahuys is instructed to pay urgent attention to the distressing situation of Raden Ayu Danukusumo (daughter of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I and mother of Dipanagara's close ally Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma II); despite her exalted royal bloodline, she endures severe poverty following the cold-blooded murder of her husband, Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma I, strangled at Banyusumurup in January 1812 on Sultan Sepuh's orders; official instructions to Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak (cataloged in Appendix / Bijlage No. 11); Van IJsseldijk's residency in Yogyakarta from 9 to 23 September 1816 awaiting instructions from Governor-General Van der Capellen; Van IJsseldijk resolves to protect diplomatic decorum by refusing to pay an unsolicited third visit to Surakarta; departs for Surakarta on 24 September 1816 to take formal diplomatic leave of Sunan Pakubuwana IV; delivers solemn Dutch government pledges of protection to PB IV and the Crown Prince (Pangeran Sugandi / future PB V); the Crown Prince personally escorts Van IJsseldijk to his residence, expressing the joint relief and satisfaction of the Sunan and himself at the peaceful outcome of the mission.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Han Tik Ko (Kapitan Cina of Pasuruan), Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing), Raden Ayu Danukusumo, Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma I, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak (Res. van Haak), Governor-General Godert Alexander Gerard Philip Baron van der Capellen, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Crown Prince of Surakarta (Pangeran Sugandi / later Sunan Pakubuwana V / CP).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Pasuruan, Banyusumurup, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kapitan Cina, *tumenggung*, appanages / royal loans (*lungguh*), Bijlage (archival appendix), diplomatic protocol, royal protection guarantee, princely succession, Banyusumurup royal cemetery.
