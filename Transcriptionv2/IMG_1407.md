# Transcription of IMG_1407.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1407.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: Algemeen Verslag Solo [AvS Gewoon], 1817 / Surakarta Residency Letters & Kedu Boundary Dispatches)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-01 to 1818-03-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Catalog of Surakarta's eastern and coastal territories, residency provisioning of the royal court, colonial salt monopoly negotiations, internal travel pass regulations, and the Kedu-Surakarta market boundary arbitration. The geographic survey concludes by listing the Eastern *Manca Nagara* (Jagaraga, Kedawang, Panaraga, Caruban, Trenggalek, Kediri, Srengat, Blitar, Lodaya) and the southern maritime coast (Pacitan, Lorog, Panggul, Segara Wedi). On 21 August 1817, official accounts record the Resident's daily provisioning of the Kraton, including food supplies and twice-daily European wine allocations for Sunan Pakubuwana IV and Crown Prince Sugandi. On 12 August, the Government Salt Monopoly Department in Surabaya proposed a treaty with the Sultan of Yogyakarta, modeled on the Madura convention, offering an annual indemnity of 2,000 Java Rupees and 40 *koyang* of salt in exchange for prohibiting local coastal salt panning along the Indian Ocean shore. On 11 November, mandatory travel pass (*passen*) regulations were enacted for all Europeans, Chinese, and Javanese traveling across the Vorstenlanden (costing 1 Real, or 10 stuivers for merchants). Finally, on 9 March 1818, F.G. Valck contested Surakarta's jurisdiction over the markets (*bazaars*) of Wanagiri in Jabarangkah, citing John Crawfurd's 1812 British Survey of Kedu to demonstrate that Wanagiri had never formed part of the Kasunanan's fiscal leases.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Franciscus Gerhardus Valck [F.G. Valck, Sec. Kedu], John Crawfurd, Sunan Pakubuwana IV [Sunan], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV [Sultan of Jogya], Crown Prince Sugandi / Pakubuwana V [Crown Prince], Agent of the Salt Department (Surabaya).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Solo], Yogyakarta [Jogya], Eastern Manca Nagara (Panaraga, Kediri, Srengat, Blitar, Lodaya), South Coast (*Segara Kidul* / Pacitan, Lorog, Panggul), Surabaya, Madura, Wanagiri [Jabarangkah], Kedhu [Parakan, Kaloran, Jetis].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Eastern *Manca Nagara* [E.M.N.], Government Salt Monopoly, *Koyang* [measure of capacity/weight approx. 1.8 metric tons], internal travel passes (*passen*), *Bazaars* / *Pasars* [markets], *Tollgates* [bandar / bukenæs], Spanish Reals [Sp. R.], *Stuiver*.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698a; continuation of Algemeen Verslag Solo (1817) and Kedu Residency Incoming Dispatches (March 1818).
* **Document Nature:** Territorial surveys, commissariat accounts, commercial treaty drafts, internal passport regulations, and inter-residency jurisdictional disputes.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates colonial efforts to close fiscal loopholes (enforcing the salt monopoly on the south coast, issuing travel passes to control merchant mobility) and litigating disputed border markets along the Kedu-Surakarta frontier using British survey records.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Assistant Resident/Secretary Valck, Resident of Surakarta, Salt Monopoly Commissioners, and royal court monarchs (PB IV, HB IV).
* **Continuation Ledger:** Direct sequence continuing the regional territorial rosters from `IMG_1406.jpg`.
* **Thematic Headings:** Margin tracks territorial and fiscal rubrics (`E. M.V.:`, `S. Sea Shore:`, `Crawfurd Report on Kědu 1812:`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, geographical zones, and treaty proposals on the left; verbatim territorial inventories, wine provisions, salt indemnities, pass tariffs, and Crawfurd market listings on the right.
