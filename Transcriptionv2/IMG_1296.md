# Transcription of IMG_1296.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1296.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, Vol. I, Doc. XLVII conclusion & Doc. XLVIII, pp. 216–217)
- **Date Range:** 1817-12-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion of Assistant Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman's strategic report on Lampung security, pepper/coffee cultivation, gunboat patrols along the Tulang Bawang, Putih, and Sekampung rivers, and diplomacy with Radin Intan; followed by Document XLVIII: comprehensive dispatch of Commissioners-General Elout and Van der Capellen to the Director-General of Colonies reviewing the political tranquility across Java and the Outer Islands; reporting on the abortive mid-1817 millenarian uprising led by a self-styled holy man (identified by Carey as Kyai Murma Wijaya) falsely claiming Pangéran Prangwedana's (Mangkunagara II) backing; swift suppression of the disturbance by Prangwedana and Resident Nahuys; appointment of Rijck van Prehn as Resident at Surakarta following the death of Willem Herman van IJsseldijk; commissioning of a Surakarta prince into the royal Dutch Hussar regiment.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johan Diederik Kruseman, Radin Intan I (Radja Intan), Cornelis Theodorus Elout, G. A. G. P. Baron van der Capellen, Kyai Murma Wijaya (*zoogenaamde heilige*), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Rijck van Prehn, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Lampung (Sumatra), Tulang Bawang, Sekampung / Assahan, Banten, Batavia, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Pepercultuur (pepper cultivation), Kanonneerbooten (gunboats), Mangkunagaran Legion, Kepundhung pesantren, Regiment Husaren (Hussar regiment), Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
