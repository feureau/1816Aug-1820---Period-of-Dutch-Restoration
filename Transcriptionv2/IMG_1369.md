  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Dj. Br. 60* (Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60), residency handover, joint European-Pakualaman guard, currency demonetization (Balinese coins replaced by copper duits), inter-residency criminal sanctuary (*magang* asylum), illegal teak logging (*jati*), royal exile petitions, royal bereavement notice.
