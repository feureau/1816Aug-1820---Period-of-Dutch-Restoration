# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

Ratu Kencana = sends fruit to van IJ., asks him to pass on. Regards to HB II when next see them.

Secadiningrat [^2] Raised by HB III & whole y loans.
cf saves HB III from murder.
Due to favour of HB III.

But apptmt. viewed w/ disgust by Yogyarese.

P.A. asks for that Secadiningrat be exiled from the court.

Kyai Adip. Purwa- / Riksanagara } also write out complaints versus Tan Jin Sing.

annoyance of P.A. that Tan Jin Sing saves HB III from murder during 1811-12.

∴ throne passes not to P.A. But to HB III

Others in court Regard him as the helper of the British.
all except for Sultan HB IV = Remembrance of his father.

Secadiningrat = complete knowledge of court. = Hatred of Regents etc at having to mix with a Chinese.
