# Transcription of IMG_1486.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1486.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Resolutions of the Governor-General in Council, August–September 1819 / Microfilm 4)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-25 to 1819-09-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official vice-regal resolutions and conference proceedings during Governor-General Van der Capellen's grand tour of Central Java: Semarang conference (25 Aug 1819 No. 7) where the Resident of Kedu reports illegal and predatory head tax collections by Chinese tax farmers; Surakarta conference (25 Aug 1819 No. 9 & 10) addressing the complete lack of land registers and ordering statistical registers compiled, while uncovering the illegal persistence of transit tolls along the Bengawan Solo river (especially across Mangkunagaran lands) despite their formal abolition by the British; Van der Capellen orders Yogyakarta Resident Nahuys to execute a comprehensive statistical survey of the Sultan's lands; and residency inquiries (6 Sept 1819 No. 24) requesting official gifts for Ratu Ageng (grandmother of HB IV) and Ratu Mas (queen consort of HB III) that had been omitted during the Governor-General's royal entry.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Resident of Kedu (F.E. Hardy), Resident of Surakarta (Rijck van Prehn / Jhr. A.M.Th. de Salis), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident of Yogyakarta), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton, grandmother of HB IV), Ratu Mas (Ratu Kencana, consort of HB III and mother of HB IV).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Surakarta (Solo), Kedu, Yogyakarta, Bengawan Solo river, Mangkunagaran territories.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Governor-General in Council (*Besluiten van den G.G. in Rade*), Chinese head tax (*hoofdgeld*), Land registers (*Statistieke Registers*), Bengawan Solo river toll abolition, *Mangkunagaran lands*, Royal court gift protocol.
