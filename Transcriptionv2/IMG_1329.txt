* 28 Aug — fixed for van IJ's departure.
  * van Braam / Nahuys informed, of letter sent 25 Aug. 1816. See Bijlage no. 3.
  * leaves on 28 Aug -> for Surakarta
  * & Kruseman = meets old translator Krijgsman at Salatiga.
* 29 Aug — goes onto Sala.
  * meets van Braam & gives him news of his embassy, explanation.

* **p. 4**: To find out what information had been given both openly and secretly by the British govnmt. about the giving back of Java -> to Sultan / Sunan.
  * [**Question of pledges of Brit. Government to Dutch.**]
  * But v. difficult in view of fact that van IJ knows few Regents at Sala & PB IV always v. secretive about affairs of importance.
* **p. 5**: R. Adipati Sasradiningrat —
  * son of R. Adip. Mangkupraja —
  * banned by Sunan =
  * named as Patih by British — without any previous Request being made to PB IV.
  * **See Solo Br. 23 Hope to Raffles, 23 Des. 1812.**
  * Johnson & Crawfurd tell van IJ that because of manner of appointment -> Sasradiningrat not viewed very well by PB IV. [*]
  * But necessary for Dutch govnmt. that Patih kept on in his post.
* **p. 6**: van IJ = idea of using & to get at secrets of PB IV.
  * $\therefore$ engages him in conversation when comes to pay Respects of PB IV.
