# Transcription of IMG_1258.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1258.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djokja Archive 1816 — Letters from Nahuys to Van der Capellen*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-26 to 1816-09-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Nahuys van Burgst's policy proposals for judicial restructuring in Yogyakarta, advocating direct European oversight over property crimes and stripping jurisdiction from the Sultan's rural police courts (*Jaksa Gunung*); bench composition of the proposed Provincial Council (*Provinciale Raad*); arrival of Special Envoy Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (10–13 September 1816) carrying gubernatorial recognition for Pangéran Paku Alam I; violent armed robbery in Jatinom (26–27 August 1816) executed by Demang Sasradiwirya of Padasuwery; Nahuys personally leading a dawn punitive cavalry expedition (16 lancers and 30 troopers) on 25 September to storm the rebel stronghold; diplomatic representations by Ratu Kencana Wulan seeking royal reinstatement for exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Pangéran Paku Alam I, Ratu Kencana Wulan, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Demang Sasradiwirya.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Jatinom, Padasuwery, Boyolali, Kedu, Batavia, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Judicial reform (*Provinciale Raad*), Jaksa Gunung (rural judges), Cavalry expedition, Jatinom robbery, Ratu Kencana Wulan petition, Copper coinage mint.
