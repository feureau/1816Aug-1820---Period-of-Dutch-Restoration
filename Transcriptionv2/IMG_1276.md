# Transcription of IMG_1276.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1276.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, *Djocjo Brieven* 1816: *Procès-Verbal of Kyai Murma Wijaya & Sèh Abubakar*, 18 November 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1792 to 1816-11-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official sworn judicial depositions (*procès-verbal*) taken from Kyai Murma Wijaya and Sèh Abubakar on 18 November 1816; biographical origins of Kyai Murma (born at Desa Mojo under Surakarta; settled in early youth at Desa Kepundhung, the ancestral estate of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I's mother); royal bounty offered in 1792 by Sunan Pakubuwana IV (10,000 Spanish Dollars) to recite four years of intercessory prayers for the spiritual recovery of the sacred Majapahit blade *Kyai Birawa* (remitted 2,000 Sp. D. by Patih Danuningrat); 1809 royal murder-prayer contract commissioned by Crown Prince Radèn Mas Suraja (later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III) offering 10,000 Sp. D. for Murma to pray for the speedy death of Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II); post-1812 financial settlements by HB III (7,000 Sp. D.) and Ratu Ibu (3,000 Sp. D.); solicitations in 1812 by Pangéran Cakrakusuma and the wife of Patih Sasradiningrat II begging Murma to pray for the victory of PB IV's rising against the British.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Murma Wijaya, Sèh Abubakar, Sultan Hamengkubuwana I, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Raja Putra Naréndra), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Pangéran Cakrakusuma, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Desa Mojo, Kepundhung (Kapundhung), Majapahit, Jakarta (ANRI).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djocjo Brieven* 1816, Procès-Verbal, Royal intercessory prayers (*doa penolak/kematian*), Sacred blade *Kyai Birawa*, Parricidal court intrigues, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
