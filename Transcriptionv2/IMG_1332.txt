### IJsseldijk 1816
**3. van IJsseldijk, Baud.**

Kruseman & van IJ talk in company of PB IV
* & CP [Crown Prince] & Buminata sitting some distance away on the floor.

* **p. 12**: To impress on PB IV new sovereign might & power of Netherlands =
  * & King called Back by whole Nation. =

* **p. 13**: Short lecture on history of Holland —
  * 17 provinces Ruled By King of Spain.
  * & Breaking away of 7 provinces.
  * cf great-grandfather of WM I [Willem I] — William Silent wins Independence from Spain.
  * 2 centuries of power of 7 provinces in Indian Ocean.
  * & French period — Bat. Rep. / Rule of Napoleon.

* **p. 14**: Defeat of Nap & Help of England / Austria / Prussia / Russia.
  * marriage of Crown Prince of Holland & d. of Emperor / Ruler of Russia.
  * "great prestige attached to Restoration of Dutch monarchy."
  * cf old Dynasty of Oranje / Nassau.

* **p. 15**: Ruler By grace of God. [Lecture on European History.]
  * PB IV listens to this course & his usual attention.
  * Declares pleasure at change in Dutch fortunes.
