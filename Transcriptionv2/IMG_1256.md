# Transcription of IMG_1256.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1256.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Soerakarta* [AVS] Gewoon 1816 / G. P. Rouffaer Transcriptions from the Surakarta Residency Archive)
- **Date Range:** 1815-06-01 to 1816-11-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Restoration of Dutch colonial authority at Surakarta; official handover of the residency on 14 August 1816 from British Resident Major Jeremiah Johnson to Dutch Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak (raising of the Dutch tricolor); diplomatic arrival of Special Envoy Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (29 August – 8 September 1816); medical history of smallpox vaccination (*koepokinenting*) inaugurated in June 1815 under British surgeon Dr. W. Ainslie; celebration of Garebeg Besar (1 November 1816); administrative staffing reorganizations: appointment of Johannes Wilhelmus Winter as official Javanese translator replacing H. Momoff; official real estate inventory of colonial government properties in Surakarta (Kleca reception villa, Séla retreat on Merapi/Merbabu, Kentungan river house, Karangbolong stone lodge); European Lifeguard company attached to Sunan Pakubuwana IV (55 European troops).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Major Jeremiah Johnson, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Dr. W. Ainslie, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, H. Momoff, Kuijpman, Lippe, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, G. P. Rouffaer, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Sala / Solo), Kleca, Séla (Merapi / Merbabu), Kentungan, Karangbolong.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AVS (*Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Soerakarta*), KITLV H 698a, Smallpox vaccination (*koepokinenting*), Court Translator (*Translateur der Javaansche Taal*), Royal European Lifeguard, Garebeg Besar.
