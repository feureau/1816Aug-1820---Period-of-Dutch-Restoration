# Transcription of IMG_1452.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1452.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Kabinet Missive van den Gouverneur-Generaal, 19 Sept. 1831, No. 1431: Priangan & Cirebon Returns, Dec. 1831)
- **Date Range:** 1831-10-03 to 1831-12-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Retrenchment and proposed revival of the Cianjur priyayi school in the Priangan, alongside an exhaustive statistical and sociological survey of 120 Islamic pesantrens across the Residency of Cirebon. The Priangan report recounts that the Cianjur model school had been abolished in 1827 under the draconian austerity regime of Commissioner-General Leonard du Bus de Gisignies; however, its graduates had become exemplary District Heads (*wedana*) and Islamic deputies (*naib*). The Resident urged its resurrection with two salaried teachers (f. 20–25 monthly) teaching Javanese, Malay, Roman and Arabic script, and traditional historical chronicles (*Babads*) for 30 to 40 pupils over a two-year course. In Cirebon (16 December 1831), the Resident surveyed 120 active *pesantrens* accommodating 2,763 *santri* pupils. The report details the traditional *guru-santri* moral economy: santris paid no monetary tuition, compensating their spiritual master by performing agricultural labor on the guru’s communal fields for part of each day. Pesantren sizes ranged from 2 to 100 pupils based entirely on the charismatic reputation of the guru for piety and mystical wisdom (*ngelmu*). Crucially, the Resident cautioned Van den Bosch that state interference or financial subsidies in Islamic religious schools was politically hazardous, recommending that colonial authorities refrain from meddling in native religious education.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes van den Bosch, Leonard Pierre Joseph burggraaf du Bus de Gisignies, Resident of Priangan, Resident of Cirebon, Islamic Kyais and Gurus of Cirebon, Santri pupils.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Cianjur [Ciancur], Priangan Regencies, Cirebon [Cheribon], Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Du Bus austerity cuts (1827), *Wedana* [District Head], *Naib* [deputy penghulu], *Babad* [dynastic chronicles], *Pesantren* network (120 schools, 2,763 pupils), *Guru-Santri* relationship, agricultural field service, Islamic piety (*ngelmu*), colonial non-intervention.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Kabinet Missive No. 1431 / Kabinet Dossier No. 20265 (Priangan and Cirebon Returns, December 1831).
* **Document Nature:** Administrative evaluation, agrarian sociological analysis of religious labor, and statistical education returns.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the sharp contrast between elite secular schooling in the Priangan and the deeply entrenched autonomous Islamic *pesantren* networks of coastal Cirebon.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Governor-General Van den Bosch, Commissioner-General Du Bus de Gisignies, Resident of Cirebon, and Priangan regents.
