# Transcription of IMG_1318.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1318.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Ministerie van Koloniën [MvK] 4491*, Archival Index & Reading Notes)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-02 to 1816-10-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival guide and folio index of the primary diplomatic dossier preserved in *MvK 4491* documenting Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s special embassy to Surakarta and Yogyakarta in September–October 1816; records Van IJsseldijk and Johan Diederik Kruseman’s 4 September 1816 audience with Sunan Pakubuwana IV, the Crown Prince (later PB V), and Patih Sasradiningrat II; catalogs secret instructions (2 August 1816), credential dispatches, the Sunan's formal responses, Raffles’s earlier mandate to Ratu Kencana regarding the young Sultan HB IV, and translation dockets by J. G. Krijgsman.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Mas Sugandi (Crown Prince / PB V), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih), Johan Diederik Kruseman, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), Thomas Stamford Raffles, J. G. Krijgsman, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo Kraton), Yogyakarta, The Hague (Koloniën Archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Ministerie van Koloniën (MvK 4491), Secret Articles of Embassy (2 Aug. 1816), Commissioners-General (C.G.), VOC Veterans in Colonial Diplomacy, J. G. Krijgsman Translation Dockets.
