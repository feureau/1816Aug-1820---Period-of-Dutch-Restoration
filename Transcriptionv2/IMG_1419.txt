4 Nov 1817:
Questions: Taxation on Toll-gates and Bazaars:
cf 3rd September 1817 - gen. generaal's Memoire

Very CONSIDERABLE DIFFERENCES.
in amount levied at toll-gates: depends entirely on whim of toll-gate keeper.

usually large tolls levied on Roads on which not many toll-gates but used very heavily By peasant traffic during the day.

JAVANESE usually pay MUCH higher toll than Chinese
cf Chinese Special Rates:
cf Bears more heavily on small trader (Javanese)
than Large Businessman (Chinese)

CHINESE must NOT be given any special Privileges above
Javanese LANDHOLDERS =
causes immense DISCONTENT.

despite LOSS of INCOME which would Result from
decline in toll rates: BUT would be balanced
By greater peace in the country side =

Way of TOLL-GATE PAYMENTS = should Be Regulated:
NOT enough to Fix Rates = But also Declining scales
Rates if traveller / trader has passed through or
will pass through other gates...

JAVANESE = CANNOT usually Read:
\therefore therefore very easily ROBBED wrongly at toll-gates
(must Be explained to them By Javanese.)

GRADUAL increase in INLAND TRAFFIC & GOODS:
\therefore Toll-gate Revenues would increase normally
with increasing Trade.
