# Transcription of IMG_1478.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1478.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 131, *Translaten en Verbalen 1816–1819* / Notarial Archive J.W. Winter)
- **Date Range:** 1817-02-01 to 1819-08-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Three remarkable records documenting mystical syncretism, dynastic death portents, and administrative corruption: the esoteric millennial prophecy of the *Luguh* of Banyumas/Ajibarang regarding Governor-General Van der Capellen's 1818 state tour, declaring Nyai Rara Kidul to be the Governor-General's consort, proclaiming a descendant of Sunan Kuning as the *Ratu Adil*, and claiming the holy cannon *Nyai Setomi*; royal court report to Sunan Pakubuwana IV on 27 August 1819 describing the ominous shriek of the supernatural spirit bird *Malar Woongo* above the Cirebon royal tombs at Jimatan (forewarning the Sunan's imminent death); and Captain Godfrey Phipps Baker's 1817 investigative report into Demang Puri's abuses at the Karangbolong birds' nest cliffs (neglecting ritual offerings to the Bed of Nyai Rara Kidul, causing harvest failure, and adulterating the workers' opium rations).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Luguh of Banyumas / Ajibarang, Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Nyai Rara Kidul (Goddess of the Southern Ocean), Adipati Broes (descendant of Sunan Kuning), Raden Panji Naradilaga, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Mas Mangkuwijaya (jurukunci of Jimatan), Captain Godfrey Phipps Baker, Demang Puri of Karangbolong, Demang Roanna, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (translator).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Banyumas, Ajibarang, Surakarta (Solo), Jimatan (Imogiri royal graveyard), Desa Geni Loyo, Cirebon, Karangbolong cliffs.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Luguh* (mystic seer), *Ratu Adil* (Just King), *Sunan Kuning* (Raden Mas Garendi), *Nyai Setomi* (sacred court cannon), *Malar Woongo* (death-omen spirit bird), *Dulkangidah*, Karangbolong birds' nests, *sajèn* (ritual food and incense offerings), Bed of Nyai Rara Kidul (*Katil Ratu Kidul*), opium adulteration.
