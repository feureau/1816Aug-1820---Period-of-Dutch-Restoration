# Transcription of IMG_1374.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1374.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Port. 5, No. 11: Nahuys van Burgst Collection / Djogja Correspondence General, Jan 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-14 to 1817-01-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Administrative, judicial, and financial governance in Yogyakarta following the suppression of the Umar Mahdi uprising. The dispatch reflects upon the extreme difficulty of subduing rural rebels (*kraman*) once they consolidate a following, citing the cautionary precedent of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III during Daendels' administration (1810), while noting the Bagelèn police action cost only 50 Spanish Dollars. Provisional Assistant Resident Kruseman strongly urges the Governor-General to exercise statutory powers to exile Kyai Murma Wijaya to the Moluccas, proposing to confiscate and auction his estate, arms, and merchandise at desa Kepundhung to Europeans and Chinese to benefit the depleted Yogyakarta treasury, while remitting an annuity for Murma's maintenance in exile. The report highlights the destitute state of the Yogyakarta residency police (25 Indo-Europeans and Javanese lacking uniforms since May 1815 and owning only two sabres, forced to borrow arms from Paku Alam I's corps), the ceremonial arrival of Pangéran Dipakusuma and the eastern regents for the Garebeg Mulud, an emergency 50,000 Guilder loan from Semarang to meet the Sultan's semi-annual treaty allowance, and Major Nahuys van Burgst's resumption of the residency.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johan Diederik Kruseman [Asst. Res.], Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Kyai Murma Wijaya [Kijai Moormo], Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [Raden Ronggo], Pangéran Dipakusuma [Dipa Kusuma], Pangéran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I [Pakoealam], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV [HB IV], C.F. Krijgsman [Asst. Res. / Translator], Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djogja], Desa Kepundhung [Kapoendoeng], Moluccas [Moluccan Islands / Ambon], Manca Nagara [Eastern Districts], Semarang, Batavia, Bagelèn.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Kraman* [rebels/bandits], *Politiedienaars* [police guards], *Lijfwacht* [residency bodyguard], sabres, Garebeg Mulud, royal treaty subsidy, treasury deficit, public auction, Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.], Java Rupees [J.R.].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 11 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection).
* **Document Nature:** Archival notes, dispatch abstracts, and intelligence memoranda detailing the residency administration of Yogyakarta under Provisional Assistant Resident J.D. Kruseman and Resident H.G. Nahuys van Burgst.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illuminates the interlocking crises facing the restored Dutch administration in early 1817: religious subversion (Kyai Murma), rural insurrection (*kraman*), crippling fiscal shortages, and institutional neglect in municipal policing.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Colonial civil administrators (Kruseman, Nahuys, Krijgsman), royal princely leaders (Dipakusuma, Paku Alam I), and Islamic religious figures (Kyai Murma).
* **Continuation Sheet:** Seamless continuation of the January 1817 archival notes from `IMG_1373.jpg`.
* **Top Marginalia:** Strategic reflections on insurgency policing and financial costs opening the top of the sheet.
* **Chronological Entries:** Structured by individual date markers (`19 Jan. 1817:`, `17 JAN 1817:`, `17 Jan.:`, `21 Jan.:`, `24 Jan.:`) organizing administrative matters into distinct thematic entries.
