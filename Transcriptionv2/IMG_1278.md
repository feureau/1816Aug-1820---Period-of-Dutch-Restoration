# Transcription of IMG_1278.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1278.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, *Djocjo Brieven* 1816: *Procès-Verbal of Abdullah & Interrogation of Sèh Abubakar*, 13 November 1816; with citations from G. P. Rouffaer and P. H. van der Kemp)
- **Date Range:** 1804-04-26 to 1816-11-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Judicial depositions of Murma's brother Abdullah and Palembang Arab merchant Sèh Abubakar conducted before Assistant Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak; Abdullah's testimony detailing Murma's money-collection couriers dispatched to Yogyakarta (Ki Suryanata for 100 Spanish Dollars); pre-1812 visits by Prince Malayakusuma and relations with former Surakarta Patih Danuningrat; revelation that Surakarta palace mantris regularly visited Kapundhung to pawn royal heirloom daggers (*kris*), pikes (*waos*), and firearms (*rifles*); dispatch of a major Dutch pursuit column (120–150 men); seven-point interrogation of Sèh Abubakar (39-year-old merchant from Palembang), confirming his intimate familiarity with Patih Sasradiningrat II, his three extended stays at Kapundhung (1810–1812), and Murma's counter-allegation that Abubakar commissioned prayers for victory in a holy war against Europeans.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Murma Wijaya, Abdullah, Sèh Abubakar, Ki Suryanata, Prince Malayakusuma, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, G. P. Rouffaer, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Kepundhung (Kapundhung), Palembang, Jakarta (ANRI).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djocjo Brieven* 1816, Interrogation of Sèh Abubakar, Weapons pawning (*kris, pikes, rifles*), Pursuit detachment, Holy war prayers (*war against Europeans*), Opium monopoly, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
