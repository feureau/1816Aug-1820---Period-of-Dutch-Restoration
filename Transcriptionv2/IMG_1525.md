# Transcription of IMG_1525.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1525.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Yogyakarta Residency Letters, Bundel PJ. Br. 63, Oct.–Dec. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-28 to 1820-12-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official rejection of Lim Nom Tja (son-in-law of Tan Jin Sing / Radèn Tumenggung Sécaningrat) for the post of Luitenant der Chinezen in Yogyakarta via colonial resolution; detailed demographic and agrarian census of the Pacitan coffee frontier submitted by Resident Assistant J.C. Enger to Acting Resident d'Abo, enumerating 3,757 peasant households (1,305 completely landless possessing only residential compounds [*pekarangan*] and 2,452 owning sawah), alongside a total stock of 341,174 coffee trees; sluggish expansion in 1820 (only 56,000 new coffee seedlings planted) caused by intense peasant resistance to forced colonial plantation labor, with cultivators preferring household hedge cultivation (*pagêr coffee*); overland expedition by pensioned Bupati Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya and 80 laborers (*batur*) to Goddong and Keduwang to procure live pepper cuttings; delays in south coast edible birds' nest collection due to monsoon torrential downpours; notification from Sunan Pakubuwana IV to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV on the demise of royal prince Pangéran Demang in Yogyakarta; urgent administrative plea by Enger to create an indigenous official hierarchy to direct Pacitan cash-crop production, recommending Jagakarya's unsalaried eldest son Nurradiwiria for appointment as Demang over the coffee and pepper tracts.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Sécaningrat / Kapitan Cina), Lim Nom Tja, J.C. Enger, G.A. d'Abo, Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya, Nurradiwiria, Pangéran Demang, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Pacitan, Keduwang, Goddong, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal* (BGG), Luitenant der Chinezen, coffee cultivation (*pagêr coffee*), pepper planting, edible birds' nests (*sarang burung*), *pekarangan* (house compound), *sawah* (irrigated rice field), *batur* (estate laborer / coolie), *bekel* (village tax-collector), *demang* (provincial superintendent).
