# Transcription of IMG_1471.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1471.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 131 / ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63A)
- **Date Range:** 1819-02-01 to 1819-03-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Contemporary Dutch translation and transcription of the celebrated prophetic and millenarian circular written in Jawi (Pegon / Arabic characters) by the wandering mystic Kyai Iman Sampurna of Lodhaya: foretelling that in the forthcoming Javanese year *Alip* (1820) a catastrophic cosmic pestilence (*Phemit Trak Nyoro*) will engulf Java; an army of demonic spirits appearing from the West in the shape of predatory insects, reptiles, and beasts (scorpions, millipedes, snakes, tigers); while from the East emerge the spirit hosts of Nyai Rara Kidul (Goddess of the Southern Ocean) and Sunan Lawu (Sovereign Spirit of Mount Lawu) accompanied by infernal legions with pikes and firearms to slaughter the arrogant, the corrupt, and gamblers in a "sea of blood"; laying down mandatory Islamic purifications, communal *slametans*, ritual foods, 44 nightly recitations of the *Syahadat*, and esoteric Sufi doctrines drawn from the *Kitab Munarat*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Iman Sampurna, Prophet Muhammad, Ratu Agama (Spiritual Sovereign / Just King), Sunan Lawu (Mountain Guardian Spirit), Nyai Rara Kidul (Queen of the Southern Ocean).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Lodhaya (Blitar / Kediri), Mount Lawu, Southern Ocean, Surakarta, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Jawi / Pegon script, Year Alip (Javanese cyclical calendar), *Phemit Trak Nyoro* (fatal epidemic / spiritual plague), *Neraka* (Hell), *Zikr* (mystical chanting), *Slametan* (communal ritual feast), *Jangan asem*, *Bayêm* (spinach), *Kitab Munarat*, Confession of Faith (*Syahadat*), Tree of Life (*Pohon Hayat*), *Kafir*.

(Mênir prayer to be used = Doorgol Jurah Blai / Kukanan / slamit soup)<br>(To guard against Earthly Dangers + assure good fortune) Each plate to be covered + yellow serviette [napkin] + each person present to utter confession of faith [Syahadat]. |

The form of the Prophet Muhammad will appear to all those whose hearts are sincere, and are pure in heart (*opregt van gemoed, en zuiver van harte zijn*) and those will also enjoy worldly and Heavenly Blessings.<br>I believe that all the miraculous signs who may manifest themselves are from Almighty God - the true and everlasting, wherefore each & every inhabitants of Java, both men + women are warned by this and enjoined to believe firmly what they have learned from the Holy Script.<br>Contents of Kitab Munarat as follows: I try to get the protection of the Almighty who is all present - who hears all and sees all - so that the evil Devils may not seek me out and will not do anything to me except let me live in peace // Those who pray to others but the Almighty will be damned as great sinners/ and for that reason the descendants of the kafir, will never reach Heaven. O great God, forgive all the sins I have committed and let me be purified by your influence. And what in you is pure love, please show that and impart that love to me. - The person who stands fast in his Religion is like the following —"<br>*(See over. Yogya Brieven 1820 Dj. Br. 63 A)* |
