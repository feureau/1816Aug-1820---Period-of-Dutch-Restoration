# Transcription of IMG_1291.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1291.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, Vol. I, Doc. XXVI & XXVIII, pp. 96–97)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion of A. M. T. de Salis's administrative dispatch on the fiscal boundary enclaves between Pekalongan and Kedu (Tersono and Kalisalak) and the burden of tollgates; followed by Document XXVIII: Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's official report to the Commissioners-General detailing his solemn public reception at the Kraton of Yogyakarta on 13 September 1816; diplomatic escort led by Pangéran Paku Alam I; formal meeting with the youthful Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV in the Traju Mas / Pendapa; intimate Kraton audience with the Sultan's grandmother (Ratu Ageng / Ratu Ibu), mother (Ratu Kencana), and official consort.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Adriaan Mauritz Theodorus Baron de Salis, Caspers, Valck, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I / Mangkubumi), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Kedhaton), Ratu Kencana (mother of HB IV).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pekalongan, Kedu (Kadoe), Tersono, Kalisalak, Yogyakarta (Djocjocarta Kraton), Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Tolpoorten (tollgates), Vorstenlanden enclaves, Groote Mendopo (Grand Pendapa), Eerste Resident (First Resident, 1786), Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
