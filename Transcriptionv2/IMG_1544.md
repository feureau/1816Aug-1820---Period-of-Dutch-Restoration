# Transcription of IMG_1544.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1544.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Surakarta & Yogyakarta Residency Records / Bundel H 698b, AvS, 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-22 to 1820-05-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Cross-border jurisdictional clash between the principalities: Assistant Resident of Surakarta forwards an intercepted letter from Radèn Ngabèhi Suradirja (Yogyakarta subject) to his younger brother Wangsadiwirya at Kibak in Sukawati Lor, translated by Carel Frederik Winter Jr.; Suradirja strips Wangsadiwirya of his rank as *Mantri Panèwu* and confiscates his 50 households (*cacah*) for treasonously obeying Surakarta commands; Suradirja orders physical beatings (*give blows*) inflicted upon Demang Sécawijaya of Karang Juni, who had attempted to deliver orders to the village of Mardsa on behalf of Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II and a European leaseholder, with Suradirja claiming the village was an inalienable ancestral grant bestowed by Sultan Hamengkubuwana III; financial inquiry from the Inspector of Finances in Semarang to Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst regarding the proposed abolition of the tobacco customs weighing station (*Gêdong Tembakan* / *Gedhong Tembakau*) at Sidayu and its impact on Vorstenlanden tobacco exports, noting border customs transit rates of 11 guilders per *kodi* (20 packs); vice-regal resolution (*Besluit van den G.G.*, 21 April 1820 No. 12) granting an annual subsidy of 4,000 guilders (f. 200 per dragoon biennially) to Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I) for the maintenance of his royal cavalry detachment (*Dragoons*); vice-regal resolution (13 May 1820 No. 32) acknowledging dispatches from the Commissioner on the Coast of Borneo regarding Nahuys' diplomatic mission.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Ngabèhi Suradirja, Wangsadiwirya, Carel Frederik Winter Jr., Demang Sécawijaya, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Mas Rangga Kertawirya, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Kibak, Sukawati Lor, Karang Juni, Desa Mardsa, Semarang, Sidayu, Sibebek, Pekalongan, Borneo (Kalimantan).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Enclave boundary disputes, *Mantri Panèwu*, *cacah* (households), ancestral royal grants, *Gêdong Tembakan* / *Gedhong Tembakau* (tobacco customs house), *kodi* (pack measure = 20 bundles), Pakualaman Cavalry Dragoons, military subsidies, Borneo Commissioner.
