# Transcription of IMG_1482.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1482.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A, *Nov. 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-11-13 to 1819-11-17
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Deeper forensic interrogations into the East Java millenarian conspiracy, military school uniforms, and inter-island textile logistics: police depositions of Mas Arja Wijaya and 25 witnesses revealing an underground movement to install a new Javanese King in Malang (*Bedirikan Raja*), wage holy war against Pasuruan, Bangil, and Surabaya, and mobilize armed conventicles in the messianic Ketangga Forest (Madiun) and Rawa under mandates from the *Jendral Diatas Angin* (General Above the Winds in Ottoman *Tanah Rum*); passive resistance of Kediri bupatis who refused to assist Chinese tax farmers; Semarang Military School director Lt.-Col. H. Cornelius designing cadet field uniforms from blue Yogyakarta *ginggang*; and Semarang Resident De Bruijn ordering 400 *corges* of Yogyakarta cotton textiles (striped sarongs and checked ginggang) for export to the Moluccas.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident of Pasuruan, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Mas Arja Wijaya, Kyai Nidu Aku, Mas Mur Jelus, Jendral Diatas Angin (mystical Ottoman general), Lieutenant-Colonel H. Cornelius (Director of Semarang Military School), J. de Bruijn (Resident of Semarang).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pasuruan, Malang (Gondanglegi, Pakenjêran), Gresik (Bengawan Nêro), Desa Blawi, Rawa (Tulungagung), Ketangga Forest (Madiun), Bangil, Surabaya, Tanah Rum (Ottoman Empire), Kediri, Semarang, Yogyakarta, Moluccas.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sufi teachings (*Panatagama*, *Slamat Bener*, *Kawin Batin*, *Dikir Sir*), *Bedirikan Raja* (enthroning a righteous king), *nprang ing jurang* (guerrilla holy war), Ketangga Forest messianism, *Jendral Diatas Angin*, Chinese tollgate farmer (*bandar*), Military cadet uniform, *ginggang*, *corge* (score of 20 pieces), Moluccan export trade.
