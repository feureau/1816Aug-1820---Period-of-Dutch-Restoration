# Transcription of IMG_1467.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1467.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 51A, *Afgaande Stukken 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-09 to 1819-11-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst's administrative correspondence: chronic crisis in appointing a qualified Javanese translator following the departure of C.F. Krijgsman (Lt. Paulus deems himself unqualified); elaborate royal reception itinerary for Governor-General G.A.G.P. van der Capellen and his high-ranking civil and military suite in Yogyakarta (met at Bantulan by Sultan HB IV on 22 August 1819); structural composition of the high indigenous tribunal (*Javansche Geregtshof*) comprising 4 inner and 4 outer bupatis chaired by the Patih, with the Pengulu and Head Jaksa as ex-officio members and residency appellate review; celebration of Garèbèg Besar; and detailed physical operations and security protocols governing the Karangbolong edible birds' nest caves (joint Surakarta-Yogya bekels, bamboo barrier gates, sorting in the Residency attic under the Resident's personal key, state trade monopoly).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), C.F. Krijgsman (former translator), Lieutenant Paulus, Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Baroness van der Capellen, Algemeene Secretaris Jean Chrétien Baud, Major Baron R. van der Capellen, Colonel Van Schelle (Chief of Staff), Lieutenant-Colonel Bouvier, Lieutenant Baron van Amerongen, Secretaris P. le Clercq, Hendrik Jacob Domis, Major Frans David Cochius, Matthijs Waterloo, Le Roux, Frans Gerardus Valck, Captain Roest, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (Patih), Deleuvaux.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Semarang, Bantulan, Batavia, Surakarta (Solo), Karangbolong birds' nest cliffs (South Coast).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Inlandsche Tolk* (vernacular translator), Governor-General's Grand Tour of Java, *Javansche Geregtshof* (Indigenous Court of Justice), Inner and Outer Bupatis (*Nayaka Lebet* & *Jawi*), Patih, Pengulu, Head Jaksa (*Hoofd-Djaksa*), Garèbèg Besar, Edible birds' nests (*sarang burung*), state trade monopoly, *pakhuizen* (warehouses), *kranjang* (bamboo packing baskets).
