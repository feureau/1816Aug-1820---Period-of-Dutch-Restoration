# Transcription of IMG_1501.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1501.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Primary Manuscript Record (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 139 / ANRI Solo 139) (Archetype 1: Primary Vernacular Manuscript (Jawi / Pegon Arabic-Script Javanese Prophetic Proclamation))
- **Date Range:** 1819-02-01 to 1819-02-21
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Complete diplomatic transcription of the rare, contemporary 1819 vernacular manuscript circular in Jawi / Pegon script (Arabic characters adapted for Javanese) circulated by the itinerant mystic Kyai Iman Sampurna of Lodhaya: invoking the divine names (*Bismillah*, *Shahadah*), warning that in the approaching year *Alip* (1820) the fatal pestilence *Phemit Trak Nyoro* will ravage Java, invoking the wrath of Sunan Lawu, the spirit armies of Nyai Rara Kidul, and infernal demons armed with pikes to purge corrupt gamblers and the arrogant, citing protective Qur'anic verses (*Surah Al-Mu'minun* 23:117–118), and laying down mandatory Islamic prayers, communal *slametans*, and mystical recitations of the *Kitab Munarat*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Iman Sampurna, Prophet Muhammad (Rasulullah), Sunan Lawu, Nyai Rara Kidul, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Lodhaya (Blitar / Kediri), Mount Lawu, Southern Ocean (*Segara Kidul*), Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Jawi / Pegon script, Year Alip (Javanese cyclical windu calendar), *Phemit Trak Nyoro* (fatal cosmic plague), *Kitab Munarat*, Qur'anic citations (*Surah Al-Mu'minun*), *Slametan*, Confession of Faith (*Syahadat*), *Kafir*.
