## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1483.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A & Kedu Residency Records, Nov. 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-11-24 to 1819-11-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse political, administrative, military, and agrarian records: biographical dossier of Pacitan Opzichter C.F. Engel; Governor-General's resolution (*BGG 24 Nov 1819 No. 13*) downscaling the Sunan's European cavalry bodyguard from 56 to 40 dragoons under 2nd Lieutenant D. Portier; forensic autobiographical deposition of Raden Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Danukrama, Bupati of Magelang) detailing his service under Danureja II, imprisonment during the 1812 British storming of the Kraton, and installation as first British Regent of Kedu; criminal inquest in Pacitan where birds' nest supervisor Kramayuda was placed in stocks for smuggling swiftlet nests to Ponorogo; and official 1818–1819 agricultural census of Pacitan revealing 3,757 peasant households and an exponential tenfold surge in cultivated pepper bushes (from 10,486 to 116,935 vines).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), 2nd Lieutenant D. Portier, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Sayyid Alwi / Kyai Ngabèhi Danukrama, Bupati of Magelang), F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, John Crawfurd, Radèn Adipati Danureja III, Wangsawijaya, Kramayuda, Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Brunswick (Germany), Pacitan, Magelang, Bojong (Kedu), Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Kêdhuwang, Gaddang, Rongkob birds' nest caves (Gunung Kidul), Ponorogo.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** European Bodyguard (*Lijfwacht*), Dragoons, 1812 Fall of Yogyakarta Kraton (*Bedhahing Ngayogyakarta*), British Regency of Kedu, Rongkob birds' nest monopoly, wooden stocks (*balok* / *pasung*), pepper cultivation census (*cacah*).
