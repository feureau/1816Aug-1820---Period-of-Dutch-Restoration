Van den Bosch:
Circular letter to Hoofd Baljuw Batavia + all Residents Assistant-Residents in Java.
19 Sept. 1831, Tasik Malaya.

Art. 97 of Reglement on conduct of government (Beleid van het Regeering)
Indies govrmt. to see that Schools in Java for primary Education.
1) Asks for Information on Native Schools in Residency.
2) Ways in which Schools can be improved + facilities Increased.
according to recommendations of Bijbel / Zendeling Genootschap.

Extr. 31 Dec. 1832 no. 20265:
Answers to kab. letter = 19 Sept. 1831 no. 1431

Van der Vinne (Hoofd Baljuw Batavia) to van den Bosch, 15 Nov. 1831:
Great numbers of Schools in Batavia area:
mainly teaching Recitation of Qur'ān + writing + Arabic letters
Jav. priests =
paid by parents of children for Education
some children of poor parents given " free.
No supervision of priests in pesantrèns.
pupils only able to recite Arabic words they do not understand
govhmt. should not involve itself + such schools.
populace v. attached to them.
No schools of Higher Learning to teach Rudiments of writing + Latin script / arithmetic.
govrmt. should encourage such schools = free of Religious training.
Teachers should not be appointed by Bijbel / Zendeling Genootschap.

Res. of Preanger Regencies to van den Bosch, 3 Oct. 1831:
No statistics given.
During period of G.G. Van der Capellen
govrmt. school set up at Ciancur.
under superintendence of Resident / Eur. Commissioner.
20-30 children of Jav. chiefs + Radens
taught to write/read Malay language in Latin / Jav. / Arabic characters
+ Arithmetic taught by Jav. Masters.
