[red ink: (Doc. A.9) / 1817 Yogya]

\therefore Idea that:
.1. Toll-gate system should be left largely as it is - But no greater sums to be extracted from the Javanese than from the Chinese.
.2. Toll-gate Rates to be POSTED in Javanese, Malay, Chinese of all toll-gates Rates in Djogja / Solo territory.
.3. Toll-gates Rent from 3 -> 2 years.
