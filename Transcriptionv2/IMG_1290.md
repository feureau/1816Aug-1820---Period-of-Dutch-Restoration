# Transcription of IMG_1290.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1290.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Citations from M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1816*, 's-Gravenhage: Martinus Nijhoff, 1891, pp. 82–83, Document XXVI: *W. H. van IJsseldijk aan Commissarissen-Generaal*, Surakarta, 8 September 1816 [Continuation from IMG_1289])
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-07 to 1816-09-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation and conclusion of W. H. van IJsseldijk's verbatim dispatch (pp. 82–83): Sunan Pakubuwana IV summons the Crown Prince (later PB V) to corroborate his denial despite Resident Pinket van Haak's report that the Crown Prince had shown knowledge of external British talk; Van IJsseldijk delivers an extensive explanation of European geopolitics, the Congress of Vienna's European balance of power (*evenwigt*), King William I's resolve to defend Java, and defensive preparations; PB IV expresses naive reassurance (*"Dit is goed, en die verzekering stelt mij zeer gerust"*); Rijksbestuurder Sasradiningrat II confidentially reports PB IV's joy at seeing the Dutch—whom he calls his "fathers"—restored; and Footnote 1 documents the 25 September farewell audience securing royal fidelity.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince (later Sunan Pakubuwana V), Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, King William I, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, M. L. van Deventer.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Soerakarta / Solo), Java, Europa, Weenen (Vienna), Nederland.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Van Deventer Document XXVI, Congress of Vienna (*Congres van Weenen*), European Balance of Power (*evenwigt*), Rijksbestuurder (Patih), Farewell Audience (25 Sept. 1816).
