# Transcription of IMG_1237.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1237.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: *Nahuys van Burgst Collection*, Portfolio 5, No. 2 / ARA / P.H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816*, 1911, pp. 248–249)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-07 to 1816-08-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst's arrival and formal takeover of the Yogyakarta Residency from British Resident John Crawfurd; Nahuys lands at Semarang on 7 August and arrives in Yogyakarta on 9 August 1816; Crawfurd hands over residency administration in profound peace, with Nahuys praising British governance and pledging policy continuity; Crawfurd departs on 10 August to superintend British troop embarkations in East and Central Java, taking leave of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV who presented Crawfurd as a beloved *"younger brother"* (*adhi*); Dutch colonial administration formally assumes oversight of the 200-man Pakualaman Corps (horses and salaries funded by Prince Paku Alam I, weapons supplied by Government); formation of a mounted constabulary force of 25 men (Dutch, German, Ambonese veterans equipped with 22nd Dragoon weaponry); placement of a 25-man palace guard (*life watch*) inside the Kraton receiving 15 to 120 Java Rupees monthly; inspection of the British-founded venereal disease hospital treating 56 Javanese patients; Pangéran Dipakusuma dispatched as royal ambassador to Batavia; reaffirmation of the annual 100,000 Spanish Dollar toll-gate indemnity under Article 4 of the 1812 Treaty; formal raising of the Dutch flag over Yogyakarta on the evening of 14 August 1816 following the arrival of Netherlands troops.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, John Crawfurd, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Pangéran Dipakusuma, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, P.H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djocjocarta), Semarang, Batavia, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Leiden BPL 616, Handover of 9 August 1816, Pakualaman Corps, 22nd Light Dragoons, Kraton Life Watch, Treaty of 1812 (Article 4: 100,000 Sp.D. toll farm indemnity), Venereal disease clinic, Raising of Dutch tricolor.
