# Transcription of IMG_1423.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1423.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 61: January–June 1818)
- **Date Range:** 1817-08-15 to 1818-05-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official verdicts and exile orders for Kyai Murma Wijaya and Raden Mas Umar Mahdi, inquiries into European highland colonization and commercial sugar cultivation, liquidation and maintenance provisions for Murma's family, emergency copper currency minting in Surabaya, counterfeit currency alerts, court festival expenses, and annual revenue totals. The document records the conclusive colonial assessment of Kyai Murma: by wielding eschatological predictions and supernatural threats, he had acquired dangerous political influence across the Yogyakarta and Surakarta Kratons, amassing money for arms and jewelry, necessitating permanent banishment from Java. Millenarian rebel Raden Mas Umar Mahdi, condemned to death for the 1817 Bagelèn uprising, had his capital sentence commuted on grounds of insanity. Both prisoners were sentenced to lifelong exile in Ambon, to be transferred immediately via Semarang, with Murma's seized goods returned and his estate distributed among relatives to provide him an annuity. On 15 January 1818, Inspector Van Lawick van Pabst launched an island-wide survey seeking temperate highland zones suited for European colonization and commercial sugar planting that would not conflict with the Land Rent (*Landhuur*) system (citing Besluit Commissarissen-Generaal of 5 Dec. 1817 no. 22). On 20 April 1818, a proclamation signed by R. Dozy authorized the minting of uniform copper *duiten* and double *duiten* in Surabaya to alleviate chronic specie shortages, while Baron von Lippe in Surakarta reported dangerous counterfeit silver Rupiahs, Spanish Dollars, and ducatoons. The document concludes with residency financial audits, including f. 820 reimbursed for the 1817 Garèbèg Puwasa, and annual 1817 tax returns: tollgates yielding f. 140,261, opium farms f. 132,000, edible birds' nests f. 80,740 to f. 96,000, and market bazaars f. 7,536.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Murma Wijaya, Raden Mas Umar Mahdi, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, D.W. van Lawick van Pabst, Hendrik Ferdinand Baron von Lippe, R. Dozy, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djogja], Surakarta [Sala], Bagelèn, Ambon [Moluccas], Semarang, Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Banishment / exile to Ambon, *Ratu Adil* millenarianism, insanity sentence commutation, European highland colonization, sugar culture (*suikercultuur*), copper *duiten* minting, counterfeit currency (*valse munten*), *Garèbèg Puwasa*, *Bandar* [tollgate farm], *Opiumpacht*, Edible birds' nests [Vogel閥stjes], *Pasar* revenue.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 61 (Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven van de Residentie Jogjakarta, 1818).
* **Document Nature:** Executive penal sentences, circular dispatches, monetary decrees, and annual fiscal balance sheets.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the liquidation of the 1816–1817 religious opposition through distant overseas deportation, alongside major state interventions in agrarian colonization, monetary stabilization, and tax monopoly audits.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, Governor-General Van der Capellen, Inspector-General Van Lawick van Pabst, Solo Resident Von Lippe, and Secretary of State.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries detail political intelligence profiles and penal judgments against Kyai Murma and Umar Mahdi.
* **Marginal Annotations:** Red ink margin notes highlighting exile transports and currency decrees (`[red ink margin: Fr. ship.]`, `[red ink: Vrijlat?]`, `[red ink: copper coins]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, decree numbers, and financial categories on the left; verbatim summaries of criminal sentencing, overseas banishment, highland colonization queries, copper minting specifications, and fiscal yields on the right.
