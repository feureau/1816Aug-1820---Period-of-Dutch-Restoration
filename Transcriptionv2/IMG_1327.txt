Surakarta: Letters of van IJ from 8 Sept. 1816 / Yogya: 14 Sept. 1816.
Letter of van IJ from Sala, 4 Sept. 1816.
*(For Yogya, see van Deventer, XXVIII, p. 97 = p. 80. In MvK. 4491, pp. 70-77, pp. 78-85. get a xerox)*

* **2 Sept.**: van IJ received v full honours at Sala Kraton.
  * Crown Prince / Buminata & all brothers / other princes come out to greet van IJ.
  * Sits on left in Sunan's coach. Ride to Sala court.
  * Meeting v PB IV at srimënganti, v Res. Col. van Wenne, Prang Wêdana.
  * Already knows PB IV for 32 years as Crown Prince.
  * 18 years ago van IJ well received by PB IV before he went as Comm. to Surabaya.
  * PB IV answers in a friendly fashion.
  * Leads van IJ by left hand to Kraton -> great pèndhapa in kraton. Prepares stool for van IJ covered v yellow damask.
  * 3 gun salute for van IJ.
  * Tells PB IV of letter from C.G. Read by Krijgsman in Javanese.
  * & letter from John Fendall read out aloud by Adjutant.
  * PB IV to come on Tuesday to house of Resident.
  * Drink some toasts / Led into private apartments by Ratu Kencana.
  * Received in a v. friendly fashion by her.
  * PB IV accompanies van IJ to gate of srimënganti.
  * Accompanied by Crown Prince & other princes to Residency.

* **2 o'clock PB IV to make a state visit to Residency.**
* **3 Sept.**: PB IV comes to Residency gardens & whole court.
  * PB IV sits on throne prepared for him.
