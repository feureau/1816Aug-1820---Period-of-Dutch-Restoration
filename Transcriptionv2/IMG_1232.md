# Transcription of IMG_1232.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1232.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Jogja Letters' No. 44: *Extracts uit het Register der Besluiten van Commissarissen-Generaal*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-19 to 1816-09-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official administrative resolutions of the Commissioners-General regulating the restoration of Dutch governance in Yogyakarta; Willem Herman van IJsseldijk arrives as Ambassador Extraordinary to reaffirm Dutch sovereignty and present King William I's letter to the rulers; permission granted for Ratu Kencana Wulan and three Radèn Ayus (Murtiningrat, Jayaningrat, and Dewaningrat) to travel to Batavia to join the exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh); Prince Paku Alam I is granted an allocation of over 400 silver Java Rupees from the Waterloo Fund; outgoing British Resident John Crawfurd directs payment of 50,000 Spanish Dollars to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV representing accumulated rents and tollgate arrears; tax collection dispute arises in Klaten over the unauthorized re-erection of a market tollgate; J.G. Diétrée, originally appointed court Javanese interpreter by Daendels in 1808 and who served under British official J.W. Moorrees, petitions for formal reinstatement as official residency translator.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Ratu Kencana Wulan, Radèn Ayu Murtiningrat, Radèn Ayu Jayaningrat, Radèn Ayu Dewaningrat, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), John Crawfurd, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, J.G. Diétrée, Herman Willem Daendels, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees (Morris).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Batavia, Klaten, Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Jogja Letters (No. 44), Besluiten Commissarissen-Generaal, Royal allowances, Waterloo Fund, Spanish Dollars (`Sp. D.`), Java Rupees (`J.R.`), Residency Translator (*Translateur*), Market tollgate fees (*bazaar gelden*).
