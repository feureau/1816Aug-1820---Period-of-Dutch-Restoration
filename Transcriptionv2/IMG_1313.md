# Transcription of IMG_1313.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1313.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI, *Solo Brieven* / Diplomatic Address: Van IJsseldijk to PB IV, Leaf 1)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Opening leaf of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's solemn bilingual diplomatic address delivered to Sunan Pakubuwana IV of Surakarta on 2 September 1816; formally proclaims the restoration of Dutch sovereignty across the East Indies in the name of King William I, Prince of Orange-Nassau and Grand Duke of Luxembourg; officially presents the credential dispatches (*Missive*) entrusted to him by Commissioners-General Elout, Van der Capellen, and Buyskes; accompanied by parallel contemporary Javanese translation in *Aksara Jawa*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), King William I of the Netherlands, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Arnold Adriaan Buyskes, J. G. Krijgsman.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Kraton of Surakarta), Netherlands (Vaderland).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Buitengewoon Gezant (Extraordinary Envoy), Commissarissen-Generaal (C.C. G.G.), *Aksara Jawa* (Javanese court script), ANRI Archival Stamp.
