# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

6. van IJ
Baud

van – IJ to Nahuys = 22 Oct. 1816.

Wiraguna – Better character than that of his father.
(later Wironegoro)

P.A. = Best / most able prince of Yogya – Nahuys not see this also.

arrogates for himself – Title of R. Adipati

temporary assumption of position Crown Prince =
But despite his masquerading as Crown Prince = should be allowed to keep seal of state until HB IV 20 years old.

also should be paid Sp.D. 6.000 per year from kraton

a sum earlier promised By Raffles.
But not given

No Really enterprising princes in Yogya / Sala.

[Robberies 1814-16.]
great Rise in Robberies 1814-16

should not be allowed to leave court & mix with negorij people.

under walls of Semarang
& in smoke of courts
