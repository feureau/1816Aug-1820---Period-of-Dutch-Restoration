# Transcription of IMG_1551.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1551.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Private Papers of H.G. Nahuys van Burgst; Confidential Memoranda & Correspondence, Sept. 1820 – Oct. 1822)
- **Date Range:** 1820-09-05 to 1822-10-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Confidential research notes on the private and financial correspondence between Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst and his French-born business partner and land-renter Joseph Donatiën Boutet; arrival memorandum (5 Sept. 1820) recording Nahuys’ return to Surakarta from an official journey to Batavia in the company of an elite suite of high colonial officials: Arnold Adriaan Buyskes, Major François Vincent Henri Antoine Ridder de Stuers, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, De Jongh, and Pieter Merkus; private ledger noting that Nahuys extracted a personal cash profit of 1,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) on this official trip to Batavia; historical reference to the devastating outbreak of the Asiatic cholera pandemic (*Asiatische cholera*) across Java in May 1821; commercial transactions between Nahuys and Boutet involving plantation investments in central Java.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Joseph Donatiën Boutet, Arnold Adriaan Buyskes, Major François Vincent Henri Antoine Ridder de Stuers, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Pieter Merkus, De Jongh.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Confidential memoranda, private land lease syndicates, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), official travel travelogue profits, Asiatic cholera pandemic (1821).
