# Transcription of IMG_1269.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1269.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, Vol. 4203 [J 181]: *Secret Weekly Reports & Intelligence Summaries*, November–December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-11-19 to 1816-12-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Protocol gift-giving between Assistant Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman and the teenage Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (bestowing a gold watch upon demand to accompany him in his carriage); recurring court panic and destabilizing rumors regarding the imminent return of exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) from Batavia to reclaim the throne; strict mandatory registration and residential pass laws imposed upon all "Moors" (Indian Muslims/Arabs) and Chinese merchants; widespread mass desertion of colonial auxiliary personnel, African soldiers, and Indian auxiliary troops fleeing into Surakarta territory and attempting to reach the northern coast (Tegal and Cirebon); intelligence alert regarding concentrations of armed deserted Indian Sepoys and Bengalis in the Yogyakarta environs.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Johan Diederik Kruseman.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia, Surakarta (Solo), Tegal, Cirebon (Cheribon), Bengal.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Vol. 4203 (J 181), Sultan Sepuh restoration rumors, Compulsory alien registration (*Moors and Chinese*), Deserted Indian Sepoys (*Bengal Volunteers*), African soldiers, Military intelligence report.
