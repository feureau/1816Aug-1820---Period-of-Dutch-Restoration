# Transcription of IMG_1537.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1537.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; Microfilm No. 4, *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, Buitenzorg, Dec. 1819 – April 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1819-12-24 to 1820-04-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official culmination of the minority and regency in Yogyakarta: Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst recommends that the guardianship (*voogdij*) exercised by Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I) over young Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV be formally concluded and the royal Great Seal (*kraton* state seal) transferred to the Sultan; Governor-General Van der Capellen assents, awarding Paku Alam I a generous honorarium of 3,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) for his faithful guardianship; construction of the post carriage road (*postweg*) connecting Yogyakarta and Surakarta, fixing transport duties and a government toll fee of ½ guilder; birth of a royal son to official consort Ratu Kencana on 25/27 January 1820; ceremonial handover of the Great Seal to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV on 27 January 1820 marking his formal assumption of sovereign majority; compassionate grant of 500 Spanish Dollars to Radèn Martakusuma to finance his return home; petition by Paku Alam I to have his eldest son Pangéran Suryaningprang (Paku Alam II) commissioned as Captain in the Netherlands Indies Army; application by 1st Lieutenant Philippus Pieter Roorda van Eysinga for an honorable military discharge to enter the colonial civil administration, citing his advanced proficiency in the Malay language.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (Sultan Séda Besiyar), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Ratu Kencana, Radèn Martakusuma, Pangéran Suryaningprang (Paku Alam II), Philippus Pieter Roorda van Eysinga.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogja), Surakarta (Solo), Buitenzorg (Bogor), Klathèn.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, royal guardianship (*voogdij*), end of royal minority, royal Great Seal (*cap kraton*), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), post road (*wagenpostery*), Pakualam Legion, Malay language proficiency, military discharge / civil transition.
