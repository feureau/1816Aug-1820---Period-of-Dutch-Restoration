7 July 1817:
Usual "gewoon Rapport" written on Mondays / Thursdays.
usually in presence of Patih, Regents (? Nayaka), + Capt Cina
(Tegen het Feest van Poewasa) (see Notig Kouffaer p. 13)
Prisoners = according to custom before Puasa: to be punished:
and those / nothing proved against them = either freed / Banished.
Because of Malang disturbances: NvB + Prang Wēdana go there
+ 2,000: Had just returned.

13 Aug 1817:
P. Tējasoema + 100 armed men -> Kediri
Res -> Kraton to ask Sunan when Feest of Puasa should be celebrated
Fixed for Friday = Sunan invited to the Residency garden.

15 Aug 1817:
Feast of Puasa celebrated
after DINNER + Princes / Regents in Residency House.

13 Oct 1817 (Monday):
-> Sunan, to tell PB IV's Intention
the following Wed. to go to Klaten to meet Res. of Yogya + Yogya Regents for regulating of Various affairs - Sunan expresses approval
(? Negotiations for 1818 Law Book?)

15 Oct 1817:
to Klaten. + Patih - First Regent (1 Bupati Jaga)
Prince Prangwēdana / J.W. Winter
to Negotiate + Res of Yogya / Yogya heads:
For preventing ROBBERIES / + good Relations between the 2 courts.
Successful meeting. Decision to meet again in 6 months

22 Oct 1817:
CELEBRATING TAHUN BĒSAR:
officers of garrison / Leading Jav. Heads / + citizens of Solo to Dinner in the Residency house.
