# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

5. van IJ.

gives assurances to courts / ordinary Jav. that British would Respect Congress of Vienna & would not be Returned. =

Rumour that Dutch only Returned for a few years.
Deep appears to have struck deep Roots = espc. in Sala.

Receives as Presents from Sunan = a kris & gold pondhok. & Team of 2 Black horses.
lengths of Batik cloth.

Sultan =
↳ a kris & gold pondhok
& Team 2 small Brown horses.
Ring & Diamonds.
5 Sarongs / 5 Handkerchiefs / lengths of Batik
5 long pieces of cloth.
From P.A. kris & gold pondhok.
From PK IV 2-Horse.

---

### C.F. Krijgsman

Situation of Krijgsman = Official placed again as Translator in Semarang & salary of Sp. D. 250

Formerly = made a good opinion of him as when van IJ acts as Commissioner for Oosthoek.

& good Report made to Daendels.
