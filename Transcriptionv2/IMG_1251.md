# Transcription of IMG_1251.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1251.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djocjocarta* [AVJ] 1816 / Residency Secret Financial Dispatches)
- **Date Range:** 1812-08-01 to 1816-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Nahuys van Burgst's comprehensive fiscal dispatch to Governor-General Van der Capellen detailing Patih Danureja IV's financial administration over the Sultan's revenues; three-year leasing of markets (*pasar*) and tollgates (*bandar*) yielding 147,818 Java Rupees annually; Pacitan land tax farmed to the Bupati of Pacitan; lucrative edible bird's nest caves at Karangbolong placed under European supervision (yielding 24 pikuls / 132,000 J.R.); bird's nest revenues of Rawa leased by the Patih (880 J.R.); opium monopoly farm leased at 132,000 J.R. requiring purchase of 40 chests of Bengal opium at 1,200 Spanish Dollars each; treaty subsidies payable to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV under the 1 August 1812 treaty (220,000 J.R. annually plus ±2,800 J.R. for five annual court religious feasts); disparity between British and Dutch residency salaries.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Mas Tumenggung Setrawijaya (Bupati of Pacitan).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Pacitan, Karangbolong, Rawa.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AVJ (*Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djocjocarta*), Treaty of 1 August 1812, Pasar Tax Farms, Tollgate Farms (*bandar*), Opium Farm, Edible bird's nest caves (*sarang burung*), Java Rupee (J.R.), Spanish Dollar (Sp. D.).
