## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1418.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Nahuys van Burgst Papers, Portfolio 5, No. 11: Djokja General Letters, September 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1817-09-01 to 1817-09-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Economic development proposal for Pacitan submitted by Carl Coster (C.H. Coster), comparative frontier settlement theory, regulatory safeguards for indigenous headmen (*bekels* and *demangs*), and formal investiture of the infant Crown Prince in Yogyakarta. In September 1817, Coster submitted his formal proposal to reactivate commercial shipping out of Pacitan Bay (*Teluk Pacitan*) to export cash crops directly to overseas markets. Nahuys noted the extreme fiscal destitution of Pacitan, where impoverished peasant families paid scarcely 1 Rupee per annum in land tax due to corrupt and defective native administration, arguing that undeveloped frontier regions were best revitalized through private European enterprise, explicitly drawing historical parallels to colonial settlement and agrarian expansion in North America and South Africa. Document A.9 establishes strict lease regulations: (1) semi-annual operational reports submitted by European planters to the Resident; (2) absolute prohibition against European planters arbitrarily dismissing Javanese village officials; and (3) an annual reporting mission to Yogyakarta for the district *demang* and two *bekels* without requiring special residency permits. On 19 September 1817, official permission was granted for the newly born royal prince (future Sultan Hamengkubuwana V) to receive the official dynastic title of Pangéran Adipati Anom Amangku Negara (Crown Prince).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Carl Coster [C.H. Coster], Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V (infant prince / Pangéran Adipati Anom Amangku Negara).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan [Patjitan], Pacitan Bay [Teluk Pacitan], Yogyakarta [Djogja], North America, South Africa.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Landhuur* [private agricultural lease], Pacitan Bay reactivation, frontier colonization, *Demang*, *Bekel*, tenure security, *Pangéran Adipati Anom Amangku Negara* [Crown Prince title], export commodities.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portfolio 5, No. 11 (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Collection, Djokja Letters, September 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Agrarian project proposal, regulatory lease charter (Doc. A.9), comparative colonial economic theory, and Kraton title proclamation.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates how Dutch administrative reformers viewed remote enclaves like Pacitan as laboratory spaces for settler-capitalist development while maintaining paternalistic oversight over native village hierarchies.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, entrepreneur C.H. Coster, district priyayi of Pacitan, and the Yogyakarta Regency council.
