# Transcription of IMG_1518.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1518.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 122 / ANRI Solo Br. 122, Part 2) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (High Political Intelligence & Dynastic Succession Crisis))
- **Date Range:** 1823-09-09 to 1824-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** High-level secret dispatch from Surakarta Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven to Governor-General Van der Capellen detailing a constitutional plan to neutralize the volatile, anti-Dutch Sunan Pakubuwana VI: threatening forced abdication in favor of Pangéran Purbaya (future PB VII) on the model of the colonial deposition of the Sultan of Palembang; recommending the formal dissolution of PB VI's marriage to Ratu Mas; establishing a tripartite Regency (Resident, Pangéran Buminata, and Patih Sasradiningrat II) under the Governor-General's secret decree of 9 September 1823; elevating childless elder prince Buminata to the dignity of *Panembahan*; and resurrecting the archaic royal title of *Pangéran Adipati* for Purbaya.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (Resident of Surakarta), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Sunan Pakubuwana VI (Radèn Mas Saperdan), Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Pangéran Buminata (elder royal uncle), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Surakarta Patih), Ratu Mas (consort of PB VI), Pangéran Ngabèhi.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Palembang (Sumatra), Kartasura.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Secret gubernatorial resolution (*BGG geheim 9 Sept 1823*), Tripartite Regency (*Regentschap*), Forced royal abdication, Palembang precedent, Sumptuary titles (*Panembahan*, *Pangéran Adipati*, *Pangéran Adipati Anom*), Dissolution of royal marriage, Tollgate commission.
