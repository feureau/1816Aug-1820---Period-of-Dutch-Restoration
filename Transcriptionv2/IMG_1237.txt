v.d.M. & PROV. RES. -> G.G.
ARA Inv. 616
[peris?] no. 2
NAHUYS van B.
AVAN B. ARCHIVE LEIDEN

Yogya 1816 (Aug)
7 Aug: Nahuys lands in Semarang
9 Aug: Nahuys arrives in Yogya
see P. H. van der Kemp "Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816" 's-Gravenhage 1911 pp. 248-248

(9 Aug 1816)
Crawfurd hands over Djocja
Nahuys praise Crawfurd's admin. in deepest peace.
would continue on the same footing - same policy as Crawfurd.
Evening of 10 Aug: Crawfurd takes leave of Yogya to go to embarkation of British troops in E/Central Java.
Presented as "younger brother" (Kemp p. 248)
"Crawfurd much loved at court of Djocja" (Kemp p. 248)
Leaves Residency in hands of (?) Assistant Natie

11 Aug.
No intrigues or evil deeds in court of D.!!
allowing corpse on court:
CORPS PAKOE ALAM: "tho' refusing Dutch would remain returning overrides policies"
P.A. CORPS taken over by Dutch:
200 men: paid by Prince: provided horses.
weapons etc provided by Govmnt.
SMALL CORPS OF "CONSTABULARS" (25 mounted constables)
corps of Dutch, Germans, Amboonese. Weapons from 22nd Dragoon Regt.

---

V.I.N.C.E. Intrigues of SOENAN and Bengal Sepoys:
1 corps of 25 sold as LIFE WATCH in KRATON.
very useful cf knowledge of malay even some t Javanese lang.
receive 15 Rupeen p. month
sergt 30
Lieutenant 120
