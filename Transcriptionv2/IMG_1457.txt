Local Education Java
(Res. of Banyumas / Prov. Res. of Tegal)
de Stürler (Res. of Tegal)
to van den Bosch,
3 Nov. 1831

No schools in Tegal for teaching Jav. writing / Malay.
about only 1000 pupils in pesantrèns. instructed in Reading Qur'ān & saying prayers.
v. few gurus know Jav. letters. Most use Arabic.
Reg. of Tegal counsels against broadening out Religious schools.
Reg. asked about whether useful to set up govrmt. schools.
Says v. difficult for Jav. of rank to give their children a good education.
& have to content themselves + incompetent teachers.

During English Period = + increasing demand for Jav. officials who could Read/write, even + Latin letters.
Jav. of Rank in Tegal begin to realize need for Education.
cf Onghokham: Reg. of Tegal instructs own children in Malay / Jav.
Were school set up in Tegal = 3 competent teachers already on Hand.
2 other Tegal Regents = can also read Latin letters + press for school in Tegal.
Schools should be set up in each of 3 kabupatèn under control / supervision of Regents.
Little opposition need be expected from Religious teachers in Regent's opinion.
