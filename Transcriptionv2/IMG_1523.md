# Transcription of IMG_1523.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1523.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63A / *PJ. Br. 63A*, May–Sept. 1820) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Residency Outgoing Letterbook))
- **Date Range:** 1819-05-18 to 1820-07-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical administrative, diplomatic, public health, and commodity dispatches: Pasuruan Resident Ellinghuizen identifying Kyai Ahmad (Kyai Temporan) of Rawa as the spiritual mastermind behind the Malang disturbances; official presentation of vice-regal honors and gifts to Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat) for his services during the West Borneo commission (alabaster clock, vases, English rifle, f. 276 cash); delivery of 1,500 pikuls of Gamping lime to Magelang; domestic murder in Pacitan; landmark public health directive from Inspector of Vaccine Dr. C.L. Blume replacing European medical officers with native Javanese *Mantris* because Europeans lack influence over the peasantry; baseline market price schedule for staple commodities; and Governor-General's decree (*BGG 8 Jul 1820 No. 4*) prohibiting coastal salt exports to the Vorstenlanden to safeguard the state monopoly.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** J.C. Ellinghuizen (Resident of Pasuruan), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Kyai Ahmad (Kyai Temporan), Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Satruna, Dr. Carl Ludwig Blume (Inspector of Vaccine), K.B. van Stenis.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pasuruan, Desa Temporan (Rawa), West Borneo (Pontianak / Sambas), Gamping, Magelang, Pacitan, Lorok, Yogyakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Islamic religious sanctuary (*pesantren* / *langgar*), Diplomatic gifts of honor (*geschenken*), Gamping limestone transport, *Mantri* Vaccinators (*inlandsche inënters*), Public health policy transition, Staple commodity price schedule, Salt Monopoly (*Zoutregie* export embargo).
