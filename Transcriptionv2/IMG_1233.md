# Transcription of IMG_1233.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1233.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' Bundel 41 & 44: *Medical Reports & Military Intelligence*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-16 to 1816-10-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Reports on early colonial public health initiatives and secret military surveillance in Yogyakarta; Residency Surgeon Dr. H.B. Menke reports to Resident Nahuys on the severe impediments hindering child smallpox vaccination due to parental reluctance and traditional customs, noting that barely 80 children had been vaccinated during Captain Garnham's British residency; Menke outlines administration for the specialized hospital treating women with venereal disease, placing operational responsibility on Patih Danureja IV and appointing a special police mantri, Jayaleksana (Joleksana), to conduct sweeps and bring afflicted women into clinical care; secret intelligence dispatch from the Army Command in Batavia alerts Nahuys that numerous English-trained Sepoys and even European deserters were illegally remaining in Java and enlisting as mercenaries in the private entourages of Sunan Pakubuwana IV and exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; re-equipment of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV's palace bodyguard with new dress uniforms, 25 pairs of cavalry pistols, and 23 cavalry sabres.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Dr. H.B. Menke, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Mantri Jayaleksana (Joleksana), Captain Robert C. Garnham, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia, Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Smallpox vaccination campaigns (*koepok-inenting*), Venereal disease hospital, Sepoy military deserters, Sultan's bodyguard (*Prajurit Kraton*), Patih executive authority, Cavalry pistols and sabres.
