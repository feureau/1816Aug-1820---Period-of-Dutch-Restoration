Letters about Tan Jin Sing

Paku Alam - van IJsseldijk.
5 Dulkangidah, pal. 1743
27 Sept. 1816.
trans. Krijgsman 30 Sept. 1816.

Paku Alam still mindful
Prediction of dalěm HB I
"not good that a Chinese should be placed in position to have close relations & Royal family"
according to HB I preparation for Disharmony in Interior.

HB IV under age ∴ trust placed in Paku Alam.
Tan Jin Sing is respected (or rather feared and shunned) by Bupatis / Regents

T.J. Sing often talks about British / Dutch govnrts.
Belittles Dutch praises British
perhaps family / children of Sultan & Bupatis also believe this.

P.A. Excuses himself from any responsibility if T.J. Sing causes disturbances in Yogya had already warned van IJsseldijk.

TJS proud man repudiates adat of Chinese.
and has the ability to sow disharmony and discord
& makes use of all sorts of lies
∴ TJS should be Banished from Yogya.
Because would always repudiate adat

Kyai Adip. Purwa - van IJsseldijk.

4 Oct. 1816.

TJS engaging in unlawful actions.
1 DR III (Sumadipura) often visits TJS by night to discuss affairs of state
2 TJS covets one of HB III's ex-selir, R.Ay. Murtiningrat in order to marry her.
