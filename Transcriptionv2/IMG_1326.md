# Transcription of IMG_1326.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1326.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Ministerie van Koloniën [MvK] 4491*, pp. 60–62)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-02 to 1816-08-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes transcribing the secret diplomatic mandate and state letters for Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's mission to Central Java; details the three Secret Articles of 2 August 1816: (1) investigating British intelligence and rumors regarding retrocession, (2) reassuring the rulers of Dutch adherence to existing contracts, and (3) asserting the irrevocable permanence of the Dutch Restoration under the Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1814; folios 61–62: official Letter of Friendship from Commissioners-General Elout, Van der Capellen, and Buyskes to Sunan Pakubuwana IV (20 August 1816) proclaiming King William I's sovereign accession, guaranteeing the 1 August 1812 British treaty, and expressing the veiled warning that armed force should not become necessary.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), King William I of the Netherlands, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Arnold Adriaan Buyskes, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, The Hague (MvK 4491).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Secret Articles of Embassy (2 Aug. 1816), Letter of Friendship (20 Aug. 1816), Treaty of 1 August 1812, Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1814, Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
