# Transcription of IMG_1402.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1402.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: Nahuys van Burgst Collection / Djocja Correspondence General, Feb 1817: Pacitan Anchorage & Murma Inquiry)
- **Date Range:** 1817-02-06 to 1817-02-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Maritime and commercial proposals for Pacitan Bay and the conclusive colonial assessment of the Kyai Murma Wijaya religious conspiracy. Nahuys advocates developing Pacitan Bay (*Teluk Pacitan*) as a deep-water naval anchorage on the southern coast, while revitalizing the local economy by importing woven textiles from the Vorstenlanden. On 14 February 1817, replying to the Commissarissen-Generaal, Nahuys provided his definitive evaluation of the Kyai Murma affair: the complicity of the Yogyakarta Kraton was far less substantial than initially feared by Provisional Assistant Resident Kruseman. The sensational rumor that Ratu Ibu (Ratu Kencana) intended to marry Kyai Murma was dismissed as absurd, with Nahuys identifying Pangéran Paku Alam I as the probable source of the malicious disinformation designed to discredit his palace enemy. The official judicial *procès-verbal* established that Ratu Ibu was merely a "dupe and not a plotter." To defuse widespread indigenous anger over the heavy-handed military raid by Dutch soldiers on Murma's estate at Kepundhung (where he was venerated as the head of an ancient spiritual family), Nahuys urged that his impounded property be auctioned quietly and the financial proceeds remitted to his family, while heartily endorsing Murma's perpetual banishment to the Moluccas.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Kyai Murma Wijaya [Moermo / Kijayi Moermo], Ratu Kencana / Ratu Ibu, Pangéran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I [Pakoe Alam], Johan Diederik Kruseman [Asst. Res.], Commissarissen-Generaal [Comm. Generaal].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan Bay, Pacitan, Yogyakarta [Djocja], Desa Kepundhung [Negori Kepundhung], Moluccas [Ambon], Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Maritime anchorage, judicial interrogation (*procès-verbal*), Kraton factionalism, impoundment of property, public auction, exile to the Moluccas (*verbanning*), Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection: Djocja Correspondence, Februari 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Executive correspondence, maritime development memoranda, and judicial evaluations submitted to the central government in Batavia.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates how colonial administrators disentangled court espionage and protected royal prestige (clearing Ratu Ibu) while managing the dangerous popular sensitivities surrounding the arrest of revered Islamic religious preceptors.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, the Commissarissen-Generaal, royal Kraton figures (Ratu Ibu, Paku Alam I), and Islamic religious leaders (Kyai Murma).
* **Section Headings:** Top register outlines Pacitan maritime and commercial infrastructure; lower register provides detailed minutes on the Kyai Murma judicial inquiry.
* **Chronological Ledger:** Left column marks exact entry dates (`6 Feb 1817`, `14 Feb.`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, addressees, and administrative rubrics on the left; verbatim maritime proposals, monetary accounts, court character sketches, and judicial findings on the right.
