# Transcription of IMG_1356.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1356.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief / ANRI: *Geheim Besluit van de Commissarissen-Generaal*, 12 December 1816, La A; continuing Kyai Adipati Purwa's formal grievance memorandum of 4 October 1816 against Tan Jin Sing)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-04 to 1816-12-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of the formal nine-point bill of indictment submitted by Kyai Adipati Purwa to Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk detailing the scandalous crimes, corruption, and protocol breaches of Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing):
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing), Kyai Adipati Purwa, Raden Adipati Danureja IV (DR IV / Carey notes DR III in error), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (HB III), Pangeran Mangkubumi, Raden Ayu Murtiningrat, Raden Tumenggung Mangkudipura, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Persian thoroughbred horse (diplomatic bribe), anti-Dutch sedition ("lackeys of the British" / *tiyang beburuh*), *Bupati Nayaka*, *sembah* (*nembah*), *Radèn Ayu* title usurpation, *bedhaya* dancers, royal concubinage (*slaapstee*), *putra-santana* seating protocol, sawah extortion, smuggling (*smukkelarijen*).
