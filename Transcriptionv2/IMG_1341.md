# Transcription of IMG_1341.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1341.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk delivers formal diplomatic guarantees to both princely courts and ordinary Javanese subjects affirming that Great Britain will strictly respect the treaties of the Congress of Vienna and will not return to reconquer Java; addressing insidious and pervasive rumors—which had struck deep roots especially across the Surakarta court and aristocracy—alleging that the Dutch retrocession was temporary and that the Dutch administration would only remain for a few years; exhaustive inventory of lavish reciprocal diplomatic gifts (*pisungsung* / *geschenken*) presented to Van IJsseldijk by the Javanese rulers: Sunan Pakubuwana IV presents an ancestral heirloom keris fitted with an embossed gold scabbard sheath (*pondhok* / *pendhok*), a matched carriage team of two black horses, and valuable bolts of royal batik cloth; Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV presents an heirloom keris with a gold *pondhok*, a team of two small brown carriage horses, a diamond-encrusted ring, 5 royal sarongs, 5 ceremonial handkerchiefs, and long pieces of fine ceremonial batik; Pangeran Paku Alam I gives an heirloom keris with gold *pondhok*; PB IV presents an additional two-horse carriage team; review of the career and welfare of government translator C. F. Krijgsman: reappointed to his official post as sworn Government Translator for Javanese at Semarang with an established salary of 250 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.); Van IJsseldijk's longstanding high opinion of Krijgsman's diplomatic interpreting services dating back to Van IJsseldijk's commissionership in the Oosthoek (East Java) and his prior favorable reports submitted to Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV / PK IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Pangeran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I (P.A.), C. F. Krijgsman, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Semarang, Oosthoek (East Java).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Congress of Vienna, British return rumors, diplomatic gift exchange (*pisungsung* / *geschenken*), keris, *pondhok* / *pendhok* (gold keris scabbard casing), carriage horses, royal batik, diamond ring, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Sworn Government Translator (*translateur*).
