# Transcription of IMG_1236.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1236.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA MvColoniën 4132: *Solo 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy Dossiers*)
- **Date Range:** 1815-10-01 to 1819-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Declassified analysis of Sunan Pakubuwana IV's 1815 conspiracy with mutinous British-Indian Sepoy battalions to assassinate all European officers in the Vorstenlanden and destroy the rival Kraton of Yogyakarta; records subsequent 1819 inquiries wherein Sunan PB IV designated his younger brother Pangéran Mangkubumi as the principal architect and weapon of the conspiracy, depicting Mangkubumi as an incurably bloodthirsty prince harboring violent grievances against Europeans; notes that while Mangkubumi was deeply enmeshed with the mutineers, the Sunan secretly feared him due to Mangkubumi's private cabals with Sepoy soldiers without royal presence; severely criticizes British administrative negligence, noting that Resident Major Johnson was absent 23 leagues away at the mountain retreat of Selo while his assistant resident was preoccupied with private business, concluding that colonial residents must permanently remain on the spot at court.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson, Thomas Stamford Raffles.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Kraton Yogyakarta, Selo (mountain station), Vorstenlanden (VA / VL).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sepoy Mutiny (1815), Kraton destruction plot, Resident absenteeism, Royal fratricidal rivalry.
