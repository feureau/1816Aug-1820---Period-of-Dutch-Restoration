# Transcription of IMG_1405.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1405.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Port. 5, No. 5: Nahuys van Burgst Collection / Djocja Correspondence Secret, 14 April 1817: Strategic Security & Colonization Doctrine)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08 to 1817-04-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Secret political memoranda and imperial governance philosophy formulated by Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst for Governor-General Godert van der Capellen. Nahuys evaluates the revanchist threat posed by the partisans of the deposed Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (*Kasepuhan*), concluding that indigenous civil and military factions lack the strength and cohesion to challenge colonial power, and asserting that a "lightning march of European troops" would swiftly extinguish any rebellion. Nahuys details his reconciliatory social diplomacy, hosting dinner parties at his Bedhaya country estate where *Kasepuhan* princes are entertained alongside key partisans of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV to dissolve court factionalism. He reiterates his August 1816 police reform doctrine: stationing three-man military pickets at every tollgate (*bandar*) funded entirely by Chinese tax-farmers, supervised by European officers. Finally, Nahuys articulates his core imperialist thesis on European colonization: expressing absolute conviction that European settlement, capital investment, and Christian-Chinese enterprise in the interior of Java would uplift the Javanese peasantry, modernize governance, and permanently consolidate European colonial supremacy, alongside the definitive abolition of judicial torture.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen [G.G.], Sultan Hamengkubuwana II [H.B. II], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV [H.B. IV], Chinese tax-farmers.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djocja], Bedhaya [Bedoyo], Vorstenlanden [VL], Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Kasepuhan* faction, European agricultural colonization (*landhuur*), tollgate military pickets (*bandar*), Chinese tax-farmers, abolition of judicial torture, imperial consolidation thesis, Bedhaya dinner diplomacy.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 5 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection: Djocja Correspondence Secret, 14 April 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Secret policy dispatch (*geheime missive*), political security evaluation, and imperialist ideological manifesto.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Represents the clearest intellectual articulation of Nahuys's liberal-colonization doctrine, arguing that direct European agricultural and commercial penetration of the Javanese interior was essential to stabilize colonial power and modernize indigenous society.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, Governor-General Van der Capellen, royal court factions (*Kasepuhan* vs. *Karaton*), and colonial garrison detachments.
* **Archival Header:** Top registers archival shelfmark and secrecy classification (`BPL 616 / NvB v.B / PORT 5 / NO. 5. / 14 April 1817 (cont.) / N van B -> G.G. / DJOCJA CORR. (SECRET 1817) / N van B ARCHIVE LEIDEN.`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Left column tracks thematic headings (`INTRIGUES of Supporting H.B. II`, `PLAN FOR Improvement of POLICE`, `Torture.`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, policy headings, and security numbered points on the left; verbatim strategic evaluations, dinner diplomacy accounts, and philosophical declarations on the right.
