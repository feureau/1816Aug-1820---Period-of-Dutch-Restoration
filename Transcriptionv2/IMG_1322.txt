### MANGKU BUMI:
MB = connection v SEPOY REBELLION — 1815 — = REASON FOR EXILE

* very UNRULY person - HATED the ENGLISH.
* particular influence on P.B. IV [*(PBV)*]
* Hatred of P.B. IV for capricious conduct of CROWN PRINCE
  $\therefore$ attempt to get 2nd son ARIA PRABU PURBAYA as CROWN PRINCE.
* cf daughter of Mangku Bumi m. to PURBAYA
  $\therefore$ STRONGEST SUPPORT.
* BUT CROWN PRINCE especially ANNOYED over PLOT
* $\therefore$ annoyance of Sunan for Brit support of CROWN PRINCE
* Dejection over LOSS OF KEDU.
* HOPE that PURBO [Purbaya] be RECOGNISED = IN RETURN FOR RESTORING KEDU, RETURNING ORDER AFTER REVOLT.
* $\therefore$ attempt to WIN OVER SEPOYS & PRANG WEDONO =
  * BUT attempt to WIN OVER P.W. proves FRUITLESS.
* SEPOYS JOIN IN PLOT
  * for hatred of BRIT GOVNMT
  * NOT for family quarrel.
* BUT CROWN PRINCE gives away PLOT to Betray main enemy Mangku Bumi.
* Mangku Bumi $\therefore$ made scapegoat when PLOT DIVULGED
* SUNAN REMAINS = Behind the scenes = REFUSES to COME OUT IN SUPPORT OF MB.
* EXTREMELY IMPORTANT TO WIN OVER MB! [P.W.] yet MORE FIRMLY TO THE GOVNMT: cf give him KOLONEL'S POSITION & pay TROOPS.
