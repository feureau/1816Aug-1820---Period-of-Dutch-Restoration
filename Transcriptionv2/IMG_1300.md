# Transcription of IMG_1300.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1300.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (P. H. van der Kemp / M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java*, secondary documentary synthesis, pp. 266–267)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-08 to 1816-09-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Scholarly archival analysis of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s diplomatic interactions with the Principalities; assessment of Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s enigmatic duplicity as characterized by Surakarta Prime Minister Sasradiningrat II; Van IJsseldijk’s arrival in Yogyakarta on 9 September 1816; deeply emotional reunion of the elderly Van IJsseldijk with Pangéran Paku Alam I and the aged Adipati Purwa (Tan Jin Sing / Secadiningrat), weeping in mutual embrace; Resident Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst's magnanimous report acknowledging his initial personal pique at the appointment of an extraordinary envoy, yet praising Van IJsseldijk’s discretion and acknowledging the immense diplomatic value of his 1786 prestige under Sultan Hamengkubuwana I.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Mas Sugandi (Crown Prince / PB V), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Radèn Tumenggung Purwa (Tan Jin Sing / Adipati Purwa), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I / Sultan Swarga), King William I of the Netherlands, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, G. A. G. P. Baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Residency House & Kraton), Netherlands (Holland).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Van IJsseldijk Special Mission (1816), Verzameling-Nahuys (Private Nahuys Archival Collection), First Resident (1786), Dutch Restoration Diplomacy, Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
