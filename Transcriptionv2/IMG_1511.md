## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1511.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Primary Printed Colonial Statute (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63 / *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, 1820, Page 3) (Archetype 1: Primary Printed Statute / Organic Public Health Regulation (Articles 10 cont.–15))
- **Date Range:** Circa 1820
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official promulgated text of the smallpox vaccination organic statute (*Reglement op de koepok-inenting*, Articles 10 to 15): requiring all private physicians and surgeons to submit annual statistical returns to Residents; establishing immediate emergency notification protocols upon outbreaks of natural smallpox (*natuurlijke kinderziekte*); mandating the strict exclusion of children from infected households from all schools; procedures for emergency replenishment of lost vaccine lymph via immediate arm-to-arm transmission from adjacent districts, docking negligent vaccinators' salaries; absolute prohibition against vaccinators demanding fees or bribes (*geene belooning, geld of geldswaarde*); and disciplinary sanctions for neglect of duty or submitting fraudulent monthly returns.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Commissioner for the Civil Medical Service, Inspector of Smallpox Vaccination, Regional Residents, Private Physicians and Surgeons (*Heelmeesters*), District Vaccinators.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Java Residencies, Urban Schools.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Natural smallpox (*natuurlijke kinderziekte*), School quarantine, Emergency lymph reintroduction, Salary forfeiture for negligence, Free public immunization (*kostelooze inenting*), Disciplinary dismissal for fraud.
