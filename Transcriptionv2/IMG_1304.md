# Transcription of IMG_1304.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1304.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], *Bundel Djokjo Brieven* / Javanese Letters of October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Original manuscript leaf written in formal Javanese cursive script (*Aksara Jawa* / *Cacarakan*); letter from Pangéran Adipati Paku Alam I to the Sultan / Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat affirming unshakeable loyalty to the Kraton, praying for the ancestral blessing of the warrior kings (*Ratu pinarjurit*), pledging to cleanse the impurities of the land of Java (*angrimpili susukering tanah Jawi*), and defending the royal house; preserved with official ANRI authentication stamps and blind-embossed paper seals.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (Sultan Swarga).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton of Yogyakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Aksara Jawa* (Javanese script), Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI), Blind-embossed colonial seal, *Ratu pinarjurit* (warrior monarchs), *Susukering tanah Jawi* (cleansing impurities of Java).
