# Transcription of IMG_1378.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1378.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Port. 5, No. 7 / ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60: Jan–Feb 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-07 to 1817-01-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Final programmatic declarations and martial regalia of the Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi millenarian uprising in Bagelèn, concluding with his capture on 13 January 1817. The document outlines Umar Mahdi's three-stage apocalyptic program: (1) coronation of the messianic sovereign (*Ratu Adil*) during the holy month of Sapar (January 1817); (2) subjugation of the Javanese population and elimination/submission of Chinese merchants across Bagelèn on 7 January; and (3) deployment of resurrected spiritual commanders—Sunan Bonang as Field Marshal at Semarang and Umar Maya as Field Marshal in Kedu—dispatched from Arabia to restore ritual purity to Javanese Islam. The text records the literal translation of the divine mandate issued in the name of the *Ratu Adil* to Tuwan Hadji Idris, ordering the mobilization of all returned pilgrims (*haji*) and proclaiming the cosmic restoration of the moral order ("Gold must become gold again and Silver again Silver"). The insurgent force displayed distinct sacred regalia: 10 white flags, 4 *umbul-umbul* (vertical pennants), 20+ pikes, and kerises carried by 50 men and 4 women, before their capture by Assistant Resident C.F. Krijgsman and colonial detachments on 13 January.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi [OEMAR MAHDI], Tuwan Hadji Idris [TUWAN Hadji Idris / Tuwan Hadi Coris], Haji Amidjah [Hadji Amidjah / Ibnu Hiaman], Sunan Bonang, Umar Maya [Usman Maya], C.F. Krijgsman [Asst. Res. Krijgsman of Djogja], Sultan of Rum [Old Sultan of Rum / Ottoman Sultan].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Bagelèn, Semarang, Kedhu [Kedu], Mecca [Mekka], Medina, Yogyakarta [Djogja], Rome / Istanbul [Sultan of Rum].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Ratu Adil* [Just Prince], *Prajurit Sultan Rum*, *Haji* [pilgrims], *Umbul-umbul* [pennants], white banners [panji pethak], pikes, kerises, "Gold must become gold again and Silver again Silver" [cosmic restoration aphorism], Sapar [Islamic month].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 7 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection); correlated with ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60.
* **Document Nature:** Translated interrogations, insurgent manifestos, and police capture reports transcribed from Surakarta and Yogyakarta residency secret dossiers.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Captures the symbolic and textual mechanics of an early nineteenth-century peasant revolt, illustrating how eschatological prophecies of cosmic rebalancing provided ideological coherence to anti-colonial and anti-tax agitation.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Assistant Resident C.F. Krijgsman, rural insurgent leaders (Umar Mahdi, Hadji Idris), and colonial intelligence informants (Hadji Amidjah).
* **Header Structure:** Top margin contains faded red ink chronological and topical markers (`[Top faded red ink: (Jan. - Feb.) 1817 / Umar Mahdi / Bagelen]`).
* **Carey Annotations:** Red ink oval highlighting informant testimony (`[Red oval: Hadji Amidjah]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Programmatic numbers, prophetic citations, and capture dates on the left; verbatim translations of the royal mandate, troop tallies, and regalia inventories on the right.
