### IJsseldijk 1816
**Rapport van W.H. van IJsseldijk omtrent de Vorstenlanden.**
11 Dec. 1816.
in Baud. private coll.? 26 a/2
**Baud private coll no. 306**
signed in Yogya, 22 Oct. 1816.

* **p. 1**: 2 Aug. 1816 van IJ appointed as Buitengewoon gezant.
  * Leaves Batavia on *Zeeploeg* = arrives Samarang 17 Aug. 1816.
  * gives notice to Res. of Sala / Yogya. See Appendix no. 1.
  * **J.A. Kruseman** = Secretary of gezantschap.
  * Introduction to Patih of Sala — Sasradiningrat, & other ambassadors from Sala. (Aim to go to Batavia, but journey cancelled.) Compliments given to G.G.
* **p. 2**: Idea of fixing ceremonial for van IJ's visit to Surakarta — but rejected — should be worked out v Van Braam first.
  * want letter of G.G. -> PB IV to take it back, but = van IJ prefers to give it to Van Braam. See Bijlage 2.
  * Thereupon Patih / ambassadors of PB IV Return.
* **p. 3**: 24 Aug. 1816 arrival of Kruseman. = Bringing letters of van der Cap -> Sunan / Sultan.
  * Kruseman takes oath -> van IJ, in presence of Res. Parvé of Samarang.
