# Transcription of IMG_1472.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1472.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A, *Jan.–March 1819* & Resolutions of the Governor-General in Council)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-07 to 1819-02-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Fundamental colonial administrative and agrarian reorganization of the Pacitan enclave: Governor-General's decree (*BGG 7 Jan 1819 No. 5*) formally exempting Pacitan from the *Landelijk Stelsel* (land rent system) in favor of forced cash crop cultivation; establishing mandatory quotas requiring every peasant household to plant 500 pepper vines interplanted with coffee trees; restructuring native leadership by elevating Demang Jagakarya to Tumenggung with a salary of f. 1,200 per annum while pensioning off the recalcitrant Demang Nitrawijaya on f. 300; commissioning a marine survey of Pacitan Bay for harbor construction; promulgation of the core administrative and judicial codes (9 & 10 Jan 1819); Surakarta Resident Rijck van Prehn's proclamation (20 Feb 1819) legally exempting European merchandise from internal tollgates (*bandars*) under penalty of a 10 Spanish Dollar fine; and the appointment (*BGG 25 Feb 1819 No. 11*) of language cadet C.F. Engel as European Opzichter of Pacitan.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Demang (later Radèn Tumenggung) Jagakarya, Demang Nitrawijaya, C.F. Engel (*Opzichter* and *élève in de Javaansche taal*), Rijck van Prehn (Provisional Resident of Surakarta), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan, Pacitan Bay (Teluk Pacitan), Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Landelijk Stelsel* (Land Rent System), compulsory export crops (pepper, coffee), *BGG in Rade* (Decree of the Governor-General in Council), *Binnenlandsch Bestuur*, *Hoog Geregtshof*, *Raad van Justitie*, *Bandar* (tollgate keeper), Spanish Dollars, *Opzichter*, *élève in de Javaansche taal*.
