# Transcription of IMG_1316.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1316.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Notes on P. H. van der Kemp, *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816*, 's-Gravenhage: Nijhoff, 1911, pp. 264–273)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-05 to 1817-01-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Scholarly research notes by Professor Peter Carey analyzing rural violence, banditry (*kècu*), and court anxieties across the Principalities during the August–December 1816 transition; records armed attacks at Tersono/Pekalongan border, arson of 150 houses in Boyolali (capturing 3 Chinese), and destruction of the Jatinom military post; outlines Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's September 1816 mission; summarizes Acting Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman's confidential reports on Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV’s domestic conduct; details widespread terror surrounding the rumored return of Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II) on New Year's Day 1817; documents Patih Danureja IV’s secret consultations with John Crawfurd; summarizes the Kyai Murma Wijaya millenarian affair.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Johan Diederik Kruseman, John Crawfurd, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Kyai Murma Wijaya, P. H. van der Kemp, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Boyolali (Bayalali), Jatinom (Jati Anom), Pekalongan (Tersono / Têrgana kulon), Yogyakarta (Kraton), Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Ambon.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Kècu* (armed bandit gangs), Arson of Chinese quarters, Verzameling-Nahuys, *Zaak-Kjai Mormo*, Secret Residency Reports.
