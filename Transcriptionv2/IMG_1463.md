# Transcription of IMG_1463.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1463.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI Exh. 31 Dec. 1832, No. 2065 Kabinet: *Pertilangan darie Banjaknja Pesantrèn die dalem Nègre Pamakasan*)
- **Date Range:** 1831-10-05 to 1832-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official native-language statistical census register documenting seventy Islamic *pesantrens* and Quranic recitation schools (*tampat orang adjar mengadje*) across the Regency of Pamekasan (Madura), submitted by Panembahan Mangkuadiningrat of Pamekasan pursuant to Governor-General Van den Bosch's education circular. The document opens with an official heading in early nineteenth-century administrative Malay (*Inie pertilangan darie banjaknja pesantrèn oetawa tampat orang adjar mengadje die dalem Nègre Pamakasan sapertie njang tarseboot die bawa inie*), stamped by the National Archives of Indonesia (*Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia*). The table catalogs seventy numbered schools across villages including Larangan, Tannempan, Toronnan, Labi, Desong, Dana-an, Balakpako, Bangkes, Todja (Bata-Bata), Branta, Gatprek, and Datok. Crucially, the right-hand margin preserves a remarkable contemporary Malay pedagogical deposition explaining the three-stage linguistic method utilized across Madurese pesantrens: (1) first, pupils are taught Arabic Quranic recitation in Arabic characters because Islam is an Arabic religion (*karna kita orang ampoenja agama tjara arap*); (2) second, once pupils can read Arabic, the teacher expounds the grammatical meaning in the Javanese language written in Arabic script, identical to Malay Jawi, known as **Pegon script** (*letter Pègo*); and (3) finally, for complete comprehension, the teacher translates the lesson into vernacular Madurese (*bahasa tjara Madura*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Panembahan Mangkuadiningrat of Pamekasan, Johannes van den Bosch, Hendrik Jacob Domis, Traditional Madurese Kyais and Gurus.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pamekasan [Pamakasan / Madura], Larangan, Toronnan, Todja (Bata-Bata), Branta, Gatprek, Datok, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Pertilangan Pesantrèn* [pesantren census register], *Mengaji* [Quranic recitation], *Pegon* script (*letter Pègo* / Javanese written in Arabic script), Madurese translation, three-tier linguistic pedagogy, *Negeri Pamekasan*, ANRI Kabinet Exh. 31 Dec. 1832 No. 2065.

#### Verbatim Marginal Annotation on Pedagogical Method

```text
[Right-hand marginal deposition in 19th-century administrative Malay:]

"Jang pertama dia-orang kasie adjar mangadje tjara Arap dengen letter arap,
karna kita orang ampoenja agama tjara arap.

Dan katiewanja apa bila soeda bole lees, lantas kasie mangartie dengen
bahasa Jawa die toelis dengen letter arap tjoega sapertie letter malayoe
njang die namaie letter Pègo, tapapee dengen bahasa Jawa,

Dan idoe doed sokatnienter kasie mangartie dengen bahasa tjara Madura. -"
```
