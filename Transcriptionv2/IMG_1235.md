# Transcription of IMG_1235.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1235.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA [Nationaal Archief] Ministerie van Koloniën No. 4132: *P.H. van Lawick van Pabst Report on the 1815 Sepoy Mutiny*)
- **Date Range:** 1815-10-01 to 1827-10-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Retrospective intelligence dossier compiled by P.H. van Lawick van Pabst (Resident of Yogyakarta 1827) analyzing the 1815 Sepoy Mutiny in Surakarta; Lawick van Pabst, who was present at the military court-martial, judges Sunan Pakubuwana IV as a far more dangerous and duplicitous adversary than Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; reveals that had the uprising succeeded, the Indian leader of the Sepoys planned to assume the supreme colonial title of Governor-General; exposes that a secret Council of War was attended by European elements during the tenure of Resident Bedwell of Semarang; Bedwell, having participated in the conspiratorial oath, committed suicide by cutting his throat when the plot was unmasked; recounts the dramatic exposure of the mutiny at the Srondol garrison when a European officer overheard Sepoy conspirators debating whether to murder the British officers dining with the Resident; 50 convicted Sepoys were court-martialed, shackled in heavy irons, and transported to Bengal; confirms that the subversive networks of the 1815 mutiny remained actively lingering when Dutch authority was restored in August 1816.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** P.H. van Lawick van Pabst, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Resident Bedwell, Thomas Stamford Raffles.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Srondol (Suonoal cantonment), Bengal, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ARA MvColoniën (Bundel 4132), 1815 Sepoy Mutiny, Council of War (*Krijgsraad*), Srondol garrison, Deportation in irons, Colonial subversion.
