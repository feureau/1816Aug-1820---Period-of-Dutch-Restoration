- **Date Range:** 1817-02-10 to 1817-11-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** The formal legal promulgation of the exile of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) and his senior dynastic partisans to the Moluccas, and royal death notices between the courts. On 10 February 1817, the General Secretariat of the Netherlands Indies issued Besluit No. 5058 N.I., recording the executive council's determination that "because of dangers to social instability," Sultan Sepuh was to be banished overseas to Ambon. The decree named his core companions in exile: Pangéran Arya Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Arya Mertasana, and Raden Tumenggung Kusumadinaya [Rad. Kusuma Diraga]. On 26 February 1817, executive orders from the Secretary of State confirmed financial and logistical arrangements for the deposed monarch. In a separate diplomatic entry dated 3 November 1817, Surakarta Resident Pinket van Haak recorded an official dispatch from Sunan Pakubuwana IV to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV conveying formal intelligence regarding the demise of Pangéran Danupaya.
