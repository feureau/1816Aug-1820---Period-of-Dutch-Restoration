# Transcription of IMG_1440.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1440.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5: Secret Letters from Solo / Van Prehn to Algemeen Secretaris, 30 March 1818, Folio 4)
- **Date Range:** 1818-03-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Fourth folio of Resident Rijck van Prehn’s Secret Dispatch No. 2 detailing the intense dynastic jealousy dividing the Surakarta royal princes and Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s unprecedented transfer of palace military command to Crown Prince Sugandi. Van Prehn analyzes the profound hatred harbored by Pangéran Purbaya (future PB VII) and his mother, the Ratu, toward the Crown Prince, noting that the Ratu—whom Van Prehn bluntly characterizes as lacking political acumen—allowed herself to be misled by flattery from palace confidantes nurturing futile hopes of usurping the succession. Van Prehn reveals that this bitter resentment infected all the Sunan’s royal sons because PB IV had entrusted **the keys of the Kraton and supreme command of the entire palace guard to the Crown Prince**. Enforcing rigorous personal discipline, Sugandi instituted a strict 9:00 p.m. palace curfew: every evening at nine, all royal princes—including Purbaya—were required to be within the Kraton walls, whereupon the inner gates were bolted and the keys delivered to the Crown Prince's quarters. Any prince caught outside was subjected to severe public reprimand and disciplinary measures the following morning.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (Sugandi / future Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII), The Ratu / "Keizerin" (mother of Purbaya), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Soerakarta], Kraton Surakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Palace curfew (9:00 p.m.), Keys of the Kraton (*sleutels van de kraton*), Palace guard command (*kratonwacht*), dynastic jealousy, princely discipline, *Kroonprins*.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5 (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Papers, Secret Letters from Solo, 1818, Folio 4).
* **Document Nature:** Secret executive dispatch (*Geheime Missive*), verbatim diplomatic reportage, and internal palace security analysis.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Documents the operational mechanisms through which Crown Prince Sugandi maintained physical control over the palace perimeter, subduing rival princely factions prior to his accession in 1820.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince Sugandi, and the Surakarta palace guard.
