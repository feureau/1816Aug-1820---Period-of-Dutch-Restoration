# Transcription of IMG_1411.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1411.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: Algemeen Verslag Solo [AvS], 1817 / Surakarta Residency Dispatches from Surabaya)
- **Date Range:** 1817-07-10 to 1817-08-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Forensic investigation into the leadership of the Malang insurrection, rural alarms in Madiun and Pacitan, illicit priyayi border crossings, and comparative grain and commodity prices between Surakarta and Yogyakarta. Writing from Surabaya on 10 July 1817, Resident Nahuys reported that the ringleader of the Malang insurgent band was Raden Jaladkara [Raden Jolo-Dono], son of Pangéran Purbanagara by a concubine, brother-in-law of Sunan Pakubuwana IV (having married the Sunan's sister), and former officer (*panji*) under Pangéran Mangkubumi of Surakarta during the British interregnum; following his capture, five of his aristocratic residences (*dalem*) were seized, while Sunan PB IV's loyalist forces in Kediri were commanded by Purbanagara's personal enemy, Pangéran Dipasana. On 18 July, the Resident of Semarang warned of a gathering of over 4,000 peasants in Sultan's territory in Madiun (evoking the 1815 Capt. Starkman unrest), prompting Pangéran Dipakusuma to investigate. On 23 July, Raden Kanardaha attacked Van der Wijck and a Chinese tax collector at Akrang Padjarowang, executing cattle raids along the Pacitan frontier, while a Javanese religious teacher from Surabaya traveling without a passport was arrested in Jagaraga. The document concludes with a detailed comparative price table for August 1817, recording market costs for meat, rice (f. 3.00 in Solo vs. f. 3.15½ in Yogya), coffee (13.6 vs. 12.0), pepper, salt, wine, and lamp oil.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Jaladkara [Raden Jolo-Dono], Pangéran Purbanagara [Poerba Negara], Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Pangéran Dipasana [Soepra Janria / Regent of Kediri], Pangéran Dipakusuma [Dipa Kusuma], Captain Starkman, Raden Kanardaha, Van der Wijck, Chinese tax collector, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surabaya [Soerabaja], Malang, Kediri, Madiun [Madioun], Pacitan [Patjitan], Jagaraga, Surakarta [Solo], Yogyakarta [Djogja], Akrang Padjarowang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Dutch Restoration archives, Physical archival leaf

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698a; transcripts from the General Report of Surakarta (Algemeen Verslag Solo, 1817: Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven).
* **Document Nature:** Executive police minutes, counter-insurgency dispatches, intelligence intercepts, and comparative municipal market price tables.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates how family ties within the Surakarta royal house complicated regional rebellions in East Java, while providing quantitative economic benchmarks on daily living costs across the two court capitals.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, Resident of Semarang, regional regents (Dipasana of Kediri, Dipakusuma of Madiun), and priyayi insurgent leaders.
* **Archival Header:** Top line registers dispatch origin and series classification (`10 July / Soerabaja / [red ink: H 698a / AvS 1817] / PANDJARANANG near DAMPENING DOWN UNREST IN Ooster-districten`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Red ink margin notes querying geographic jurisdiction (`[red margin note: Surakarta? / Pacitan?]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, regional loci, and commodity labels on the left; verbatim genealogical profiles, confiscation orders, border cattle-raiding reports, and comparative price schedules on the right.
