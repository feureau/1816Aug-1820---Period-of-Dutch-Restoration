DJOGJA CORRESPONDENCE (GENERAL)
1817 (JANUARY)
ASST RES -> G.G.
[Red ink: Yogya 1817 (Jan)]
[N. van B ARCHIVE LEIDEN.]

9 Jan 1817:
Local payment = 800 Rups. per month for an Asst. Res.

14 Jan 1817:
MOVEMENT IN BAGELEN: caused by a certain Raden Oemar Madie
[Red ink: Umar Mahdi movement]

Collection of 60 people:
pretended to have been sent by the Great Ruler of Turkey (Groote Heer van Turkije)
and High Priest in Mekka
to rid Java of all unbelievers & to give the Island a new PRINCE.

IN ORDER TO INCREASE COLLECTION:
3 white banners planted on mosque of desa Jancopo in Bagelen.

orders: Mas Tumenggoeng of Area ordered to give up = authority

all Javanese in Bagelen ordered to assemble in Areas YONO *
TO MURDER Rich Chinese in vicinity.
& then to go to Magelang & afterward to Djogja:
wished to bring about ABDICATION OF SUNAN.

Asst Res (Kruseman) + Outrée + Mas Dem. of Bagelen (Mas Tum. JAWONG SANNG.)
& captures Raden Oemar Madie:
36 people & 3 white banners.

SUNAN advised to RAISE: Mas Tum. to status of Rad. Tum.
as example to other Regents.

Madie: pretended to BE AN ARAB: but can speak no Arabic
only Javanese (+ Djogja accent.)
according to law:
to be executed by kris -
followers to be flogged.
