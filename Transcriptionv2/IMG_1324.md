## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1324.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Ministerie van Koloniën [MvK] 4491*, Secrete Brieven van Comm.-Gen. 1816, pp. 55–59; citing Secret Dispatch of 9 October 1816, La B)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-03 to 1816-10-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes examining the strategic rationale of the Commissioners-General in dispatching Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk to the Vorstenlanden in September 1816; the dual objectives: renewal of the 1812 treaties and countering British diplomatic insinuations that the Dutch were ignorant of court statecraft; comparative psychological and political analysis of the two royal capitals: Yogyakarta characterized as less sophisticated but more open, sincere, and stably governed under the minority of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Ratu Ageng, and Resident Nahuys; Surakarta characterized as a labyrinthine, refined court governed by the subtle, cunning, and unfathomable Sunan Pakubuwana IV, whose duplicity during the 1815 Sepoy Rebellion remained fresh in colonial memory.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton), Surakarta (Solo Kraton), Batavia, The Hague (MvK 4491).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Secrete Brieven (Secret Dispatches), Treaties of 1812, 1815 Sepoy Rebellion, Court Psychology (Yogya Sincerity vs. Solo Subtlety), Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
