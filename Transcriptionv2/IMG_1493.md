# Transcription of IMG_1493.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1493.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI H 698b, *Archief van Jogja 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-10-25 to 1819-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Princely ceremonial protocols, metropolitan textile intelligence, and constitutional transitions: detailed parade, escort, and saluting duties of the Sultan's European Cavalry Lifeguard (escorting HB IV to country estates, parading for gubernatorial dispatches, firing triple pistol salvos during the three Garèbègs, and participating in Saturday Paseban tournaments); market report of Resident Nahuys and C.F. Winter Senior comparing Yogyakarta and Surakarta textile consumption, detailing preferences for vertically striped weaves (*Kaku Bonten Gandweso*) and the booming commercial triumph of English machine-printed batiks commissioned by John Crawfurd; and Nahuys' formal petition of 24 December 1819 terminating the nominal Regency of Paku Alam I and restoring the Royal State Seal (*Cap Kraton*) to the adult Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Carel Frederik Winter Senior (Surakarta translator), John Crawfurd (former Resident), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Sala), Paseban Lor, Fort Vredeburg, Batavia, Great Britain (Manchester/Glasgow).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** European Lifeguard Dragoons (*Lijfwacht*), Garèbèg pistol salvoes, *Watangan* (Saturday lance jousts), *Paseban*, Textile market (*Lijnwaden*), *Kaku Bonten Gandweso*, English printed imitation batiks, Royal State Seal (*Cap Kraton*), Termination of Regency (*meerderjarigheid*).
