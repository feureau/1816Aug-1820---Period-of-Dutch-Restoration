# Transcription of IMG_1268.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1268.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djogja Corr. [November 1816] — Acting Resident to Governor-General*)
- **Date Range:** 1792 to 1816-11-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official report on the capture and interrogation of Kyai Murma Wijaya; escape of Murma's brother Abdullah and 15 armed retainers into the Kapundhung mountains; Murma's flight into Surakarta territory and receipt of massive aristocratic subsidies from high Surakarta princes and consorts (Pangéran Sasradiningrat [5,000 Sp. D.], Pangéran Danupura [2,000 Sp. D.], Ratu Kencana [3,000 Sp. D.], Pangéran Dipokusumo, and Radèn Tumenggung Cakrakusuma); Sunan Pakubuwana IV's clandestine meetings with Murma following Special Envoy Van IJsseldijk's departure; Sunan's patronage at the Krapyak mosque; formal *Procès-Verbal* of Kyai Murma taken on 13 November 1816 detailing decades of gun-running and dynastic payoffs (1792 weapon smuggling from Majapahit for PB IV, 1809–1811 accession payouts from HB III).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Murma Wijaya, Abdullah, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Pangéran Sasradiningrat, Pangéran Danupura, Ratu Kencana (Surakarta), Pangéran Dipakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Cakrakusuma, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Kepundhung (Kapundhung), Krapyak, Majapahit, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Procès-Verbal of Kyai Murma, Arms smuggling, Gun-running (*arms from Majapahit*), Royal Krapyak Mosque, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Kraton Surakarta.
