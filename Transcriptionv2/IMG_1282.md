# Transcription of IMG_1282.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1282.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 18: *Solo Secret Letters 1816–1818*; and Extracts from Governor-General Resolutions 1821 No. 8)
- **Date Range:** 1815-10 to 1816-09-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Assistant Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak's secret intelligence dispatch to the Commissioners-General unraveling the inner dynamics of the 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy (*Sepoy Plot*); Pangéran Mangkubumi's direct complicity and subsequent banishment; identification of Sunan Pakubuwana IV as the true royal instigator of the mutiny; clandestine court scheme to depose the pro-British Crown Prince (later Pakubuwana V) in favor of Pangéran Purbaya (supported by his father-in-law Mangkubumi); royal grievance over Raffles's 1812 annexation of Kedu; Pangéran Prangwedana's (Mangkunagara II) steadfast refusal to join the anti-British plot; Crown Prince's secret betrayal of the conspiracy to British authorities to preserve his succession; colonial policy recommending the strategic bolstering of the Mangkunagaran Legion as a military counterweight against the Sunan.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince (later Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Purbaya (later Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Thomas Stamford Raffles.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Kedu (Kedhu), Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy (*Sepoy Plot*), Bengal Volunteer Battalions, Mangkunagaran Legion, Succession rivalry, Annexation of Kedu, Banishment decrees.
