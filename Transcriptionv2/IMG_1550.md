# Transcription of IMG_1550.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1550.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; Microfilm No. 4, *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal Buiten Raad*, April–October 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-04-06 to 1820-10-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Vice-regal administrative resolutions issued outside council (*Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal Buiten Raad*); decision (6 April 1820 No. 6) reviewing a dispatch from the Resident of Kedu regarding the critical post of Head Demang (*Hoofddemang*) of Magelang: rejecting the appointment of police inspector (*oppasser*) Kèwa Uèntika, Batavia emphasizes the core colonial administrative principle that sensitive police and revenue posts must be filled exclusively by men of established social standing and local aristocratic weight (*men of some weight in local community*), ordering the Resident to nominate a qualified candidate from among the sub-demangs; formal condolence decree (21 July 1820 No. 13) acknowledging dispatches from Yogyakarta Resident Nahuys van Burgst reporting the demise of Queen Grandmother Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton, grandmother of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV), instructing the Resident to convey the deep official sympathies of the Netherlands Indies Government to the Sultan (cross-referenced to the *Babad Dipanagara*); resolution (1 Sept. 1820 No. 1) appointing Natadiwiria, Captain of the *Jayèng Sekar* armed rural constabulary in Semarang and son-in-law of the Bupati of Semarang, as the new Head Demang of Magelang, raising the office's salary from f. 100 to f. 200 per month; initial endorsement (20 Oct. 1820 No. 25) by the Resident of Yogyakarta supporting the petition of Lim Nom Tja (Liem Vouwtja), son-in-law of the late Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Sécaningrat), to be commissioned as *Luitenant der Chinezen* in Yogyakarta (subsequently rejected by the Governor-General on 28 Oct. 1820; cf. `IMG_1525`).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Resident of Kedu, Kèwa Uèntika, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Natadiwiria, Bupati of Semarang, Lim Nom Tja (Liem Vouwtja), Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Sécaningrat).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu, Magelang, Semarang, Yogyakarta (Jogja), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal Buiten Raad*, Head Demang (*Hoofddemang*), police overseers (*oppassers*), indigenous prestige (*priyayi* status), royal mortuary condolences, *Babad Dipanagara*, *Jayèng Sekar* (cavalry constabulary), *Luitenant der Chinezen*, Chinese municipal officers.
