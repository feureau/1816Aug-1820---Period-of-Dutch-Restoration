# Transcription of IMG_1351.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1351.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306, section 8 / page 12 continued: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys*, 22 October 1816; and *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Exhaustive dynastic, genealogical, and security assessment of Pangeran Dipakusuma (DPK / formerly Raden Tumenggung Natayuda II): characterized by supreme royal lineage through both paternal and maternal bloodlines; his grandmother was the sister of Raden Mas Garendi ("Sunan Kuning", the rebel monarch of the 1740–1743 Chinese War); direct descent from Sunan Amangkurat Mas (Amangkurat III, exiled to Ceylon by the VOC in 1708), with Dipakusuma himself having been born in Ceylon; colonial and kraton paranoia (*Fear of Dipakusuma*): colonial administrators must handle him with extreme caution, literally "proceeding with leaden shoes" (*met loode schoenen te laten toegaan*); acute threat analysis: the explosive combination of sacred royal pedigree and vast executive power as Bupati Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara (Madiun) equips him with the prestige to orchestrate a major dynastic upheaval; British intelligence revelation: male descendants of the Amangkurat royal house were repatriated by the British administration from Ceylon to Madura (noted in British residency correspondence); fear that these repatriated princes could coalesce with Dipakusuma into the nexus of a general insurrection; policy recommendation: Van IJsseldijk suggests it would be far safer to recall Dipakusuma from his territorial power base in Madiun and maintain him under direct colonial surveillance in a high court post at Yogyakarta; ministerial alternative: if Raden Adipati Danureja IV ever needs to be removed from the grand vizierate, Pangeran Dipakusuma represents a viable, high-prestige candidate for Patih.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangeran Dipakusuma (DPK / Raden Tumenggung Natayuda II), Raden Mas Garendi ("Sunan Kuning"), Sunan Amangkurat Mas (Amangkurat III), Raden Adipati Danureja IV (DR IV), Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Ceylon (Sri Lanka), Madura, Eastern Mancanagara (Madiun), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Amangkurat royal lineage, Ceylon exile, Sunan Kuning (1742 Geger Pacinan), "met loode schoenen toegaan" (Dutch diplomatic idiom: tread with extreme caution), Bupati Wedana of Madiun, British repatriation of exiles, dynastic rebellion nexus, Patih succession.
