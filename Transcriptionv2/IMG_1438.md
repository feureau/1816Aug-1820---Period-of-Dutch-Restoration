# Transcription of IMG_1438.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1438.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5: Secret Letters from Solo / Van Prehn to Algemeen Secretaris, 30 March 1818, Folio 2)
- **Date Range:** 1818-03-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Second folio of Resident Rijck van Prehn’s Secret Dispatch No. 2 recording Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s solemn personal declaration regarding dynastic succession, the urgent diplomatic quest for a male heir through a state marriage, and the formal refutation of palace coup rumors. Sunan PB IV explicitly revealed to Van Prehn that his singular desire was for Crown Prince Sugandi (future PB V) to ascend the throne of Surakarta—ideally during the monarch’s lifetime or immediately upon his demise—describing Sugandi as his loyal and beloved son, while dismissing Pangéran Purbaya (future PB VII) as too timid and unreliable for supreme rule. Crucially, the Sunan urged the Resident to privately counsel the Crown Prince in gentle terms to contract a prestigious state marriage (*staats huwelijk*) with an aristocratic consort of his own choosing to produce a male heir, as his two current official wives had borne no sons. Van Prehn uses this confidential royal testimony to dismantle prevailing colonial rumors claiming that the Sunan had petitioned Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) for a daughter’s hand in marriage on behalf of Purbaya to alter the succession.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (Sugandi / future Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Soerakarta], Kraton Surakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Troonopvolging* [succession to the throne], *Kroonprins*, *Staats huwelijk* [dynastic state marriage], male heir (*mannelijke opvolger*), royal wives (*twee echte vrouwen*), palace rumor refutation.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5 (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Papers, Secret Letters from Solo, 1818, Folio 2).
* **Document Nature:** Secret executive dispatch (*Geheime Missive*), verbatim diplomatic reportage, and dynastic matrimonial negotiations.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Captures the internal biological anxiety of the Surakarta dynasty over the lack of a male heir in the direct line, while documenting Dutch mediation in royal matrimonial alliances.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, and Crown Prince Sugandi.
* **Archival Header:** Continuation of primary secret dispatch text from `IMG_1437`.
* **Marginal Annotations:** Analytical section markers identifying royal disclosures and marriage directives.
* **Two-Column Organization:** Diplomatic themes and succession points on the left; verbatim nineteenth-century Dutch text of the secret dispatch on the right.
