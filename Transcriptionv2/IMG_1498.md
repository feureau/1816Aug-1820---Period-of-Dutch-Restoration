# Transcription of IMG_1498.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1498.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Scholarly Research Correspondence (Trinity College, Oxford to Prof. G.W.J. Drewes, Leiden)
- **Date Range:** 1981-10-06 to 1819-08-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Scholarly correspondence from Dr. Peter Carey (Trinity College, Oxford) to eminent Dutch Orientalist Professor G.W.J. Drewes (Noordwijk/Leiden): expressing gratitude for philological corrections on Javanese millenarian texts, referencing the publication of the *Babad Dipanagara* by the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (MBRAS), and providing the exact literal Dutch transcript of the 1818 Banyumas/Ajibarang millenarian letter (*Translaat uit een Javaansche Harab brief*) translated in Surakarta on 22 August 1819 by residency translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Dr. Peter Carey, Professor Gerardus Willebrordus Joannes Drewes (G.W.J. Drewes), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (Surakarta translator), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen (*de Radeen Generaal*), Sunan Kuning (Raden Mas Garendi), Lurung Tengah (*Vorst der Rechtvaardigen / Ratu Adil*), Raden Tumenggung Udanagara, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Trinity College (Oxford), Noordwijk, Leiden, Banyumas, Ajibarang, Brebes, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Philological textual criticism, *Babad Dipanagara* (MBRAS edition), *Javaansche Harab brief* (Jawi/Pegon script letter), *Loegoe* (Luguh / village elder), *Maand Repit*, Cosmic inversion of the world, *Vorst der Rechtvaardigen* (Ratu Adil).
