# Transcription of IMG_1262.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1262.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, *Djocjo Brieven* No. 40 [1816]: *Ingekomen en Uitgaande Stukken*, October–November 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-01 to 1816-11-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Petitions and administrative deliberations concerning the reinstatement of Court Translator J. C. Dietrée into colonial civil service; strategic rationale for employing an independent European translator to bypass Patih Danureja IV and correspond directly with high Javanese nobles and provincial bupatis; formal joint investigation into the violent armed assault, arson, and robbery of the Chinese tollgate (*bandar*) at Jatinom; investigative report compiled by Surakarta Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter with native police mantris; arson of the tollgate house and identification of Mas Sasradiwirya as the chief perpetrator.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Court Translator J. C. Dietrée, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Mas Sasradiwirya.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Jatinom, Jakarta (ANRI).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djocjo Brieven* No. 40, Court Translator (*Translateur der Javaansche Taal*), Tollgate arson (*Bandar Jatinom*), Village alarm (*kenthongan* / rice blocks), Mantri police, Joint inter-residency jurisdiction.
