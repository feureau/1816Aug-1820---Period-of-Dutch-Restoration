# Transcription of IMG_1492.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1492.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI H 698b, *Archief van Jogja 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-10 to 1819-12-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Multifaceted political, military, financial, and logistical residency dispatches: royal edible birds' nest tribute quotas delivered to Sultan HB IV (analyzing British reductions from 72 catties annually under the VOC to 18–24 catties); court mourning for Pangéran Arya Kusumayuda; distribution of official seal fees (*zegelgeld*) for investiture patents celebrating HB IV's coming of age; requisitioning 500 Yogyakarta corvée laborers for the Batang–Kalikuto highway at 2½ stuivers daily pay; and Nahuys van Burgst's landmark historical report to the Army Commander detailing the origins of the Sultan's European Cavalry Bodyguard established by Dr. Daniel Ainslie and Raffles in January 1816 to counter the Sepoy Plot, documenting its daily dragoon guard at the innermost portal of the royal *Dalêm*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Arya Kusumayuda (great-uncle of HB IV), Resident of Pekalongan, Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Major-General Commander of Netherlands Indies Army, Dr. Daniel Ainslie, Thomas Stamford Raffles, C.F. Engel.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Fort Vredeburg, Semarang, Batang, Kalikuto River, Klerom Kamiyana, Pêntul, Pacitan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Edible birds' nests (*catties*), Garèbèg Mulud, Seal fees (*zegelgeld*), Public highway corvée, Daily wage (*stuivers*), European Bodyguard (*Lijfwacht*), Sepoy Mutiny (1815), Dragoons, *Dalêm* sentry guard, *Pesanggrahan*.
