## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1393.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: AvJ 1817 [Yogya], July–November 1817 / Nahuys Dispatches to Commissarissen-Generaal & Raad van Financiën)
- **Date Range:** 1817-06-06 to 1817-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Fall of princely conspirators following the Malang revolt, archival lacunae from the 1812 British conquest, court ceremonial calendars, and the royal salt-trading monopoly along the Bengawan Solo river. In a dispatch from Surabaya dated 10 July 1817, Resident Nahuys reported the fallout from the Malang insurrection: Pangéran Purbanagara was dismissed as Bupati of Kediri and banished, his factional rival Pangéran Dipasana was appointed in his stead to lead the Sunan's troops in East Java, and Sunan Pakubuwana IV's brother, Pangéran Mangkubumi of Surakarta, fell into official disgrace and was barred from the royal presence at court (*pasowanan*). On 6 June, Nahuys notified financial authorities that official British correspondence from the critical transition of 29 May to 26 August 1812 was missing from the residency archives. Court religious observances are recorded for *Garèbèg Puasa* (15 August) and *Garèbèg Běsar* (22 October). Finally, on 13 September, Nahuys exposed the commercial mechanics of the Sultan’s salt trade: the royal vessel *Praau Penghoeypoeng* (Prahu Panglipur) voyaged to Gresik not for public provisioning, but as a private financial venture of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, who leased his royal river toll exemptions to commercial syndicates for 200 Spanish Dollars per vessel; once British authorities abolished river tollgates along the Bengawan Solo, the trade collapsed because the court had only been interested in selling tax exemptions for cash.

  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surabaya, Kediri [Kedhiri], Pasuruan, Malang, Yogyakarta [Yogya], Surakarta [Solo], Gresik [Grisik], Bengawan Solo [Solo River].

D.].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698b; transcripts of Yogyakarta Residency Dispatches (Juli–November 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Executive administrative summaries, fiscal exposés on royal commercial monopolies, and archival management reports.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the ruthless factional purging of dissident priyayi lines in Surakarta and Kediri, while revealing how traditional royal prerogatives (such as HB II's riverine toll exemptions) functioned as commercialized revenue concessions.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, royal dynasts (Purbanagara, Dipasana, Mangkubumi), the Commissarissen-Generaal, and indigenous riverine merchant guilds.
* **Archival Header:** Top line records volume and chronological markers (`H 698b. / AvJ 1817 / (Yogya) - 1817 - July - Nov. 1817`).
* **Chronological Flow:** Left column marks precise dispatch and festival dates (`10 July`, `6 June 1817`, `15 Aug 1817`, `22 Oct 1817`, `13 Sept 1817`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, correspondents, and festival rubrics on the left; verbatim political purges, archival audit notes, and riverine salt trade analyses on the right.
