30 Nov
NvB -> Insp. Finances Semarang:
Reports concerning Wang Pêniti / Pakimitang / Runtikan:
1) Wang Pêniti: [pin money / levy on goods at tollgates]: Not more than an enkripall unfair way of raising money by Chinese Tax-collector:
would be best to be abolished for lightening of Javanese and encouragement of trade:
as well as Pasarangs: (Market money) Very oppressive for the small man.

2) Wang Pakimitang: [Protection money]: Payment much otherwise - although no tari Tariffs by merchant / farmer for PROTECTION from the tax farmer of the Tollgate / Rangka for certainty of surets.

Adat lama: Nothing should be changed

3) Mutian: Raised at Pasar Gêdé / Imagiri / Têmbayat:
for the advantage of the guardians of the Royal Graves / separate from GOVNMT INCOME.
