# Transcription of IMG_1225.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1225.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' Bundel 41: *Aug. 1816 – Sept. 1816*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-16 to 1816-08-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official documents recording the British retrocession and restoration of Dutch authority across South-Central Java; Commissioners-General decree that John Crawfurd remain temporarily in Central Java to superintend the embarkation of departing British-Indian troops; Commandant Deeling reports from Klaten that withdrawing Sepoy detachments set fire to their local garrison kampong; formal retrocession ceremony held at Batavia on 19 August 1816; colonial government maintains the venereal disease hospital in Yogyakarta on the English footing while prohibiting new admissions; Chinese civil inheritance dispute involving Oei Binjoe over real estate in Yogyakarta and Semarang (Gang Tengah storehouse and 2,000 Spanish Dollars); Governor-General Van der Capellen demands an urgent accounting of the exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's seized gold and jewel treasures; Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk reports his arrival in Surakarta; Resident of Kedu reports an armed bandit raid burning 20 houses at Desa Brunjak.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** John Crawfurd, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Oei Binjoe, Resident Parvé (Semarang), Commandant Deeling.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Semarang (Gang Tengah), Klaten, Kedu (Kedoe), Desa Brunjak.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Djokjo Brieven (Bundel 41), Commissioners-General (*Commissarissen-Generaal / CCGG*), Retrocession of 19 August 1816, Venereal disease clinic, Spanish Dollars (`Sp. D.`), Sepoy garrison arson.
