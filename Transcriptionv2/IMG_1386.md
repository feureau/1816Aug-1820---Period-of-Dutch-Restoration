# Transcription of IMG_1386.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1386.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], Solo Brieven Bundel 20 No. 169: 12 Feb 1831 / Archive of Resident Smissaert: 21 Feb 1824)
- **Date Range:** 1824-02-21 to 1831-02-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Two distinct archival records illustrating aristocratic financial ruin from colonial land-rent cancellations (1824) and the post-Java War political surveillance of displaced priyayi elites (1831). The first entry records a joint formal undertaking dated 21 February 1824 by Pangéran Mangkubumi and Pangéran Dipanagara addressed to Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert; acting with the authorization of the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V and Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kencana), the two royal guardians declared their readiness to pay a massive indemnity of 20,000 Spanish Dollars to compensate for the cancellation of the lease on Major Nahuys van Burgst's country estate at Bedhaya, leaving any shortfall to Smissaert's discretion. The second document is an intelligence register compiled on 12 February 1831 by Commissioner for the Vorstenlanden J.I. van Nouhuys (Solo Brieven Bundel 20 No. 169) entitled "List of persons in Magelang, or at Courts whose behaviour merits attention from Dutch Government"; it catalogs disenfranchised bupatis dismissed following colonial annexations, including Pangéran Blitar in Magelang, former regents of Panaraga (Prawirabrata), Srengat (Bratawijaya), Kediri (Sasranubrata), and Pacé (Bratakusuma) residing in Surakarta, and former regents of Tunggul (Prawirasentika), Magetan (Sasrawinata), and Karta Trayen (Reksapraja) under surveillance in Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V [HB V], Ratu Kencana / Ratu Ageng [Ratu Agēng], Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [Bedhaya estate], J.I. van Nouhuys [Comm. of V.L.], Pangéran Blitar, Raden Ria Nuryawirata, Jayengtipura, Raden Tumenggung Aria Prawirabrata [Ex Reg. of Panaraga], Raden Panji Wiradiningrat, Raden Tumenggung Bratawijaya [Ex Reg. of Srengat], Raden Mas Tumenggung Ria Sasranubrata [Ex Reg. of Kedhiri], Raden Mas Tumenggung Bratakusuma [Ex Reg. of Pacé], Raden Rongga Prawirasentika [Ex Reg. of Tunggul], Raden Tumenggung Sasrawinata [Ex Reg. of Magetan], Raden Panji Prawirajasra, Raden Tumenggung Reksapraja [Ex Reg. Karta Trayen], Mas Ngabèhi Tirtawijaya, Demang Mas Danutirta.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Bedhaya estate, Yogyakarta, Magelang, Surakarta [Solo], Panaraga, Srengat, Kediri [Kedhiri], Pacé, Tunggul, Magetan, Karta Trayen.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Land rent indemnities (*landverhuur*), Bedhaya indemnity (20,000 Spanish Dollars), apanage sequester, royal guardianship (*voogdij*), political surveillance register (*politiek toezicht*), dismissed regents (*gewezen regenten*), Spanish Dollars [Sp. ### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI, Solo Brieven Bundel 20, No. 169 (12 Februari 1831: Commissaris van het Bestuur der Vorstenlanden J.I. van Nouhuys); correlated with Yogyakarta Residency Records (February 1824).
* **Document Nature:** Executive authorization letters regarding royal indemnity payments (1824) and colonial post-war political police muster-rolls of suspicious priyayi (1831).
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Links the severe fiscal extortion imposed on Dipanagara and Mangkubumi during the 1824 land-rent crisis directly to the pervasive disaffection of the eastern bupatis, whose systematic dispossession created the core leadership network of the Java War (1825–1830) and required continued Dutch surveillance in 1831.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident A.H. Smissaert, Commissioner J.I. van Nouhuys, Kraton guardians (Dipanagara, Mangkubumi, Ratu Ageng), and disenfranchised regional regents (*gewezen bupati*).
* **Dual Section Layout:** Top third transcribes the critical 1824 indemnity letter; bottom two-thirds transcribes the 1831 surveillance register organized by geographic residence.
* **Geographic Headings:** Lower register structured by regional loci (`at MAGELANG:`, `at SURAKARTA:`, `at YOGYAKARTA:`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, archival shelfmarks, and geographic headings on the left; verbatim indemnity amounts, royal signatories, and tabular lists of monitored bupatis on the right.
