Dj. Br. 60
Yogya Res. Letters
Jan - Jun. 1817 / Jan. - May 1817

Nahuys to Pres. Alg. Rekenkamer, 25 Jan. 1817:
Kruseman hands over Residency again to Nahuys.

BGG, 12 Jan. 1817 no. 1.
Res. Yogya / Solo Besides a guard of 6 Dutch soldiers under an N.C.O. also same of 7 PWP / Paku Alam troops.

B of Pres. & Raad van Finantiën, 13 Jan. 1817 no. 7:
Phasing out of circulation Balinese / war. & other sorts of native minted coins with copper Duits. [Margin: Native minted coins]

van Haak to Nahuys, 13 Jan. 1817:
* Nurawedana, Pěmagē of Desa Sěmbawa who had robbed desa of Brunjaek (Pekalongan) flees to Yogya, takes service as magang at court.

de Salis (Res. Kědhu) to Nahuys, 24 Jan. 1817:
Flight of Nurawijaya, Pěmangg of Magelang.
Leaves 2 horses / 2 pikes at House of R.T. Danunagara.
[pencilled: & Mantri of Purpadiwiria]

Lippe (Asst. Res. Solo) to Parvé (Res. Semarang) H.A., 3 Feb. 1817:
Govmnt. Inhabitants Pr. be th subjects to be allowed to fell timber in th areas.
If not opposed would have ruinous consequences for Teak forests in th - strict conservation necessary.

BGG. 26 Feb. 1817 no. 6:
(1) HB II wants R.Ay. Mistaningrat / Dewaningrat / Dayaningrat + Ratu K. Wulan to accompany him to Ambon.
(2) Mangkudiningrat \
    Měrtanara       / want to be excused journey & allowed to take service & govnmt.
Nahuys instructed:
(1) to ask HB II's wives if want to accompany HB II to Ambon.
(2) to report on behaviour of MP / MN see Adj.

van Haak to Nahuys, 3 Mar. 1817:
Letter of Sunan Relating Death of P. Danupaya in Solo.
