## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1448.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Ingekomen Berigten van de Residentie Tegal over het Inlandsch Onderwijs, 1819 & 1822)
- **Date Range:** 1819-04-29 to 1822-08-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Educational blueprints for public school networks across the Tegal Residency, demographic literacy projections, curricular specifications, and Governor-General Van der Capellen's 1822 instructions mandating strict secularism and Islamic moral instruction while outlawing Christian proselytization. In 1819, Resident J.W. Meilan reported that while children of bupatis and high priyayi received private tuition in Jawi (Arabic-script Malay) from district *penghulus* inside the *Regent's Dalem*, secular arithmetic was completely unknown, and religious teachers could not read or write Javanese script. Meilan proposed establishing 34 to 42 public primary schools across the three regencies of Tegal (18 schools), Brebes (12 schools), and Pemalang (12 schools), accommodating 2,100 pupils taught by salaried teachers earning f. 10 monthly. Out of Tegal's total population of 120,000 (including 4,000 children aged 8–10), Meilan projected that educating 2,000 pupils annually would achieve 66% literacy among the youth within a decade, provided attendance was legally enforced against parental demands for child agricultural labor. On 29 August 1822, following an on-site inspection tour by Governor-General Van der Capellen, official instructions finalized a three-point curricular framework: (1) government-printed small-format textbooks on Javanese grammar; (2) practical moral instruction based on official extracts from the Holy Qur'an; and (3) an absolute, strict ban prohibiting Christian doctrines from entering native schools or reading primers.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Resident of Tegal (J.W. Meilan), District Penghulus of Tegal, Brebes, and Pemalang, Bupatis of Tegal, Brebes, and Pemalang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegal, Brebes [Brèbès], Pemalang [Pamalang], Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Public primary schools (*volksonderwijs*), Javanese grammar primers, Jawi script, arithmetic, compulsory school attendance, Islamic moral curriculum (*Qur'an extracts*), strict prohibition of Christian doctrine, Van der Capellen's 1822 inspection tour.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Ingekomen Berigten over het Inlandsch Onderwijs (Residentie Tegal, 1819–1822).
* **Document Nature:** Educational planning memoranda, demographic literacy models, and state inspection directives.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates early liberal colonial educational theory under Van der Capellen, emphasizing vernacular literacy and Islamic ethical instruction while rigorously excluding Christian missionary influence to prevent native unrest.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Governor-General Van der Capellen, Resident Meilan, and regency bupatis.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries detail educational deficits and elite priyayi tutoring practices.
* **Two-Column Organization:** Statistical rubrics, school planning models, and inspection dates on the left; verbatim summaries of educational proposals, teacher salaries, demographic targets, and Van der Capellen's 1822 curricular guidelines on the right.
