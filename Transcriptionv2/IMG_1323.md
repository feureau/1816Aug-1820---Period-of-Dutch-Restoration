# Transcription of IMG_1323.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1323.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-16 to 1816-09-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak's 16 September 1816 dispatch to Governor-General Van der Capellen regarding the financial and military status of Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) and the Mangkunagaran Legion; cites the *Proces-Verbaal* of 16 August 1816; details the financial strain on the Mangkunagaran principality: supporting an extensive royal household on 1,200 Spanish Dollars annual subsidy and 500 cacahs (1,200 jung of sawah); severe impoverishment caused by maintaining, uniforming, and arming the 800-man Legion at his private expense since 1808; terms of British service: independent indigenous command under Prangwedana without European interference for internal operations, with a bilingual (Javanese/Malay) European officer seconded only during external defense; urges the Dutch Restoration Government to assume full salary and equipment upkeep to bind Prangwedana to Dutch colonial interests.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Herman Willem Daendels, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Sala / Mangkunagaran), The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Mangkunagaran Legion (created 1808), *Proces-Verbaal* of 16 August 1816, Military Subsidies (1,200 Sp. D.), *Cacah* (household metric), *Jung* (acreage metric), Command Autonomy.
