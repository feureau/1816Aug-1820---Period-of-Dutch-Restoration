### IJsseldijk 1816
**2. van IJsseldijk, Baud coll.**

* S [Sasradiningrat] tells van IJ = that govnmt. appoints him.
* Despite what happens to his father -> tries to bring PB IV -> govnmt. together.
* ? asks if S = trusted by PB IV.
* **p. 7 [Patih]**: S says that since Mangkubumi exiled to Batavia — no reasons to complain about PB IV.
  * says that -> (zijn) (zinw) "PB IV too refined and cunning to be fathomed" about secrets.
  * S says Remembers nothing about Reactions of PB IV to letter from British govnmt. telling of arrival of C-Gs.
  * Later told to go to Batavia — But only gets as far as Cirebon from where he was Recalled.
  * $\therefore$ van IJ breaks off conversation, determines to tackle PB IV personally on issue.

* **p. 8**: 2 Sept. 1816 fixed as day for public Reception of van IJ at the Sala court.
  * & Handing over of letters from Lt. Gov. Fendall & van der C -> Sunan.
  * 9 o'clock a.m. Reception. = cf Dagverhaal of Kruseman deals v ceremonial questions.
  * Tells Sunan of van der C's letter.
  * But has conversation in Javanese v PB IV first = cf Bijlage no. 6.
  * cf art. no. 6 of Instruction -> To ask special assurances of PB IV's loyalty to new Dutch govnmt.
