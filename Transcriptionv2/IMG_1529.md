# Transcription of IMG_1529.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1529.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Yogyakarta Residency Letters / Bundel PJ. Br. 51 B, *Afgaande Stukken 1820*, July–Nov. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-07-11 to 1820-11-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official order from Assistant Resident G.A. d'Abo to the artillery commander of Fort Lodewijk / De Geus directing a 15-gun royal artillery salute in honor of the Javanese-Islamic festival of Garebeg Sawal (Puasa / Eid al-Fitr); ongoing crisis regarding the smallpox vaccination campaign across Yogyakarta and Surakarta, reaffirming the operational reliance on local Islamic religious officials (*priests* / *santris*) working under European medical inspection; financial and logistical memorandum from Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst to the Inspector of Finance in Semarang concerning colonial salt administration, noting the impossibility of storing hygroscopic North Sea (*Pasisir*) salt in inland packhouses (*pakhuizen*) during the monsoon and permitting indigenous consumers to purchase local South Sea sun-evaporated salt; historical recollection by Nahuys regarding the Daendels regime (1808–1810) when he served in the Forestry Administration (*Administratie der Houtbosschen*), highlighting chronic failures to recruit forced forest corvée laborers (*blandhong*) from the Yogyakarta Kraton despite lavish promises; island-wide police circular dispatching a physical description and arrest warrant (*opsporing*) for a Buginese suspect accused of committing homicide in Kampong Cina.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** G.A. d'Abo, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Inspector of Finance (Semarang).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Solo), Fort Lodewijk / De Geus, Semarang, Pekalongan, Kampong Cina (Yogyakarta), Pasisir (North Coast), South Coast.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Afgaande Stukken*, Garebeg Puwasa (Garebeg Sawal), artillery salutes, cowpox vaccination (*koepok-inenting*), salt monopoly (*zoutregie*), *pakhuizen* (warehouses), *Administratie der Houtbosschen* (Forestry Administration), *blandhong* (corvée forestry laborers), Kampong Cina, police fugitive warrants (*signalement*).
