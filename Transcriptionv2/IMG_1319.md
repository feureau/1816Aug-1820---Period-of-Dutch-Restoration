# Transcription of IMG_1319.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1319.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie Schneither*, No. 108 & *MvK 4491*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-15 to 1826-09-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes connecting the 1816 Restoration dispatches with the dramatic 1826 wartime restoration of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh); cites Schneither dossier No. 108 regarding HB II's re-elevation at Buitenzorg (17 August 1826) and Yogyakarta (20 September 1826); audit of royal heirlooms (*pusaka*), regalia, and precious gems in the Kraton; notes that non-pusaka jewelry and gold ornaments were auctioned off by Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, with f 20,000–30,000 purchased by Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven; records Nahuys van Burgst’s proposal to preserve remaining assets for the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V; crucial historical insight regarding the blood-feud between HB II and Pangéran Dipanagara (because Dipanagara aided his father HB III during the 1812 British storming, HB II wanted him assassinated, ensuring Dipanagara would never submit to HB II during the Java War); MacGillavry’s report on leased lands (*verhuurde landen*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Pangéran Dipanagara (DN / Diponegoro), Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), G. J. Chr. Schneither, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton), Buitenzorg (Bogor), Semarang, Surabaya, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Schneither (No. 108 / 267a), *Pusaka* (sacred royal regalia), Auction of Court Jewels, Blood Feud between HB II and Dipanagara, *Verhuurde Landen* (agricultural land leases).
