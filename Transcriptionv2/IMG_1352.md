- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical survey and assessment of the lesser regents (*bupati*) and senior palace officials at Yogyakarta: Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk judges none of them to be particularly remarkable or competent, most having been appointed under British patronage by John Crawfurd (Cr.); Raden Tumenggung Mangkudipuran noted as being of common, non-noble birth; Raden Tumenggung Mintanagara noted as the son of Pangeran Demang; exhaustive review of the four sons of Kyai Adipati Purwadipura (all grandsons of the legendary founding Grand Vizier, Raden Adipati Danureja I):
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, John Crawfurd (Cr.), Raden Tumenggung Mangkudipuran, Raden Tumenggung Mintanagara, Pangeran Demang, Kyai Adipati Purwadipura, Raden Adipati Danureja I (D R I), Raden Tumenggung Jaganagara (Kyai Ngabèhi Jaganagara), Raden Sindadiwiria, Raden Tumenggung Mandura, Raden Sindunagara, Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (HB I), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (HB III), Raden Adipati Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Lesser regents (*bupati*), *gladhag* (transport / porterage guild), opium addiction, testament of HB I, Danurejan dynastic clan, Grand Vizier (*Radèn Adipati* / Patih), British colonial veto.
