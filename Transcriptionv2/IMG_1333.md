# Transcription of IMG_1333.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1333.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, pp. 16–22)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s second private diplomatic conference with Sunan Pakubuwana IV; investigation into prior British communications from Lieutenant-Governor John Fendall and the summoning of Patih Sasradiningrat II to Batavia; confrontation over the 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy and pervasive rumors of British reconquest within three to four years; Crown Prince Radèn Mas Sugandi’s (PB V) testimony denying knowledge of subversive plots; Van IJsseldijk’s explanation of the Congress of Vienna and the geopolitical doctrine of European equilibrium (the necessity of creating a powerful, independent Netherlands as a barrier against France, dictating the unanimous return of Java); the Sunan’s disingenuous, naive response expressing profound relief that Java would not be ceded to any third European power.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Mas Sugandi (Crown Prince / Sunan Pakubuwana V), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, John Fendall, Napoleon Bonaparte, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo Kraton), Batavia, Vienna (Congress of Vienna), France, Britain, The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy, Congress of Vienna Settlement, European Balance of Power, Barrier State Concept, Javanese Feigned Naivety (*Dissimulation*).
