H. de Stürler (Res.d Banyumas)
to v. d. Bosch,
27 Jan. 1832
*Banjar Negara* (Banyumas)

Only a few *Pesantrèns* in Banyumas.
Teachers not so skilful as on N. Coast.
Schools too orientated to Islam.

- 2 sons of Regent being instructed by Jav. Clerk of Reg.
- + 3 sons of pensioned Regent can read/write in latin characters. Learn't skill in 1 year.

Less fanaticism in Banyumas than other Residencies. Jav. / Malay. / Ar. / (lat. letters) No *Hajis* in Banyumas. *+ arithmetic* No Arabs.
*Banjar Negara* = one govt. school should be set up.
Teachers would have to come from other Residencies not suitable under supervision of Regent. Candidates in Banyumas.
To allay any suspicions anti-Religion. Kept in school.
*Successful pupils should enter govt. service.*

---

Res. of Rembang
to v. d. Bosch,
30 Nov. 1831.

Various *Pesantrèns* in Rembang.
Education mainly Religious Instruction.
*Teacher in every Regency* for Jav. language/writing. Monthly payment f. 8.

---

Res. of Besoeki / Banyuwangi
de Bruin Prince
to v. d. Bosch,
26 Nov. 1831.

5-600 *Religious schools in area*:
almost entirely orientated to Religion. Mostly reading Qur'an.
Young men only really learn how to write when taken on by Regents or Démangs.
Few pupils can read/write Malay-Jav. at all properly.
*Teachers would have to be sent from other Residencies.*
*Govt. schools:*
1. 3 large schools to be erected in Residency.
2. Educational writing materials / Books to be given out free.
3. Successful pupils to go into govt. service.
4. Instruction about Education to be given by govt.

---
