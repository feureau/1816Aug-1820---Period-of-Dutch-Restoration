# Transcription of IMG_1484.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1484.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A & Resolutions of the Governor-General, Nov.–Dec. 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-11-30 to 1819-12-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse high-level administrative, infrastructural, and agricultural resolutions: appointment (*BGG 30 Nov 1819 No. 16*) of C.A. van den Berg and J.A.V. de Stürler as language students (*élèves in de Javaansche taal*) stationed in Yogyakarta; suspension of Batang–Pekalongan highway corvée during the rainy season to allow Yogyakarta laborers to cultivate sawahs; Semarang cloth orders for the Moluccas; historic decree (*BGG 30 Dec 1819 No. 11*) formally concluding the Regency of Paku Alam I, mandating the return of the Royal State Seal (*Cap Kraton*) to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, and granting Paku Alam a token gift of f. 250; Kedu Residency contract for 800 pikuls monthly of Gamping limestone transported by native packhorse teams; and Opzichter Engel's technical silvicultural report from Pacitan critiquing Dadap tree spacing (planted 12 feet apart, providing insufficient shade), accompanied by a comprehensive register of 29 coffee and pepper estates across the Pacitan valley.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** C.A. van den Berg, J.A.V. de Stürler, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), C. Vos (Resident of Pekalongan), J. de Bruijn (Resident of Semarang), Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Breton van Groll (Secretary of Kedu), Demang Ningdinanai Wiradrana (of Gamping), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia, Pekalongan, Batang, Semarang, Moluccas, Kedu, Gamping, Pacitan (and 29 plantation villages).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *élèves in de Javaansche taal*, agricultural corvée release, Royal State Seal (*Cap Kraton*), termination of Regency, Gamping limestone quarries (*kalkbranders*), packhorse logistics (*pakpaarden*), *Dadap* shade canopy metrics, Pacitan coffee estate gazetteer.
