Letter in Jawi (Arabic characters) [Kyai Iman Sampurna crossed out]
given out by Iman Sampurna warning that in coming year Alip
(1820) a mortal/virulent plague will descend on Java called Phemit
Trak Nyoro. Following warning given to I.S. like a mist
Plague will come from West - countless numbers of soldiers will come in
shape of mosquitoes, millipedes, scorpions, snakes and tigers (each of which
will have a particular skill)
Plague from East Nyai Rara Kidul - + all her soldiers + Sunan Lawu
with his soldiers accompanied by Devils in Neraka will have all sorts
of weapons: awls, pikes, muskets, cangas, and snares. These soldiers hate
all men - who are not favourable to them + would cause them to meet
+ die unpleasantness. The proud ones will be struck through + pikes [*]
The gamblers will be opposed so that they do not gamble any longer - those who
arrogate themselves will be disappointed - the discontented will be put aside.
Immediately on the arrival of the Devils. "A sea of blood will be seen + many corpses
will be found" In order to oppose this + drive out the devils one will have to
do the five daily prayers. + singing of pzikr. // + giving of slametans + balls
of Rice - 1 for each person present // + few offerings to Ratu Agama.
Flesh of a chicken must be used. + sayur + Jangan asem + Bayêm. (Mênir
prayer to be used = Doorgol Jurah Blai / Kukanan / slamit soup)
(To guard against Earthly Dangers + assure good fortune) Each plate to be
covered + yellow serviette + each person present to utter confession of faith.
Once one knows it by heart - confession of faith to be spoken each
evening 44 x // "I believe in the Religions the Muhammadan which is the right [one],
whereby the Soul, which lies in the middle of the heart, is the Tree of Life, the root of the
Almighty, who must be feared and honoured. The form of the Prophet
Muhammad will appear to all those whose hearts are sincere, and are
pure in heart (opregt van gemoed, en zuiver van harte zijn) and those will also
enjoy worldly and Heavenly Blessings. I believe that all the miraculous
signs who may manifest themselves are from Almighty God - the true and
everlasting, wherefore each & every inhabitants of Java, both men + women are
warned by this and enjoined to believe firmly what they have learned from
the Holy Script. Contents of Kitab Munarat as follows: I try to get the
protection of the Almighty who is all present - who hears all and sees all -
so that the evil Devils may not seek me out and will not do anything to me
except let me live in peace // Those who pray to others but the Almighty
will be damned as great sinners/ and for that reason the descendants of the
kafir, will never reach Heaven. O great God, forgive all the sins I have
committed and let me be purified by your influence. And what in you is
pure love, please show that and impart that love to me. - The person who
stands fast in his Religion is like the following
See over. Yogya Brieven
1820 Dj. Br. 63 A
