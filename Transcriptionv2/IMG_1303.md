# Transcription of IMG_1303.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1303.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (P. H. van der Kemp [ed.], *Oost-Indië's Herstel in 1816*, 's-Gravenhage: Martinus Nijhoff, 1911, pp. 272–273)
- **Date Range:** 1816-07-29 to 1816-08-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resident Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst’s 25 August 1816 dispatch recounting celebrations in Yogyakarta for King William I's birthday; dynastic anxieties expressed by Ratu Ageng and her extraordinary personal intimacy with Nahuys (sharing tea cups and exchanging kisses); court docility during the minority of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV; P. H. van der Kemp's iconic historiographical passage contrasting this courtly Francophile decadence with the solitary, introspective wandering of the young Sultan’s elder half-brother, Pangéran Dipanagara, viewing Nahuys’s influence with profound religious disgust; Section 4: diplomatic negotiations regarding the retrocession of West Java, specifically British Secretary Charles Assey’s request for the immediate handover of Banten following warnings from Resident Lt. Col. Yule.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** P. H. van der Kemp, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Pangéran Dipanagara (Diponegoro), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), King William I of the Netherlands, Charles Assey, Lt. Col. Udny Yule, Dozy.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton and Residency), Banten (West Java), Batavia, Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** King's Birthday Celebrations (*'s Konings Verjaardag*), Convention of 23 July 1816 (Art. 5), Retrocession of Banten, *Babad Dipanagara*, Islamic Religious Reformism.
