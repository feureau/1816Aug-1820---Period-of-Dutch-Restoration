# Transcription of IMG_1354.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1354.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306, section 10 / page 14: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Nota voor den Provisioneel Resident den Majoor Nahuys te Djocjocarta*, 22 October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Final administrative articles of Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk's handover memorandum delivered to Provisional Resident Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst at Yogyakarta (22 October 1816); grievances of dismissed court officials: examination of the dismissed Raden Tumenggung Purwadipura (former bupati and commander of the failed 1810 expedition against Raden Rongga) and his loyal retainers, specifically Kyai Demang at Tangkisan (Matangrana) and Kyai Paliwara, who have been denied any alternative appointments and complain bitterly of harsh and unjust treatment; tragic humanitarian appeal of the venerable royal widow Raden Ayu Sumayuda: her husband, Raden Sumayuda, was murdered in 1788 by retainers of Pangeran Mangkunagara I (Raden Mas Said); following the murder, she had been forcibly captive within the palace (*dalěm*) of Mangkunagara I until she was liberated through the personal diplomatic intervention of Van IJsseldijk during his first residency (1786–1798); Raden Ayu Sumayuda begs for colonial assistance to improve her impoverished condition; dynastic pedigree: Raden Sumayuda was a descendant/son of Pangeran Rangga Jajar, an exalted 18th-century lineage that once commanded immense authority across Java; formal conclusion of the handover directive: *Nota voor den Provisioneel Resident den Majoor Nahuys te Djocjocarta*, dated Yogyakarta, 22 October 1816.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Raden Tumenggung Purwadipura, Kyai Demang (Tangkisan), Kyai Paliwara, Raden Ayu Sumayuda, Raden Sumayuda, Pangeran Mangkunagara I (Raden Mas Said / MN I), Pangeran Rangga Jajar.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djocjocarta / Yogya), Tangkisan (Matangrana), Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Handover memorandum (*Nota voor den Provisioneel Resident*), dismissed regents (*bupati*), 1788 murder by Mangkunagaran forces, captivity in the Mangkunagaran dalěm, Rangga Jajar lineage, aristocratic relief petition.
