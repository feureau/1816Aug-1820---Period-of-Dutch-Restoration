# Sept. 1816

**10 Sept. 1816 letter of van IJ from Sala:**
* Sunan's letter to C-G = not usually compliments to Comm-Gen. as in last letter of Sunan to Fendall.
* van IJ asks Patih of Sala -> says Sunan thinks it not suitable for a lesser to send compliments to a greater — when they had not met.
* But in last of Sunan's letters — same attachment for new Dutch govnmt as he had had for British.
  * Stiff phrasing, but not equivocal.
* **van IJ** — known attachment of Sunan to British not v. great.
  * But Patih assuers [assures] van IJ that Sunan has no other meaning.
  * pledging of loyalty / PB IV genuinely pleased at Return of Dutch.
* Despite talk v Patih, closed up in his heart. Necessary to keep an eye on Sunan.
  * Looseness / kortswijl of Sunan.
* Between English -> Sultan of Madura — closer relationship than C-G deem necessary.
* No cause for Disquiet / But should keep an eye on courts.
* Need for Troop Reinforcements in Java [Sumatra?].
