# Transcription of IMG_1315.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1315.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI, *Solo Brieven* / Diplomatic Address: Van IJsseldijk to PB IV, Leaf 3)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding leaf of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's formal diplomatic address to Sunan Pakubuwana IV of Surakarta; calls upon the Sunan to tender solemn assurances of fidelity and attachment to the Royal Netherlands Government; invokes the historic alliance once maintained under the VOC (*de nu geheel vernietigde Compagnie*) to be renewed for all eternity now that the Kingdom of the Netherlands has grown in sovereign power; dated Surakarta, 2 September 1816, signed by Van IJsseldijk and certified by official Javanese translator J. G. Krijgsman; accompanied by parallel Javanese text in *Aksara Jawa*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), J. G. Krijgsman (Jv. Translateur), King William I of the Netherlands.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Souracarta Kraton), Netherlands (Rijk van Nederland).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie (VOC / *de nu geheel vernietigde Compagnie*), Koninglyk Nederlands Gouvernement, Javanese Court Translator (*Jv. Translateur*), *Aksara Jawa*.
