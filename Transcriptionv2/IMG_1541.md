# Transcription of IMG_1541.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1541.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Portefeuille 6 No. 13, Nahuys van Burgst Archive; Surakarta Residency Dispatches, Feb.–March 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-15 to 1820-03-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** This folio captures the climax of the February–March 1820 administrative crisis in Surakarta, preserved in Leiden University Library (BPL 616, Port. 6 No. 13).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Panji Anom Binang, Priyayi Raja Manggala, Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara, Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Djogja), Batavia, Leiden (BPL archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Rijksbestierder* (Grand Vizier / Patih), royal *damnatio memoriae*, Kalang community, Batavian Republic (1808 Daendels treaty), 1812 Raffles treaty, dual residency commission, treaty integrity (*geen verandering in betrekkingen*).
