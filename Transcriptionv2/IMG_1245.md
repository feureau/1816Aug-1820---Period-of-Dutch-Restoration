# Transcription of IMG_1245.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1245.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 3: *Voorzieningsraad te Soerakarta*, December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1807 to 1816-12-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Judicial sessions of the *Voorzieningsraad* (Judicial Council / Court of Justice) at the Surakarta Kraton presided over by Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak alongside Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II and Court Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter; adjudication of disputed land rental contracts (*piagem*) involving European leaseholders (estate of Philip Franke) and Pangéran Mangkubumi regarding Desa Tegalur (1807–1816); land lease dispute between Ngabèhi Bobot and Chinese tenant Lim Otiet over Desa Kewaran.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Philip Franke, Ngabèhi Bobot, Lim Otiet, Jacob Andries van Braam, Radèn Tumenggung Wiradigda, Radèn Tumenggung Mangkuyuda, Radèn Tumenggung Wiraguna, Radèn Tumenggung Padmadipura.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Desa Tegalur, Desa Kewaran.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Voorzieningsraad te Soerakarta*, Piagem (royal land grant deed), Landhuur (land rent leases), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Kraton Surakarta.
