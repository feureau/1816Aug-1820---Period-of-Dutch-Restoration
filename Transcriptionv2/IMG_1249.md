# Transcription of IMG_1249.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1249.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: *Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djocjocarta* [AVJ] Gewoon 1816 / Residency Secret Letterbook)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-11 to 1816-10-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official diary and dispatch logbook of Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst during the inaugural months of the Dutch Restoration in Yogyakarta; Sultan's bodyguard details; Nahuys's critique of British public instructions intended to obscure treaty commitments; ceremonial raising of the Dutch tricolor flag with a 21-gun salute (14 August 1816); negotiations with John Crawfurd over the bi-annual 50,000 Spanish Dollar indemnity payment to Sultan Hamengkubuwana III; British prohibition of gambling and cockfighting; arrival of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk; departure of Ratu Kencana Wulan and consort retinue to Batavia to rejoin exiled Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II); financial dispute with Kedu Resident over tollgate incomes and royal stipends.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Paku Alam I, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Ratu Kencana Wulan, Radèn Ayu Murtiningrat, Radèn Ayu Jayaningrat, Radèn Ayu Dewaningrat, John Crawfurd, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Frans Gerhardus Valck.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Semarang, Batavia, Kedu (Magelang).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AVJ (*Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djocjocarta*), KITLV H 698b, Treaty of 1812, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Dutch flag salute, Tollgate farms (*bandar*).
