# Transcription of IMG_1227.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1227.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' Bundel 41 / Solo Brieven Bundel 37: *Population Figures 1816*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-19 to 1816-11-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historical correspondence and demographic/fiscal surveys compiled during the 1816 restoration; Sunan Pakubuwana IV communicates with Patih Sasradiningrat II in Semarang regarding the official retrocession ceremony in Surakarta, noting that the Resident gave assurances of treaty continuity, Commissioner Van IJsseldijk was en route to Solo, and Prince Dipakusuma Jr. had departed as Yogyakarta's envoy to Batavia; records the 1816 Surakarta census (derived from Raffles' survey) calculating a total population of 842,591 inhabitants categorized by gender, age, and province (Solo, Pajang, Mataram, Bagelèn, Sukawati, Gunung Kidul, Banyumas, and the Eastern Mancanagara); documents the personal credit and debt ledger of Kyai Murma Wijaya in Spanish Reals, recording heirloom weapon acquisitions (Javanese broadswords, daggers) and financial debts owed to officers of the Mangkunagaran Legion.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Pangéran Dipakusuma Jr., Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Pangéran Suryadipura, Que Ning Sing (Capitan Cina), Thomas Stamford Raffles.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Semarang, Pajang, Mataram, Bagelèn, Sukawati, Gunung Kidul, Banyumas, Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Solo Brieven (Bundel 37), Population census (1816), Kécu (armed bandits), Mangkunagaran Legion, Reals (`Sp. D.`), Pradata debt obligations.
