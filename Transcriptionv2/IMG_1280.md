# Transcription of IMG_1280.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1280.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djocjakarta Corr. [Dec. 1816] — Acting Resident to Governor-General*, Continuation from IMG_1279)
- **Date Range:** 1814-02-11 to 1816-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Final political marginalization of Prince Regent Pangéran Paku Alam I by late 1816, retaining only an honorary ceremonial seat at court; consolidation of executive power under Dutch-compliant Patih Danureja IV (*"an active and firm Resident can always guide him"*); four-tier judicial hierarchy in Yogyakarta (Patih's criminal court with *Jaksa* and *Pengulu* subject to mandatory Resident review; agrarian civil law administered by village heads and *Gunung* police; Resident's Court for Europeans, Chinese, and Arabs decreed by Raffles on 11 February 1814); persistence of territorial chaos caused by fragmented inter-court enclaves; fiscal accounting of colonial state monopolies (opium monopoly farm leased to Chinese for 332,000 Java Rupees, Pacitan lease at 3,658 J.R., Karangbolong bird's nests producing 24 pikuls / 132,000 J.R., Rawa bird's nests at 330 J.R., and postal stations at 100 J.R.).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Johan Diederik Kruseman.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Pacitan, Karangbolong, Rawa, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Judicial organization (*Pradata / Surambi / Resident's Court*), Jaksa, Pengulu, Enclave territorial fragmentation, Opium monopoly farm, Edible bird's nest monopoly, Java Rupees (J.R.), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
