# Transcription of IMG_1284.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1284.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Citations from P. H. van der Kemp, *Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1817, tot het Vertrek der Engelschen*, 's-Gravenhage: Martinus Nijhoff, 1913, pp. 26–28: *Banishment of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-14 to 1817-02-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official colonial proceedings resulting in the perpetual deportation of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) and his royal faction to Ambon; surveillance of HB II, Pangéran Mangkubumi, and the exiled Sultan of Banten at Weltevreden under Major Muller; acute panic in the Yogyakarta Kraton regarding HB II's potential return, rendering diplomatic assurances from Van IJsseldijk, Nahuys, and Crawfurd ineffective; colonial decrees of 10 January 1817 and 10 February 1817 (*Besluit No. 11*) ordering the banishment to Ambon of HB II, Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Mertasana, and Tumenggung Radèn Kasana Pinja for covertly spreading restoration rumors and reviving the *Kasepuhan* party; exile monthly maintenance stipends (1,500 Rijksdaalders for HB II; 1,100 Rijksdaalders for Mangkubumi); embarkation of the royal exiles from Batavia on 5 February 1817 aboard the warship *Reijgersbergen*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Mangkubumi, Sultan of Banten, Major Muller, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk, John Crawfurd, Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Mertasana, Tumenggung Radèn Kasana Pinja, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia (Weltevreden), Ambon (Amboina), Banten, The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Banishment decrees (*Besluit 10 Jan. & 10 Feb. 1817*), *Kasepuhan* party revival, Warship *Reijgersbergen*, Weltevreden military cantonment, Rijksdaalders (D.r. / RD).
