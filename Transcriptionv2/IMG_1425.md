# Transcription of IMG_1425.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1425.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 61: August–December 1818)
- **Date Range:** 1818-08-12 to 1818-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** De-monetization of local copper duiten, rescue of survivors from the wrecked French vessel *La Légère* along the Bagelèn coast, census warrants for European males, bulk textile consignments to the Moluccas, travel reimbursement for Assistant Resident R.C.N. d'Abo, and cross-border highway extortion in Bagelèn. On 12 August 1818, Resident of Semarang J. de Bruin repatriated f. 1,767.17.1 worth of old Yogyakarta copper *duiten* phased out under monetary reform decrees. By decrees of 16 and 18 September 1818, dispatches recorded the shipwreck of the French brig/sloop *La Légère* in heavy South Sea surf off Desa Wera (Wiro) in Bagelèn: four French sailors came ashore at Walang after two shipmates drowned in the breakers, receiving generous emergency hospitality from local Surakarta and Yogyakarta district heads Demang Wargakusuma and Demang Sasradiwiria, whom Resident Nahuys rewarded with fine silk clothes and two pistols each. On 21 September, Batavia requested an exhaustive census of all European males over 16 years of age residing in the Sultanate as of 1 January 1819. In September and October, Semarang ordered massive consignments of Javanese cottage textiles for the Moluccan market, requesting light blue *lurik* and specifically noting that blue-checked cloths and blue batiks were far more sought after than plain blue fabrics (50 *kodjes* lurik, 10 *kodjes* batik, 10 *kodjes* slendangs). On 19 October, Assistant Resident R.C.N. d'Abo was awarded f. 143 in relocation allowances for his December 1817 journey to Yogyakarta. On 9 November 1818, a highway robbery complaint was registered by Sikai Loo of Wedi (Bagelèn): his son-in-law Baru Keswi, transporting 17 pikuls of tobacco and garlic purchased in Kedu, was intercepted and plundered by pike-bearing retainers at Medono led by Kyai Risayuda, patih of Mas Demang Mertawijaya of Kali Gowong. Marginalia notes record settlement permissions for French nationals (W. Costau and P. d'Ingreville) upon payment of f. 110 in taxes.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo, J. de Bruin, Demang Wargakusuma, Demang Sasradiwiria, Kyai Risayuda, Mas Demang Mertawijaya, Sikai Loo, Baru Keswi, W. Costau, P. d'Ingreville, French shipwreck survivors (*La Légère*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djocja], Surakarta [Solo], Semarang, Bagelèn, Desa Wera [Wiro], Walang, Kedu [Kědhu], Wedi [Wedhi], Medono, Kali Gowong, Ambon [Moluccas], Anyer, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Copper *duiten* demonetization, Shipwreck (*La Légère*), South Sea rollers (*segara kidul*), priyayi hospitality, European male census (1819), textile exports to Moluccas (*lurik*, *batik*, *slendang*, *kodje* / *corge*), Assistant Residency of Yogyakarta, highway robbery / border confiscation, pike-bearing retainers.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 61 (Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven van de Residentie Jogjakarta, August–December 1818).
* **Document Nature:** Inter-residency correspondence, maritime casualty reports, imperial census circulars, commercial shipping manifests, and criminal assault depositions.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the day-to-day administrative complexity of Central Java: managing European maritime disasters on the treacherous southern shore, fostering cottage textile exports, and adjudicating border banditry between rival priyayi retainers.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Nahuys van Burgst, Assistant Resident R.C.N. d'Abo, Semarang Resident J. de Bruin, local district demangs, and coastal priyayi.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries register correspondence channels and dates (`Res. Djocja to Res. Solo / 31 Dec. 1818`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Margin entries recording European settler taxes and textile shipments to Ambon (`[Margin note: Res. Semarang to Nahuys 6 Dec. 1818]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, decree numbers, and correspondent markers on the left; substantive accounts of demonetized coin shipments, the French shipwreck narrative, demographic census orders, textile specifications, and border banditry on the right.
