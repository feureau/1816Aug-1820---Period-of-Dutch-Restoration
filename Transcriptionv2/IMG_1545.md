# Transcription of IMG_1545.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1545.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Residency Records & Algemeen Rijksarchief; *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, May–Sept. 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-05-13 to 1820-09-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resolution of Governor-General Van der Capellen in Council (13 May 1820 Nos. 32 & 33) awarding prestigious diplomatic gifts to former Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Sécaningrat) for providing crucial political and commercial intelligence regarding Dutch diplomatic missions to the coast of Borneo (Kalimantan), comprising an alabaster table clock, two ornamental alabaster vases, an English hunting rifle, and a monetary cash advance of Dfl 1,276; official quarterly payroll accounts (first quarter of 1820) for the native civil administration of the Semarang Regency and Pasisir dependencies (Semarang, Demak, Kendal, Kaliwungu, Grobogan), fixing monthly salaries for Kliwons (f. 20), District Heads / Demangs (f. 60), Assistant Demangs (f. 30), Mantris (f. 20), and mosque personnel (Pengulus for Grobogan, Wirosari, and Purwodadi at f. 8 per month alongside two Ketibs); infrastructure labor requisition (12 July 1820) by the Resident of Pekalongan to Yogyakarta Resident Nahuys van Burgst, demanding 500 corvée laborers from Yogyakarta to complete the strategic highway passing through the southern forest of Kedawung; Semarang military warehouse report (17 July 1820) detailing cavalry saddlecloths (*waltrap* / *schabrak*) and textile supplies (coronline shipments of *celas*, *ginghams*, and sarongs); colonial gazette decree (*Staatsblad 1820 No. 39a*, 11 Sept. 1820) reaffirming the British proclamation of 15 May 1812 imposing a head tax on domestic slaves (10 Spanish Dollars or 2.6 guilders in gold stuivers); royal letter from Sunan Pakubuwana IV to Nahuys (29 Sept. 1820) nominating the leadership of the Surakarta Mantri Anom company: Radèn Ngabèhi Sasrawijaya and Ki Ngabèhi Nayatruna.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Sécaningrat / Kapitan Cina), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Resident of Pekalongan, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Radèn Ngabèhi Sasrawijaya, Ki Ngabèhi Nayatruna.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogya), Surakarta (Sala), Semarang, Demak, Kendal, Kaliwungu, Grobogan, Wirosari, Purwodadi, Pekalongan, Kedawung, Borneo (Kalimantan).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, alabaster clock and vases, English hunting rifle, Pasisir native civil administration salary schedules, *demang* (district head), *mantri*, *pengulu*, *ketib*, corvée highway labor, military saddlecloths (*schabrak* / *waltrap*), Indian textiles (*celas*, *ginghams*), *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, slave head tax (*hoofdgeld op slaven*), Mantri Anom royal corps.
