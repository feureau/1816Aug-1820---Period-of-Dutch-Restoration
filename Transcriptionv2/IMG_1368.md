  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II / Sultan Sepuh), Pangeran Mangkubumi of Surakarta, Pangeran Purbaya, Crown Prince Sugandi (later PB V), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Ratu Kencana Wulan.

  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Kasepuhan* / *Kanoman* factions, Moluccas naval expedition, monthly exile stipend (1,500 & 1,100 Java Rupees), tollgate/market lease deduction (*bandaran* / *pasar*), *Dj. Br. 60* (Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60), royal favorite vacillation, entourage restriction.
