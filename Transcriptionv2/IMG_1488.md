# Transcription of IMG_1488.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1488.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Letters of Adriaan David Cornets de Groot Jr., Koninklijke Bibliotheek, The Hague, 1819, pt. 2)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-17 to 1819-10-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Vivid private correspondence of language cadet Adriaan David Cornets de Groot Jr. describing Governor-General Van der Capellen's vice-regal progress through Surakarta and Yogyakarta: Surakarta Resident Van Prehn incapacitated by apoplexy; an acute comparative analysis of the two capitals and their monarchs, disparaging the seventeen-year-old Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV as an unmannered, awkward "farmer's boy" (*boerenjonge / toute bête*) while extolling Sunan Pakubuwana IV as a paragon of courtly refinement who "would grace a European Court"; and an expedition to the sacred South Coast (crossing the Opak River at Mancingan via the Chinese toll ferry, bathing in the thermal springs of Parangwèdang, inspecting the cliff caves of Parangtritis, and being dissuaded by Resident Nahuys from the perilous descent to the mystical hermitage of Guwa Langsé).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Adriaan David Cornets de Groot Jr., Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Rijck van Prehn (Provisional Resident of Surakarta), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Opak River ferry, Mancingan, Parangwèdang hot springs, Parangtritis caves, Guwa Langsé (sacred sea cave), Southern Ocean (*Segara Kidul*), The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Gubernatorial Grand Tour, Courtly refinement (*halus*) vs. rural coarseness (*kasar* / *boerenjonge*), Sacred royal geography of Mataram, Nyai Rara Kidul shrines, Guwa Langsé royal retreat, Opak ferry tax farm.
