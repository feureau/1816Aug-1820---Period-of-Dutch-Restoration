* **p. 9**: afterwards Van der C's letter & Javanese translation handed over.
  * But explanation in Malay necessary — Because govnmt. Translator in Samarang (J. Krijgsman) translates letters into Jav. v. Badly —
  * pretends to know court Jav. Better than officials in Batavia.
  * PB IV opens letter himself & Reads Jav. translation.
* **p. 10**: Reason for explanation of Dispatch of van IJ to Court By Van der C.
  * & sign of friendship / Renewal of Trust PB IV - new Dutch govnmt.
  * v. gracious Reply of PB IV
  * But indications of genuine feelings at time of Departure for Restoration of Dutch govnmt.
  * & following Day = PB IV to make a Return visit to Residency. v court. See Dagverhaal.
* **p. 11**: 4 & 8 Sept. two separate conferences v PB IV (aparte conferentiën).
  * in accord v 2 article of Instruction -> to give PB IV an Idea of an increase in power of kingdom of Holland.
  * & To ask PB IV way in which English had broken news of handing Back of Java.
  * [**Rumours of Brit. Return.**]
  * & to Dispel Rumours spread by British in Sala & other parts of Java that Return of Island entirely conditional on British, & in 2 or 3 years British would Be Returned.
