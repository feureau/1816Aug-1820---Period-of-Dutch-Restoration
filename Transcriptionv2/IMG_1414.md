# Transcription of IMG_1414.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1414.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a / H 698b: Algemeen Verslag Solo [AvS], Gewone Rapporten 1817 & 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1817-10-05 to 1819-10-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical diplomatic assessments of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV's court, the birth of the Yogyakarta Crown Prince (future Sultan Hamengkubuwana V), security origins of the European Sultan's Guard, imperial itineraries for Governor-General Van der Capellen, comprehensive inland waterways navigability surveys across Central and East Java, and highway planning through the Weleri forest. On 5 October 1817, the Commissioners-General proceeded from Salatiga to Surakarta. An excerpt from Commissioner Goldberg (15 October 1817) delivers a scathing assessment of the 17-year-old Sultan HB IV as intellectually pliant and dominated by his mother (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng) and stepmother (Ratu Kencana Wulan, consort of the banished Sultan HB II), noting that his consort had recently delivered a son (born 8 September 1817, installed as Crown Prince, future HB V). Entries from 1819 include an intelligence report from Rembang concerning the Pati of Panolan; a revelation by the Resident of Yogyakarta that the European Sultan's Guard was formed in January 1816 in direct response to the 1815 Sepoy mutiny conspiracy; ceremonial itineraries for Governor-General Van der Capellen's visit to Surakarta (29–31 August 1819); an extensive hydrographic report on the Bengawan Solo demonstrating navigability from Ketiwang to Gresik (16–18 days transport, prahus carrying 50–60 *koyangs* of rice and salt, requiring lighters at Ngawi), alongside comparative river surveys for Bagelèn, Banyumas, Kediri (Kali Peleburan), Pace, Jagaraga, and Panaraga (Kali Semangka); and joint priyayi surveys laying out a new post road through the Weleri forest running 12 leagues across *Vorstenlanden* territory.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Johannes Goldberg, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V (infant Crown Prince), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Ibu), Ratu Kencana Wulan, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Resident of Surakarta, Resident of Yogyakarta, Resident of Rembang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Salatiga, Surakarta [Solo], Yogyakarta [Djogja], Panolan, Ketiwang [Kiduang], Gresik, Ngawi, Bagelèn, Banyumas, Kediri, Pacé, Jagaraga, Panaraga, Weleri forest.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Commissarissen-Generaal, Bengawan Solo navigability, *Prahu Panglipur*, *koyang* [bulk measure], Sepoy conspiracy (1815), European Sultan's Guard, *Vorstenlanden* [VK], road engineering, *Manca Nagara*.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698a / H 698b (Algemeen Verslag Solo: Gewone Rapporten en Brieven, 1817–1819).
* **Document Nature:** Executive diplomatic assessments, biographical extracts, hydrographic surveys, infrastructure plans, and ceremonial itineraries.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Combines contemporary Dutch political evaluations of the Yogyakarta Kraton elite with strategic infrastructure surveys (riverine commerce on the Bengawan Solo and new post roads) underpinning colonial state-building.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Governor-General Van der Capellen, Commissioner Goldberg, Residents of Solo and Yogya, and Surakarta royal commissioners.
* **Archival Header:** Top entries classify the Surakarta general report series (`H 698 b. a / AvS. (Gewoon) / 1817 (Solo) / [red ink: Solo 1817]`).
* **Marginal Annotations:** Substantial right-margin scholarly excerpt recording Commissioner Goldberg's famous portrait of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (`[right margin note: Goldberg 15 Oct. 1817]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, correspondent headings, and archival years on the left; substantive diplomatic evaluations, Sepoy mutiny retrospects, river navigability tables, and road surveys on the right.
