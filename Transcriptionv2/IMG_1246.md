# Transcription of IMG_1246.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1246.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 3: *Voorzieningsraad te Soerakarta* & Judicial Correspondence)
- **Date Range:** 1816-12-12 to 1818-04-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion of Ngabèhi Bobot vs Lim Otiet land litigation with Bobot reinstated and arrears awarded; colonial decree of 9 April 1818 (*Besluit 3*) curtailing Sunan's judicial sovereignty by requiring High Court of Batavia (*Hoog Gerechtshof*) review of all capital sentences and exiling decrees, and transferring Eastern Salient (Besuki/Banyuwangi) cases to Semarang; Sharia capital homicide ruling submitted on 3 March 1817 by Surakarta *Pengulu* regarding Djang Tiban's murder of Radèn Ajeng Kilda (infant daughter of Pangéran Mangkubumi) under Islamic jurisprudence (*Kitab Muharram*, *Kitab Muhalali*, *Ibnu Hadjar*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Radèn Ajeng Kilda, Djang Tiban, Ngabèhi Bobot, Lim Otiet, Surakarta Pengulu.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Batavia, Banyuwangi, Besuki.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Voorzieningsraad, High Court of Batavia (*Hoog Gerechtshof*), Pengulu / Surambi court, Kitab Muharram / Muhalali / Ibnu Hadjar (Islamic jurisprudence texts), Kris, Capital sentence ratification.
