# Yogyakarta Aug 1816
## Nahuys to Commissioners General

P.H. van der Kemp, *Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1817, tot het vertrek der Engelschen naar oorspronkelijke stukken.*
's-Gravenhage, 1913.

* **p. 24**: Nahuys reaches Yogya 9 August before arrival of Dutch troops.
* (14 Aug.) takes over authority from Crawfurd.

### Nahuys to Commissioners-General, 11 Aug. 1816 no. 2.
* **p. 320 Bijlage III**: HB IV very obedient to Paku Alam (Regent) + Patih.
* Crawfurd does much to reassure Yogya court concerning handing back of Java to Dutch.
* Ratu Agöng — 3x entrusts HB IV into care of Nahuys.
* Brit. Appt. Resident remains; Crawfurd very much esteemed by Paku Alam, Danuredjo [Danurêdjo] Patih, Ratu Agöng.
* Nahuys able to see this for himself at Party in Residency House before Crawfurd's departure.
* **p. 321**: Crawfurd very much loved at the court. Nahuys to continue ('s) style of administration.
* Paku Alam owes life to Nahuys cf saves him from Daendels' execution order. [*]
* Very pleased at appointment of Nahuys.
* P.A. afraid that a friend of Daendels would be appointed — cf *Herstel*, p. 223. Tiedeman first appointed to Yogya, earlier governt. Sec. to Daendels. [*]
* Paku Alam legion to be taken into Dutch service — everything prepared.
