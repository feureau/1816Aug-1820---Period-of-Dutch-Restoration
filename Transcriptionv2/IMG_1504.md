# Transcription of IMG_1504.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1504.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 139 / ANRI Solo 139, *Translaaten en Verbaalen 1817 tot 1818*) (Archetype 1: Archival Translation (Contemporary Dutch Translation by J.W. Winter, Final Folio))
- **Date Range:** 1818-12-01 to 1819-08-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding folio and legal colophon of Surakarta translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter's Dutch translation of the Ajibarang/Banyumas prophecy: finalizing the chain of petition to the Grand Vizier (*den Rijksbestierder*), the two reigning monarchs of Mataram, and Governor-General Van der Capellen; establishing that the sacred court pusaka cannon *Nyai Setomi* (*het kanon Nyai Stommie*) belongs by divine right to the Governor-General; praising Van der Capellen's supernatural insight into the intentions of all mortals; dedicating the letter to the "mighty Raden General"; and concluding with the official translator's signature and date at Surakarta on 22 August 1819.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (*den Rijksbestierder*), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (*vader de Generaal*), Baroness van der Capellen (*de Princes*), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (translator).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Soerakarta / Solo), Yogyakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Rijksbestierder* (Grand Vizier / Patih), *De beide vorsten* (the two sovereigns of Surakarta and Yogyakarta), Sacred pusaka cannon *Nyai Setomi* (*Nyai Stommie*), Divine omniscience, Official translation colophon (*Vertaald te Soerakarta*).

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Collection:** Peter Carey Archival Research Notes
* **Archive Reference:** Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 139 (ANRI Solo 139, *Translaaten en Verbaalen 1817–1818*, Folio 3)
* **Subject / Context:** Concluding text of the official Dutch translation of the 1818 Ajibarang prophecy, establishing the transfer of royal pusaka legitimacy to the Dutch Governor-General, certified by J.W. Winter.
