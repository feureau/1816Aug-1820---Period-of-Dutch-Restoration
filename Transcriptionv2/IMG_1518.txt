State of Solo Court in 1824 (cont.)
Setting up of Regency + Resident:
way of giving govnmnt. greater say in affairs of Solo
in accordt. G.G.'s secret decision of
9 Sept. 1823.
* If PB VI persists in behaviour should be forced to abdicate in
favour of P. Purbaya
same as had happened in Palembang, see Woelders.
Similarities courts of Solo / Palembang,
although Jav. nobles not so disharmonious + morally reprehensible
as those of Palembang, also less cunning / hypocritical.
Following proposals = (1) Permission to dissolve marriage: PB VI - Ratu Mas.
(2) Actions of PB VI make it necessary to take
control of Admin. away from him.
(3) Setting up of Regency Res / Buminata / Patih
Pang. Buminata to be given title of Pan. to give him more
Influence when appointed as Regent.
Buminata does not want throne of Solo because has no children:
Purbaya to be given title of P. Adipati = title earlier given to all
legal sons of Ruler but fallen into disuetude after
Kartasura war: although continues to be given to
Crown Prince P. Adip. Anom.
