# Transcription of IMG_1346.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1346.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816; and *Nota voor den Prov. Res.*, 22 October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Ongoing illicit correspondence maintained between the exiled former monarch Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) in Batavia and loyalist courtiers in Yogyakarta, actively plotting his restoration to the Sultanate; a clandestine delegation of 50 Yogyakarta subjects travels to Batavia to pay homage and pledge fealty to HB II; Pangeran Paku Alam I and several senior bupatis hold secret conferences with Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk to register their strenuous protests against any contemplated return of HB II; key members of the royal family likewise petition against his return; analysis of Sunan Pakubuwana IV's opportunistic geopolitical strategy: PB IV would exploit the return of HB II to plunge Yogyakarta into fratricidal war and expand Surakarta's hegemony; historical retrospective on the 1812 Anglo-Javanese crisis: PB IV had prepared a coordinated plan to slaughter all Europeans in Surakarta if the British assault on Yogyakarta had been repulsed; review of Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III's 1810 rebellion: covert instigation and sympathy provided by PB IV; Raden Rongga's mystical messianism, claiming the identity of *Batara Guru* (the supreme deity in the Javanese *wayang* cosmos); Rongga diagnosed as the tragic victim and pawn of dual conspiracies hatched between Surakarta and Yogyakarta; categorical policy recommendations: Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Pangeran Mangkudiningrat I, and Pangeran Mangkubumi of Surakarta must be exiled permanently from Java; Ratu Kencana Wulan (HB II's former favorite) may be permitted to join HB II in exile if necessary, though she is not keen to do so and remains deeply mistrusted by Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu) and Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II / Sultan Sepuh), Pangeran Natakusuma / Paku Alam I, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III (R. Rangga), Pangeran Mangkudiningrat I (MD), Pangeran Mangkubumi of Surakarta (MB of Sala), Ratu Kencana Wulan, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia, Surakarta (Sala), Madiun, Pulau Pinang, Ambon.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sultanate restoration plots, clandestine correspondence, Batara Guru (*wayang* mythology), Raden Rongga's rebellion (1810), anti-European conspiracy (1812), permanent overseas exile, *kasepuhan* faction.
