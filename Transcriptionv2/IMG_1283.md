# Transcription of IMG_1283.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1283.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive; and Nationaal Archief The Hague, Private Collection of J. C. Baud No. 306: *Secret Letters from Solo*, September–November 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1812-06 to 1816-11-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official gubernatorial dispatch of 19 November 1816 ordering an exhaustive inquiry into Sunan Pakubuwana IV's complicity with Kyai Murma; Resident Pinket van Haak's report of 27 November 1816 denouncing PB IV as an untrustworthy ruler; strategic military memorandum from Pinket van Haak to Governor-General Van der Capellen (16 September 1816) evaluating the political and military role of Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II); monthly colonial subvention of 1,200 Spanish Dollars granted for the maintenance of the Mangkunagaran Legion; the widening personal rift between Prangwedana and Sunan PB IV; Lt. Col. von Jett's military inspection report; complete organizational muster roll and troop strength table of the Mangkunagaran Legion as of 22 August 1816 (730 officers and men across infantry, cavalry, and horse artillery).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Aria Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Jean Chrétien Baud, Lieutenant-Colonel von Jett.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Mangkunagaran compound, Leiden, The Hague.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Mangkunagaran Legion, Regimental muster roll, Horse artillery (3-pounders), Military subvention, Balance of power doctrine, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
