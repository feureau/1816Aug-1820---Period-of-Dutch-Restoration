SOLO 1815 [1815]
1815. QUESTION OF SEPOY TROOPS: 1815 MUTINY

LAWICK VAN PABST.
Lawick van Pabst Report
10 Mar 1827 - Oct 1827
(Res of Yogya)

Considers P.B. IV MUCH MORE A DANGEROUS PERSON THAN H.B. II
in contrary to what was in many ways:

Secret - affairs - Lawick present at TRIAL
Chief of Sepoys if rebellion successful
would have taken TITLE of GOV-GENERAL.
und OUBELOT VERANDSH.

One of members of COUNCIL OF WAR: Council of Sepoy officers:
was presided over by a EUROPEAN OFFICER
Bedwell at that time Res of SEMARANG.
(Yekke knew about this + killed)

BEDWELL: takes part in OATH:
but when discovered escapes secretly
call on morning of execution to and x throat cut.

Under cover of darkness. Marches troops (t other members of Semarang to council given)
from Suonoal [Srondol].

PLOT UNCOVERED as follows:
On very night that mutiny to break out:
European officers sit at same TABLE
t Resident of Semarang.

One officer goes outside in the dark.
(2 guards)
Not seen by sepoy troops who are discussing
whether to kill officers or not.

Returns to TABLE - and tells companions situation.
Afterwards: 2 sepoys questioned
PLOT UNCOVERED: 50 sepoys
sent back in IRONS -> Bengal.
Plot not over when island
returned to Dutch.
