# Transcription of IMG_1533.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1533.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, October 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-17 to 1820-10-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official vice-regal instruction and administrative minute from the colonial government in Batavia directing the Resident of Yogyakarta to make formal diplomatic notification to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV regarding the demise of Sunan Pakubuwana IV on 1 October 1820 and the succession of the Crown Prince as Sunan Pakubuwana V in Surakarta, maintaining high dynastic protocol between the two rival courts of the Vorstenlanden.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (Sultan Séda Besiyar), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Sunan Pakubuwana V, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya / Djogja), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, diplomatic notifications, royal succession (*troonsopvolging*), inter-court protocol (*kraton* etiquette).
