# Transcription of IMG_1464.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1464.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI Exh. 31 Dec. 1832, No. 2065 Kabinet: *Pertilangan Pesantrèn Pamakasan*, Folio 2)
- **Date Range:** 1831-10-05 to 1832-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding folio of the official statistical census of Islamic *pesantrens* and recitation centers across the Regency of Pamekasan (Madura), listing entries 71 through 97, concluding with the official signatures of the traditional ruler Panembahan Mangkuadiningrat of Pamekasan and Resident of Surabaya Hendrik Jacob Domis. Entries 71 to 85 detail rural centers across villages such as Tana Balie, Pagendengan, Balakkan, Kadaan, Candi, Tambong, Balembongan, and Toronnan. Entries 86 to 97 catalog twelve urban Islamic academies clustered in the capital (*Kotta Pamakasan*), centered around the Great Mosque (*Maka, masdjet*) and surrounding wards (Cabi, Kunci, Kojappar, Krajan, and Batok Kramat). On 5 October 1831, the official census was formally ratified and signed by Panembahan Mangkuadiningrat of Pamekasan (*De Panëmbahan van Pamakassan*), and countersigned as verified (*Accordeert*) by Resident H.J. Domis.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Panembahan Mangkuadiningrat of Pamekasan, Hendrik Jacob Domis (Resident of Surabaya), Johannes van den Bosch, Madurese Kyais and Mosque Officials.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pamekasan [Pamakassan / Madura], Kota Pamekasan [Kotta], Candi [Tjandie], Balembongan, Surabaya, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Pertilangan Pesantrèn*, *Panëmbahan van Pamakassan*, urban mosque schools (*masdjet*), official ratification, *Accordeert*, ANRI Kabinet Exh. 31 Dec. 1832 No. 2065.
