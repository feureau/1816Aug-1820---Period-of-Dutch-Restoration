# Transcription of IMG_1450.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1450.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Ingekomen Berigten over het Inlandsch Onderwijs, 1819: Rembang, Jipang, Gresik, Surabaya, and Pasuruan)
- **Date Range:** 1819-04-19 to 1819-06-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Exhaustive ethnographic audit of the traditional Islamic educational curriculum (*pesantren* / *langgar*), higher Islamic pilgrimage scholarship, village literacy levels, and state school proposals across Eastern Java (Rembang, Jipang, Tuban, Gresik, Surabaya, and Pasuruan). In Rembang (200 pupils) and Tuban (100 pupils), advanced students seeking theological scholarship traveled to renowned centers in Ponorogo (*Pesantren Tegalsari*) or Madiun. In Jipang/Panolan, instruction was conducted by mosque *ketibs*, with *modins* stationed across Padangan (2), Bauwerno (1), Rajegwesi (2), and Blora (8). The report documents the classical eleven-stage curriculum of Islamic learning: (1) *Alif-alifan* (alphabet); (2) *Turutan* (*Al-Fatihah* & *Juz 'Amma*); (3) *Nyasin* (*Surah Ya Sin*); (4) *Al-Qur'an Alif-Lam*; (5) *Kitab Asmarakandi* (theology of Al-Samarqandi); (6) *Kitab Wiqayah* (*Wiqayat al-Riwayah* / fiqh); (7) *Kitab Surat*; (8) *Kitab Nahwu* (Arabic syntax); (9) *Kitab Syarah*; (10) *Kitab 'Amil* (*Al-'Awamil* in grammar); and (11) *Kitab Jurumiyyah* (*Al-Ajrumiyyah*), noting that mastering the final four texts unlocked the full theological meaning of the Qur'an. In larger pesantrens, senior students acted as teaching assistants (*badal* / undermasters). In Gresik (1,400 villages), Resident dispatches noted that boys over seven received free prayer training from village priests, while priyayi sons were tutored at home in Javanese and Malay; the Resident proposed a 300-teacher network funded by a school tax on village *lurahs* (2½ stuivers per guilder of tax). Dispatches from Surabaya and Pasuruan confirmed that Islamic teachers were supported exclusively through community tithes (*Zakat* and *Zakat Fitrah*), emphasizing the severe cultural difficulty of imposing compulsory secular schooling upon peasant families.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Resident of Rembang, Resident of Gresik, Resident of Surabaya, Resident of Pasuruan, Village Modins, Ketibs of Panolan and Jipang, Pesantren teachers of Ponorogo (Tegalsari) and Madiun.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Rembang [Rimbang], Tuban, Jipang, Panolan, Padangan [Badangan], Bauwerno [Bauwërna], Rajegwesi, Blora, Ponorogo [Pasaraga], Madiun, Gresik [Grësik], Surabaya, Pasuruan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Islamic curriculum (*Alif-alifan*, *Turutan*, *Nyasin*, *Kitab Asmarakandi*, *Jurumiyyah*, *Nahwu*), *Langgar* schooling (6:30–10:00 AM), senior pupil undermasters (*badal*), *Pesantren Tegalsari* (Ponorogo), village education tax, *Zakat* & *Beras Fitrah*, *krama* and *ngoko* registers.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI Ingekomen Berigten over het Inlandsch Onderwijs, 1819 (General Secretariate Dossier: East Java Residencies).
* **Document Nature:** Educational survey returns, curriculum inventories, fiscal school proposals, and ethnographic descriptions of Islamic village pedagogy.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Completes the monumental 1819 educational inquest, providing the definitive contemporary Western inventory of Islamic scholasticism (*pesantren* learning) across Java before the colonial state established secular primary schools.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Residents of Rembang, Gresik, Surabaya, and Pasuruan, alongside native Islamic religious authorities (*kyai*, *ketib*, *modin*).
* **Archival Header:** Top entries detail regional pupil enrollments and the ecclesiastical network of the Jipang-Rembang borderlands.
* **Two-Column Organization:** Regional headings, curriculum stages, and taxation models on the left; verbatim transcriptions of pupil counts, the 11-stage Islamic syllabus, daily school hours, and residency fiscal proposals on the right.
