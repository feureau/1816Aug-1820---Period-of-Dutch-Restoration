# Transcription of IMG_1485.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1485.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 131, *LEDAYA en KEDHIRI verhalen* / *Verbalen en Rapporten Solo 1816–1818*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-02-11 to 1819-02-17
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Extraordinary archival file detailing the mystical catechism, ascetic practices, occult folklore, and state interrogation of Kyai Iman Sampurna: the Jawi text translated into Dutch by J.W. Winter recording the esoteric dialogue of the "Land of the Half-People" (*het land van de halve menschen / het oude geslacht*), obedience to the *Ratu Panatagama* of Mataram royal blood, spiritual devotion to *Raden Putra* (residing in a space small as a peppercorn), and mystical communion with *Bok Fatimah* (Fatimah az-Zahra); intelligence report by Kedu Resident F.E. Hardy recounting Sampurna's vigils on Mount Lawu, his conversion of an ancient Brahmin shrine (*sanggar*) into an Islamic sanctuary in the weretiger-infested forest of Lodhaya (*alas Lodhaya / macan gadungan*), and his distribution of apocalyptic circulars under threat of tiger attacks; his arrest and examination before the Surakarta Pengulu; and the expert judicial assessment of Raden Tumenggung Danuningrat of Magelang (translated by F.G. Valck) finding no treasonous intent in the Qur'anic-based text.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pèndita / Kyai Iman Sampurna, Prophet Muhammad (Rasulullah), Raden Putra, Bok Fatimah (Fatimah az-Zahra), F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pengulu of Surakarta (and son, the Ketib), Kyai Abdul Rahman (of Ampel Denta, Surabaya), Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Bupati of Magelang), Frans Gerardus Valck (Secretary of Kedu), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (Surakarta translator).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Lodhaya (Blitar / Kediri), Mount Lawu, Surakarta (Solo), Magelang (Kedu), Ngampel Denta (Surabaya), Mataram.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Halve menschen* (Half-people / the ancient primordial race), *Ratu Panatagama*, *Raden Putra*, *Bok Fatimah*, Hindu-Buddhist *sanggar* (sanctuary), *macan gadungan* (weretigers), *wayang kulit*, *gamelan*, *slametans*, Islamic heresy tribunal, Qur'anic textual exegesis.
