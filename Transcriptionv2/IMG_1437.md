# Transcription of IMG_1437.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1437.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5: Secret Letters from Solo / Van Prehn to Algemeen Secretaris, 30 March 1818)
- **Date Range:** 1818-03-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Opening folio of Secret Letter No. 2 dispatched by Resident of Surakarta Rijck van Prehn to the Secretary of State of Governor-General Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, resolving the high-stakes succession crisis inside the Kasunanan Kraton. Van Prehn expresses satisfaction at being able to bring certainty to the colonial government regarding the true intentions of Sunan Pakubuwana IV, an affair previously enveloped in secrecy. Acting on confidential intelligence supplied by an eminent and trusted Javanese nobleman (*Javaan van aanzien en vertrouwens*), Van Prehn learned that Crown Prince Sugandi (future Sunan Pakubuwana V), and NOT his charismatic rival Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII)—contrary to universal public assumption—was the Sunan’s sole beloved son and designated heir. Van Prehn describes orchestrating a confidential personal dialogue directly with Sunan PB IV during an evening banquet hosted at the Residency House to celebrate the Resident’s birthday, where the monarch opened his heart on dynastic succession.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (Sugandi / future Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Secretaris van Staat.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Soerakarta], Kraton Surakarta, Residency House, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Secret No. 2*, Surakarta throne succession (*Troonopvolging*), *Kroonprins*, dynastic factions, private birthday banquet diplomacy.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portfolio 6, Part 5 (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Papers, Secret Letters from Solo, 1818).
* **Document Nature:** Secret executive dispatch (*Geheime Missive*), verbatim diplomatic reportage, and high-level palace intelligence.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Represents the foundational diplomatic text establishing the legal succession of Sunan Pakubuwana V, disproving widespread colonial rumors of a palace coup favoring Pangéran Purbaya.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Resident Rijck van Prehn, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, and Governor-General Van der Capellen.
