# Transcription of IMG_1250.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1250.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Algemeen Verslag der Residentie Djokjokarta 1816* / ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundels 40 & 41)
- **Date Range:** 1816-11-01 to 1816-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** General Report of Yogyakarta Residency (AVJ 1816) detailing the Garebeg Puasa celebration; Major Nahuys van Burgst reports to Governor-General Van der Capellen that Ratu Ibu (mother of Sultan HB IV) was at the head of Kyai Murma's anti-colonial conspiracy seeking to restore Java to pre-1811 VOC conditions; report on the capture of Kyai Murma in Desa Kapundhung, with Patih Danureja IV promising surrender of escaped followers; transfer of Radèn Panji Brangtakusuma and the panakawan (royal retainers) of exiled Sultan HB II to Batavia; chronic border hatred and jurisdictional friction between Surakarta and Yogyakarta village populations over stray buffaloes; administrative instruction confirming that under the 1812 Treaty, Pacitan remains under Yogyakarta Residency control (rented for 36,528.18 Java Rupees); audit revealing Paku Alam I controlled the Sultan's treasury until late 1815 and acquired extensive land without Resident permission; treasury regulation restored to Ratu Ibu with appointment of a court regent to oversee finances and Sultan HB IV's education.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Kyai Murma Wijaya (Kyai Moormo), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Radèn Panji Brangtakusuma, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Desa Kapundhung (border enclave), Pacitan, Surakarta border villages, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Garebeg Puasa, VOC (*Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie*), Pamorigtong / Pangawengan taxes, Treaty of 1812, Pakualaman, Sultan's Treasury (*gedhong kemarogan*), Panakawan, Inter-court jurisdictional dispute.
