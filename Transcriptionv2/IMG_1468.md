# Transcription of IMG_1468.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1468.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 51A, *Afgaande Stukken Nov.–Dec. 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-08-19 to 1819-12-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Forensic residency memoranda by Major H.G. Nahuys van Burgst: denouncing abusive transit and tollgate taxes (*Wang Pêkêti*, *Wang Pakhêmitan*, and *Wang Mutian* levied at Pasar Gedhé, Imogiri, and Tembayat for royal tomb guardians); organizing regional cottage textile shipments to the Moluccas via the Capitan Cina; recording the fixed royal tribute of edible birds' nests delivered to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV; containment of anti-colonial unrest in Eastern Java (Malang/Pasuruan) through the arrest of 11 rebels by the Bupati of Rawa and calling on Pangéran Prangwedana's Mangkunagaran Legion; monitoring commercial coffee progress in Pacitan under Opzichter C.F. Engel; assessment of urban ground rent (*grondbelasting*) on European-leased houses in Yogyakarta; Dr. Harvey Thomson's formal petition for full legal landholder status; and an analysis of the Bengawan Solo river salt trade following British toll deregulation, highlighting coastal smuggling into Gresik and Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Capitan Cina of Yogyakarta, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Resident of Pasuruan, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Bupati of Rawa, C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Dr. Harvey Thomson (*Tuan Tom*), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Algemeene Secretaris Jean Chrétien Baud.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Semarang, Pasar Gedhé (Kota Gede), Imogiri, Tembayat, Moluccas, Pasuruan, Malang, Rawa (Tulungagung), Pacitan, Bengawan Solo river, Gresik, Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Wang Pêkêti* (illicit tollgate surcharge), *Wang Pakhêmitan* (unregulated toll protection fee), *Wang Mutian* (pilgrimage/sacred tomb tax), *Jurukunci* (tomb keepers), *catti*, *prajurit* / auxiliary troops, Mangkunagaran Legion, *landhuur* (plantation leaseholding), ground tax, Salt Monopoly (*Zoutregie*), river tolls.
