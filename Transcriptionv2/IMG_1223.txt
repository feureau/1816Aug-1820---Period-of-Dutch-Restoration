Solo Letters
1812 / 1816

Solo Br. 27

6 March 1816 – 27 July 1816

* **Johnson → Raffles**
  6 Mar. 1816
  * Offence of Banũkoa = cutting Waringin trees on paséban + beating the Imperial gamelans without orders.

* **Johnson – Fendall**
  29 Mar. 1816
  * Solo peaceful connections PB IV / Sepoys. No links outside kraton.
  * Cf on Ahmad Jedik (already annotated).

* **Id. to C. Assey**
  23 Apr. 1816
  * On removal of Mangkubumi.
  * PB IV declares approbatory removal MB. Impression of Buminoto & Sunan's brothers.
  * MB should be removed to Moluccas or any place where he could be under least restraint.
  * PB IV concerned that few restraints should be placed on MB.

---

### PB IV to dispatch

* **Methven → Johnson**
  21 Jun. 1816
  * Letter announcing arrival Dutch Commission in Batavia & suggesting PB IV dispatch a trustworthy person with full powers to act on his part in Batavia. (Blunder of British)
  * "In concluding the hand-over Brit. Govt. will not lose sight of his interests and of the sit. [situation] in which he stands regarding by existing Treaties."

---

### Handover of Java

* **Johnson – Fendall**
  6 Jul. 1816
  * PB IV requests loan of Sp. D. 16,000 Brit. Govt. refuses.

* **Assey – Res. Rembang**
  4 Jul. 1816
  * Commissioners recruiting in Java for Col. serving Dutch King.
