Yogya letters 1819   Dj. Br. 62 A
Aug. - Nov. 1819

Res. Semarang to Res. Yogya,
14 Aug. 1819.
BGG 13 Aug. 1819 no. 3
Prov. Transl. in Semarang + Instructor in
mil. school in Semarang
+ salary f. 250 p. month pensioned Lt. Thomas Paulus.

BGG 4 Sept. 1819, no. 9

Dutch cloth factories take part in trade to Java.
:. should be informed of what sorts of cloth
most in fashion + Javanese.
+ prices
see Reply of Nahuys to BG, 25 Oct. 1819. in Dj. Br. 51

Maj. Gen comm. of Troops de Kock
-> Nahuys,  Asking for Information on Bodyguard of Sultan.
4 Sept. 1819  see Reply of Nahuys Oct. in Dj. Br. 51

Engel to Nahuys, [circled in red: Pacitan]
12 Sept. 1819.  List of goods left Behind in Pacitan by Nêtrawijaya.
goods in a v. poor situation.

van Prehn to Nahuys,
14 Sept. 1819  Generaal gives Instructions for survey
of Teak Forests in Naganaga:
a great deal of wood had recently been felled
there by govant. Inhabitants.
see Earlier letters of Nahuys
advantage taken of change of
govant. in 1816 to cull wood.

23 Sept. 1819
Permission for Louis Mille
to Reside in Java.

21 Sept. 1819 H. Thomson.

Engel to d'Abo, [circled in red: Pacitan]
30 Sept. 1819.  approaching Rainy season suitable for
planting pepper bushes - Tum. Jagakarya
sent 1,130 men to Kêdhuwang to collect Bushes.
First dadap trees to be planted
would wait for a few months Before planting out coffee trees.
