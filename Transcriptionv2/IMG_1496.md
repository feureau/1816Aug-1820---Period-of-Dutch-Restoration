## Metadata

- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI H 698b, *Archief van Jogja 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Forensic political analysis by Resident Nahuys van Burgst uncovering the secret constitutional history of the Pakualam Regency: documenting how Prince Regent Paku Alam I's extensive private land acquisitions immediately after 1814 prompted British Resident Captain Garnham to suspect embezzlement of the boy-Sultan HB IV's revenues, leading the British in late 1815 to strip Paku Alam of all financial administration and transfer it to Ratu Ibu; revealing that Paku Alam was reduced to a mere custodian of the physical State Seal (*Cap Kraton*), while all royal charters (*piagem*) were drafted without his consultation; and explaining how the young Sultan and his court cabal completely bypassed Paku Alam by executing state decrees through Grand Vizier Radèn Adipati Danureja IV using the Patih's own seal.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Captain R.C. Garnham (former British Resident), Ratu Ibu (Ratu Kencana), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (Patih).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Kraton Yogyakarta, Puro Pakualaman, Kepatihan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Regency (*Regentschap*), Misappropriation of royal finances, Financial guardianship (*curatele*), Royal State Seal (*Cap Kraton*), Patih's executive seal (*Cap Patih*), *Piagem* (royal letters patent), Bypassing of regency authority.
