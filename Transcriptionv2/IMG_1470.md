# Transcription of IMG_1470.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1470.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 51A, *Afgaande Stukken Dec. 1819* & Solo Brieven Bundel 131, *Translaten en Verbalen 1816–1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-02-21 to 1819-12-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Commercial, political, and judicial reports: economic intelligence on the massive market for English-manufactured imitation batiks and chintz in Yogyakarta and Surakarta produced according to John Crawfurd's designs (popularizing forbidden patterns like *Parang Rusak* and *Kembang Cina*); constitutional restitution of the royal state seal (*Cap Kraton*) to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV from Prince Regent Paku Alam I upon the Sultan's coming of age; administrative history of British Resident Garnham stripping Paku Alam I of royal financial custody and granting it to Ratu Ibu; resulting bureaucratic ascension of the Patih (Danureja IV); and the Surakarta royal crackdown on Kyai Iman Sampurna's apocalyptic agitation (Sunan PB IV's edict of 21 February 1819 ordering the trial of all persons possessing Iman Sampurna's prophetic circulars before Patih Sasradiningrat II and residency translator J.W. Winter).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, John Crawfurd (former Resident), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Captain R.C. Garnham, Ratu Ibu (Ratu Kencana), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (Sumadipura), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Surakarta Patih), Kyai Iman Sampurna, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (Surakarta translator).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Kepatihan, England.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** British textile trade, *Chintz*, *Batik*, *Parang Rusak*, *Kembang Cina*, Royal State Seal (*Cap Kraton*), Regency (*Regentschap*), *Piagem* (royal letters patent), *Patih's Court* (*Bale Mangu* / Kepatihan), millenarian sedition.
