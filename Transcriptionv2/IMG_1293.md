# Transcription of IMG_1293.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1293.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, Vol. I, Doc. XXVIII continued, pp. 100–101)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's 13 September 1816 secret report on the internal dynamics of the Yogyakarta Sultanate; royal complaint by Pangéran Ngabèhi regarding the catastrophic loss of his Kedu appanage revenues and Van IJsseldijk's firm rejection of territorial retrocession; character and political ambitions of Pangéran Paku Alam I (Pangéran Natakusuma); analysis of Paku Alam's 1811 arrest by Daendels and his British elevation in 1812; political machinations surrounding the 1814 death of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, the minority succession of Hamengkubuwana IV, and Raffles's appointment of Paku Alam as Prince-Regent (*Regent*) with custody of the Great Seal (*Rijkszegel*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Pangéran Ngabèhi (Jayakusuma I), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Raja Putra Naréndra), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Pangéran Natakusuma), Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat (Paku Alam II / Suryadiningrat), Herman Willem Daendels, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Capt. Robert Clement Garnham, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Ratu Kencana.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu (Kadoe), Yogyakarta (Kraton), Batavia, Cirebon.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Sentono* (princes of royal blood), Appanages, Pakualaman Principality, *Pangeran di Patti* (Pangéran Adipati / Crown Prince title), *Rijkszegel* (Great Seal of the Realm), Regency Council (1814), Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.).
