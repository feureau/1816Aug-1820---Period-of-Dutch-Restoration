# Transcription of IMG_1305.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1305.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], *Bundel Djokjo Brieven* / Javanese Letters of October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Original manuscript letter in cursive Javanese script (*Aksara Jawa*); memorandum authored by Kyahi Adipati Purwa (or submitted under his name) cataloging the egregious political transgressions, social presumption, and illicit influence of Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat); detailing nocturnal visits between Secadiningrat and Patih Danureja IV, attempts to marry a former royal favorite (Radèn Ayu Murtiningrat), acquisition of a royal Teji horse, disrespectful boasting that the Dutch are mere coolie laborers (*beburuh*) to the British who could never harm him, and drunken revelry during wedding celebrations where Javanese royal women (*bendara-bendara putri*) were served spirits; preserved with blind-embossed paper seal and ANRI authentication stamp.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyahi Adipati Purwa, Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat / Jim Sing), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), Radèn Ayu Murtiningrat, Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton, Kadanurjan, Chinese Quarter / Kongsi).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Aksara Jawa*, Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI), Kapitan Cina, *Beburuh* (laborers / hirelings), *Kapal Teji* (prized Persian cavalry horse), *Kadanurjan* (palace of the Patih), Blind-embossed seal.
