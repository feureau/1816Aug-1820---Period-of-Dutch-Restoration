# JOGJA LETTERS 1816 (Aug - Sept)
[Batavia - Jogja]
Extracts uit het Register der Besluiten v. Commissarissen-Generaal
(Res. Jogja)

---

## Marginalia & Notes

- **A.N. Jogja Letters No. 44**
- **19 Aug 1816:** Commt. v. Dutch E. Indies Zaken overg. v. BRITISH.
- **23 Aug:** Dg receiving an Ambassador extraordinary: **– Van IJsseldijk**
- **3 Sept 1816:** Ratu Kencana Wulan / 3 R. Ayus
- **6 Sept 1816:** Questions PUASA Feast:
- **9 Sept 1816:** Crawfurd to Nahuys
- **9 Sept: Djogja: IJsseldijk to Nahuys**
- **11 Sept 1816:** Winter & Resident
- **12 Sept 1816**
- **22 Sept 1816:** Diétrée to Nahuys.

---

## Body Text

Ayus:
- Moortiningrat
- Djayaningrat
- Dewaningrat
} go to Batavia to join old Sultan in exile:

over 400 silver Rupiahs given to PAKU ALAM (out of Waterloo Funds)

50,000 Sp. D. demanded from old Sultan: for Rents, tollgates / bagan etc. already paid.

Reason for visit: To re-affirm establishment of DUTCH AUTHORITY:
exact letter from King to SUNAN:
G.W. Neêrlands.

*11 Sept 1816*
Market at Klaten - toll-gate for bazaar fees set up.

*12 Sept 1816*
Keeup Bazaar toll collection to ask money from 4 market inhabitants:

*22 Sept 1816*
**Diétrée**
Diétrée placed as Interpreteur Javanese in Jogja by Daendels in 1808.
Asked for Resignation 1810 because of poor Dutch, but had served England / Morris well.
Now asks to be reinstated:
