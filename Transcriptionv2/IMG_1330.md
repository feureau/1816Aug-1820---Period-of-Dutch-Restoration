# Transcription of IMG_1330.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1330.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, pp. 7–8)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-30 to 1816-09-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes examining Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s confidential cross-examination of Surakarta Prime Minister Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II; Sasradiningrat’s confession regarding Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s impenetrable dissimulation (*"PB IV is too refined and cunning to be fathomed"*); Sasradiningrat’s aborted August mission to Batavia (recalled at Cirebon); Van IJsseldijk’s decision to bypass the Patih and confront the Sunan directly; preparations for the 2 September 1816 state reception at the Surakarta Kraton; private preliminary conversation conducted in Javanese between Van IJsseldijk and PB IV; formal execution of Article 6 of the Secret Instructions demanding solemn royal guarantees of permanent loyalty to the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), John Fendall, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo Kraton), Cirebon, Batavia, The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Secret Instructions (Art. 6: Royal Assurances of Loyalty), *Dagverhaal* of J. D. Kruseman, Diplomatic Dissimulation, State Audience (2 Sept. 1816).
