# Transcription of IMG_1542.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1542.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Surakarta Residency Secret Records / Bundel H 698b, AvS Geheim, Dec. 1820; *Procès-Verbaal der Terechtstelling van den Res. van Prehn en der Translateur Winter*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-17 to 1820-12-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official minutes of the judicial interrogation and trial (*procès-verbaal van verhoor*) conducted in December 1820 against dismissed Surakarta Resident Rijck van Prehn and court translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter for corruption, extortion, and unlawful judicial usurpation; testimony of senior Surakarta court witnesses: Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (age 47), Dalem Bupati Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat (age 40), Bupati Wedana of Kediri Pangéran Purbanegara (age 60), Tumenggung of Gladhag Jaganegara, Regent of Ayah Radèn Tumenggung Dipayuda, Kliwon of Gladhag Jagawadana, and Kepatihan Patih Kertapraja; forensic financial evidence exposing that Bupati Wedana Purbanegara of Kediri (who paid 10,000 Spanish Dollars annual tax [*pajêg*]) was extorted by Van Prehn into paying 4,500 Spanish Dollars (Sp. M. / Real de a Ocho) as an illicit personal tribute (*uang bêkti*); examination into the administrative role of the Surakarta Capitan Cina as liaison between European officials and the kraton; role of former Kalang chief Radèn Tumenggung Sécanagara; severe judicial indictment charging that in January 1819, when the Ngabèhi of Sêlakatunggal (Sumadirja) and two Javanese men were accused of double homicide, Van Prehn unlawfully convened and personally presided over the Sunan's traditional supreme royal tribunal (*Vierschaar van den Raad des Soenans*) with Winter acting as secretary; formal determination by the President of the Court of Inquiry that Van Prehn’s presiding over native royal courts flagrantly violated Article 8 of the Anglo-Javanese Treaty of 1 August 1812 and Article 3 of the Resident's Standing Instructions, despite Van Prehn’s recorded signature in the sentence books for sessions on 18 May and 8 July 1819.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Rijck van Prehn, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, Pangéran Purbanegara, Kyai Ngabèhi Jaganegara, Radèn Tumenggung Dipayuda, Jagawadana, Ranawijaya, Kertapraja, Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara, Radèn Panji Anom Binang, Joh. Geer.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Kediri, Ayah, Gladhag, Sêlakatunggal, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Procès-Verbaal der Terechtstelling*, judicial interrogation, *uang bêkti* (illicit personal tribute / bribery), *pajêg* (land tax), Spanish Dollars / Matten (Sp. M. / Sp. D.), Capitan Cina, Kalang corporation, Gladhag transport guild, *Vierschaar van den Raad des Soenans* (Sunan’s Royal Court), 1812 Anglo-Javanese Treaty (Art. 8), Resident's Instructions (Art. 3), judicial usurpation.
