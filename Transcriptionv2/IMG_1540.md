# Transcription of IMG_1540.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1540.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616, Portefeuille 6 No. 13, Nahuys van Burgst Archive; Surakarta Residency Secret Dispatches, Feb.–March 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-01 to 1820-02-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Promulgation of Governor-General Van der Capellen’s secret executive order of 1 February 1820 enacting the punitive dismissal and judicial investigation of Surakarta Resident Rijck van Prehn and Residency Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter; formal aristocratic petitions submitted by senior Surakarta princes and nobles (including Pangéran Aria Tirtakusuma, Aria Martanegara, and Radèn Panji Lurah Adinegara) protesting arbitrary dismissals from office, unlawful replacements without royal assent, and the unauthorized political banishment of court officials (Tirto Jono and Toon Jayaningrat); Supreme Government findings determining that Van Prehn and Winter systematically abused vice-regal authority; fourfold punitive decree: (1) Van Prehn dismissed from office and replaced provisionally by Assistant Resident Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe, (2) Winter summarily dismissed and ordered to proceed immediately from Semarang to Batavia to surrender to the *Procureur-Général*, with the Resident of Semarang enforcing compliance, (3) Van Prehn banished from the Principalities of Surakarta and Yogyakarta under total communication embargo (all domestic correspondence subjected to mandatory postal censorship by the Resident of Semarang) alongside a formal judicial subpoena, and (4) transfer of all documentary evidence to the *Procureur-Général*; urgent dispatch from Assistant Resident Lippe (11 Feb. 1820) reporting the explosive atmosphere in Surakarta, an icy audience with Sunan Pakubuwana IV, and the Sunan’s defiance in reorganizing the wealthy Kalang community under Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara and Setranegara.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Rijck van Prehn, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Hendrik Ferdinand Lippe, Pangéran Aria Tirtakusuma, Aria Martanegara, Radèn Panji Lurah Adinegara, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara, Setranegara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Djokjo), Semarang, Batavia, Leiden (BPL archive).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bibliotheca Publica Latina (BPL), Nahuys van Burgst Archive (Port. 6 No. 13), *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* (BGG Geheim), *Procureur-Général* (Supreme Court Attorney-General), judicial subpoena (*dagvaarding*), administrative banishment, postal censorship, Kalang community.
