# Transcription of IMG_1398.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1398.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Port. 5, No. 7: Nahuys van Burgst Collection / Secret Letters Solo 1817, Jan–May)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-27 to 1817-05-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Surveillance of exiled aristocratic conspirators from the 1810 and 1812 rebellions, colonial intelligence assessments linking past revolts to contemporary millenarian movements, and court dynastic marital diplomacy in Surakarta. On 27 January 1817, Surakarta Resident Pinket van Haak reported a visit from Raden Tumenggung Cakranegara, who was living in internal exile in Surabaya following his complicity in the 1810 Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III rebellion and his role in organizing the clandestine 1812 inter-court anti-British conspiracy between Surakarta and Yogyakarta; Cakranegara petitioned the Governor-General to allow his wives to join him in Surabaya. On 1 February, Van Haak highlighted a perplexing intelligence connection linking the ideological networks of Raden Rongga's 1810 rebellion to the recent millenarian uprising of Raden Mas Umar Mahdi and the mystical circle of Kyai Murma Wijaya. On 5 February, the Governor-General formally rejected Cakranegara's petition to return to Surakarta, maintaining his banishment. Finally, on 2 May, dispatches from Van Haak and Van IJsseldijk document dynastic marital alliances arranged by Sunan Pakubuwana IV, including the marriage of the daughters of Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) to senior princes including the Crown Prince Pangéran Adipati Purbaya (future Pakubuwana V).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Tumenggung Cakranegara [Tjakra Negara], Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III [Raden Rongga / RR], Raden Tumenggung Danuningrat, Raden Mas Umar Mahdi [Umar Mandi], Kyai Murma Wijaya [Kijai Moermo], Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak [Haak], Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Sunan Pakubuwana IV [Sunan], Pangéran Adipati Purbaya / Pakubuwana V [P. Adg. PURBA], Pangéran Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II [P.W.], Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen [G.G.].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Solo], Surabaya, Yogyakarta [Djocja], Bagelèn, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Raden Rongga rebellion (1810), anti-British secret league (1812), internal exile (*verbanning*), *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal*, dynastic marriage politics, royal succession.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 7 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection: Geheime Brieven Solo 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Secret residency dispatches, petitions from exiled priyayi, viceregal resolutions (*Besluiten*), and Kraton marital notices.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates how the colonial regime tracked the enduring network of political dissidents originating from Daendels' and Raffles' campaigns, tracing ideological continuities between the 1810 anti-European jihad of Raden Rongga and the 1817 Umar Mahdi movement.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Residents Pinket van Haak and Van IJsseldijk, the Governor-General in Batavia, royal dynasts (Sunan PB IV, Crown Prince Purbaya, Prangwedana), and exiled priyayi (Cakranegara).
* **Archival Header:** Top registers shelfmark notations (`BPL 616 / NvB v.B / No. 5. / 7 / SECRET LETTERS / SOLO 1817 / JAN - FEB.`).
* **Chronological Ledger:** Left column marks precise intelligence dates (`27 JAN 1817`, `1 FEB 1817`, `5 FEB 1817`, `2 May 1817`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates and administrative officials on the left; verbatim surveillance summaries, conspiratorial links, executive refusals, and dynastic marital agreements on the right.
