# Transcription of IMG_1308.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1308.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], *Bundel Djokjo Brieven* / Javanese Court Transliterations)
- **Date Range:** 1810-11-17 to 1816-10-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's scholarly Romanized Javanese transliteration of two crucial Kraton documents: (1) Section 5–6: Pangéran Natakusuma's (Paku Alam I) pledge of loyalty to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II through Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, vowing to prevent enemies from entering the Kraton, invoking the blessing of the warrior kings (*Ratu pinarjurit*), and pledging to sweep clean the impurities of Java (*angrimpili susukering tanah Jawi*); (2) Section 7: Kyahi Adipati Purwa's memorandum (*pemut*) cataloging the insolent actions of Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), including nocturnal meetings with Patih Danureja IV, attempts to acquire royal women and a Persian horse (*kapal teji*), boasting that the Dutch are mere British hirelings (*tiyang beburuh*), and lavish wedding banquets where royal women were intoxicated.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sampeyandalem / Sultan Sepuh), Kyahi Adipati Purwa, Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat / Jim Sing), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Radèn Ayu Murtiningrat, Mertapiyoga, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Kangjeng Sinuhun), Radèn Ayu Secadiningrat (Nyonyah Kapitan).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton, Kadanurjan, Chinese Quarter / Kongsi).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Ratu Pinarjurit* (warrior monarchs), Javanese Calendar (20 Sawal AJ 1737 / 11 Dulkangidah AJ 1743), *Kapal Teji*, *Beburuh* (laborers / hirelings), Kapitan Cina nobility.
