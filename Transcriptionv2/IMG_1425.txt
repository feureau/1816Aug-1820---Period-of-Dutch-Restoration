BGG. 16 Sept. 1818 no. 9
Letting Res. Sala 11 Sept. 1818 no. 52
Reporting wreckage of French ship on Coast of Bagelen at Desa Wera (Wiro) (in Yogya area)

BGG. 18 Sept. 1818 no. 12
Letting Res. Yogya 10 Sept. 1818 no. 13.
4 crew of wrecked French ship La Légère (sloop) (Brig) arrive in Yogya.
Say that + 2 other crew mates try to go ashore + sloop
= but crewmates lost in Rollers.
Land at Walang = receive Hospitality from Solo / Yogya Demangs: Wargakusuma, Sasradiwiria.
Nahuys gives Demangs - 2 pistols each + silk clothes as Reward.
La Légère passes Anjer about to arrive in Batavia.

see list in ARA.
