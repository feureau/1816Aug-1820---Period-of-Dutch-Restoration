## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1389.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: May–Oct 1817 / Yogyakarta & Surakarta Residency Dispatches, General Secretarial Decrees)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-13 to 1817-05-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Key administrative, monetary, judicial, personnel, and infrastructural milestones of the Dutch Restoration in early 1817. On 13 January, the *Raad van Financiën* decreed the total demonetization of all non-governmental indigenous currencies (Balinese *picis*, lead coins, regional cash) across Java. On 10 February, Governor-General Van der Capellen issued Secret Decree No. 11 ordering the banishment of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Pangéran Arya Mangkudiningrat, Raden Arya Mertanagara, and Raden Tumenggung Kusumadinaya to the Moluccas (Ambon). On 14 March, Secret Decree No. 25 reorganized Vorstenlanden residency personnel: Resident Pinket van Haak was transferred from Surakarta to Pekalongan, and Commissioner Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk was appointed Resident of Surakarta (formally taking over on 15–18 April). The decree confirmed that the administration of Surakarta and Yogyakarta would remain anchored to the British treaties of 1 August 1812, emphasizing Articles 15 and 19. Finally, a comprehensive 5 May survey documents the staging posts (*poststations*) of the new military road system linking Semarang, Magelang, Surakarta, and Yogyakarta through the strategic junction of *Tangan Tiga* (Bawèn).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB], Johan Diederik Kruseman, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II [HB II], Pangéran Arya Mangkudiningrat I, Raden Arya Mertanagara, Raden Tumenggung Kusumadinaya, Raden Tumenggung Sumadiwirya [Patih of HB III], Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen [G.G.].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Yogya], Surakarta [Solo], Ambon / Moluccas, Pekalongan, Semarang, Tangan Tiga / Bawèn [Drie Ruvier], Magelang, Salatiga, Boyolali, Klaten [Klarèn], Prambanan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Geheim Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal*, *Raad van Financiën*, demonetization of indigenous currency, Treaty of 1 August 1812, military post-roads (*postwegen*), staging stations (*pasanggrahan* / poststations), exile to Ambon.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** Koninklijk Instituut voor Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde (KITLV), Leiden, Collection H 698b; transcripts of Yogyakarta Residency Dispatches (May–October 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Official administrative ledger, executive council resolutions (*Besluiten*), and topographic itinerary tables.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Chronicles the institutional consolidation of the Restoration regime: replacing debased indigenous currencies with colonial guilders, exiling dangerous dynastic rivals to the Moluccas, aligning judicial relations with the 1812 treaties, and establishing rapid military road communications across Central Java.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Residents Nahuys, Van Haak, and Van IJsseldijk; the Governor-General in Council; royal Kraton exiles (HB II, Mangkudiningrat).
* **Archival Header:** Top line records archival volume and chronological scope (`H 698b. / May 1817 - 1817 - June / Oct. 1817 (Yogya)`).
* **Chronological Ledger:** Left column marks precise decree and dispatch dates (`25 Jan.`, `13 Jan.`, `10 Feb. 1817`, `14 March`, `18 April`, `5 May 1817`).
