# Transcription of IMG_1334.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1334.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, pp. 23–25)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-08 to 1816-09-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s journey from Surakarta to Yogyakarta; Johan Diederik Kruseman’s advance mission to Klaten to coordinate logistical relays, horses, and food; Van IJsseldijk’s dawn arrival on 9 September 1816 in Resident Nahuys's state carriage; emotional greetings from senior princes and regents remembering his 1786 residency under Sultan Hamengkubuwana I; formal delivery of Governor-General Van der Capellen’s letter to the young Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV on 12 September 1816; intimate Kraton audience with the royal matriarchs: grandmother Ratu Kedhaton (Ratu Ageng) taking Van IJsseldijk’s hand, seating him at her side, and repeatedly pleading for protection for her grandson; reciprocal state visit of HB IV to the Residency House; explaining the European geopolitics of the Dutch return (finding far greater sincerity and trust than in Surakarta); Pangéran Paku Alam I's confirmation of British Resident John Crawfurd’s correct conduct.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Johan Diederik Kruseman, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Ratu Kedhaton (later Ratu Ageng), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), John Crawfurd, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Klaten (Klathèn midway post), Yogyakarta (Residency House and Kraton), The Hague (Collectie Baud).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Klaten Staging Post, Royal State Carriage, 1786 VOC Seniority, Dynastic Maternal Pleas, Pakualaman Reassurances, Retrocession Diplomacy.
