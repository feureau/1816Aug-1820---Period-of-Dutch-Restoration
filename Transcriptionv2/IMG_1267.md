# Transcription of IMG_1267.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1267.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI [Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia], Yogyakarta Residency Archive, Vol. 4203 [J 181]: *Geheime Stukken en Verhooren*, November 1816 – November 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1814-11 to 1817-11-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official residency interrogation into Ratu Ibu's (Ratu Kencana / Ratu Ageng) patronage of the Kyai Murma conspiracy following the death of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III in late 1814; Ratu Ibu utilizing British treaty compensation funds to finance Islamic religious scholars (*priests* / *santri*) and regional headmen to undermine European colonial hegemony and restore traditional VOC-era court parity; Kyai Murma consulting divine portents using the young Sultan's consecrated state robes (*state clothes*); links to Pangéran Buminata of Surakarta; financial audit revealing Murma received over 4,000 Spanish Dollars from the Yogyakarta court; Acting Resident Kruseman's confrontation of Ratu Ibu on 12 November 1816; Ratu Ibu claiming she was spiritually duped by Murma's offers of ancestral prayers and tomb pilgrimages (*makam*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Johan Diederik Kruseman, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Djojo Kario.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Kepundhung.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Vol. 4203 (J 181), Kyai Murma Conspiracy, Ratu Ibu's political financing, Royal consecrated robes (*busana-dalem*), Inter-court princely intrigue, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
