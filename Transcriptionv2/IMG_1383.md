# Transcription of IMG_1383.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1383.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia [ANRI], Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60 / Nationaal Archief, Exhibitum [Exh.] 12 Jul. 1817 Secret La A & La C; Exh. 7 Aug. 1817 Secret La A)
- **Date Range:** 1817-07-07 to 1817-08-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Dual security alarms in Central and East Java during July–August 1817: the Katangga messianic assembly scare in Madiun, and the aristocratic-led insurrection in Malang. On 11 July 1817, Provisional Resident H.M. Parvé of Semarang forwarded an alarming report from the Bupati of Grobogan claiming 4,000 Javanese were massing with harvested rice (*padi*) at the village enclave of Katangga in Madiun, driven by ancient prophecies that a mighty new monarch (*Ratu Adil*) would manifest there; suspicion fell on Pangéran Dipakusuma (Bupati Wedana of Madiun) and the exile network of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II. However, Resident Nahuys investigated directly and reported to Batavia on 18 July and 5 August that the Katangga panic was completely groundless. Concurrently, an armed revolt broke out in the Malang district of East Java, led by high-ranking Surakarta priyayi: Raden Jaladkara (son of Pangéran Purbanagara, Surakarta Bupati of Kediri), Raden Kramadipura and Raden Kramadiwiria (sons of Raden Natapura of Pacitan), and Nutadiwiria. The rebels, who claimed authorization from Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), retreated to Srengat [Blitar] to mobilize peasant support. Nahuys rushed to Surakarta on 7 July, completely exonerated Prangwedana of treasonous complicity, and enlisted him and his sons to march to Surabaya, dispatching 60 elite soldiers of the Mangkunagaran Legion and 50 ammunition cases to suppress the rebellion.

  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Katangga attroupement* [unlawful assembly], *Ratu Adil* prophecies, Eastern Manca Nagara [E.M.N.], Mangkunagaran Legion, ammunition cartridges, rebellion (*kraman*), *Exhibitum* [Exh.], *Besluit van de Gouverneur-Generaal* [BCCGG].

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** ANRI, Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60; Nationaal Archief, Exhibitum 12 Juli 1817 Secret La A & La C; Exhibitum 7 Augustus 1817 Secret La A.
* **Document Nature:** Secret intelligence dispatches, administrative evaluations, and inter-residency security correspondence between the Residencies of Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Semarang, and Surabaya.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the hyper-nervous security posture of the colonial state in mid-1817, where rumors of millenarian mobilization (*Ratu Adil* prophecies at Katangga) and aristocratic insurrections in the *Manca Nagara* (Malang/Kediri) triggered rapid military mobilization and deep surveillance of royal lineages.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Dutch Residents (Nahuys, Parvé, Von Lippe), royal priyayi military commanders (Pangéran Prangwedana), regional bupatis (Grobogan, Kediri, Madiun), and elite legionnaires.
* **Divided Sheet Structure:** The page is split into two major thematic registers separated by a horizontal dashed dividing rule.
* **Upper Register:** Multi-column intelligence timeline comparing Semarang alarms and Nahuys’s investigations of the Katangga gathering.
* **Lower Register:** Secret exhibitum minutes documenting the aristocratic conspiracy in Malang and Nahuys’s alliance with Prangwedana.
