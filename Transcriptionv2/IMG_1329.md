## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1329.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Collectie J. C. Baud*, No. 306, citing Van IJsseldijk’s master report, pp. 4–6)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-25 to 1816-08-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes tracing Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk’s inland journey from Semarang through Salatiga to Surakarta; meeting veteran Javanese court translator J. G. Krijgsman at Salatiga; strategic conferences with Resident Jacob Andries van Braam in Surakarta; intelligence mandate to uncover secret British promises regarding the retrocession; political and biographical profiling of Surakarta Prime Minister Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II; his imposition upon the court by British Resident Hugh Hope in December 1812 following the exile of his father Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II; British Residents Johnson and Crawfurd's confirmation that Sasradiningrat was deeply distrusted by Sunan Pakubuwana IV; Van IJsseldijk's strategy of utilizing the isolated Patih as an intelligence channel into the Kraton's secrets.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Johan Diederik Kruseman, Jacob Andries van Braam, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), J. G. Krijgsman, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih), Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II, Hugh Hope, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Major Jeremiah Martin Johnson, John Crawfurd, Jean Chrétien Baud, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Salatiga, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** British Imposition of Prime Ministers, Solo Brieven (Bundle 23), Court Intelligence, Javanese Translator (*Javaansch Translateur*), Regency Surveillance.
