# Transcription of IMG_1431.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1431.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 50: Financial Year 1818 / Monthly Tollgate Statements)
- **Date Range:** 1818-01-01 to 1818-01-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Quantitative administrative balance sheet recording the monthly and bi-monthly tax yields for the principal customs tollgates (*bandar*) and local market bazaars (*pasar*) across the Residency of Yogyakarta for January 1818. The audit lists gross rental yields and bi-monthly tax assessments (*1½ mths tax*, *1 mth*, *Rent for 1–15 Jan 1818*) across twenty-three strategic toll barriers and markets. Highest revenue yields were concentrated at Bantul (18,838), Juana [Janna] (18,361.20), Kemlaka (17,920), Serenan (13,603), Boyolali (9,583), Madiun (8,749), Gamping (7,331), and Wates [Wated] (6,708), alongside major eastern tollgates at Rawa (20,000+), Kalangbrèt, Kertosono [Kartasanna], Punggur [Piangir], Pacitan [Patjitan], and the Yogyakarta central bazaars.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Chinese toll-farmers [Bandar], Patih Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djogja], Juana [Janna], Kadilangu [Cadilangu], Rama [Rêma], Brosot [Broudot], Wates [Wated], Gamping, Kemlaka [Kemlaca], Bantul [Bantool], Krètèg [Kretek], Banaran, Boyolali [Boiyalalie], Kalasan [Kalassan], Borongan [Borrongan], Jati Anom, Serenan, Pantongan, Mawahan [Madjaran], Madiun, Rawa, Kalangbrèt, Kertosono [Kartasanna], Punggur [Piangir], Pacitan [Patjitan].
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Bandar* [customs tollgate], *Pasar* [bazaar tax-farm], Bi-monthly tax assessment, *Pachtgelden* [lease rent], Eastern *Manca Nagara*, Central Java transport bottlenecks.
