# Transcription of IMG_1535.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1535.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / Nationaal Archief Den Haag; *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, 5 Dec. 1820 No. 17 cont.)
- **Date Range:** 1820-10-21 to 1820-12-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** In-depth colonial council deliberations in Batavia regarding Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst’s acceptance of the 16,000 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) coronation chest from the newly crowned Sunan Pakubuwana V; review of defense arguments citing European diplomatic precedents where cash gratuities were customary during peace treaties and imperial accessions, alongside the immense hospitality overhead of the Surakarta Residency (hosting daily Javanese delegations at the Resident’s dining table); examination of the foundational Instructions of the Commissioners-General (*Instructie voor de Residenten*, 29 July 1816), which strictly mandated that any royal gift received from the Sunan or regents had to be matched by a reciprocal gift of equal value; severe displeasure expressed by the Netherlands Indies Supreme Government (*Hoge Regering*), which concluded that the Sunan's unilateral monetary gift conveyed an unacceptable aura of sovereign independence and condescension toward the King’s representative; formal reprimand ruling that Nahuys should have immediately returned the money chest to the Kraton; financial compromise reached by the Governor-General in Council: to prevent personal financial ruin while preserving institutional purity, Nahuys was granted permission to draw the full, combined salaries of both the Surakarta and Yogyakarta Residencies simultaneously, with instructions to submit a formal itemized expense ledger to Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sunan Pakubuwana V, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Commissioners-General (Commissarissen-Generaal 1816–1819).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, Instructions of the Commissioners-General (1816), diplomatic reciprocity, royal coronation gift (*kado*), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), dual residency salary, diplomatic hospitality expenses (*representatiekosten*).
