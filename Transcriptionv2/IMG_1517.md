# Transcription of IMG_1517.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1517.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nahuys van Burgst Portfolio 6, Part 13 & Surakarta Secret Dispatches, Feb. 1820) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Secret Political Intelligence))
- **Date Range:** 1820-02-11 to 1820-10-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** High diplomatic crisis in Surakarta: date of accession of Sunan Pakubuwana V (10 Oct 1820); secret dispatches of Provisional Resident Lippe (*Secret No. 1 & No. 2*, February 1820) following the sensational dismissal of Resident Rijck van Prehn and translator J.W. Winter; Sunan PB IV's icy reception of Lippe and sweeping purge of Kraton officials (dismissing Kalang chief Sécanagara and Cakrakusuma's bupati Rèksanagara, elevating Rajamenggala); and an alarming constitutional misunderstanding where Patih Sasradiningrat II announced that Governor-General Van der Capellen had retroceded all sovereign prerogatives enjoyed by the Sunan under the pre-1806 Batavian Republic/VOC, prompting PB IV to publicly proclaim the total restoration of his ancient sovereign independence and the de-facto nullification of the 1812 treaty.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana V (Sugandi), Provisional Resident Lippe, Rijck van Prehn (dismissed Resident), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (dismissed translator), Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara (Head of the Kalangs), Mas Ngabèhi Rèksanagara, Pangéran Cakrakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Arungbinang (Outer Bupati), Raden Ngabèhi Rajamenggala, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Surakarta Patih), Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Kraton Surakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *BGG geheim*, Royal accession (*1 Sura, Ehé, 1748*), Secret Residency dispatches, *Golongan Kalang* (hereditary woodcutters/carpenters), Sovereign rights retrocession, Pre-1806 Batavian Republic status, 1812 British-Javanese Treaty abrogation controversy.
