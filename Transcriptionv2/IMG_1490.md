# Transcription of IMG_1490.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1490.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI H 698b, *Archief van Jogja 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-09 to 1819-11-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse chronological memoranda from the Yogyakarta Residency letterbook: cadastral surveying and village subdivision in Kedu (Probolinggo, Sewuan); official deposition of Raden Tumenggung Danuningrat of Magelang affirming his former service as Patih to Danureja II; strict garrison artillery protocols for the thrice-annual Garèbèg court festivals (15-gun morning salute, 17-gun evening salute from Fort Vredeburg, and kraton gunpowder distributions); condominium of the holy dynastic shrines (*Pajaran* at Pasar Gedhé, Crapin, Tembayat, and Wongga, divided equally between Yogyakarta and Surakarta); extreme timber shortages in Yogyakarta prompting timber reconnaissance in Prangwedana's forests for the Boyolali bridge; and road distance schedules for postal relay stations between Yogyakarta and Surakarta (Jamplong, Kalidenkung, Klaten, Karang Jati).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident of Yogyakarta), Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Danukrama, Bupati of Magelang), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), Rijck van Prehn (Resident of Surakarta), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu (Probolinggo, Sewuan, Magelang), Yogyakarta, Fort Vredeburg, Surakarta (Sala), Pasar Gedhé (Kota Gede), Crapin, Tembayat, Wongga, Pacitan, Gasatan, Boyolali, Post of Jamplong, Kalidenkung, Klaten, Karang Jati.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Cadastral surveys, Garèbèg (Mulud, Puasa, Besar), 17-gun artillery salute, Gunpowder rations (*buskruit*), Shared royal shrines (*Pajaran*), Teak lumbering (*houtkapper*), Postal staging leagues (*poststations*).
