# Transcription of IMG_1520.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1520.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 122 / ANRI Solo Br. 122, Part 1) (Archetype 1: Narrative Notes, Diaries & Letters (Psychological Profile & Political Intelligence))
- **Date Range:** 1823-08-19 to 1824-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Forensic political and psychological evaluation of the young Sunan Pakubuwana VI (Radèn Mas Saperdan, aged 18) submitted by Surakarta Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven to Governor-General Van der Capellen: documenting PB VI's volatile temperament and fierce anti-colonial defiance (*"Resident along with all the other Dutchmen are my subjects"*); deliberate violations of court etiquette (elevating his royal throne / *dhampar* atop a table to humiliate senior princes); violent outbursts (assaulting courtiers with whips and swords, striking his uncle the Colonel-Commandant, attacking royal *Bedhaya* dancers, and armed friction with Kyai Maja's *Bulkio* santri guard); matrimonial crisis (repudiating his official consort Ratu Mas, daughter of Pangéran Ngabèhi, in a bid to marry Mangkubumi's daughter, with the Pengulu barred from granting a divorce); and the joint consensus of Resident Van Sevenhoven, elder prince Pangéran Buminata, and Grand Vizier Sasradiningrat II that PB VI must be stripped of all administrative authority and placed under a Regency.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (Resident of Surakarta), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, Sunan Pakubuwana VI (Radèn Mas Saperdan / *Sinuhun Bangun Tapa*), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Buminata, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Surakarta Patih), Ratu Mas, Pangéran Ngabèhi, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Adinegara, Kyai Maja, Pengulu of Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Kraton Surakarta, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Accession crisis (1823), *Dhampar* sumptuary protocol, *Bulkio* troop detachment (Kyai Maja), *Bedhaya* court dancers, Royal divorce (*talak*), Anti-colonial monarchical defiance, Proposed Regency (*Regentschap*).
