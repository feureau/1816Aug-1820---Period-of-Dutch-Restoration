- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: Algemeen Verslag Jogja [AvJ], 1818: Ingekomen en Uitgaande Brieven)
- **Date Range:** 1818-01-15 to 1818-10-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official inquiries into European agrarian colonization and land leasing (*landhuur*), secret state resolutions upholding the Moluccan exile of royal princes, territorial tollgate boundary disputes, and technical evaluations of the sugar industry in the *Vorstenlanden*. On 15 January 1818, Inspector-General of State Domains D.W. van Lawick van Pabst issued a circular to all Residencies across Java soliciting data on: (1) regions suitable for European white settlement; (2) uncultivated or uninhabited lands available for colonial concessions; and (3) methods to stimulate commercial sugar cultivation. Writing from Cirebon on 20 January, Van Lawick van Pabst specifically investigated terms for leasing agrarian lands to European and Chinese planters. On 24 June 1818, Secret Government Decree No. 2 formally denied the return petitions submitted by exiled princes Pangéran Arya Mangkudiningrat I and Pangéran Arya Mertasana, ruling that because both had been captured bearing arms inside the Kraton during the British assault of 20 June 1812, their presence in Madiun and Kertosono posed an intolerable threat to public order. On 19 September, Resident Nahuys was granted permission to travel to Batavia. In October, complex border tollgate jurisdictions were investigated between Surakarta, Yogyakarta, and Kedu: Van Lawick van Pabst examined the Wanasari barrier in Tahanangka, while the Resident of Kedu queried the status of the Gunung Tawang tollgate in Ledok (*Gedhong Tembakan*). Finally, an excerpt from Nahuys' letter of 17 February 1818 diagnosed the decay of indigenous sugar manufacture, warning that primitive, poorly constructed cattle-driven wooden mills resulted in declining annual yields.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** D.W. van Lawick van Pabst, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Arya Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Arya Mertasana, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Resident of Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Yogya], Surakarta [Sala], Cirebon, Madiun, Kertosono [Kertasana], Ambon, Batavia, Kedu [Kědhu], Wanasari, Tahanangka, Gunung Tawang, Ledok, Pekalongan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Inspector-Generaal der Domeinen*, European colonization, Land leasing [*Landhuur*] to Europeans and Chinese, Secret Government Decree [*Geheime Besluit*], Kasepuhan faction, Tollgate border disputes [*Bandar*], Sugar cultivation [*suikercultuur*], wooden mill cylinders, *Gedhong Tembakan*.
