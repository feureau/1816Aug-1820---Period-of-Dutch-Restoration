- **Date Range:** 1819-03-08 to 1864-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historiographical transcription of the four foundational investigative questions formulated by Governor-General Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen in Government Resolution No. 9 (Batavia, 8 March 1819), as analyzed in J.A. van der Chijs' seminal study (*TBG*, Vol. 14, 1864, pp. 215–220). Van der Capellen's circular directed all Residents across Java to provide exhaustive empirical answers to four core policy queries:
