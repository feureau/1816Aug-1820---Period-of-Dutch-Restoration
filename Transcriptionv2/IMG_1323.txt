**Proces-verbaal of 16 Aug. 1816.**

Prang Wedana & Legion need to be taken into Dutch pay.

Huge family of PW =
* 1200 Sp. D. payment
* + 500 cacahs = 1200 jung of Ricefields.

Great difficulty of PW to cloth / equip his force.

Great impoverishment for PW in keeping up troops on his own cost for so long.
* + can no longer pay their upkeep.

Legion in existence since 1808.

British allow him to command Legion personally without interference of a European officer.
* if question of moving against an internal enemy.

Only if used v. external enemy should a European officer be
$\left( \begin{array}{l} \text{seconded to his legion.} \\ \text{-- should be able to speak Jav. / Malay.} \end{array} \right.$

But salary / upkeep to be paid by Dutch govnmt.

Necessary to attach PW to Dutch govnmt. interests. $\therefore$ should agree to his Request.
