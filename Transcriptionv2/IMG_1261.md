# Transcription of IMG_1261.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1261.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 5: *Djogja Corr. [Secret] 1816–1817*; and P. H. van der Kemp, *De Economische Oorzaken van den Java-Oorlog van 1825–30*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10 to 1817-04-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Formulation of Dutch gubernatorial policy regarding the perpetual exile of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) to prevent political subversion on Java; mass arrests of kraton religious scholars (*santri clique*) intercepting their journey to Batavia to petition British and Dutch authorities for HB II's reinstatement; colonial resolution of 30 January 1817 authorizing Ratu Kencana Wulan to join HB II in exile at Ambon; Nahuys van Burgst's sweeping historical dispatch of 24 April 1817 to Governor-General Van der Capellen characterizing the "unlucky court of Djogja" afflicted by territorial dismemberment and loss of Kedu under Daendels and Raffles; court panic regarding a potential third restoration of HB II; Dipanagara's personal apprehension of dynastic upheaval; the Kyai Murma conspiracy (*kasepuhan* faction) agitating for royal restoration to recover lost appanage lands in Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Dipanagara, Ratu Kencana Wulan, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Ageng), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Kyai Murma Wijaya, P. H. van der Kemp.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Ambon, Kedu, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kasepuhan faction, Perpetual banishment, Kraton Santris clique, Loss of Kedu, Territorial secession, Restoration fears.
