# Transcription of IMG_1406.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1406.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: Algemeen Verslag Solo [AvS Gewoon], 1817 / Surakarta Residency Dispatches & Handover Memoranda)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-01 to 1818-01-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Succession of colonial residents in Surakarta, the court ceremonial calendar, administrative handover memoranda, Bengawan Solo riverine flotillas, and the territorial classification of the Kasunanan. On 15 April 1817, Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak handed over the Surakarta Residency to Commissioner Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, who died suddenly of a stroke in Surakarta on 12 May 1817; Assistant Resident Von Lippe administered the residency until 30 January 1818, when succeeded by Rijck van Prehn. The report records the dates of the three major court religious feasts (*Garèbèg Mulud* on 31 January, *Garèbèg Puwasa* on 15 August, and *Garèbèg Besar* on 22 October 1817). Van Haak’s 14 April *Memorie van Overgang* emphasized key personnel, particularly Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, head of the translation department and supervisor of edible birds' nest revenues, who attended Kraton meetings to provide written Dutch translations of proceedings formerly reported orally under the British. On 5 May 1817, a joint flotilla of 24 river craft sailed down the Bengawan Solo to Gresik (10 boats from the Sunan, 10 from the Sultan including *Prahu Panglipur* under Demang Trunaminggala, and 4 from Pangéran Prangwedana). Additional entries record a 5.5% administrative fee on tollgate warrants (*piagem*) and catalog the territorial domains of Surakarta across the core *nagara agung* (Pajang, Sukawati, Gunung Kidul, Bagelèn) and the western *manca nagara* (Banyumas, Dayeuhluhur, Karangkobar, Karangbolong).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak [Res. Solo], Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Von Lippe [Asst. Res. Solo], Rijck van Prehn, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter [J.W. Winter], Sunan Pakubuwana IV [Sunan], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV [Sultan], Pangéran Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II, Demang Trunaminggala.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Solo], Yogyakarta, Gresik, Bengawan Solo [Solo River], Pajang, Sukawati [Sokawati], Gunung Kidul, Bagelèn, Banyumas, Dayeuhluhur [Dayu Luhur], Karangkobar [Karang kobor], Karangbolong.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Memorie van Overgang* [administrative handover memorandum], *Algemeen Verslag*, *Garèbèg Mulud*, *Garèbèg Puwasa*, *Garèbèg Besar*, *Piagem* [royal administrative patent/charter], *Prahu Panglipur*, western *manca nagara* [W.M.N.], tollgate lease fees (5.5%).

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698a; transcripts from the General Annual Report of Surakarta (Algemeen Verslag Solo Gewoon, 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Official administrative ledger, executive handover memoranda, court ceremonial registers, and geographic surveys of the Kasunanan.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Chronicles the institutional continuity and administrative fragility of the early Restoration regime in Surakarta, mapping the physical riverine communication arteries connecting the court to coastal Gresik and defining the regional boundaries of indirect rule.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Residents Van Haak, Van IJsseldijk, Von Lippe, and Van Prehn; court translator J.W. Winter; royal dynasts (PB IV, HB IV, Prangwedana); and riverine transport officers.
* **Archival Header:** Top left displays collection code and volume (`H 698a / AvS (Gewoon) / 1817 / (Solo) / (1817)`).
* **Chronological Flow:** Left column marks precise entry dates (`15 April`, `14 April`, `5 May 1817`, `12 Sept 1817`, `1 Jan 1817`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, personnel transitions, and administrative headings on the left; verbatim succession notes, festival calendars, translation procedures, river boat manifests, and territorial lists on the right.
