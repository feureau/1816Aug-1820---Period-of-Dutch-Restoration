# Transcription of IMG_1238.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1238.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: *Nahuys van Burgst Papers / Diary of August 1816*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-16 to 1816-08-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Fiscal administration, garrison financial stringency, rural anti-tax arson, and dynastic celebrations during the initial weeks of the Dutch Restoration; accounts of the opium monopoly established under the British period allocating 40 chests of opium per year to Chinese revenue farmers; severe complaints that colonial garrison funds were not forthcoming, inflicting acute hardship on troops and common inhabitants, prompting permission to borrow emergency funds from Muntilan in Kedu; reports an attempt on 20 August 1816 to burn down the Chinese customs toll post in Boyolali led by an indigenous former British army lieutenant from Magelang; Carey emphasizes that rural uprisings and incendiary attacks typically break out following the Garebeg Puasa when Javanese peasants are subjected to annual tax demands; local Demang neglected to suppress the blaze, with four Surakarta subjects implicated; grand celebration of King William I's birthday on 25 August 1816; Pangéran Ngabèhi (elder brother of HB II) and Resident Nahuys inspect the garrison; Nahuys conducts a formal review accompanied by Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, principal consort Ratu Kencana, mother Ratu Ibu, grandmother Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), and aunt Ratu Mas, followed by a diplomatic luncheon at the Residency House hosted by the widow of Baumgarten.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Ngabèhi, Ratu Kencana, Ratu Ibu, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Ratu Mas, King William I of the Netherlands, Widow Baumgarten.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djocja), Boyolali, Muntilan (MNT / Kedu), Magelang (Moerang), Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Opium farm monopoly (40 chests), Post-Puasa tax protests, Boyolali customs post, Garrison review, Birthday celebration of King William I.
