# Transcription of IMG_1322.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1322.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, *BPL 616*, Nahuys van Burgst Portfolio 5, No. 7; citing P. H. van der Kemp, *Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1816*, Bijlage VI & *Collectie Baud*, No. 306)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Scholarly research notes by Professor Peter Carey analyzing Major Nahuys van Burgst’s 12 September 1816 secret dispatch regarding Pangéran Mangkubumi of Surakarta and the 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy; uncovers the dynastic intrigue behind the plot: Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s intense antipathy toward Crown Prince Radèn Mas Sugandi (later PB V) and secret plan to substitute his second son, Radèn Mas Arya Prabu Purbaya (later Sunan Pakubuwana VII, whose wife was Mangkubumi's daughter); the court’s dejection over the British annexation of Kedu; plan to use a Sepoy mutiny to recover Kedu and obtain British recognition of Purbaya; Pangéran Prangwedana’s (Mangkunagara II) steadfast refusal to join the conspiracy; the Crown Prince’s betrayal of the plot to the British to destroy Mangkubumi; PB IV's abandonment of Mangkubumi as a scapegoat; recommendation to reward Prangwedana with a Colonel's rank and state funding for the Mangkunagaran Legion.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Mas Sugandi (Crown Prince / Sunan Pakubuwana V), Radèn Mas Arya Prabu Purbaya (Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), Jean Chrétien Baud, P. H. van der Kemp, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Kedu (Kadoe), Batavia, Leiden (BPL 616).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy, Succession Rivalry (Sugandi vs. Purbaya), Mangkunagaran Legion (Colonel's rank), Political Scapegoating, Collectie Baud (No. 306), Leiden University Library (BPL 616).
