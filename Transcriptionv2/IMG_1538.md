# Transcription of IMG_1538.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1538.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Surakarta Residency Letters / Bundel M 698a, AvS, 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-06-06 to 1820-11-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resolution of Governor-General Van der Capellen in Council (6 June 1820 No. 6) establishing a regular stagecoach and post horse relay system (*wagenpostery*) connecting the northern port of Semarang with Surakarta; delineation of intermediate relay staging posts between Salatiga and Surakarta (Ampêl, Bayalali / Boyolali, Malati / Mlati, Kartasura, and Karangjati); cost of maintaining relay horses split equally between the Sunan of Surakarta and the Sultan of Yogyakarta; fixing travel tariffs at 1 Indies Guilder (20 stuivers) per postal league (*paal*) to compensate the royal courts for horse and carriage maintenance (*geschag / ter vergoeden*); series of intimate royal Javanese diplomatic letters sent by Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV to the Crown Prince / new monarch of Surakarta following the death of Sunan Pakubuwana IV (6, 15, and 18 October 1820 / 27 Besar Alip 1747 to 10 Muharram Ehé 1748); HB IV offers condolences on PB IV’s demise on Sunday evening, 1 October (25 Besar Alip 1747), expresses joy at the accession of PB V on 2 Muharram Ehé 1748, and congratulates the new Sunan on the promotion of court queens, elevating Ratu Kencana to Queen Mother (*Ratu Ageng*), Ratu Kadipatèn Sepuh to Ratu Kencana, and Ratu Kadipatèn Anom to Ratu Mas; border boundary dispute between the Residencies of Kedhu and Surakarta regarding the enclave village of Sawikot (Dabangangkan).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Sunan Pakubuwana V, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (Sultan Séda Besiyar), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ageng), Ratu Mas.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Salatiga, Ampêl, Bayalali (Boyolali), Mlati, Kartasura, Karangjati, Surakarta (Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Kedhu, Sawikot (Dabangangkan).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Post carriage service (*wagenpostery*), horse relay staging stations, Indies Guilder / stuivers, *geschag* (royal horses and carriage teams), Javanese calendar (Alip 1747 / Ehé 1748), court condolences (*bela sungkawa*), royal titulature (*Ratu Ageng, Ratu Kadipatèn, Ratu Mas*), boundary demarcation.
