# Transcription of IMG_1373.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1373.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Port. 5, No. 11: Nahuys van Burgst Collection / Djogja Correspondence General, Jan 1817)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-09 to 1817-01-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Outbreak and rapid suppression of the Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi millenarian movement in the western *nagara agung* of Bagelèn. Claiming apostolic authorization from the Ottoman Sultan (*Groote Heer van Turkije* / Sultan Rum) and the Sharif/High Priest of Mecca to extirpate unbelievers and inaugurate a new righteous monarch, Umar Mahdi erected three white banners upon the mosque at desa Jancopo, ordered local bupatis to surrender authority, and commanded peasants to assemble, slay wealthy Chinese merchants, and march on Magelang and Yogyakarta to force the abdication of the Sunan. The uprising was swiftly decapitated through joint action by Provisional Assistant Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman, Outrée, and Mas Demang Jawongsang of Bagelèn; Umar Mahdi and 36 followers were captured, with the leader sentenced to execution by keris (*hukuman keris*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi [Oemar Madie], Johan Diederik Kruseman [Asst. Res.], Outrée, Mas Tumenggung Jawongsang [Mas Demang of Bagelèn], Sunan Pakubuwana IV [PB IV], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV [HB IV], Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst [NvB].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Bagelèn, Desa Jancopo, Yono, Magelang, Yogyakarta [Djogja], Surakarta [Solo], Mecca [Mekka], Turkey [Ottoman Empire / Rum], Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Kraman* [rebels/bandits], *Prajurit Sultan Rum*, *Ratu Adil* [Just Prince], *Mesjid Tiban*, white banners [panji pethak], Chinese merchants [tax farmers], execution by kris (*hukuman keris*), flogging, Assistant Resident salary (800 Java Rupees).

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 11 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection).
* **Document Nature:** Archival dispatch summaries, intelligence abstracts, and administrative reports compiled from Yogyakarta residency correspondence addressed to the Governor-General (G.G. Van der Capellen) and Raad van Indië during January 1817.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Chronicles the volatile transition in early 1817 during Nahuys's temporary medical convalescence, illustrating how rural distress, fiscal extortions by Chinese tollgate keepers, and post-war displacement ignited acute Islamic millenarian fervor in Bagelèn.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Colonial civil administrators (Provisional Assistant Resident Kruseman, Outrée) operating in close coordination with indigenous regional administrators (Mas Tumenggung Jawongsang) to execute swift counter-insurgency policing.
* **Header Structure:** Top left records archival shelfmark notations (`BPL 616 / N van B / LEIDEN / PORT 5 / NO. 11.`), followed by series title (`DJOGJA CORRESPONDENCE (GENERAL) / 1817 (JANUARY) / ASST RES -> G.G.`).
* **Carey Annotations:** Red ink topical additions (`[Red ink: Yogya 1817 (Jan)]`, `[N. van B ARCHIVE LEIDEN.]`, `[Red ink: Umar Mahdi movement]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Chronological ledger format pairing archival source dates and topical margin markers on the left with detailed intelligence abstracts on the right.
