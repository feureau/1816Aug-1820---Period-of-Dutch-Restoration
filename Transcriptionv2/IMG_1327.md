# Transcription of IMG_1327.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1327.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Ministerie van Koloniën [MvK] 4491*, pp. 70–77; citing Van IJsseldijk dispatches of 4 & 8 September 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-02 to 1816-09-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes reconstructing the ceremonial state reception of Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk at the Kraton of Surakarta on 2 September 1816; cortege led by Crown Prince Radèn Mas Sugandi (later PB V), Pangéran Buminata, and royal brothers; state carriage ride into the city; royal reception at the Sri Menganti gate by Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Resident Col. Van Wenne, and Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II); 3-gun royal artillery salute; presentation of a seat of honor draped in royal yellow damask beneath the Grand Pendapa (*ageng pèndhapa*); public reading of the Commissioners-General's letter in Javanese by translator J. G. Krijgsman alongside John Fendall's British dispatch; audience in the private royal apartments with Queen Consort Ratu Kencana; reciprocal state visit of PB IV to the Residency gardens on 3 September 1816.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Mas Sugandi (Crown Prince / Sunan Pakubuwana V), Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Col. Van Wenne, Ratu Kencana (Surakarta Queen Consort), John Fendall, J. G. Krijgsman, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Kraton, Sri Menganti, Grand Pendapa, Residency House & Gardens), Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Ceremonial Court Protocol, *Sri Menganti* (royal gate of audience), Yellow Damask Throne Stool, 3-Gun Artillery Salute, Royal Toasts, *Krama Inggil* Reading, Reciprocal State Visit.
