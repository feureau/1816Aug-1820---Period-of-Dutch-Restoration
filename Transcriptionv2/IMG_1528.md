# Transcription of IMG_1528.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1528.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Yogyakarta Residency Letters / Bundel PJ. Br. 51 B, *Afgaande Stukken 1820*, March–July 1820)
- **Date Range:** 1820-03-13 to 1820-07-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Dramatic public health incident in Yogyakarta where Surgeon-Major Gauffre administered a cowpox vaccination to the adolescent Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, resulting in a severe, life-threatening bout of smallpox (*kinderpokken*); while HB IV survived and did not oppose the campaign, popular suspicion (*prejudice*) exploded across Yogyakarta; Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst personally financed Chinese and Javanese infant lymph carriers and recruited Muslim religious leaders (*priests* / *santris*) to perform vaccinations, proposing to station pensioned soldiers at tollgates to supervise inoculation certificates; dispatch from Acting Resident d'Abo to Nahuys (then serving as Acting Resident of Surakarta) reporting widespread banditry (*kampuaks* / *wong durjana*) on the southern slopes of Mount Merapi (Pakem, Jangalan, Kembang Arum, Bedhaya), where roving bands of 7 to 10 armed outlaws armed with axes (*bylen*), pikes, and firearms raided villages, directing Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV to mobilize indigenous security; formal report by the Resident to Governor-General Van der Capellen reporting a double capital homicide on the post road by two European soldiers from the Yogyakarta garrison who murdered two Javanese travelers to steal their sacred krisses, warning that the "godlessness" (*goddeloosheid*) of the military endangered the entire moral and physical prestige of European rule; re-equipment subsidy of f. 4,000 per annum alongside pistols and sabres granted to the Pakualaman Legion; minimal civilian readership of the official colonial gazette (*Bataviasche Courant*) in Yogyakarta, with only a single non-official subscriber paying f. 20 for a half-year run.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Surgeon-Major Gauffre, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, G.A. d'Abo, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya / Djogja), Mount Merapi, Pakem, Jangalan, Kembang Arum, Bedhaya, Surakarta (Solo), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Cowpox vaccination (*koepok-inenting*), smallpox (*kinderpokken*), infant lymph carriers, *wong durjana* / *kècu* / *kampak* (armed bandits), tollgates (*bandar*), Pakualam Legion, *Bataviasche Courant*, military garrison discipline (*goddeloosheid*).
