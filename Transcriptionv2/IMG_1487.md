# Transcription of IMG_1487.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1487.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Resolutions of the Governor-General in Council / *Besluiten van den G.G. in Rade*, 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-15 to 1819-11-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official gubernatorial resolutions across administrative, fiscal, judicial, and humanitarian spheres: formalizing C.F. Engel's appointment as Opzichter of Pacitan; appointing indigenous sub-collectors of land revenue (Jumawijaya and Tisnanijaya at f. 100 per month); native district administration shuffles in Kedu (pensioning the ailing Demang of Menoreh on f. 20 per month, appointing Mertadangsa, and promoting Singadiwiria as Sub-Demang of Boro); landmark enforcement of the prohibition against the slave trade (*BGG 18 Jun 1819 No. 28*) rejecting the petition of Sayyid Hasan to land and sell a slave purchased in Penang pursuant to Article 113 of the Constitutional Regulation (*Regeeringsreglement*), ordering the captive returned to the Straits Settlements; and land alienation reviews in Jombang.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Jumawijaya, Tisnanijaya, F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Demang of Menoreh, Mertadangsa, Setrawijaya, Singadiwiria, Sayyid Hasan, Abdul Hamid, Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan, Yogyakarta, Kedu (Menoreh, Boro), Penang (Pulau Pinang), Jombang, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten in Rade*, *Opziener*, *Landelijke Inkomsten*, *Demang* / *Sub-Demang*, Abolition of the Slave Trade, *Regeeringsreglement* (Article 113), Private land sales (*particuliere landerijen*).
