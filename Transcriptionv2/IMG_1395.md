# Transcription of IMG_1395.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1395.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698b: AvJ 1817 [Yogya], July–Nov 1817 / Royal Diplomatic Correspondence HB IV – PB IV & Patih Correspondence Danureja IV – Sasradiningrat II)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-09 to 1817-12-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** High diplomatic correspondence between the royal courts of Yogyakarta and Surakarta, royal bereavement, and the interstate administration of justice under traditional Javanese law (*Serat Anggèr*). On 17 Sapar 1745 AJ [late 1817], Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV addressed Sunan Pakubuwana IV affectionately as *"Oom Prabu"* (Uncle Sovereign), formally conveying grief over the premature death of the infant Yogyakarta Crown Prince (his son by Ratu Kencana), receiving the Sunan's formal condolences on 2 December. Concurrently, a series of diplomatic letters between Patih Raden Adipati Danureja IV of Yogyakarta and Patih Raden Adipati Sasradiningrat II of Surakarta regulated cross-border policing and judicial procedures under the *Serat Anggèr* (inter-court legal code ratified on 19 June 1804). The patihs debated the legal protocols for pursuing highway robbers (*kècu*) across residency borders in Sukawati following a report by *mantri mergangsal* Ngabèhi Brajaninggala; cited Article 10 regarding the 36-day relay tracking (*pendhak*) obligations of village headmen (specifically Bekel Mukidin of Krapyak) and the sounding of warning slit-drums (*kenthongan* / *gěpèntang*); reviewed Article 19 imposing a 50 Spanish Real fine on officials who allow detained robbers to escape; and detailed the dispatch of Raden Tumenggung Ranadiningrat and Raden Tumenggung Sasrawirana with 100 Kraton prajurit to suppress rural disturbances incited by Sawunggaling in Bagelèn.

  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Serat Anggèr* [interstate Javanese law code], *Contract of 19 June 1804*, *Pendhak diensten* [relay tracking corvée], *Gěpèntang* / *Kenthongan* [slit-drum alarm], *Denda pati* / escape fine (50 Reals), royal bereavement (death of Crown Prince), royal prajurit, *Mantri mergangsal*, Sapar 1745 Wawu.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** KITLV H 698b; transcripts of official diplomatic correspondence between the Yogyakarta and Surakarta Kratons (1817); supplemented by Crawfurd Manuscript collections.
* **Document Nature:** Diplomatic court letters (*surat kiriman*), interstate judicial warrants, and forensic crime reports exchanged between the two prime ministers (*Patih Dalem*).
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the operational reality of traditional Javanese interstate law (*Serat Anggèr Nawala Pradata*), showing how Yogyakarta and Surakarta cooperated to suppress rural dacoity, enforce joint village tracking responsibilities, and maintain dynastic protocol despite colonial oversight.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Reigning monarchs (HB IV, PB IV), grand viziers (Danureja IV, Sasradiningrat II), regional bupatis (Mertanagara, Ranadiningrat, Sawunggaling), and village bekels.
* **Archival Header:** Top left displays collection code and period (`H 698b / AvJ 1817 (Yogya) / [1817] / July - Nov. 1817`).
* **Chronological Ledger Entries:** Individual entries headed by precise court dates (`17 Sapar Wawu 1745`, `15 NOV. 1817`, `5 June 1817`, `5 May 1817`, `9 Jan 1817`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, correspondents, and treaty articles on the left; verbatim court letters, condolence exchanges, criminal pursuit procedures, and troop deployments on the right.
