## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1499.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Scholarly Research Correspondence (Trinity College, Oxford to Prof. G.W.J. Drewes, Leiden, Page 2)
- **Date Range:** 1981-10-06 to 1819-08-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding folio of Dr. Peter Carey's research letter to Professor G.W.J. Drewes: completing the diplomatic Dutch transcription of the 1818 Banyumas/Ajibarang millenarian tract concerning Governor-General Van der Capellen's supernatural wisdom and spiritual ownership of the holy cannon *Nyai Setomi*; historical colophon of translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter dated Surakarta, 22 August 1819; Dr. Carey's historiographical footnote referencing G.P. Rouffaer's critique of Winter's unpolished Dutch prose in *BKI* (1902); and formal valediction and signature.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Dr. Peter Carey, Professor Gerardus Willebrordus Joannes Drewes (G.W.J. Drewes), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (*de Generaal*), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (Surakarta translator), Gerret Pieter Rouffaer (G.P. Rouffaer).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Trinity College (Oxford), Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Pusaka* cannon *Nyai Setomi*, Dutch archival translation colophon, Historiographical critique (*Rouffaer in BKI 1902*), Academic correspondence.
