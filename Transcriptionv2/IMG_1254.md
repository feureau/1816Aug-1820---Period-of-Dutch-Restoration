# Transcription of IMG_1254.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1254.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Jogja Archive for 1816 — Letters from Nahuys to Van der Capellen and Elout*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-24 to 1816-08-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official celebrations at the Yogyakarta Residency House commemorating the birthday of King William I of the Netherlands (25 August 1816) and Garebeg Puasa (24 August 1816); presence of Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV and over 120 court relations, breaking royal precedent by remaining outside the kraton past midnight for dancing; influential role of the Sultan's mother, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Ibu); Paku Alam I's contribution of 400 Silver Rupees to the Waterloo Fund; security breakdown at the Yogyakarta municipal jail; flight of Chinese and Javanese prisoners arrested at Boyolali; suspected bribery of Demang Gung and attempted poisoning of a Chinese inmate by Wireng Demang.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, King William I, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ageng / Ratu Ibu), Pangéran Paku Alam I, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Demang Gung, Wireng Demang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Boyolali, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Garebeg Puasa, Waterloo Fund, Royal birthday fête (*K. Willem's Birthday*), Residency jail security, Kraton Yogyakarta, Spanish Dollars / Silver Rupees.
