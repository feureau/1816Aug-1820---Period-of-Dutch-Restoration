# Transcription of IMG_1294.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1294.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (M. L. van Deventer, *Het Nederlandsch Gezag over Java en Onderhoorigheden sedert 1811*, Vol. I, Doc. XXVIII continued, pp. 102–103)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-13 to 1817-01-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's 13 September 1816 secret report on Yogyakarta; constitutional analysis of Pangéran Paku Alam I's role as custodian of the Great Seal of the Realm (*Rijkszegel*); proposal to define Paku Alam's semi-autonomous status analogous to Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) in Surakarta, backed by an annual court subsidy to maintain his 100 cavalry dragoons; identification of persistent palace terror regarding the potential return of the deposed Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) and Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I from Batavia; comprehensive breakdown of the three competing royal factions (*partijen*) within the Yogyakarta Kraton; subsequent colonial resolution to exile HB II and Pangéran Mangkubumi to Ambon (10 January 1817).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Ngabèhi (Jayakusuma I), Ratu Kencana Wulan, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Ratu Kencana, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), John Crawfurd.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Kraton), Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Ambon (Moluccas).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Great Seal of the Realm (*Rijkszegel*), Pakualaman Dragonders (cavalry force of 100 horsemen), *Pangeran di Patti* (Pangéran Adipati title), Three Kraton Factions (Kasepuhan, Mangkudiningrat, Karajan/HB IV), Secret Resolution of 10 January 1817, Spanish Dollars (Sp. M. / Sp. D.).
