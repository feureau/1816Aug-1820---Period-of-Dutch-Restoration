# Transcription of IMG_1532.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1532.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief Den Haag / Ministerie van Koloniën, MvK 4526; *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, Geheim, 17 Oct. 1820 no. 1a. A)
- **Date Range:** 1820-09-26 to 1820-10-17
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Passing of Sunan Pakubuwana IV on the evening of 1 October 1820 at 8:00 PM following a rapid, fatal illness, ending a momentous 32-year reign (1788–1820); failure of Acting Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst to obtain the dying monarch’s oral designation of an heir; Nahuys' refusal to open formal court inquiries for fear of provoking fierce succession intrigues between the Crown Prince (*Pangéran Adipati Anom*) and PB IV’s ambitious younger brother Pangéran Purbaya; Nahuys' immediate unilateral proclamation of the Crown Prince as the legitimate successor to forestall dynastic factionalism; dramatic discovery following PB IV’s state burial of a handwritten testament affixed to his imperial bedstead—written and signed in the Sunan’s unmistakable hand—explicitly naming his eldest son as sole heir; official proclamation of Sunan Pakubuwana V on 10 October 1820; archival chaos in Surakarta resulting from the loss or misplacement of foundational precedents, including the original 1788 Accession Treaty (*Acte van Verband*); vice-regal censure from Governor-General Van der Capellen, who chided Nahuys for proclaiming a sovereign without prior governmental authorization; appointment of a high tripartite colonial commission—composed of H.G. Nahuys van Burgst, Pieter Merkus, and P.H. baron van Lawick van Pabst—to supervise the transition, draft the new *Acte van Verband*, administer the state accession oath, and secure public confirmation.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Sunan Pakubuwana V, Pangéran Purbaya (future Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pieter Merkus, Pieter Herbert baron van Lawick van Pabst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Ministerie van Koloniën (MvK), *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade* (BGG Geheim), royal succession, *Acte van Verband* (Accession Treaty of 1788 / 1820), Crown Prince (*Pangéran Adipati Anom*), imperial bedstead testament, dynastic factionalism, Tripartite Commission of Regency.
