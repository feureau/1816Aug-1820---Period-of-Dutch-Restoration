Iman Sampurna
1819 Feb.
Solo. Br. 131
LEDAYA en KEDHIRI verhalen.
Translit. 'Verbalen en Rapporten Solo 1816-1818'

From what land are you? From the land of the ½ people.
What are the ½ people called? The old Race.
Who do they belong to? Under the Ratu Antik Paniteg Panatagama.
Those who believe in God, will obtain his Love. "   "   "   " was the first of men to accept the True Faith - this King is a good-natured Ruler sprung from Mataram blood // In general it is necessary to follow in the steps of the old race (oude geslacht) God is long before this time already in existence with his wonderful power, and he shall exist forever - we are born into the world from God, but the warning everything has been given to us by the Prophet Rasulullah (Mohammed). From what race are you sprung? From the old race. What is your job? With whom do you work? With Raden Putra, the rep. of God, our father & mother who resides in a place so small that it resembles a peppercorn. I love the Bok Fatimah, the most good-hearted woman in heaven and Earth. Always be conscious to say this: "The Nense (genou) which Bok Fatimah has, that have I also" = Trans. Solo 17 Feb. 1819

Eenige Berigten wegens de uitgever van dit geschrift = Magelang 11 Feb. 1819 (J.E. Hardy)
Pèndita Iman Sampurna often sleeps on Mt. Lawu and receives geschrift during meditation there / afterwards goes to wood of Lèdaya in Kèdhiri where he turned a Brahmin Temple (sanggar) into an Islamic Temple - finds a gamelan there & set of wayang kulit. prays / give slametans in village of Lèdaya + local inhabitants who could all turn themselves into macan gadungan / Sanggar of Sampurna held in reverence by local inhabitants = Because by means of offerings can achieve riches and high positions // No one can go alone to the Temple without being devoured by tigers / Sampurna sleeps in Temple & has idea of making geschrift. publish & distribute copies of above. Those who did not make copies were devoured by Tigers / when copies become public PB IV & Res. Solo hear about it = Sampurna arrested & interrogated by Res. Solo & given over to Pengulu Solo & Son of Pengulu ketib d'émmemoris = If anyone asks him where he was from tells questioner to ask Kyai Abdul Rahman from Dwa Ampil (Surabaya)

According to testimony of R.T. Danuningrat = letter of Sampurna has no evil intent mainly drawn from Qur'an / Trans. from Malay trans of Bupati By Valk sec. of Res. Kedhu.
* not from Jav.
