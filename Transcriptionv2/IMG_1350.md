# Transcription of IMG_1350.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1350.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306, section 8 / page 12: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816; and *Nota voor den Prov. Res.*, 22 October 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical genealogical and structural analysis of Yogyakarta kraton politics and aristocratic kinship: Raden Adipati Danureja IV (D R IV) is uncle-in-law to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV); Danureja IV's wife is the sister of Ratu Ageng (Ratu Ibu / Ratu Kencana, mother of HB IV); both royal women are daughters of Raden Tumenggung Sasradiningrat, the Mancanagara bupati of Rajegwesi (Bojonegoro); on his maternal side, Danureja IV descends from the royal Madurese dynasty of Cakraningrat; political and dynastic comparison drawn with Pangeran Dipakusuma (DPK / Raden Tumenggung Natayuda II): previously stripped of office and deported in exile to Batavia for an extended period under Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh); dismissed from his post as bupati because he was descended through the female line from Sunan Amangkurat Mas (Amangkurat III, reigned 1703–1708), making him an object of intense dynastic jealousy and paranoia for HB II; subsequently rehabilitated and reappointed as joint Bupati Wedana of the Eastern Mancanagara (E. MN. / Madiun); colonial surveillance in Batavia: ongoing anxiety that the exiled Sultan Sepuh is establishing a dangerous personal following and political network while residing in Batavia; formal request submitted by HB II petitioning that his son-in-law, Raden Tumenggung Rangga Prawiradiningrat, be appointed Bupati Wedana of Madiun to succeed his deceased father, Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III; Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk's highly favorable appraisal of Pangeran Dipakusuma (DPK): exceptionally well-suited for his high post as Bupati Wedana of Madiun, consistently friendly toward Van IJsseldijk, and loyal toward the colonial government across decades of personal acquaintance.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Adipati Danureja IV (D R IV), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV), Ratu Ageng / Ratu Ibu (Ratu Kencana), Raden Tumenggung Sasradiningrat of Rajegwesi, Pangeran Dipakusuma (DPK / Raden Tumenggung Natayuda II), Sunan Amangkurat Mas (Amangkurat III), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (HB II / Sultan Sepuh), Raden Tumenggung Rangga Prawiradiningrat, Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Rajegwesi (Bojonegoro), Madura, Batavia, Eastern Mancanagara (Madiun).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kraton kinship networks, uncle-in-law (*paman ipe*), Bupati Mancanagara, Madurese royal lineage (Cakraningrat), Amangkurat III dynastic line, political banishment to Batavia, Bupati Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara, dynastic succession petition.
