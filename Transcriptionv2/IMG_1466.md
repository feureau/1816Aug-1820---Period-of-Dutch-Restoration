# Transcription of IMG_1466.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1466.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 51A, *Afgaande Stukken 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-01-06 to 1819-05-07
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official correspondence of Assistant Resident Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo: ordering a 17-gun artillery salute from Fort Vredeburg for Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV on Garèbèg Mulud; forensic report on the nocturnal bandit attack on the residence of the widow of Dr. Friedrich Baumgarten; compilation of official local commodity and grain price benchmarks; reporting the death of Pangéran Arya Kusumayuda in Surakarta; administration of the poll tax (*surat kondé*) on Chinese, Peranakan, Malay, Moorish, and Bengali merchants; armed raid on Desa Penting near Bedhaya by outlaws based in Desa Tambon; early intelligence on the subversive millenarian letters in Arabic script circulated by Kyai Iman Sampurna; and royal banishment of Raden Ayu Japan, Raden Nurtamajaya, and Raden Mertawijaya for extorting and oppressing the common villagers (*wong cilik*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Captain Chevalier (Garrison Commander), Dr. Friedrich Baumgarten (widow of), Pangéran Arya Kusumayuda, Kyai Iman Sampurna, Rijck van Prehn (Provisional Resident of Surakarta), Raden Ayu Japan, Raden Nurtamajaya, Raden Mertawijaya, Sultan Hamengkubuwana I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Fort Vredeburg, Surakarta (Sala / Solo), Desa Penting, Bedhaya, Desa Tambon, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Garèbèg Mulud, Fort Vredeburg garrison artillery, *surat kondé* (queue/poll tax certificate), Raad van Financiën, *pikul*, *padi*, *wong cilik* (common people), Pegon / Arabic script prophetic letters, royal banishment.
