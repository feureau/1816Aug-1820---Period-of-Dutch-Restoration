# Transcription of IMG_1415.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1415.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698a: Algemeen Verslag Solo [AvS], 1817: Gewone Rapporten en Residentie Notulen)
- **Date Range:** 1817-07-07 to 1817-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Institutionalization of the bi-weekly residency reporting cycle (*gewoon rapport*), judicial purges preceding Islamic court feasts, counter-insurgency expeditions in East Java, and the historic interstate diplomatic summit at Klaten. On 7 July 1817, the Surakarta Residency recorded the routine execution of the Monday and Thursday *gewone rapporten* conducted jointly by the Resident, Patih Sasradiningrat II, court regents (*nayaka*), and the Kapitan Cina, adhering to the traditional pre-Puasa judicial protocol: prisoners underwent formal sentencing, while detainees lacking evidence were discharged or banished. Resident Nahuys and Pangéran Prangwedana returned from their counter-insurgency expedition to Malang (having mobilized 2,000 men to quell regional rebellions). On 13 August, Pangéran Tejasukma and 100 armed men were dispatched to Kediri, while the Resident coordinated the date of Garèbèg Puasa with Sunan Pakubuwana IV, culminating in a joint banquet in the Residency gardens on 15 August. On 13 October, the Resident consulted Sunan PB IV regarding an upcoming border summit; on 15 October 1817, a high-level Surakarta delegation—comprising Patih Sasradiningrat II, First Regent (*Bupati Jaga*), Pangéran Prangwedana, and court translator J.W. Winter—convened at Klaten with Resident Nahuys and Yogyakarta court regents to establish bilateral treaties against highway robbery (*kècu*) and negotiate the seminal 1818 Interstate Law Book (*Serat Anggèr 1818*). On 22 October, the Kraton celebrated *Tahun Besar* (Garèbèg Besar) with garrison officers, priyayi dignitaries, and European citizens in the Residency House.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Johannes Wilhelmus Winter, Pangéran Tejasukma, Kapitan Cina, Bupati Jaga.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta [Sala / Solo], Yogyakarta [Djogja], Klaten, Malang, Kediri, Residency House & Gardens.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Gewoon Rapport* [ordinary bi-weekly report], *Nayaka* [senior executive regents], *Puasa* [Ramadan], judicial amnesty/punishment, *Kècu* [highway dacoity], *Serat Anggèr 1818* [interstate penal code], *Garèbèg Puwasa*, *Garèbèg Besar* [*Tahun Besar*].
