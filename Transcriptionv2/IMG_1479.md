# Transcription of IMG_1479.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1479.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Surakarta Residency Archive Bundel 131, *Translaten en Verbalen 1816–1819* / Notarial Archive J.W. Winter)
- **Date Range:** 1817-04-19 to 1818-12-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Three critical archival dossiers from the Surakarta Residency: continuation of Captain Baker's forensic inquiry into the predatory tyranny of Demang Puri at the Karangbolong birds' nest caves (collusion with thieves, forced cottage weaving of *ginggang*, monopoly markups on gambir, wage delays, currency manipulation, and coercing peasants to purchase rotting buffalo carcasses); royal transport economics on the Bengawan Solo river via the Sunan's heavy freight barge fleet (*Prau Penghêput*) carrying 100–120 *koyangs* of salt from Gresik (comparing Dutch VOC and British interregnum price and weight structures); border skirmish at Tembayat where Mangkunagaran troops intercepted retainers of Pangéran Jumendi; and an eyewitness report on the catastrophic volcanic eruption of Mount Kelud on 19 April 1818 submitted by Pangéran Tepasana of Kediri.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Demang Puri (of Karangbolong), Captain Godfrey Phipps Baker, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Pangéran Jumendi (of Tembayat), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Pangéran Tepasana (First Regent of Kediri), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (translator).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Karangbolong, Surakarta (Solo), Bengawan Solo river, Gresik, Tembayat, Desa Jlimanar, Mount Kelud, Kediri.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Labor exploitation, forced textile weaving (*ginggang*), currency depreciation (copper *duits* vs. silver Spanish Dollars), *Prau Penghêput* (Sunan's heavy river barges), *koyang*, *pikul*, Salt trade economics, Mangkunagaran patrol detachments, volcanic eruption (*erupsi Gunung Kelud*).
