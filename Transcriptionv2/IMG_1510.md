# Transcription of IMG_1510.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1510.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Primary Printed Colonial Statute (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 63 / *Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië*, 1820, Page 2) (Archetype 1: Primary Printed Statute / Organic Public Health Regulation (Articles 5 cont.–10))
- **Date Range:** Circa 1820
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding text of the organic smallpox vaccination decree (*Reglement op de uitoefening der koepok-inenting in Nederlandsch Indië*, Articles 5 to 10): establishing monthly reporting schedules to Residents; mandating semi-annual rural circuit inspection tours by European surgeons with a travel allowance of f. 4.50 per day; codifying shipping protocols for sealed cowpox lymph (*koepokstoffe*) or living vaccinated human carriers to Outer Island settlements; obligating Residents to cross-reference vaccination returns against demographic birth registers; and binding all colonial medical practitioners to state vaccination service.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Inspector of Smallpox Vaccination, Regional Residents, Residency Surgeons (*Heelmeesters*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Java Residencies, Outer Islands (*Plaatsen buiten Java*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Reglement op de koepok-inenting*, Inspection circuits, Per diem travel allowance (*vier en een halve gulden*), Cowpox lymph preservation (*koepokstoffe*), Demographic audit of births, Public health statutory duties.
