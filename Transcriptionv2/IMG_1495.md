# Transcription of IMG_1495.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1495.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Resolutions of the Governor-General in Council / *Besluiten van den G.G.*, 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-05-18 to 1819-12-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official vice-regal resolutions on princely titles, Indian cavalry veterans, native corruption trials, and royal succession: Resident Nahuys' dispatch of 18 May 1819 during his diplomatic mission to West Borneo proposing that Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat) be elevated to the exalted title of *Radèn Adipati* (deferred for further court dialogue in Yogyakarta); imperial amendment (1 June 1819) adjusting pay schedules for non-commissioned officers of the Bengal Lancers remaining in colonial service; high judicial inquest (*BGG 13 Sept 1819 No. 12*) investigating the defiance and financial corruption of the Demang of Jètis in Kedu, tried before the Resident, Pengulu, and Minta Pati, resulting in his expulsion from office; and formal receipt (30 Dec 1819) of Nahuys' landmark dispatch terminating the Pakualam regency.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (Resident), Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Governor-General Baron Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen, F.E. Hardy (Resident of Kedu), Demang of Jètis, Pengulu of Kedu, Minta Pati, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** West Borneo (Pontianak / Sambas), Yogyakarta, Batavia, Kedu (Jètis).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Besluiten van den G.G.*, Borneo Special Commission, Aristocratic title elevation (*Radèn Adipati*), Bengal Lancers, Inquest tribunal (Resident, Pengulu, Minta Pati), Administrative dismissal, Regency termination.
