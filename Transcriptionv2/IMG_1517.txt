11 Feb. 1820
Lippe to Sec. of State.
Secret no. 1.
Lippe passes on news of exile / [dismissal crossed out] of van Prehn/winter by PB IV from Solo
+ close investigation of case.

PB IV unusually cool reception to Lippe.
PB IV decision to Dismiss: Mas Tumenggung Sécanagara Head of Kalangans
+ Non Mas Ngabèhi Rèksanagara, Bupati of Pangeran Cakrakusuma

After returning from kraton - receives delegation from
outer Bupati Arungbinang
+ R. Ng. Rajamenggala
PB IV orders that no one should speak namely
Sécanagara - R. Ng. Rajamenggala
appointed in his place.

Secret 25 Feb. 1820 no. 2.
Patih Sasradiningrat comes to Lippe - tells him in name of PB IV that G.G. allows PB IV to take back all the rights he had previously enjoyed under the Batavian Republic pre 1806
+ proclamation to all Solo Inhabitants to that effect.

Lippe points out that G.G.'s letter misunderstood.
Patih says he knows nothing about it.

No translation of G.G.'s letter available ∴ unable to solve mystery. - had G.G. abrogated 1812 Treaty - Renewed by Dutch govnmnt. in 1816?

Asks for instructions from G.G.

PB IV already gives out that he had been reaccorded the rights he had enjoyed under V.O.C.
