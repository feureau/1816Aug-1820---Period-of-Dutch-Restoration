# Solo 1817 Difficulties

van der Kemp, *Het N.I. Bestuur in 1817*

p. 30
Difficulties of van Haak + Commandant Le Sueur or ? Le Clercq.
Reason for quick replacement to another Residency. Replaced briefly by van IJsseldijk.

p. 31
van IJ leaves Yogya court in 1799. Appointed as Raad van Indië.

p. 230
[van der Kemp, *O.I. Herstel in 1816*]
Great disagreement van Haak - Le Sueur / princes of Solo court also involved.
24 Jan. 1817 Lippe takes over Residency.

p. 231
Nahuys on sick leave to Batavia until 21 Nov. 1816.
[Margin: Kruseman deputies leaves Yogya 24 Jan. 1817.]
Nahuys goes to Solo to report immediate appointment of new Resident in Solo. 19 Nov. 1817 to Solo.
N speaks of "miserable disharmony in Solo between Resident, Commandant. Between civilians, soldiers. Jealousy between two leading princes."
cf Frederik Willem / Hendrik Willem P. van Haak: Speedy appointment of new Resident v. necessary.

p. 231 n. 2
22 Jan. 1817 no. 13
van Haak to C.C.G.G.
One of Solo princes in domestic service serves Major Le Sueur. Raden Kaap. Despite his dislike in so doing overheard all political talk in house of Major Le Sueur. Knows Dutch.
cf Raden Kaap's talk about European politics related to Solo court.
Idee England making war again on Holland / Java returning to English.
Refusal of van Haak to allow Solo princes to visit Major Le Sueur's house.
