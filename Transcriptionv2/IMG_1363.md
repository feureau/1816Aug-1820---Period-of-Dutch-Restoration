## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1363.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library: Nahuys van Burgst Collection, *BPL 616*, Portfolio No. 5, Item 7: *Procès-Verbal van het verhoor van Raden Kaap [Raden Anggadiredja]*, 23 January 1817; recorded by J. W. Winter)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official forensic interrogation (*Procès-Verbal*) and security dossier concerning Raden Kaap (Raden Anggadiredja / Raden Kassan Sedana), conducted at the Surakarta Residency on 23 January 1817 by Sworn Government Translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (J. W. Winter):
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Raden Kaap (Raden Anggadiredja / Raden Kassan Sedana), Johannes Wilhelmus Winter (J. W. Winter), Major Le Clercq, Captain Achenbach, Mr. van Oudshoorn, Mr. van Pol, Lieutenant Cock, Mas Mustapha, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Pekalongan, Yogyakarta (Djogjakarta), Cape of Good Hope (Kaap de Goede Hoop, South Africa).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Procès-Verbal* (official judicial interrogation record), Dutch language fluency, seditious rumor-mongering, 6-year hard labor sentence in chains (1809 Daendels precedent), military fraternization prohibition, Cape of Good Hope exile network, north coast travel passes (*passenstelsel*).
