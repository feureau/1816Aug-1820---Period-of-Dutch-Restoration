# Transcription of IMG_1344.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1344.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Baud Collection No. 306: W. H. van IJsseldijk, *Nota voor den Prov. Res. den Majoor Nahuys*, 22 October 1816; and *Rapport omtrent de Vorstenlanden*, 11 December 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-10-22 to 1816-12-11
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Demographic and agrarian analysis of Central Java: dramatic population expansion recorded between the settlement of the Third Javanese War of Succession (1758) and the Dutch restoration (1816); acute population pressure on arable land in the core *Negaragung* provinces; policy recommendation: surplus agrarian population should be actively encouraged to migrate and resettle in fertile, uncultivated wilderness areas; Commissioner W. H. van IJsseldijk's philosophical reflections on Javanese social conditions: he observes that an excessively prolonged peace has proven just as economically and socially disastrous for Central Java as prolonged warfare, due to unsustainable population pressure; records the tragic peasant fatalism wherein impoverished families sometimes regard infant and child mortality as a merciful blessing of divine providence (*wise provisioning faith*); unchecked demographic multiplication of the royal houses: vast proliferation of princes (*sentana*) and bupati lineages demanding aristocratic appanages (*lungguh*) from an increasingly depleted territorial base; radical colonial policy forecast: if peace endures in Europe following the Napoleonic Wars, the colonial government in Batavia should systematically annex the entire *Vorstenlanden* into direct administrative rule; public security measures: urgent clampdown required on armed rural banditry (*kècu*), proposing that indigenous regents (*bupati*) be held strictly legally and financially accountable for all robberies committed within their districts.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk (v.v.I / van IJ), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Central Java, Vorstenlanden (Princely States), Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Europe.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Demographic growth (1758–1816), internal migration / resettlement, Malthusian pressure, princely proliferation (*sentana* / Regent families), proposed colonial annexation of the Vorstenlanden, collective district liability (*tanggung-renteng*), rural banditry (*kècu*).
