# Transcription of IMG_1271.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1271.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 5: *Djogja Corr. [Secret] 1816–1819 — Memoranda on Dynastic Exile & Character of HB II*)
- **Date Range:** 1812-06 to 1819-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Nahuys van Burgst's secret political memorandum (c. post-March 1819) firmly opposing the repatriation of Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I and his son Radèn Tumenggung Mertawijaya (Mertojono) from exile in Ambon; recollection of their armed capture during the British storming of the Kraton (June 1812) and Mangkudiningrat's assassination attempt against Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing; danger of renewed insurrection in Madiun; Nahuys's secret retrospective analysis of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (14 September 1816), evaluating his intractable character, megalomaniacal building projects (*lust for building*), fiscal extortion of the peasantry, and paradoxical personal virtues (loyalty to companions and revulsion against torture).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I, Radèn Tumenggung Mertawijaya (Mertojono), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana I, Pangéran Ngabèhi, Tan Jin Sing (Kapitan Cina / Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Ambon, Madiun, Surakarta (Solo), Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Dynastic exile, Mangkudiningrat I conspiracy, Assassination attempt on Tan Jin Sing, Madiun rebellion, Character evaluation of HB II, Kraton building mania.
