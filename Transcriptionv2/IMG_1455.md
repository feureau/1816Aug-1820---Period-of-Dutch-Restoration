# Transcription of IMG_1455.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1455.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Kabinet Dossier No. 2065: Ingekomen Berigten over het Inlandsch Onderwijs, 1831–1832 / Sumenep, Surabaya, Gresik, and Madura)
- **Date Range:** 1831-10-10 to 1832-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Exhaustive inventory of traditional Islamic Quranic schools across the Kraton of Sumenep (Madura) under Sultan Paku Nataningrat, Resident H.J. Domis' polemical assault on unregulated Surabaya *langgars* as hotbeds of political rebellion, and proposals for state licensing (*Acte van Verstand*). On 10 October 1831, Sultan Paku Nataningrat submitted a village-by-village registry of Islamic academies across Sumenep: advanced centers teaching both the Qur'an and classical theological kitabs operated at Limbung, Batu-ampar, Ambunten, Gapura, Telangus, Negri Juma'ah, Gobewan, and Gresik Putih, alongside twenty-six village schools providing elementary Quranic recitation. On 16 December 1831, Resident of Surabaya H.J. Domis warned Governor-General Van den Bosch that hundreds of private prayer houses (*langgars*) operated without state oversight, sustained by *Zakat Fitrah* tithes, characterizing them as "breeding hotbeds of superstition, fanaticism, and opposition to government" that had fueled the Java War. Domis proposed authoritarian state regulation: (1) mandatory licensing through an official permit (*Acte van Verstand*) registered with the Resident; (2) prohibiting any school from operating unless it taught vernacular Javanese reading and writing; (3) issuing formal graduation certificates required for government employment; (4) limiting instruction for children under twelve to three hours daily with a subsidized meal; and (5) an absolute ban barring Mecca pilgrims (*Hajis*) from serving as schoolmasters. Reports from Gresik (11 Nov. 1831: 238 mosque schools, 2,603 pupils) and Bangkalan (12 Dec. 1831: 10 pesantrens) confirmed widespread religious schooling alongside private priyayi tutoring.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes van den Bosch, Sultan Paku Nataningrat (Sultan of Sumenep), Resident of Surabaya (Hendrik Jacob Domis), Panembahan of Bangkalan, Regency Regents of Gresik, Village Modins and Priests.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Sumenep [Sumënëp], Batu-ampar, Ambunten, Gapura, Surabaya, Gresik [Grësik], Madura, Bangkalan, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sumenep Quranic inventory (28 village centers), *Langgar* prayer houses, *Acte van Verstand* [state operating license], anti-colonial fanaticism, prohibition against Hajis as teachers, *Zakat Fitrah*, priyayi household tutoring, civil service employment prerequisites.
