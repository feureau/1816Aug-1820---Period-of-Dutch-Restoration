# Transcription of IMG_1264.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1264.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library, BPL 616 / Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Port. 5, No. 2: *Djokja Corr. 1816 [Sept.–Oct.] — Letters from Nahuys to Van der Capellen*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-20 to 1816-10-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatches of Resident Nahuys van Burgst detailing residency administrative operations; high praise for Court Translator J. C. Dietrée and formal recommendation for an 80-Rupee monthly salary; Nahuys's philosophical reflection on colonial governance (*"a calm, fair resident will always be the best guarantee of peace on Java"*); severe breakdown of Nahuys's physical health in late September 1816; medical order by residency physician Dr. Bowi directing Nahuys to evacuate to Batavia for recuperation (departing 7 October 1816); transfer of executive authority to Assistant Resident Johan Diederik Kruseman; half-yearly taxation returns; Kruseman's initial administrative survey of indigenous Islamic schools (*pesantrén* / religious schoolmasters) distributing unremunerated religious literature in Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Johan Diederik Kruseman, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Court Translator J. C. Dietrée, Dr. Bowi, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Batavia, Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Residency executive transfer, Medical evacuation, Court Translator salary, Java Rupees (J.R.), Islamic religious schoolmasters (*guru ngaji* / *pesantrén*), Taxation arrears.
