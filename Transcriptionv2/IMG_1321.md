# Transcription of IMG_1321.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1321.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Notes on P. H. van der Kemp, *Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1817, tot het vertrek der Engelschen*, pp. 321–322)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-14 to 1816-08-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on the military units and ceremonial retrocession of Yogyakarta; composition and financing of the Pakualaman cavalry corps (100 cavalrymen equipped by the British, receiving a monthly colonial subsidy of 750 Spanish Dollars); formation of a specialized auxiliary troop of 25+ Christian European Dragoons / Hussars (Dutch, German, and Indo-European soldiers) raised in January 1816 following the 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy to guard the Kraton; ceremony of retrocession on 14–15 August 1816: farewell lunch in Fort Vredenburg with Paku Alam I, his sons, Tan Jin Sing, and British/Dutch officers; solemn flag-raising ceremony on 15 August 1816 (lowering Union Jack, hoisting Dutch tricolor); Resident Major Nahuys van Burgst's official notification to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Paku Alam I (PA / Natakusuma), Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst (NvB), John Crawfurd, Kapitan Tan Jin Sing (Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV (HB IV / Jarot), P. H. van der Kemp, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Surakarta (Solo), Java
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Pakualaman Corps (100 cavalrymen), European Dragoons / Christian Troops, 22nd Dragoon Regiment, 1815 Sepoy Conspiracy, Retrocession Ceremony (15 August 1816), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
