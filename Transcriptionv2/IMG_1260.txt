14 Sept 1816 (CONT)
* M.B.II extraordinary stubborn=
* makes Res's position very difficult as position of ARH
* S S in Java - very shaky.

* Revan & Raden Rangga = encouraged by him = to attack solo territory = up to profit from solo

* AREA CONQUERED By English = H.B.II seizes opportunity to restore power.
* Danureja killed / murdered (1811-16)
* secret dispatches of BRITISH period = underline weakness of BRIT govmnt POSITION:
  * @ March in 1812 encouraged open tent not aggression
  * @ Reeng Mangkurat that would be murdered or poisen

  * O.W & weakness - OWN AN again recognized
  * V.d.M. v. Lial reconnect for British Res.
