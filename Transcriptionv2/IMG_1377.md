# Transcription of IMG_1377.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1377.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616, Port. 5, No. 7: Nahuys van Burgst Collection / Secret Letters Solo 1817, Jan–Feb)
- **Date Range:** 1817-01-02 to 1817-01-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official secret correspondence from Surakarta and judicial translations prepared by court translator Johannes Wilhelmus Winter regarding the Umar Mahdi uprising in Bagelèn. On 10 January 1817, Provisional Resident Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak alerted Governor-General Godert van der Capellen that Mas Umar Mahdi of desa Sambirata (Yogyakarta territory) had positioned himself as the leader of militant Muslim devotees in Bagelèn, transmitting sworn depositions from the religious preceptor Ibnu Hiaman [Haji Amidjah]. The first deposition, translated from a report by the Ngabèhi of Tanjong, Juma Tirta, to Kertaredja at Surakarta, describes a clandestine gathering on 2 January of 50 followers in desa Uneba, dressed entirely in white garments with multi-hued turbans, centered around Umar Mahdi's apocalyptic declarations of a heavenly mosque descending to earth (*Mesjid Tiban*) and mystical Qur'anic chronograms. The second deposition, taken directly from Ibnu Hiaman, details his personal interrogation of Umar Mahdi, who claimed to be a *Prajurit Sultan Rum* [Soldier of the Ottoman Emperor] acting under secret orders of Sunan Pakubuwana IV. Umar Mahdi prophesied that the Ottoman Sultan, accompanied by thirty holy Arab sheikhs including Sheikh Sayyidna Muhammad and Sheikh Sahman Waliullah, would arrive at Sambirata to crown Sheikh Sahman as the new messianic sovereign (*Ratu Adil*) of Java.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Umar Mahdi [Mas Omar Madi], Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak [van Haak], Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen [G.G.], Johannes Wilhelmus Winter [J.W.   - **Key Entities (Places):** Sambirata [Sambiratta, Djogja], Desa Uneba, Bagelèn, Surakarta [Solo], Tanjong, Mecca / Arabia [Arabic Lands], Rome / Istanbul [Sultan Rum / Ottoman Empire], Leiden.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Prajurit Sultan Rum*, *Ratu Adil*, *Mesjid Tiban*, *Santri* millenarianism, Qur'anic numerology / *primbon*, sworn judicial translations (*procès-verbaux*), white robes / turbans.

### Header & Archival Classification

* **Archival Series:** BPL 616, Portefeuille 5, No. 7 (Universiteitsbibliotheek Leiden, Nahuys van Burgst Collection: Geheime Brieven Solo 1817).
* **Document Nature:** Official Dutch translations of Javanese correspondence and judicial depositions (*verbaal van verhoor*) executed by Residency Translator J.W. Winter and transmitted by the Resident of Surakarta to Batavia.
* **Chronological / Thematic Trajectory:** Illustrates the cross-border intelligence network operating between Surakarta and Yogyakarta to monitor rural religious movements that invoked the name of the Surakarta Sunan to legitimize anti-colonial rebellion.
* **Administrative & Military Actors:** Surakarta colonial administration (Pinket van Haak, J.W. Winter), the Surakarta Kraton (Sunan Pakubuwana IV), and rural religious actors (Ibnu Hiaman, Umar Mahdi).
* **Archival Header:** Top left displays archival provenance notations (`N van B / BPL 616 / No. 5 / 7 / SECRET LETTERS / SOLO 1817 / JAN - FEB`).
* **Carey Annotations:** Red ink chronological and topical headings (`[Red ink: 1817 (Jan - Feb)]`, `[Top right: SECRET LETTERS SOLO]`, `[Red ink: Umar Mahdi / Bagelen]`, `[Red ink: Sayiddina]`).
* **Two-Column Organization:** Dates, translation headings, and official dispatch citations on the left; verbatim judicial texts, theological claims, and interrogation summaries on the right.
