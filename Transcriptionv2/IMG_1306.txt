### Physical Description and Layout
A historical manuscript letter written in cursive Javanese script (*Aksara Jawa*) on aged laid paper, with horizontal fold lines and staple rust marks at the top edge.

*   **Body Text:** 18 lines of Javanese cursive script in dark brown/black ink, containing formal correspondence and signatures.
*   **Archival Stamp:** At the top left, a circular purple ink stamp of the National Archives of Indonesia (*ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA*) with the *Garuda Pancasila* coat of arms.
*   **Embossed Seal:** Immediately below the purple stamp is a round blind-embossed wax/paper seal impression.
