# Transcription of IMG_1476.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1476.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62, *May–Aug. 1819*)
- **Date Range:** 1819-04-06 to 1819-05-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse inter-residency, agrarian, and fiscal dispatches: delivery of a ceremonial gift chest and valedictory letter to Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV from departing Commissioner-General Cornelis Theodorus Elout; criminal complaint from Pekalongan Resident Vos regarding the theft of gold jewellery by Raden Ratanan; Opzichter Engel coordinating with Pangéran Prangwedana to obtain pepper nursery plants from Surakarta for Pacitan; Nahuys requesting archive copies of the founding 1812–1813 British treaties with Sultan HB III and Paku Alam I; Dr. Harvey Thomson's tenancy confrontation at Rajawinangun over overlapping land tax demands (*pajak*) levied by appanage holder Ngabèhi Nayapirenta on leased sawah in Desa Kapayin; and Engel's Pacitan agricultural report on dry-season planting risks, surveying 40,000 coffee saplings on Prangwedana's plantations, and appointing 4 district constables (*oppassers*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** J. de Bruijn (Resident of Semarang), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Cornelis Theodorus Elout (Commissioner-General), C. Vos (Resident of Pekalongan), Raden Ratanan, Tumenggung Nayaningrat, C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Dr. Harvey Thomson (*Tuan Tom*), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Ngabèhi Nayapirenta, Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Yogyakarta, Pekalongan, Rememe, Pacitan, Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Rajawinangun, Desa Kapayin.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Commissioners-General (*Commissarissen-Generaal*), *Piagem* (royal charter), *jung*, *pajak* (appanage land tax), *bekel* (village tax collector), Spanish Dollars, *landhuur* (land lease), dry season cultivation, *oppassers* (police peons).
