1819.  June 1819 - March 1819 [June crossed out and Jan written in red]
Pacitan

[circled: BGG, 7 Jan. 1819 no. 5] zie BGG 26 Dec. 1817 no. 38 (18?)
PACITAN is not to be included in Landelijk Stelsel.
(1) Res. to Report on pop. in Pacitan. within 3 months
(2) Each family to plant 500 pepper bushes.
(3) + between pepper bushes = coffee to be planted trees.
If not enough coffee plants
Res. to sow coffee seeds (2 pikuls)
coffee/pepper to be sold exclusively to govant.

Demang Jagakarya - to be appointed Tum. + f. 1,200 p.a. salary.
as long as he sees to production of cash crops.

Demang Nitrawijaya - to be dismissed + given a pension
of f. 300 p.a.

Eur. Opzichter to be appointed in Pacitan + f. 200 p.m.
+ to be helped by native tan. officials drafted from other Res.
Bay of Pacitan to be surveyed for use as a port.

9 Jan. 1819 "Reglement op het Binnenlandsche Bestuur
en dat der Financiën op Java"

Prov. Reglement op de Criminele Regtsvordering by het
Hoog Geregtshof en de Raden van Justitie 10 Jan. 1819.
