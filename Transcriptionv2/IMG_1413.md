## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1413.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 60: July–December 1817 / KITLV H 698a)
- **Date Range:** 1812-06-21 to 1817-12-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Forestry administration conflicts, indigenous logistical support for the military, the pivotal historical coronation of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III following the 1812 British assault on Yogyakarta, early private land leasing initiatives for indigo cultivation, and court ritual expenses. By decrees of 6 September (No. 41) and 8 October 1817 (No. 73, Surabaya), the Commissioners-General (*Commissarissen-Generaal*) placed all teak forests across Java under the General Administration of Inspector of Forests Von Winckelmann, provoking deep diplomatic apprehension in the Kratons of Surakarta and Yogyakarta because the sovereign forestry rights had not been surrendered under the 1812 treaties. On 15 October, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV supplied baggage porters (*batur*) and relay horses for the Dutch garrison, expressing unbounded admiration for European military discipline. On 16 October 1817, C.F. Krijgsman (Government Translator in Semarang) transmitted a vital retrospective report to Resident Nahuys recounting the coronation ceremony of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III on 21 June 1812 (the day following the British storming of the Kraton): held on the steps of the Residency House at 4:30 p.m. amid a 19-gun artillery salute, British regular infantry, Bengal Sepoys, Dutch European Dragoons (Sultan's bodyguard), the Mangkunagaran Legion of Pangéran Prangwedana, and mounted Jayeng Sekar cavalry, with the coronation proclamation read trilingually in English, Dutch, and Javanese. On 25 October 1817, Dr. M. Thomson petitioned Nahuys to lease land in the Yogyakarta environs for commercial indigo cultivation, marking a foundational moment in European private agrarian enterprise. Additional notes record a colonial treasury reimbursement of f. 412 for the Garèbèg Besar festival, the death of Sunan PB IV's female relative Raden Ayu Sasradiwirya, and the illicit sale of Bengal and Levant opium in Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Inspector of Forests Von Winckelmann, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Raja Putra), Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Lieutenant-Governor Thomas Stamford Raffles, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), C.F. Krijgsman, Dr. M. Thomson, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Raden Ayu Sasradiwirya, Anthony Hendrik Doorn.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta [Djogja], Surakarta [Solo], Surabaya, Semarang, Fort Vredeburg, Gresik, Levant, Bengal.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Teak forests [Djati-bosschen], *Batur* [baggage coolies], British conquest of Yogyakarta (Bedhahing Ngayogyakarta, 20 June 1812), Coronation protocol, Mangkunagaran Legion, *Jayeng Sekar* [mounted border cavalry], private land lease [Landhuur], Indigo cultivation, *Garèbèg Besar*, Bengal & Levant Opium [Candhu].
