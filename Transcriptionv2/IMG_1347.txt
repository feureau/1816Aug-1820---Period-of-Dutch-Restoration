# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

7. van IJ
Baud 306.

Robbers plans – laid
often in Opium Dens
or Jandakan (?)

Bupatis = should question members of Opium Dens

If under influence of opium =
Javanese would Tell all.

& Inquiries into murder on Highways.

200 sp.D. = very often on defenceless Chinese.
charged to / from surrounding villages. For Body found on the High Road.
closest village } must pay ½. = those further away less decreasingly.

most evil-doers found in opium dens in the Towns.
cf discovered with in 40 days, 200 sp.D. paid Back

Has a very good effect during van IJ's period as Resident. 1798.

Sometimes 3x proper Tax Demanded By lesser officials from populace.

Sometimes farmers lose everything – have to give up their land to lead a Roving Existence.
