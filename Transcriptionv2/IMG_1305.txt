### Physical Description and Layout
A historical manuscript letter written in flowing cursive Javanese script (*Aksara Jawa*), on cream-colored unlined paper with visible horizontal fold lines across the center and staple rust stains at the top/bottom edges.

*   **Heading / Cartouche:** At the top right, enclosed within an elongated double-ruled rectangular border/box with rounded loops, is a Javanese invocatory line/title.
*   **Body Text:** Approximately 17 lines of Javanese cursive script in dark ink, arranged with indented salutations and formal epistolary structure.
*   **Seal:** On the lower-left margin is a round blind-embossed seal impression.
*   **Archival Stamp:** At the bottom right is a faint purple ink circular stamp of the Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (*Garuda Pancasila* emblem).
