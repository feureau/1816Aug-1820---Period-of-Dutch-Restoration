# IJsseldijk 1816[^1]

Robberies would increase as long as Rule in Vl. is corrupt / inefficient.

Would lead to Disturbances.

princes at Both courts would like it
cf enabling them to Fish in Troubled waters.

(1) Princes should not be allowed to leave kraton or dalěm.
(2) watch to be kept of Japas kyais / Hajis
(3) cutting Down of kêcus. = more orderly Rule necessary. = Patihs at Both courts must be made Responsible.
(4) Corruption = causing common man to leave his village = Turn into aimless wanderer.

Administrations must protect local Farmers.

Idea of incorporating of land of courts in = Dutch Admin.
Despite =
Difficulties of dealing & Dynasties and large families.

should cut off princes = from ordinary people –
cf situation as in Kědhu should be Repeated
