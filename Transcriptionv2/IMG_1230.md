# Transcription of IMG_1230.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1230.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: *Nahuys van Burgst Collection*, Portfolio 5, No. 7: *Secret Letters*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-08-20 to 1816-11-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Confidential correspondence and political intelligence analyzing the Kyai Murma investigation and the deep-seated dynastic crisis in Surakarta; Governor-General approves Assistant Resident Kruseman's aggressive handling of the Kyai Murma inquiry while demanding rapid resolution regarding Murma's court connections; colonial authorities examine Pangéran Mangkubumi's direct involvement in the 1815 Sepoy mutiny as the principal justification for his banishment from Surakarta; Mangkubumi is characterized as an unruly prince who harbored virulent anti-British hatred and exerted dangerous influence over his brother Sunan Pakubuwana IV; details the succession feud between PB IV's eldest son (the Crown Prince / later PB V) and second son Pangéran Purbaya (later PB VII, married to Mangkubumi's daughter), with the Crown Prince threatening to mobilize village inhabitants to defend his succession prerogatives; reveals that British plans to restore Kedu to Surakarta by recognizing Purbaya collapsed, triggering resentment that fueled the Sepoy conspiracy; PB IV exploits the crisis to permanently expel Mangkubumi from court.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Crown Prince of Surakarta (later PB V), Pangéran Purbaya (later PB VII), Kyai Murma Wijaya, Assistant Resident Kruseman, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Djocja), Kedu, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** BPL 616 (Nahuys Collection), Sepoy plot (1815), Court succession dispute, Annexation of Kedu, Royal exile to Ambon.
