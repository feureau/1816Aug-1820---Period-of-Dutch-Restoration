# Transcription of IMG_1475.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1475.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 62A & Resolutions of the Governor-General, March–April 1819)
- **Date Range:** 1819-03-22 to 1819-04-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Diverse fiscal, judicial, and agricultural dispatches: Governor-General's resolution (*BGG 9 Apr 1819 No. 10*) mandating an island-wide survey of all indigenous irrigation networks and dams; commercial protest from British agency Deans, Scott & Co. challenging Assistant Resident d'Abo's dismissal of opium smuggling indictments; family crisis in Pacitan as Tumenggung Jagakarya arrests his incorrigible son Kariadipura for outlawry, putting him in stocks and petitioning for his penal exile; official funding (*BGG 22 Mar 1819 No. 22*) for renovations to the Yogyakarta Residency House; Kariadipura's escape at Nawahan en route to trial; and Opzichter C.F. Engel's first concrete agricultural breakthrough in Pacitan with the planting of 26,420 Dadap shade trees (*Erythrina*) across eleven villages preparatory to large-scale coffee cultivation.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General Baron G.A.G.P. van der Capellen, Deans, Scott & Co. (British merchants), Robbert Christiaan Nicolaas d'Abo (Assistant Resident), C.F. Engel (Opzichter of Pacitan), Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya, Kariadipura, Nanatruna, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Yogyakarta, Pacitan, Surakarta, Nawahan, Danaraja, Wêdi, Plunren, Puji, Asêm, Gêtas, Karanganyar, Gasing, Mantrên, Kêbon palêm, Kêdongang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Hydraulic survey / dams, Opium farm enforcement, wooden stocks (*balok* / *pasung*), penal transportation, *Dadap* trees (shade trees for coffee), water buffalo draft teams, forced export cultivation.
