# Transcription of IMG_1325.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1325.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, Den Haag: *Ministerie van Koloniën [MvK] 4491*, Van IJsseldijk to CC. GG., 10 September 1816)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey's archival research notes on Commissioner-General Extraordinary Willem Herman van IJsseldijk's 10 September 1816 confidential dispatch from Surakarta; analysis of Sunan Pakubuwana IV’s formal letter of response to the Commissioners-General; diplomatic inquiry into why PB IV omitted the customary court compliments previously tendered to British Lieutenant-Governor John Fendall; Patih Sasradiningrat II's defense of Javanese court etiquette; Van IJsseldijk’s wry retort regarding the Sunan's notoriously dubious loyalty to the British; Patih's assurance of the Sunan's genuine joy over the Dutch return; warning to keep a vigilant watch over PB IV's impenetrable dissimulation; British diplomatic relations with the Sultan of Madura; recommendation for European troop reinforcements.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Herman van IJsseldijk (vIJ), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (PB IV), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih), John Fendall, Sultan of Madura, Cornelis Theodorus Elout, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Madura, Batavia, The Hague (MvK 4491).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Diplomatic Etiquette (*Court Compliments*), Epistolary Analysis, Javanese Dissimulation, Commissioners-General (C.C. G.G.), Troop Reinforcements.
