# Transcription of IMG_1229.jpg

## Metadata

- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_1229.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' Bundel 40: *Register der Resolutiën van den Raad van Indië / Kyai Murma Affair*)
- **Date Range:** 1816-09-01 to 1816-12-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official proceedings and government resolutions investigating the Kyai Murma affair and court security in late 1816; Assistant Resident reports on widespread public alarm and court anxiety in Yogyakarta sparked by rumors that exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) was about to be reinstated by the restored Dutch government; Council of the Indies (Raad van Indië) conducts an inquiry into Kyai Murma's correspondence network, interrogating the Islamic scholar Sèh Abubakar and three court mantris (Wangsa Dipa, Kerta Jaya, and Sewu Wangsadipa) regarding illicit correspondence involving Sunan Pakubuwana IV ("His Majesty the Emperor"); Governor-General in Council orders the Assistant Resident of Yogyakarta to round up all Indian (Bengalee and Malabar) residents residing across the Vorstenlanden and deport them to Semarang as an indispensable measure for public peace.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Kyai Murma Wijaya, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sunan Pakubuwana IV ("Z.H. The Emperor"), Sèh Abubakar, Mantri Wangsa Dipa, Mantri Kerta Jaya, Mantri Sewu Wangsadipa, G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen, Resident van Maark.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djokja), Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Vorstenlanden (VL).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Djokjo Brieven (Bundel 40), Raad van Indië (Council of the Indies), Bengalee & Malabar deportations (ex-Sepoys), Restoration anxieties, Mantri court messengers.
