Van IJsseldyk
Band 10

* Robberies would increase as long as rule in *uk* [^1] corrupt/inefficient.
* Would lead to disturbances.
* Princes at both court would like it if enabling them to fish in troubled waters.
* Prince should not be allowed to leave kraton on dalém [^2].
* Watch to be kept at Japan Kymia/Hagie [^3]
* Cutting down trees [^4] = more orderly rule necessary. = Path at both court must be more responsible.
* Corruption = causing common man to leave his village = Java into aimless wanderer.

Administration must protect local farmers.

* Doing incorporating of land [^5] = court in = Dutch Admin.
* Dispute
* At difficulties of dealing & dynesties and large schmetics [^6].

Should cut off princes = from ordinary people.
If not war in Kraton should be repated.
