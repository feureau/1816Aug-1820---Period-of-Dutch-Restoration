Jogya Letters – Jan – May 1820
Pj. Br. 63 A

- de Bruin (Res. Pinaragy)
  - K. Nahuyi,
  - 17 Jan. 1820
  - *Lady Nophia*: Naram... nhys... s... list... from G... Groot Oost & 7ths. cotton from Josyn.
- Pram. Jan. Wou.: 3 muls. 1747 (20 Dec. 1819)
  - 2 European Putut in Paranya
  - ... bu. t. w. pass through Desa Prampalan
- Pasuran Jogya 22 Jan. 1820
  - Inr. Pith to Groot Oost
  - 7 packs y. ctic. y. 700
  - 36 pax. Karang. f. 1980
  - 12 ½ Np. P. per corge. f. 2288.13
- Enga ti
  - Nahuyi,
  - 31 Jan. 1820
  - no more copper plats.
  - available in Keduwang.
  - only near 100 pikuls in Sollang.
- Planting of Palmy Trees + cleaning of Peterie [^1]
  - boko Nahuyi?
  - 30 puy 3 N. P. P. + give opium to 3rd W. Nest cu... on
  - in Ronyton.
- Trans. Jan. Wou., 6 1747 (1819)
  - large pasar bring nut at Sudiran on wage [^2]
  - Pasar Nging Ngajen...
- Beirig Kust + permintaan
  - K. Nahuyi,
  - 6 Feb. 1820
  - Capitai lara di Josya play
  - Tax Farm is old copper Puits
  - From P. Pendel. Brit. Period
  - Has K. pky. 3-4 Ambilus + entry No. 2
- Nakuyi - kippe
  - 8 Mar. 1820 (original note)
  - Asking for new copper Puits
- Trem. Jan. Wou.
  - As. a Kipendurung near Delanggu [^3]
  - 8 Feb. 1820, no. 9
  - P. Dinraprang, J. Paksi Alam. + Melay... let i...
