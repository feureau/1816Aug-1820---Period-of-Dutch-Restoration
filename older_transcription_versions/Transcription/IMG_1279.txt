REPORT ON H.B. III = BRIS GOVERNMENT TO HAND OVER LANDS WHICH WERE TAKEN OVER BY BRIT GOVERNMENT.
- VIEWING *MATUR* [^1] AS CHIEF-MINISTER OF DANUBOYODDO:
- "PRINCE WHOSE MIND AND VINDICATIVE CHARACTER HAD ALREADY WAITED SEVEN MONTHS WHILE HE HAD PROCURED THE THRONE, NOW SHOWN HIM TO BE UNWORTHY RULER."

---

### CONTRACTS IN 1812:
- UMUN DARET OF PATALAM [^2]: RETAINED A DEGREE OF INDEPENDENCE. CONTRACTS RESIGNED.
- OPIUM MONOPOLY / BARNOWARD: BIZARRO NEWS: UNDERGOVERNMENT CONTROL.
- 3RD NOV. 1814 H.B. III ACCREDITED TO THE THRONE.
- RESPECT YOUTH = M.B. IN OPPOSITION TO THE THRONE.

---

### IN ORDER TO CURB *PATALAM* INFLUENCE & REGENCY =
1. APPOINT 
   - © PATAM 
   - © PATIR 
   - © PATIR PUNCH + *DURAT* TUMENGONGS.
2. POLICE FILE: IN CHARGE OF EUROPEAN & PRINCE/DOMESTIC AFFAIR IN KRAM.

- SEE SULTAN PATAM ALAM [^3]

BUT THESE WERE REJECTED BY SULTAN:
- FEARING MUNICIPAL POWER BY THESE PEOPLE - SHOWING MORIAL DISTURBANCES IN CRIME & UNCERTAINTY.

... IN VIEW OF THE RANK AND THE STATUS OF THE PANG. PATADAM = TO GIVE RECONCILIATION OVER YOUNG SULTAN.

- 17 DEC: RECEIVES RANK & REGENT; GETS RIDGEBERET [^4] FROM RES. SARAWAN.
- P. G. DID NOT RECEIVE ANY DUTIES, EMOLUMENTS = BUT GIVEN CHARGE OF OMAR'S PRIVATE ENTERPRISES -> ENDING 1815 FROM RES. SARAWAN.
- BUT GIVEN CHARGE OF OMAR'S PRIVATE ENTERPRISES -> ENDING 1815 WITH OWN REVENUE.
- BUT MINISTERS TOOK PRIVILEGE TO BUY UP LAND ON HIS OWN ACCOUNT.
- REMOVED FROM CHARGE OF OMAR'S REVENUES IN DANUBOYODDO:
- BECOMES IN CHARGE OF ARMS & INVESTMENT IN CHOPPE & SARAWAN & PARTIAL EXCEPTION.
