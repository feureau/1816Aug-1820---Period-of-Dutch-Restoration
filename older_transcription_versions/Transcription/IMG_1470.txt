# Imam Sampurno Feb 1819

Jogjakarta
1819. Dec. 1819

*   Naluynt S.S. 10 Dec. 1819
    *   Market for cloth – Nogyk: 3000 piece Chitz
    *   Large pop - sale: 4–4,500 white cloth
    *   Greater consumption.
    *   Preference for imported cloth.

*   **Bathki printed in England according to specification.**
    *   *Crawford. Preferred because they do not run.*
    *   *Do not want to Javanese import these cloths.*
    *   *Will trim to three children.*
    *   *Parang Rusak / Kembang Cika + Kiroc-Procos. N.P.P. 3*

*   *N.L. 629. 24 Dec. 1819*
    *   Naluynt state to be returned to Sultan.
    *   From P.A. Blecck Reack age of minority.
    *   Become P.A. Buyup land extra appointed to Regent.
    *   Prit. Res. Gordon suspects owing Sultan's Revenue.
    *   + R.U. Lenco King Admin's revenue.
    *   giver to Ratu Ibu.
    *   Priyatna + Wancris H.B. II drawn up without intelligence P.A.
    *   Taken by Trusto Tim. & be scaled by P.A.

*   *If H.B. IV wants to keep order neat from P.A.*
    *   *Works through Patih using Patih's seal.*
    *   *# Growth of importance of Patih*

*   *Neat to be handed over & ceremony.*
    *   *P.A. to register neat present of J. 250 for*
    *   *paint in Reg – even though completely negative good.*
    *   *Niru does not miss position.*
    *   *Solo Br. 131. "Translation on Verbatim van N.C.A. Jaar 1816 to 1816 X.*
    *   *P.P. IV to all Inner/Outer Regents | Tom/Kurnomo | Village head.*
    *   *N.U. Religion of 7i volo. 21 Feb. 1819*
    *   *If anyone Guiencco Heaty Imam Sampurno or if*
    *   *Luta joined in their honor they would be punished like I.D.*
    *   *Bygon*
    *   *X Case Heard in Paris court in Nala*
    *   *Trans. of J.H. Wintin 1816–1819*
