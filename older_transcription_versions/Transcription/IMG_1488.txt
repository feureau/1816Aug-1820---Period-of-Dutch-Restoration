Van der Capellen in Surakarta

vander C in Jogya
Van Prehn in bed with apoplexy

Visit to Jogya — shorter than Solo
Not as pretty — but surroundings more pleasant
Sultan is like a farmer's boy. Farmer's boy, Solo
*Boerjong* [^1] more cultivated land man
Lang, dik [^2], *bobbeltop*
*Zeer loamp* [^3], 7 years old
*voute bitte* (PB IV)
*Amanaer* [^4]
exactly opposite of the Suraman
who is so civilised that the gg.
himself says he would grace a European Court

Visit to South Sea
Big carriage — *Cresses* at Marangan
Opak (Chinese Tax Farmer Organise)
Spends night — morning to warm springs
*Parang Wedang*[^5]
20 paces from the Sea

Visit to Parang Tritis — 2 grottoes clift
could not see the grotto at hangse
Story of Sultan fleeing here
Nahuys Consoles us not
to go there many years there
Nahuys-journey too dangerous
to go there

d'Abo family amusing
Van Prehn too close to Nahuys
