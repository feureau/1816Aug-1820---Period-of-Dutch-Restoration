Yoga - Nov. 1819

**Aug. - Nov. 1819**

Ro. Nindering  
K. Res. Yogya.  
14 Aug. 1819.  
BGS 13 Aug. 1819, no. 3  
Prov. Trend. in Nahnary & Instructor in Mr. School in Nahnary.  
Salary fl. 250 p. month, pensioned Lt. Thomas Paulus.  

BGS 4 Sept. 1819, no. 9  

> Dutch East Factories take part in Frank Java.  
> How to be informed of what sort of cloth most in fashion in Java.  
> prices  

No reply of Nahkary to BGS 25 Oct. 1819, in Dj. Br. 51.  

Maj. Gen. Comg. Troops Pck.  
-> Nahkary,  
4 Sept. 1819.  
Asking for information on So. Jogjakarta Nottas.  
No reply of Nahkary dt. in Dj. Br. 51.  
Need of irrigation.  

Erga to  
Nahkary,  
12 Sept. 1819.  
List of goods ever behind in **Pacitan** by Nitiwiclja.  
Goods in a poor condition.  

Van Prehn  
to Nahkary,  
14 Sept. 1819.  
General gives instructions for survey.  
**1 Teak Forest in Jogjakarta.**  
A great deal of wood recently destroyed.  
Are by govt. prohibited.  

23 Sept. 1819  
Permission for Louis Mille to reside in Java.  

21 Sept. 1819.  
H. Romnor.  
Erger to  
d'Abro,  
30 Sept. 1819.  
Approaching Rainy season suitable for planting pupu oulos. Tim. Jogjakarta.  
Next 130 per. to Kathowary to collect plant.  
First 10ider trees to be planted.  
Would wait for a few months before planting out coffee trees.
