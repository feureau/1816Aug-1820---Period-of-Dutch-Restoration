5 Oct. 1819           Visit to Yogya - shorter than Solo
pp. 25-26             not as pretty - but surroundings more

                                      more cultivated land than Solo
                      Sultan is like a boerenjonge / farmer's boy,
                      lang, dik, bobbekop
                      zeer lomp (17 years old)
                      unmannerly toute bête
                      exactly opposite of the Sunan (PB IV)
                      who is so civilised that the G.G.
                      himself says he would grace a
                      European court.
