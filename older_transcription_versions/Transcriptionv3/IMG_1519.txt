7 Jav. letters translated
made in Aug. 1819.
-> P. Aria Ciptasana, Radèn Aria Rêksanagara, Raden Panji Suma-atmaja.
all complaining about unfortunate dismissal from their positions.
+ goods unlawfully given to others.

[in red margin:] Van Prehn Dismissal.

-> Letter of Res. van Prehn: 5 Oct. 1819 no. 55.
marginal dispatch 17 Aug.
arguing against his dismissal.
good name brought into disrepute.

on Instructions of PB
P. Aria Ciptasana dismissed on name of PB IV
great annoyance of PB IV & Ciptasana complains to govnmt.
wishes to exile him and his son Jayaningrat.

Govnmt views that ->
van Prehn & Winter behave in an unlawful way in Surakarta:

(1) Van Prehn Dismissed as Resident.
(2) Lippe made provisional [Resident].
(3) Winter also dismissed.
then at Semarang.
to go to Batavia to be at disposition of Proc.-General.
(4) Van Prehn not allowed to exchange letters + those living in Yogya / Sala placed sub-poena.

Procuror-General to be placed in charge of the case.

Lippe to Inform PB IV of situation:
vis-à-vis Prehn.

Case of Van Prehn / Winter to be fully investigated.
