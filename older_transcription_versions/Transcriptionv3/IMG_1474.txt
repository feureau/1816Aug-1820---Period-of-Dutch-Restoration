                          Because of lack of Bushes / seeds.
                          Tum. Jagakarya Recommends that Res. should write to
                          Prangwedana in Sala to get coffee trees.
                          Pepper Bushes - Has a large Estate.
                          Coffee Had never been planted in Pacitan before.
                          plants would have to come from neighbouring Res.
                          In next Rainy season plants could be dug in.
