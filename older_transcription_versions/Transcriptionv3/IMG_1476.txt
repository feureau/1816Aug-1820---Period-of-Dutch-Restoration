H. Thomson                Jun.
to d'Abo                  Early 1818 Receives Piagem of 50 jungs of land
(Rêjawinangun)            from Patih
26 May 1819.              Asks him to give a person to point out lands / Desas
                          to Thomson.              [In red]: Thomson Renting Desa
                          in Desa Kaparjin - 1 jung = 3/4 Rented to Thomson. for
                          Belonging to Ng. Jayapiranta            24 Sp. D. p.a.
                          JP Rents it to Bekel for Sp. D. 7 who Rents it to Thomson.
                          [In red: 1819]
                          Ng. Jayapiranta comes to Thomson to ask him to
                          pay pajak of Sp. D. 40 for Tax to R. Adip.
                          Thomson refuses.
                          should Thomson pay him for same Rate as in Piagêm.

Engel to d'Abo,           [Circled in red]: Pacitan
29 May 1819               Tum. Jagakarya counsels against planting
                          pepper in Dry season - But Engel sends him
                          to Inspect coffee Bushes in PWd's Estates
                          & correct places to plant them out in Pacitan.
                          40,000 young coffee plants 6-12"
                          Govnmt. to appoint 4 oppassers in District Each &
                                                           Sp. D. 18 p.a.
