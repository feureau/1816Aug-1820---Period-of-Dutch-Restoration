Secret Articles for van IJ:
2 Aug. 1816.

(1) Inquiry into what British had said about
    Return of Java to Dutch to courts.

(2) Should quieten — any feelings of Disquiet on part
    of Rulers vis-a-vis New Dutch govnmt
    & abiding by contracts.
    good attitude of Dutch to courts.

(3) To press on Independent Rulers — permanence
    of Dutch restoration & Treaty & English.

                               (1812)
