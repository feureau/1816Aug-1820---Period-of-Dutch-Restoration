1815. QUESTION of SEPOY TROOPS: 1815 / MUTINY
Consideration P.B. IV much more as dangerous person than H.B. II
in contrary to what Nahuys van Burgst says.

Secret affairs: Lawick present at TRIAL
One of Sepoys if Rebellion successful:
1. would have taken TITLE of Gov.-General.
2. shot outside VERANDAH.

One of members of COUNCIL OF WAR: Council of Sepoy officers:
was presided over by a European Officer
Bedwell at that time Res. of SEMARANG.
(Ykhe knew about this & killed)

BEDWELL: Takes part in OATH:
1. but when discovered escapes secretly
2. caught on morning of execution found & throat cut.
under cover of darkness. Marches troops (& other members of council of war) from Semarang to Djoenoet.

PLOT uncovered as follows:
on very night that mutiny to break out:
European officers sit at same TABLE & Resident of Semarang.
1. One officer goes outside in the dark:
   not seen by Sepoy troops (2 guards) who are discussing whether to kill officers or not.
2. Returns to TABLE: and tells companions of situation.
Afterwards: 2 Sepoys questioned.
Plot uncovered: 50 Sepoys sent back in irons to Bengal.
Trial not over when island returned to Dutch.
