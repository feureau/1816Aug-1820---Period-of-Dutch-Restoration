Letter in Jav (Arabic characters) [In red ink, struck through: Kyai Imam Sampurno]
given out by Iman Sampurna warning that in coming year Alip
(1820) a mortal / virulent plague will descend on Java called Phemik
Trak Nyono. Following warning given to I.S.
Plague will come from West = countless numbers of soldiers will come in
shape of mosquitoes, millipedes, scorpions, snakes and tigers (each of which
will have a particular skill)       like a mist
Plague from East Nyai Rara Kidul = & all her soldiers & Sunan Lawu
with his soldiers accompanied by all Devils in Java will have all sorts
of weapons: owls, pikes, muskets, cangas, and snares. These soldiers hate
all men who are not favourable to them & would cause them to meet [Red asterisk]:
& with unpleasantness. The proud ones will be struck through & pikes.
The gamblers will be opposed so that they do not gamble any longer - those who
arrogate themselves will be disappointed - the discontented will be put aside.
immediately on the arrival of the Devils. "A sea of blood will be & many corpses
will be found." In order to oppose this & drive out the devils one will have to
do the five daily prayers & singing of Dzikr. // & giving of slametans & Balls
of Rice - 1 for each person present // & for offerings to Ratu Agama.
flesh of a chicken must be used. & sayur & Jangan asem & Bayêm. (mênir
Prayers to be used = Doorgo / Jurah Blai / Kêkanan / Slamêt       soup)
(To guard against Earthly Dangers & ensure good fortune) Each plate to be
covered & yellow serviette & each person present to utter confession of faith.
Once one knows it by heart: confession of faith to be spoken each
evening 44 x // "I believe in the Religions the Muhammadan whereby the
soul, which lies in the middle of the heart, is the Tree of Life, which is the sight of the root of the
Almighty who must be feared and honoured. The form of the Prophet
Muhammad will appear to all those who hearts are sincere, and are
pure in heart (opregt van gemoed, en zuiver van harte zijn) and these will also
enjoy worldly and heavenly blessings. I believe that all the miraculous
signs who may manifest themselves are from Almighty God - the true and
everlasting, wherefore each & every inhabitant of Java, both men & women are
warned by this and enjoined to believe firmly what they have learned from
the Holy Script. Contents of kitab Munajat as follows: I try to get the
protection of the Almighty who is all-present - who hears all and sees all -
so that the evil Devils may not seek me out and will not do anything to me
except let me live in peace // Those who pray to others but the Almighty
will be damned as great sinners / and for that reason the descendants of the
kafirs will never reach Heaven. O great God, forgive all the sins I have
committed and let me be purified by your influence. and what in you in
pure love, please show that and impart that love to me. - The person who
stands fast in his Religion is like the following   See over. Yogya Brieven
                                                    1820 Dj. Br. 63 A
