                       Iman Sampurna                 LÊDAYA / KEDHIRI
                       1819 Feb.                     Solo. Br. 131
                                                     'Translaten en Verbalen
                                                     en Rapporten Sala 1816-1819'

From what land are you?  From the land of the 1/2 people.
What are the 1/2 people called?  The old Race.
Who do they belong to?   Under the Ratu Artik Panteg Panatagama.
Those who believe in God, will obtain his Love. "  "  " was the first
of men To accept the True Faith - this King is a good-natured Ruler sprung
from Mataram blood // In general it is necessary to follow in the steps
of the old race (oude geslacht) God is long Before their Time already in
existence with his wonderful power, and he shall exist forever - we are born
into the world from God, but the meaning of every thing has been given to us
by the Prophet Rasulullah (Mohammed).  From what race are you sprung?  From
the old race.  What is your job? With whom do you work? With Raden Putra,
the rep. of God, our father & mother who resides in a place so small that it
resembles a pepper corn.  I love the Bok Fatimah, the most good-hearted
woman in heaven and earth.  Always Be cautious to say this: "The
Nease (gevoel) which Bok Fatimah has, that Have I also = Trans. Sala 17 Feb. 1819

Eenige Berigten wegens de uitgever van dit geschrift = Magelang 11 Feb.
1819 (F.E. Hardy)  Pêndita Iman Sampurna often sleeps on Mt. Lawu and
receives geschrift during meditation there / afterwards goes to wood of
Lêdaya in Kêdhiri where he turned a Brahmin temple (langgar) into an
Islamic temple - finds a gamelan there & set of wayang kulit. prays / give
slamêtans in villages of Lêdaya & local inhabitants who could all turn
themselves into macan gadungan / langgar of Sampurna held in reverence
by local inhabitants = Because by means of offerings the can achieve riches
and high positions // No one can go alone to the Temple without Being devoured
By Tigers / Sampurna sleeps in Temple & Has Idea of making geschrift
public // & distribute copies of above. Those who did not make copies were
devoured by Tigers / When copies become public PB IV & Res. Sala hear
about it = Sempurna arrested, interrogated By Res. Nahuys given over to
Penghulu anom & son of Penghulu kêtib d'émmêmmis = If anyone asks him
where he was from tells questioner to ask Kyai Abdul Rahman from
Desa Ampêl (Surabaya)

According to testimony of R.T. Danuningrat = letter of Sampurna has no
