[Yogya Letters - Dj. Br. 63 A / Jan. - May 1820]

                                Yogya letters           Dj. Br. 63 A
                                - 1820 -       Jan. - May 1820 [in red]

17 Jan. 1820
de Bruin (Res. Semarang) to Nahuys:
Lady Sophia & Mariam ships sailing from Bat. to Groote Oost to take linen / cottons from Yogya.

Trans. Jav. letter: 3 Mulud 1747 (20 Dec. 1819)
2 Europeans present in Panaraga
seen to pass through Desa Prampelan. [in red margin: Prampelan]

Factuur Yogya 22 Jan. 1820 for dispatch to Groote Oost:
7 packs of citis of Nono [Solo] -- Each pack 2 corges: f. 308
36 packs sarongs: f. 1,980.
12 1/2 Sp.D. per corge: f. 2,288.

[in red:] Pacitan
31 Jan. 1820
Engel to Nahuys:
No more pepper plants available in Kêduwang. Only finds 100 pikuls in Godang.
planting of Dadap trees & cleaning of Estates.
Asks Nahuys to pay 3 Sp.D. + give Opium to Birds' Nest collectors in Rongkob.

Trans. Jav. letter, 6 [Mulud] 1747 (1819):
Large pasar being held at Godian on Wagé / Paing.
Pasar Ngajug suffers because of it.
Being held & permission of R.T. Sasranêgara (Godian), Mas Ng. Nardikarsa (Nganjuk).

6 Feb. 1820
Domis (Sec. Semarang) to Nahuys:
Capitan Cina in Yogya pays Tax-Farm in old copper duits from? Daendels / Brit. period.
Has to pay 3-4 dubbeltjes & every Sp.D.

8 Mar. 1820 (pencilled note)
Nahuys - Lippe:
Asking for 1500 new copper duits from Surabaya.

Trans. Jav. letter:
Desa Kêpundhung near Dêlanggu.

BGG 8 Feb. 1820, no. 9:
P. Suriaprang, P. Paku Alam.
Appointed as Capt. of King's Armee = & salary of 1st Lt. of Infantry.
