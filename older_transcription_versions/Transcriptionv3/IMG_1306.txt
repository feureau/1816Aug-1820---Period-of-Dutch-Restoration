[Archival Document: Surat Jawa / Original Javanese Manuscript of Kyahi Adipati Danureja IV (Purwo)]

[Stamps and Seals]
- Top-Left: Circular purple ink stamp of the National Archives of Indonesia:
  "ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA" (with Garuda Pancasila coat of arms).
- Immediately below stamp: Circular blind-embossed paper seal impression.
- Top edge: Rust stains and twin puncture marks from wire fasteners/staples.

[Physical Description]
Single folio sheet of laid paper, horizontal center fold. 
18 lines of flowing cursive Javanese script (Aksara Jawa / Hanacaraka) in dark brown/black ink.

[Context / Identification]
This manuscript is the original Javanese text corresponding to items 7 and 8 of the contemporary Dutch administrative transcriptions (cf. IMG_1308 and IMG_1309), being the formal memorandum (*pemut*) of the Grand Vizier of Yogyakarta, Kyai Adipati Purwa (Danureja IV), dated Friday, 11 Dulkangidah A.J. 1743 (c. September 1816), setting forth detailed accusations regarding the improper influence, court irregularities, and arrogant conduct of Tan Jin Sing (Captain Jim Sing / Raden Tumenggung Secodiningrat).

[Romanized Javanese Transliteration / Content]
Punika pemut sahuninganipun kangjeng kyahi Dipati Purwo, prakawis sapatrap-patrapipun kapitan Jim Sing, kang nama raden tumenggung Secadiningrat, tindak-ipun ing Negari, ingkang rumiyin gadhah atur dhateng kangjeng Ratu Ibu, asring nyahosi serat pyambak, kalih Raden Dipati asring rembagan dhateng pagriyani-pun raden Secadiningrat wanci dalu, kalih raden Secadiningrat agadhah kajeng tilas kalangenandalem, raden ayu Murtiningrat kadamel semanipun, ingkang di-pun pulas ipeneipun radenayu Murtiningrat, anama Mertapiyoga, mantri Jawi, yen ngantos kelampahan kaebang kadamel bupati, raden Dipati ingkang dipun tempanaken kengingipun, kalih gadhah kapal teji badhe kalambangaken kalih randhanipun Kangjeng Sinuhun. Kalih wonten tiyang jawil dhateng kapitan, yen temtu Inggris kagantos ing Welandi wonten watiripun dhateng kapitan, kapitan wicanten: Welandi mangsa sageda bilaheni dhateng piyambakipun, sawab piyambakipun kaangkat ing Ratu kahiden ing Gupermen Inggris. Dene Welandi punika tiyang beburuh dhateng Gupernemen Inggris. Kalih saweg marengi memantu anak-ipun jaler, Kangjeng Sinuhun miyos kalih Kangjeng Ratu Ratu, Raden Dipati sakancanipun Bupati sami derek sadaya, sami sela kemawon wonten ing kongsi, sami anedha eca, mawi bendara bendara putri sentana, bupati, sasampunipun bendara bendara putri sentana, nunten bendara putri kalih nyonyah kapitan, kang nama radenayu Secadiningrat, inggih sami nedha eca, mawi kasegan inuman, bendara-bendara kathah ingkang sami wuru, kalih raden dipati gadhah parentah dhateng kancanipun bupati, yen utusan dhateng raden Secadiningrat, kadosa yen utusan dhateng raden Dipati, punapadene yen utusan dhateng denayu Secadiningrat, inggih kadosa yen utusan dhateng Denayu Danureja samiya mawi nyembah, kalih kapitan purun purun anglambangi sitinipun tiyang kang kamantunan raden Mangundipura, kalih gadhah bedhaya estri. Kalih yen mawrengi lenggahan wonten Kadanurjan, amor kalih bendara bendara putra-sentana, jajar kalih bendara Pangeran Mangkubumi, kalih yen nginggahaken bupati mawi nedha sabin.

Sinerat ing dinten Jumuwah tanggalping sawelas ing wulan Dulkangidan ing tahun Dal angkaning warsa 1743.

Punika tandha astanipun kyahi Dipati Purwo.
