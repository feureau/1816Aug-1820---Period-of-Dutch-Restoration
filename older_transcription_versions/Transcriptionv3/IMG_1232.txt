Extract van Registers der besluiten v. Commissaris-Generaal

19 Aug 1816
Govnmnt of Dutch E. Indies taken out of hand of BRITISH

23 Aug
D.G. Receiving an Ambassador extraordinary
[in red ink: - Van IJsseldijk]

3 Sept 1816
Ratu Kentjana Woelan
[3 R. Ayus: Ayus Moortiningrat, Djayaningrat, Dewaningsiat] go to Batavia to join old Sultan in exile.

6 Sept 1816
Questions PUASA feast over 400 silver Rupiahs given to PAKU ALAM [gift of Waterloo Fund]

Sept 9 1816
Crawfurd to Nahuys
50,000 [crossed out: claimed from due to Sultan] for Rents of Casungs / Bagelen etc already paid.

9 Sept
Djogja
[IJsseldijk to Nahuys]
Reason for visit to Re-affirm establishment of DUTCH AUTHORITY:
Expects from King & SULTAN: of the Netherlands.

11 Sept 1816
Willem to Resident
Market of Klaten – Toll-gate for Bazaar fees set up

12 Sept 1816
Relief Bazaar toll collector to ask money from market Inhabitants.

22 Sept 1816
Dictrée to Nahuys.
[in red ink: Dictrée]
Dictrée placed as Interpreting Javanese in Jogja By Daendels in 1808.
Asked for Resignation in 1810 because of poor Dutch, but had served English and Moorish well.
Now asks to Be Reinstated.
