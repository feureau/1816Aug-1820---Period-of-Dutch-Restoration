MANGKU BUMI:
MB = connection & SEPOY REBELLION = reason for exile -1815-

very unruly person — hatred for ENGLISH.
particular influence on P.B. IV (PBV)
[There is a copy of this letter in Baud priv. coll. no. 306.]

Hatred of P.B. IV for capricious conduct of CROWN PRINCE
.: attempt to get 2nd son ARIA PRABU PURBAYA as CROWN PRINCE.
cf daughter of Mangku Bumi m. to PURBAYA
.: STRONGEST SUPPORT.

BUT CROWN PRINCE especially annoyed over plot
.: annoyance of Sunan for Brit support of CROWN PRINCE
dejection over loss of KEDU.

HOPE C-I-N PURBOJO RECOGNISED = in return for restoring order after revolt.
WEST or UPLAND KEDU?
.: attempt to win over SEPOYS & PRANG WEDONO =
BUT attempt to win over: P.W. proves fruitless.

SEPOYS join in plot
for hatred of BRIT GOVNMT
NOT for Family Quarrell.

BUT CROWN PRINCE gives away plot to betray sworn enemy MangkuBumi.
Mangku Bumi .: made scapegoat when plot divorced
SUNAN remains: Behind the scenes —
refuses to come out in suppression.

[in red ink]: PW
EXTREMELY IMPORTANT TO WIN OVER MB YET MORE FIRMLY
to the GOVNMT: cf give him KOLONELS POSITION & pay TROOPS.
