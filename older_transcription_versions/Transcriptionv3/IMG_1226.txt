A.J. v.d. de Salis to CCGG
21 Aug. 1816
[in red ink: Desa Brunjak]
Burning Down of 20 Houses in Pekalongan on 5 Aug. 1816 Roofpartij.
14 Buffaloes taken & 258 Rups. worth of goods.
[underlined in red: 2 Demangs have evil Reputation.]

BCCGG.
30 Aug. 1816.
Letter of Nahuys 19 Aug.
HB II Asks British to Send him Ratu Kencana / 2 R. Ayus from Yogya.
2 R. Ayus Refuse to make journey.
Should be sent by sea from Semarang.

BCCGG.
Sept. 1816
Letter of Nahuys 23 Aug.
Ratu Kencana Wulan / R. Ay. Murtiningrat / Dayaningrat } prepared to leave for Batavia.
Necessary passes to be given

BCCGG.
Sept. 1816
Letter of Nahuys, 27 Aug.
Garibeg Puasa passes off without trouble.
Paku Alam gives 400 silver Rupiahs for Waterloo Fund.
[marginal note: BCCGG, 27 Sept. 1816. Nahuys pays Rp. 1219.24 on Drink for Birthday Party of K. William + Garebeg Puasa party.]
