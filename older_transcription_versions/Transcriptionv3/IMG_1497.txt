[Solo Br. 131 - Prophetic letter from Ajibarang / Banyumas men]

                                                      [Solo Br. 131 - in red]

We, a true-born Ajibarang man and a true-born Banyumas man begin to write this letter on Sunday in the month Repit. The latter, the Banyumas man, is the one who knows the revolution of heaven and earth, of the moon and the sun. He is also aware of the course of life of my lord the Raden General, who is very powerful, and who | brought his brother and his consort and the Princess (read: gemalin en de P. ?), and who is accompanied by eleven Mantris. The aforesaid Raden General had departed one evening in the month of Repit on a journey, and already for two apits or years was staying in a Palace, but at present, just before the setting in of the good season, he is here. The Ajibarang man also knows that my two rulers (?) were just now busy taking counsel in the kraton in order to appoint four prominent persons, and it shall happen this very day that the world will be reversed: my lord who is in the east will go to the west, the elder brother of my lord will go to the south, and the son of the Adipati of Brebes will go to the north - this is a descendant of Sunan Kuning - , and his younger brother named Lurung Tengah will be made king of the Righteous (or, perhaps, Ratu Adil?). All my friends the Europeans will ac-| company my lord the Raden General, and he who wishes to make the world anew will | take father the General as a witness to his action. All those living in this world are my brothers, if they are men; all those who are in Heaven are my consorts, if they are women.
        This is the instruction of my brother from Ajibarang who knew how to still the southern ocean. I felt very much worn out, having served the Raden Tumenggung for two apits or years and until now, just before the start of the good season (text: seasons).
        This letter must first be handed to Raden Tumenggung Udanagara; secondly to my lord Pangeran Prangwadana; thirdly to my lord the Patih; fourthly to my sovereigns the two Princes; fifthly to father the General who has powerfully ruled and was possessed of all lands, and likewise ^to his daughter, the Princess, my sovereign.
        It is also established that the cannon Nyai Setomi is the property of my lord and father the General, who through his great genius knows to fathom the intentions | of every mortal. Finally, I recommend the content of this letter to the favourable notice of my superior, the mighty Raden General.
