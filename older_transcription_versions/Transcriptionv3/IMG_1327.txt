2 Sept. = van IJ Received & Full honours at Sala
                                         Kraton.
Crown Prince / Bumirata
& all Brothers / other princes come out
to greet van IJ.
sits on left in Sunan's coach.
Ride to Sala court.
meeting & PB IV at Srimënganti
& Res, Col. van Wenne, Prang Wëdana.
already knows PB IV for 32 years
as Crown Prince.
18 years ago van IJ well Received by PB IV
before he went as Comm. to Surabaya.
PB IV answers in a friendly fashion.
leads van IJ by left hand to kraton.
-> great pendhapa in kraton
prepares stool for van IJ
covered & yellow Damask.
3 gun salute for van IJ.
tells PB IV of letter from C-G.
Read by Krijgsman in Javanese.
& letter from John Fendall
Read out aloud by Adjutant.
PB IV to come on Tuesday to house of Resident.

private apartments
by Ratu Kencana.
