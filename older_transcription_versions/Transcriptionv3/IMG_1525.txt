[Yogya Letters - Dj. Br. 63 / Oct. - Dec. 1820]

                                Yogya letters           Dj. Br. 63
                                - 1820 -        Oct. - Dec. 1820 [in red]

BGG 28 Oct. 1820 no. 21:
SÊCADININGRAT:
Son-in-law of Tan Jin Sing = Lim Nom Tja asks to be appointed Lt. Cina in Yogya. Application Refused.

Report on coffee plantation PACITAN
Engel, 29 Oct. 1820:
1,305 Families have no sawah   } only pêkarangan
2,452    "     have some sawah }
Pacitan: 3,757 Families.
341,174 coffee trees planted 1819 making Total of 341,174.

29 Oct. 1820
Engel to d'Abo:
Only 56,000 coffee trees planted in 1820.
Peasants not used to working for govnmt. plantations!!
Prepared to plant pager coffee in Desas.
"Different qualities of land from Desa to Desa."

30 Oct. 1820
Id. to Id. [in red margin: Pacitan]:
Tum[ênggung] Jagakarya to Kiduwang to get pepper plants from MN I.
Belated planting of coffee / pepper in 1820.
Difficulty of procuring pepper plants.

30 Nov. 1820
Engel to d'Abo:
Difficulty of collecting Birds' nests because of heavy surf.
Tum. & 80 Baturs to collect pepper plants at Goddong.

16 Dec. 1820 (10 Rabiulawal 1748)
PB V - HB IV:
Receives news of Death of P. Dêmang in Yogya.

[in red:] Pacitan
23 Dec. 1820
Engel to d'Abo:
Need for active Jav. officials in Pacitan. Bêkêls to oversee coffee / pepper.
Eldest son of Jagakarya -
Nuradiwiria v. active for past 2 years but enjoys no salary. Should be appointed as Dêmang.
Population busy since May -- cleaning out Estates, would soon Begin planting more coffee.
