[Carey's Handwritten Notes: Nahuys to Commissioners-General, August 1816]

[Header in red ink]
Yogyakarta Aug 1816
Nahuys to Commissioners General

P.H. van der Kemp.   Het Nederlandsch-Indisch Bestuur in 1817,
                     tot het vertrek der Engelschen
                     Naar Oorspronkelijke Stukken.
                     's-Gravenhage, 1913.

p. 24       Nahuys reaches 9 August
            Yogya before arrival of Dutch troops.

            [circled in red]: 14 Aug. takes over authority from Crawfurd.

Nahuys to
Commissioners-General,
11 Aug. 1816  no. 2.
p. 320 Bijlage III   HB IV very obedient to Paku Alam (Regent)
                                            & Patih
            Crawfurd does much to reassure Yogya court
                       concerning handing back of Java to Dutch.

Ratu Ageng — 3x entrusts HB IV into care of Nahuys.

Brit. Appt. Resident remains; Crawfurd very much
                    esteemed by Paku Alam, Sultan, Patih,
                                           Ratu Ageng.
            Nahuys able to see this for himself at
                    party in Residency House before Crawfurd's
                    departure.

p. 321      Crawfurd very much loved at the court
            Nahuys to continue C's style of administration.

Paku Alam owes life to Nahuys if saves him from Daendels' execution order. [marked with red brackets and asterisks]
            Very pleased at appointment of Nahuys.
P.A. afraid that a friend of Daendels would be
            appointed — cf Herstel, p. 223. Tiedeman
            first appointed to Yogya
            earlier govt. sec. to Daendels.

Paku Alam legion to be taken into Dutch service
                      everything prepared.
