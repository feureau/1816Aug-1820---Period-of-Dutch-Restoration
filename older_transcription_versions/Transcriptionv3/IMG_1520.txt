[Solo Br. 122 - State of Solo Court in 1824 / PB VI Erratic Behaviour & Regency Plan]

Solo. Br. 122
Van Sevenhoven to G.G.
22 Oct. 1824

State of Solo Court in 1824 = See Litt. P.
Reports of Temp. Res at Sala 19/26 Aug. 1823 & 2/4 Sept. 1823

[In red margin:] PB V Bapak was too young when acceded throne.

Reporting on circumstances of PB VI's Accession to Sala throne.
Shortly after accession shown signs that has a will of his own.
Buminata / Sasradiningrat & Dutch have to prevent him contracting a marriage & giving lesser rank.
Had m. d. of P. Ngabèhi = PB IV wants her to be PB VI's principal wife.
*
Soon after marries another girl / + increases seraglio /
d. of Ngabèhi (Ratu Mas) PB VI makes life so unpleasant for her that she had to appeal to her father. Ratu Mas not a beautiful woman But a v. charming / v sweet person.
PB VI's sudden appointment seems to have turned his Head.
Cuts off close links & Buminata / foster-father Sasradiningrat.
Refusal to accept long titles in Reports submitted by Patih only allows mention of name. Says that "Resident along with all the other Dutchmen are my subjects."
On meetings & lunatics = PB VI places dhampar on a table = & many of actions gravely insulting to older princes.
Despite promise at apptmt. as Sultan refuses to follow advice of Patih & Buminata.
"Irregularities & cruel deeds of PB VI" Tortures people to Death.
But Res. not able to make an open investigation :. facts not proved.
Throwing money away / hitting people & Riding whips & swords of anyone who crosses him. & cf attacks on Bedayas. & attacks on Kyai Maja's Bulkiar.
cf had hit his uncle kol. comm. of Troops // At night pretends to see strange faces & acts Absurdly. See Winter, p. 101 n. 6 20 Feb. 1824 PB VI n. dg p. MB
Completely Immovable will = secretly helps of wants to marry daughter of P. Mangkubumi / P. Adinagara :. Tries to break marriage to P. Ngabèhi's daughter.
Van S. allows PB VI to send Ratu Mas home to her father.
Pengulu of Sala instructed not to permit Divorce without permission of G.G.
"Fears of what PB VI would do when He becomes a man & learns his own strength"
PB VI now 18 years old (:. Born 1806) But shows a Boldness of character without parallel.
P. Ngabèhi / Ratu Mas = Deeply embittered by PB VI's actions.
Van N's involvement in affair makes impression on PB VI earlier speaks directly to Res. in Malay / now speaks to Buminata in Jav.
Thanking Van N. for his Advice.
Buminata / Patih advise that all involvement of PB VI in state affairs should be ended in order to avoid a disaster.
