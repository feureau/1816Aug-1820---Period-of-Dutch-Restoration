p. 30. Difficulties of van Braam + Commandant Le Sueur
       - quick transfer to another residency - replaced

       to Raad van Indië.

p. 230. Great disagreement van Braam - Le Sueur -
        princes of Sala court also involved. 24 Jan 1817
        Lippe takes over Residency.

p. 231. Nahuys on sick leave to Batavia until
        21 Nov 1816 (Kruseman deputises, leaves Yogya 24 Jan 1817)
        Nahuys to Sala to report on appointment of new Resident
        (19 Nov 1817 to Sala).
        [in left margin:] [Red ink:] Nahuys on Sala in 1817
        Nahuys speaks of "miserabele oneenigheid te Sala
        tusschen Resident, Commandant / tusschen burgers, militairen
        / naijver tusschen twee aanzienlijke vorsten." Speedy
        appointment of new Resident necessary.

p. 231 n. 2. 22 Jan 1817 no. 13 van Braam to C.G.G. - one of
             Sala princes in domestic service serves Major Le Sueur
             - Raden Kaap - overhears political talk - knows Dutch.
             Rumours of England making war on Holland.
             Van Braam refuses to allow Sala princes to visit
             Major Le Sueur's house.
