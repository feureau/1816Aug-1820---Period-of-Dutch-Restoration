[Archival Cover Sheet / Folder Divider]

[Physical Description]
A blank folio sheet (showing mirrored ink bleed-through from its recto) with heavy age toning, rust, and water stains along the edges, placed on top of a sheet of blue-lined paper. In the upper margin of the lined backing sheet, Peter Carey has written an archival classification header in red/pink ink.

[Carey's Handwritten Inscription in Red/Pink Ink]
van IJsseldijk to PB IV 2 Sept. 1816
