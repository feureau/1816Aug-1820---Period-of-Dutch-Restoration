[Archival Document: Surat Jawa / Original Javanese Manuscript]

[Stamps and Seals]
Top-Left: Circular purple ink stamp of the National Archives of Indonesia:
"ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA" (with Garuda Pancasila emblem).
Directly below stamp: Circular blind-embossed paper seal impression.
Bottom-Left: Faint, faded circular archival stamp.
Top edge: Rust stains and puncture holes from two wire fasteners/staples.

[Physical Description]
Single folio sheet of laid paper, horizontal center fold. 
18 lines of elegant, flowing Javanese cursive script (Aksara Jawa / Hanacaraka) in dark brown/black ink.

[Context / Identification]
This manuscript is the original Javanese text corresponding to entries 5 and 6 of the contemporary Dutch administrative transcriptions (cf. IMG_1308), dated Saturday, 20 Sawal, year Wawu, A.J. 1737 (c. 1810 A.D.), addressed to Kangmas Tumenggung Sumadiningrat regarding court security and loyalty during unrest in the Sultanate:

[Romanized Javanese Transliteration / Content]
... yen wonten mengsah, sampun ngantos lumebet ing Kratondalem. Wondene kula boten pisan-pisan yen balelaha ing Sampeyandalem. Boten langkung pandonga-Dalem ingkang kula suwun sarta berkah panjurungipun ing leluwurdalem ingkang sami ambeg Ratu pinarjurit, nanging Sampeyandalem sampun ngantos kelajeng-lajeng gadhah karsa adamel ing kemlaratan kula, saklangkung atas sanget ing panuwun kula mila sayektos, kula temen-temen sumedya ang rimpili susukering tanah Jawi, sukur malih ing tembe kula saged adamel ingkang dados ecanipun ing Kratondalem tuwin suka leganipun ing nala Dalem.

Ingkang punika kangmas tumenggung Sumadiningrat, unjukipun kahuiningan ing Sampeyandalem kula pitajeng ing sampeyan.

Sinerat ing dinten Saptu tanggalping kalihdasa wulan Sawal ing tahun Wawu angkaning warsa 1737.
