Had been laid up for 4 years under British period =
Travel costs too High despite ending of tolls on Sala River.
could carry 100/120 coyangs salt from Gresik But only 1 x p.a.

4-5 journeys p.a.
1 coyang salt (40 pikuls / 5000 ponden) & Sp. D. 12 in Gresik //
under Dutch.
During Brit. period 1 coyang Reduced to 30 pikuls (3750 ponden)

Usually 10 prau penghuput go to Gresik at end of Dec. laden & pepper.
Bring Back salt.
