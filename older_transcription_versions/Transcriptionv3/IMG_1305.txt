[Archival Document: Surat Jawa / Original Javanese Manuscript of Paku Alam I to W.H. van IJsseldijk]

[Note on Image Orientation]
The photograph IMG_1305.jpg was taken inverted (180° upside down relative to the text flow). When viewed upright:

[Stamps and Seals]
- Upper section: Prominent blind-embossed circular paper seal impression on the margin.
- Lower margin: Faint purple circular archival ink stamp of the Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (Garuda Pancasila emblem).
- Along edge: Rust stains and twin puncture marks from wire fasteners/staples.

[Physical Description and Layout]
- Single folio sheet of antique unlined laid paper, with horizontal center fold.
- Head of document: An ornamental rectangular cartouche/border with delicate decorative loops containing the formal Javanese opening invocation / heading.
- Body text: 17 lines of elegant cursive Javanese script (Aksara Jawa / Hanacaraka) in dark ink, with indented salutations and formal courtly epistolary structure.

[Context / Identification]
This manuscript is the original Javanese letter from Pangeran Adipati Paku Alam I to Commissioner Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk concerning the conduct of Tan Jin Sing (Captain Jim Sing / Raden Tumenggung Secodiningrat), dated 5 Dulkangidah A.J. 1743 (September 1816). The conclusion and Dutch translation appear on IMG_1307, and a typed transliteration appears on IMG_1309 (items 9–10).

[Romanized Javanese Transliteration (Opening Section)]
Saudara kula, Tuwan Idelir Wouter Hendrik van Yseldyk, aprakawis pun Jim Sing, yen boten kagalih ingkang temen, dhateng Tuwan Idelir, asanget dados sumelangipun manah kula, kula enget kala maksih panjenenganipun Bapak Kangjeng Sultan Swarga, asring ngendika yen bangsa Cina kacelakaken ing Ratu boten prayogi, asring dados witing resan ing Negari, kalih dene malih sawaruhipun Tuwan Idlir, jumeneng utusanipun para Kangjeng Tuwan ingkang Ageng, sawarninipun tiyang Ngayogya, saking pangraos kula menggah Tuwan Idlir ingkang dipun percaya, sarta pitajeng ing galih, sarehning Sultan maksih timur, boten liya amung pitajeng dhateng kula, pun Jim Sing punika kedhep-ming para bupati, wicantenipun asring ngundhakaken Gupermen Welandi, ugi kapituhu dhateng putra sentana, tuwin para bupati...
