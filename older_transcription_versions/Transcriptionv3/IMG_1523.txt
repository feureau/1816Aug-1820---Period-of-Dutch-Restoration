10 May 1820
J.C. Ellinghuysen (Res. Pasuruan) to Nahuys:
(Rawa) Desa Têmporan = a hide-out of priests, all study & Kyai Ahmad, also called Kyai Têmporan, great influence in area :.
[in red margin:] Malang Disturbances / Temporan

BGG 18 May 1819 no. 11 [superseding 13 May 1820 no. 32]:
Great praise of Nahuys for Tan Jin Sing's Help &cv. during Inspection in Borneo.

        2 " vases              } *

18 May 1820
F.E. Hardy (Res. Magelang) to d'Abo:
Delivery of Lime (1500 pikuls) from Gamping to Magelang.

[in red:] Pacitan
30 May 1820 (12 May)
Engel to Nahuys:
Murder committed By Satruna. Flees to Lorok. Because man tries to Elope & wife.

5 Jun. 1820
Insp. of Vaccine C.L. Blume to Nahuys [Dj. Br. 63 A]:
Decision to appoint Jav. Mantris as vaccinators = instead of Europeans.
Europeans not sufficient Influence over local pop. [in red: Vaccination]
