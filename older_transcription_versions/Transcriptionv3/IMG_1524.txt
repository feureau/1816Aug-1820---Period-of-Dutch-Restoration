[Dj. Br. 63 A & Besluiten - July - Sept. 1820 / Pacitan Buildings & Delivery of Cloth]

BGG 21 Jul. 1820 no. 13:
Death of Ratu Agêng - grandmother of Sultan in Yogya on 14 Jul. 1820.

St. Bos. Iv. 2nd Mil. Afdeeling, 25 Jul. 1821:
BUILDINGS PACITAN:
Repairs to Log. Opzieners / Tumênggung Pacitan: f. 200
Building wooden sheds for Drying coffee: f. 7,000
Plastering of Drying lawn: f. 700
Stone Packhouse & Sirap Roof / wooden floor: f. 20,000 *

8 Aug. 1820
Res. Kêdhu (Hardy) to Nahuys:
Panakawan of R.T. Danuningrat: Jakidin, whom he had taught to Read / write. Born Desa Cêleppan, District Ballah (Magelang). Since 1816 goes to Yogya & Takes service as magang & Sultan. Leaves Magelang without a pass.

6 Sept. 1820
Id. to Id. [Hardy to Nahuys]:
Bauwêntara earlier Patih of Dêmang of Ballah guilty of scheide delicten = flees to father-in-law R.T. Jayaningrat in desa Remame, 20 leagues from Kali Nengking, Division Probolinggo / Yogya.
Asks N. to Help Take Bauwêntara prisoner.

[in red:] Pacitan
20 Sept. 1820
Engel to d'Abo / Nahuys:
Not much Fish caught in Pacitan.
Labourers in pepper plantations - do not all have sawah.
Increase in coffee plants in Pacitan:
164,826 planted in Estates.
Pager coffee planted in Desas.

25 Sept. 1820
Delivery of cloth from Yogya: d'Abo / Capitan Cina Yogya
124 netts to govnmt:
135 corges sarongs @ 13 1/2
105 Jav. Citis @ 11
 75 corges ginggang @ 11
