4 Nov             Questions of Taxation on Toll-gates and Bazaars:
1817                   cf 3rd September 1817: gen. generals memoire        * [in red]

|                 Very CONSIDERABLE DIFFERENCES:
|                      in amount levied at toll-gates = depends entirely
|                      on whim of toll-gate keeper.
|
|                 usually large tolls levied on Roads on which not many
|                 toll-gates but used very heavily by peasant traffic
|                 during the day.
|
|                 JAVANESE usually pay MUCH higher toll than the CHINESE
|                      cf Chinese special rates:
|                      cf BEARS MORE HEAVILY on small trader (Javanese)
|* [in red]                 than Large businessman (Chinese)
|
|                 CHINESE must NOT be given any special privileges above
|                                               JAVANESE LAND HOLDERS =
|                                     causes immense DISCONTENT.
|
|                 despite LOSS OF INCOME which WOULD RESULT from
|                      decline in toll rates: BUT would be balanced
|                      By greater PEACE in the countryside =
|
|                 ways of TOLL-GATE PAYMENT = SHOULD BE Regulated:
|                      NOT ENOUGH TO FIX Rates = But also Declining scale of
|                      Rates if traveller / trader has passed through or
|                                             will pass through other gates...
|
|                 JAVANESE = CANNOT usually READ:
|                      .·. therefore very easily ROBBED blindly at Toll-gates
|                      (must be explained to them By Javanese.)
|
|                 GRADUAL INCREASE IN INLAND TRAFFIC & GOODS:
|                      .·. TOLL-GATE REVENUES would increase normally
|                                             with increase in Trade..
