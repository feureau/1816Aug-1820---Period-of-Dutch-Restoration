SECRET LETTERS
tomo 170 Djokja 1817

19 Nov. 1816
Batavia - Djogja.
QUESTION of K. MOORMO:
arguments G.G. & handling of Asst. Res Kruseman of Kijai MOORMO affair.
Extreme speed to progress to Investigating:
- Relations of K. MOORMO to Court of SOLO
- escape Investigating giving presents etc...

20 August
G.G. - Djogja
wants immediate report on implication of M.B. in Sepoy affair.

12 Sept 1816
Solo - Batavia.
M.B. LINK & SEPOY PLOT REASON:
for exile from SOLO:
Recognised as proud and unruly man, especially hated British Govnmnt., especially influence on Emperor P.B. IV.

Removal of P.B. IV's second son Pan. Purboyo: married to daughter of M.B.
cf Annoyance at criticism of conduct by CROWN PRINCE.
CROWN PRINCE prepared to call on desa inhabitants to preserve his rights against PURBOYO.

Extreme unrest over loss of Kedhu in SOLO:
idea of BRIT GOVNMNT to restore SOLO areas of Kedhu: to buy back peace at COURT by creating Purboyo as C.P.

But disappointment leads to:
SEPOY PLOT originates & emperor, M.B.
Buy support of Prang Wedana. Sepoys.
But fails cf married of P.W. for Sunan.
essentially FAMILY Quarrel Sunan – M.B.
enables Sunan to expel M.B. from court.
