[Header, right:]
AN Geheim Besluit
Commissaris-Generaal,
12 Dec. 1816 La A.

van IJ – van Braam,
21 Aug. 1816     Patih of Sala + Bupatis come to Semarang
Semarang         to compliment van IJ
                 Does not wish special ceremonies on arrival at Sala.
                 would attend Garêbêg Puasa at Court.

van IJ – van Braam,
28 Aug. 1816.    van IJ to journey to Sala following Wednesday 28 Aug.
                 Arrival on 29 Aug.
                 would spend one night at Salatiga.

van IJ to van Braam, }
          Nahuys     }
29 Aug. 1816     Announcing arrival at courts as Buitengewoon Gezant
                 to announce to PB IV / HB IV
                 that N.-Indië once more in hands of Dutch

Major Nahuys to van IJ,
Yogya 11 Sept. 1816
                 Had Informed HB IV of impending arrival of van IJ
                 only replies Terima kasih
                 Because of HB IV's little knowledge of Malay tongue
                 was not able to say more.

van IJ – J.D. Kruseman
6 Oct. 1816      that Kruseman
                 had received order from Batavia to take over
                 duty as Prov. Resident of Yogya during absence of
                 Nahuys.

Parvé – van IJ,
9 Oct. 1816      J.D. Kruseman appointed as Prov. Resident of
                 Yogya dating from 6 Oct.

27 Nov. 1816     Tax of :
J. Dirksen
