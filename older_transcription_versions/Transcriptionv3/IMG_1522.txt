[in red: Pacitan]
29 Feb. 1820
Engel to Nahuys:
Govnmt. Estates suffer from Heavy falls of Rain. Dadap trees grow well - coffee Bushes Being planted in Between.

31 Mar. 1820
Id. to Id.:
Singadana = from Desa Nalla murders another villager Drunadipa + kris, flees into forest.

BGG 21 Apr. 1820, no. 12:
Every 2 years = f. 4,000 to be paid to Paku Alam as clothing allowance, f. 20 for every cavalryman.

20 Apr. 1820
Kwee Hikoëy (Tax Farmer Tollgate: Kritik) to Nahuys:
Attacked By Robber Band of 50 persons + Tollgate plundered.
Despite sounding of Riceblocks = no-one appears.

5 May 1820
Res. Pasuruan (Elsingwitsch) to Nahuys:
Captures Yogya Inhabitants involved in affair in Malang at Desa Têmporan (Rowo):
Tirta Diraya, Markum, Amad Piyo, Amad Narjo, Dokirti, Krama Sari, Nur Sarip, Nur Rahman, Saliman, Jaka, Mêrtajaya, Santika, Wirajaya.
To be sent Back by sea to Semarang & overland via Magelang.

Nurrano  }
Nurosman } Ceribonners who had gone to Têmporan for Religious
