[Carey's Handwritten Notes: Paku Alam Corps & Handover Ceremony, August 1816]

Paku Alam corps = 100 men.
paid by PA, given horses by him.

uniforms / arms provided by British.
P.A. receives monthly Sp.D. 750 from govnmt.
for upkeep.

also small corps of European Dragoons: [circled in red]
or rather "Christian troops"
Dutch - German - and Inlandsche Kinderen.
many had earlier seen service & Dutch.
p. 322     25 + set up 8 months ago — ? Jan. 1816
in aftermath of Sepoy plot.
mounted as hussars.
Have to return weapons lent them by British from
22nd Dragoon Regiment.

Since Sepoy plot used for service in kraton.
very well suited cf know Malay
"some even Javanese"

Corps to be taken into active service & Dutch.
-------------------------------------------------------

[in red ink]: Ceremony for returning Yogya to Dutch.

Nahuys to
Commissioners-General
15 Aug. 1816. no. 7.

English commandant annoyed
wishes to march out of Yogya
before arrival of Dutch troops.

14 August.
Lunch & P.A. + sons / Tan Jin Sing / English & Dutch in Fort.

Nahuys reports official take over of Residency
