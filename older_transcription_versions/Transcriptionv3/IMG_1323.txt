Proces-verbaal of 16 Aug. 1816.

Prang Wedana & Legion need to be
taken into Dutch pay.

Huge family of PW =
1200 Sp.D. payment
+ 500 cacahs = 1200 jung of
               ricefields.

Great difficulty of PW to cloth / equip his force.

Great impoverishment for PW
in keeping up troops on his own cost for
so long.
& can no longer pay their upkeep.

Legion in existence since 1808.

British allow him to command Legion
personally without
Interference of a
European officer.
if question of moving against an
Internal enemy.
only if used v. external enemy
should a European officer be
(seconded to his legion. -> should be able to speak Jav. / Malay.
But salary / upkeep to be paid by
Dutch govnmt.

Necessary to attach
PW to Dutch govnmt. Interests. .: should
agree to his Request.
