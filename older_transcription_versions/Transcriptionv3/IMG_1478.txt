Lugu of Banyumas knows passages of Sun / Moon / Stars / Earth
     Adverbs of frequency:
     She always works hard.       & knows life history of Gusti
     She is always late.          Raden Generaal & comes & wife Princess
                                  & 11 Martens daughter (Ratu Kidul) wife of GG.
     She has always been a good girl.
General (van der Capellen) passes through Banyumas 1818 Aug.
Brother of General goes to S. & son of Adip. Brêbês to North.
                                (a descendant of Sunan Kuning)
Brother of Adip. Brêbês will
     be Ruler of the West = all my friends the Europeans will
     accompany GG = & THOSE WHO WISH TO RENEW THE WORLD.
& all women in Heaven are my consorts = heard by Lugu Aji Barang from S. Neal
& cannon Nyai Sitomi = property of GG = who knows arms of each mortal.
