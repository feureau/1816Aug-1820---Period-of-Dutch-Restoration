                Het N.I.
                Bestuur in 1817.

p. 24   31 March 1817 no. 33
        Residents of Sala / Yogya
        ordered to make a population census in
        2 weeks!
        Both Residents think that this can only take place
[In red ink, left margin:] pop. census
        + a combined mission from both courts
        with kraton representatives.
        But they feared unease it would cause
        population.
        [underlined in red: 1 May 1817 Order Rescinded.]

p. 25   Report of 14 Apr. 1817 by Nahuys important.
        not only for character sketch of PA.
        + on argument for settlement of
        Europeans in VL.
        + Better police.

        Report of Nahuys:
        "great number of Europeans in VL would raise
        standard of Industry.
        Javanese round Semarang, Surabaya, Sala, Yogya
        at present idiot lazy.
[In red ink, left margin:] N v B on / Landhuur
        Dutch should spur Javanese on to Industry
        at present only initiative in hands of Chinese
        as in Residencies governed directly by Dutch.
        greater numbers of Dutch should settle in interior
        would be a safeguard against attack by a
        European enemy"
