[Solo Br. 122 - State of Solo Court in 1824 / Title of P. Adipati]

Solo. Br. 122
Van Sevenhoven to G.G.
22 Oct. 1824 (cont.)

State of Solo Court in 1824 (cont.)
setting up of Regency & Resident:
way of giving govnmt. greater say in affairs of Sala in accord with G.G.'s secret Decision of 9 Sept. 1823.

* // If PB VI persists in behaviour should be forced to abdicate in favour of P. Purbaya.
Same as had happened in Palembang. See Woelders.
Similarities courts of Sala / Palembang, although Jav. nobles not so disharmonious & morally reprehensible as those of Palembang, also less cunning / hypocritical.

Following proposals =
(1) Permission to dissolve marriage. PB VI - Ratu Mas.
(2) Actions of PB VI make it necessary to take control of Admin. away from him.
(3) Setting up of Regency Res / Buminata as Patih

Png. Buminata to be given title of Pan[êmbahan]. To give him more Influence when appointed as Regent.
Buminata does not want throne of Sala because has no children:
Purbaya to be given title of P. Adipati = Title earlier given to all legal sons of Ruler But fallen into dissuetude after Kartasura war: although continues to be given to Crown Prince P. Adip. Anom.

Notes of Deliberations of Commission entrusted with the organisation of Tollgates
