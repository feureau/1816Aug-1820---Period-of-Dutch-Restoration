[Circled in red]: BCG, 7 Jan. 1819 no. 5    see BGG 26 Dec. 1817 no. 38 (18?)
PACITAN:
(1) not to be included in Landelijk Stelsel
(2) Res. to report on pop. in Pacitan within 3 months
(3) Each family to plant 500 pepper bushes.
(4) & between pepper bushes = coffee to be planted / trees.
If not enough coffee plants
Res. to sow coffee seeds (2 pikuls)
coffee / pepper to be sold exclusively to govnmt.

Dêmang Jagakarya = to be appointed Tum. & f. 1,200 p.a. salary
as long as he sees to production of cash crops.

Dêmang Nitrawijaya = to be dismissed & given a pension
of f. 300 p.a.

Eur. Opzichter to be appointed in Pacitan & f. 200 p.m.
& to be helped by skilful nat. officials drafted from other Res.

Bay of Pacitan to be surveyed for use as a port.
