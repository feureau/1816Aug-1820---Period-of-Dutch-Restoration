See BGG geheim, 1 Feb. 1820, no. 2.

11 Feb. 1820
Lippe to Sec. of State.
Secret no. 1.

Lippe passes on news of exile [written above: dismissal (stricken through)] of Van Prehn / Winter to PB IV from Solo + close investigation of case.
PB IV unusually cool reception to Lippe.
PB IV decision to Dismiss:
        Mas Tumênggung Sêcanagara, Head of Kalangans
        + son Mas Ngabèhi Rêksanagara, Bupati of Pangeran Cakrakusuma

After Returning from kraton - receives delegation from outer Bupati Arungbinang + R.Ng. Rejamênggala.
PB IV orders that no one should speak name of Sêcanagara - R.Ng. Rejamênggala appointed in his place.
[double red underline]

Secret
25 Feb. 1820 no. 2.

Patih Sasradiningrat comes to Lippe - tells him in name of PB IV that G.G. allows PB IV to take back all the rights he had previously enjoyed under the Batavian Republic pre 1806 + proclamation to all Solo Inhabitants to that effect.

Lippe points out that G.G.'s letter misunderstood.
Patih says he knows nothing about it.

No translation of G.G.'s letter available :. unable to solve mystery. - had G.G. abrogated 1812 treaty - Renewed by Dutch govnmt. in 1816?
Asks for instructions from G.G.
PB IV already gives out that he had been reaccorded the rights he had enjoyed under V.O.C.
